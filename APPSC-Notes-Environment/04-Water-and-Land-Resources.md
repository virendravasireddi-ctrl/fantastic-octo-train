## PART A — WATER RESOURCES

## 1. The water endowment — the numbers to quote ⭐⭐

- **India = ~18% of world population but only ~4% of global freshwater** ⭐⭐ (the single most-quoted stat).
- **Annual precipitation ~4,000 BCM** → after evaporation/runoff, **utilizable water ≈ 1,123 BCM (690 surface + 433 groundwater)** ⭐.
- **Per-capita availability falling ~1,400–1,500 m³/yr** — India is **"water-stressed" (<1,700 m³)** and approaching **"water-scarce" (<1,000 m³)** on the **Falkenmark index** ⭐.
- **Groundwater supplies ~62% of irrigation and ~85% of rural drinking water** — India is the **world's largest groundwater user**; over-extraction has created "dark/critical" blocks (Punjab, Haryana, Rayalaseema).

## 2. Types / sources of water resources

| Type | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Surface water** | Rivers, lakes, tanks, reservoirs — ~60% of utilizable; **Ganga–Brahmaputra–Meghna** carries the bulk; peninsular rivers (Godavari, Krishna) are **rain-fed & seasonal** |
| **Groundwater** | Aquifers — recharged by rain/rivers; over-drafted in hard-rock peninsula |
| **Glacial / snow-melt** | Himalayan "water towers" — perennial flow of north Indian rivers |
| **Rainwater** | Direct harvest; monsoon-dependent (~75% of rain in 4 months) |
| **Frozen/soil moisture, etc.** | Minor for use |

- **Blue vs. Green water:** *blue* = liquid surface+ground water; *green* = soil moisture used by plants (a modern exam distinction).
- **Virtual water & water footprint** — water "embedded" in traded goods (rice, sugar exports = exporting water) — a good analytical point.

## 3. Water stress — causes & consequences

- **Causes:** monsoon variability & uneven distribution (space & time), population/urban demand, **water-guzzling crops (paddy, sugarcane) + free power → over-pumping**, pollution shrinking usable water, deforestation & reduced recharge, inter-state disputes, climate change (glacier melt, erratic monsoon).
- **Consequences:** falling water tables, **fluoride/arsenic/salinity** in groundwater, drying rivers & wetlands, agrarian distress, urban "Day-Zero" risks, inter-state conflict, **subsidence**.
- **Inter-state river disputes (cite):** **Krishna & Godavari tribunals (KWDT, GWDT)** — central to AP; Cauvery, Ravi-Beas.

## 4. Watershed management ⭐ (direct syllabus item)

- **Watershed = a drainage basin — the area that drains to a common outlet (river/stream/reservoir).** **Watershed management = integrated development of land + water + vegetation within that unit**, so that runoff is slowed, recharge & soil are conserved, and land becomes productive ⭐. The unit of planning is the **micro-watershed (ridge-to-valley)**.
- **Core techniques:** **contour bunding, gully plugs, check dams, percolation tanks, farm ponds, staggered trenches, afforestation on ridges, nala (stream) bunding** — the **"ridge-to-valley"** treatment sequence.
- **Rainwater harvesting** — rooftop harvesting, recharge pits/shafts, revival of **traditional systems** (johads, tanks, stepwells; **Tamil Nadu made rooftop RWH mandatory**).

### 4.1 Programmes & missions ⭐
| Programme | Note |
|---|---|
| **Integrated Watershed Management Programme (IWMP)** → now under **PMKSY-Watershed Development Component** | Rain-fed area treatment |
| **PMKSY — Pradhan Mantri Krishi Sinchayee Yojana (2015)** ⭐ | *"Har Khet Ko Pani" + "Per Drop More Crop"* (micro-irrigation) |
| **Atal Bhujal Yojana (2019)** ⭐ | Community-led **groundwater** management in stressed blocks |
| **Jal Jeevan Mission (2019)** | Functional tap water (**"Har Ghar Jal"**) to every rural home |
| **Jal Shakti Abhiyan / Catch the Rain** | Rainwater-harvesting campaign; **Ministry of Jal Shakti (2019)** unified water governance |
| **Namami Gange** | Ganga rejuvenation |

### 4.2 Andhra Pradesh water resources & watershed work ⭐⭐
- **Rivers:** **Godavari & Krishna deltas** (the rice bowl), **Penna, Vamsadhara, Nagavali**; interior **Rayalaseema = chronically drought-prone** ⭐.
- **Projects:** **Polavaram — national project on the Godavari (AP Reorganisation Act 2014)** ⭐, **Pattiseema lift (2015) — first Godavari→Krishna delta link operational** ⭐, **Srisailam & Nagarjuna Sagar (Krishna, shared)**, proposed **Godavari–Banakacherla** link to Rayalaseema (label *proposed*).
- **Watershed / groundwater:** AP's **"Neeru–Meeru" ("Water & You")** watershed & water-conservation programme is a standard state exhibit; **APFAMGS** (Andhra Pradesh Farmer-Managed Groundwater Systems — a globally cited participatory groundwater model) ⭐; revival of the **Kakatiya tank/cascade tradition** (history-series tie-in).

## PART B — LAND RESOURCES

## 5. Land use & the land resource

- **Geographical area = 328.7 million ha**; **net sown area ≈ 140 m ha (~43%) — among the world's highest cultivated shares** ⭐; forest ~21.8% (File 02); the rest = pastures, fallow, wasteland, built-up.
- **Land degradation ⭐:** **~30% of India's land is degraded** (erosion, salinity, waterlogging, desertification); India pledged to **restore 26 million ha of degraded land by 2030** (UNCCD / Bonn Challenge commitment).
- **Mobilisation reforms (economy link):** digitised records (**SVAMITVA**, AP resurvey), **land pooling (Amaravati — 30,000+ acres)**, RFCTLARR Act 2013 for acquisition.

## 6. Soil types of India — the ICAR / classic scheme ⭐⭐

| Soil | Where | Character & crops |
|---|---|---|
| **Alluvial** (largest, ~40%) | Indo-Gangetic–Brahmaputra plains, coastal & delta (**Godavari–Krishna**) | Fertile, K-rich/N-poor; *khadar* (new) vs *bhangar* (old); rice, wheat, sugarcane |
| **Black / Regur ("cotton soil")** ⭐ | Deccan trap — Maharashtra, MP, Gujarat, **AP (parts of Rayalaseema/Telangana border)** | Clayey, moisture-retentive, self-ploughing; **cotton, pulses, oilseeds** |
| **Red & Yellow** | Peninsular crystalline — Tamil Nadu, Karnataka, **most of AP/Rayalaseema** ⭐ | Iron-rich (red colour), low fertility, needs fertiliser; millets, groundnut, pulses |
| **Laterite** | High-rainfall hills — Western Ghats, Eastern Ghats, plateau edges | Leached, acidic; tea/coffee/cashew; used as brick |
| **Arid / Desert** | Rajasthan, Kutch | Sandy, saline, low humus |
| **Saline & Alkaline** (*reh, kallar, usar*) | Arid/irrigated tracts, coasts | Salt-affected — needs **reclamation** |
| **Peaty & Marshy** | Kerala backwaters, Sundarbans, deltas | Organic-rich, waterlogged |
| **Forest & Mountain** | Himalaya, hill forests | Variable, humus in valleys |

- **AP soils in one line:** **red soils dominate (Rayalaseema/uplands), rich alluvium in the Godavari–Krishna deltas, black/regur patches, coastal saline & laterite** ⭐ — a common APPSC direct question ("characterise AP/Black/Alluvial soils").

## 7. Soil degradation & Soil reclamation ⭐ (direct syllabus item)

**Degradation processes:** water & wind **erosion (biggest — sheet, rill, gully, ravine — Chambal badlands)**, **salinisation/alkalinisation & waterlogging (canal over-irrigation)**, nutrient depletion, acidification, desertification, loss of organic matter, industrial/mining contamination.

**Soil reclamation — the toolkit (learn by problem):**

| Problem | Reclamation measure |
|---|---|
| **Saline soil** (white salts) | Leaching with good water + **drainage**; salt-tolerant crops |
| **Alkaline/sodic soil** (*usar*) | **Gypsum application** ⭐ (replaces Na with Ca), + leaching & organic manure |
| **Acidic soil** | **Liming** (add lime) |
| **Waterlogging** | Drainage channels, tube-well drainage, avoid over-irrigation |
| **Water erosion** | **Contour bunding/ploughing, terracing, check dams, gully plugging, afforestation** |
| **Wind erosion** | **Shelterbelts / windbreaks, sand-dune stabilisation** |
| **Ravines/badlands** | Ravine reclamation, afforestation, check dams |
| **Fertility loss** | Crop rotation, legumes/green manure, organic & bio-fertilisers, **fallowing** |

- **Institutions & schemes:** **ICAR / CSSRI (Karnal — salt-soil reclamation), Soil Health Card scheme (2015), National Mission for Sustainable Agriculture, Desertification & Land Degradation Atlas (ISRO/SAC)**, **compensatory afforestation** and **watershed treatment** (Part A) double as soil conservation.
- **AP link:** delta **salinity/waterlogging** from canal irrigation (Godavari–Krishna) → drainage & gypsum reclamation; Rayalaseema **red-soil + drought** → watershed + micro-irrigation.

## 8. Prelims nuggets

1. India **~18% people, ~4% freshwater**; utilizable **~1,123 BCM (690 surface + 433 ground)**; per-capita **~1,400–1,500 m³ → water-stressed (<1,700)**.
2. Groundwater = **~62% of irrigation, ~85% of rural drinking**; India = **world's largest groundwater user**.
3. **Watershed = drainage basin**; treatment = **ridge-to-valley** (contour bunds, check dams, percolation tanks).
4. **PMKSY 2015 (Per Drop More Crop); Atal Bhujal 2019 (groundwater); Jal Jeevan 2019 (Har Ghar Jal); Ministry of Jal Shakti 2019**.
5. **AP: Polavaram (national project, 2014 Act); Pattiseema 2015; Neeru-Meeru; APFAMGS**; Rayalaseema = drought-prone.
6. Geographical area **328.7 m ha**; NSA **~140 m ha (~43%)**; **~30% land degraded**; restore-target **26 m ha by 2030**.
7. Soils: **Alluvial (largest ~40%), Black/Regur (cotton), Red-Yellow (peninsula/AP), Laterite, Arid, Saline-Alkaline, Peaty, Forest**.
8. **AP = red soils dominate + delta alluvium + regur patches + coastal saline**.
9. Reclamation: **alkaline→gypsum; acidic→lime; saline→leaching+drainage; erosion→contour bunds/terracing**.
10. **CSSRI Karnal** (salt-soil), **Soil Health Card 2015**, **Chambal ravines** (gully erosion icon).

## 9. Mains angles

1. **"Water, not land, is India's binding resource constraint." Examine** — 4%-freshwater/18%-people → groundwater over-draft → demand-side reform (pricing, crop-mix, micro-irrigation) → AP's Godavari-surplus-to-Rayalaseema-deficit engineering + inter-state caveat (KWDT/GWDT).
2. **Watershed management as the answer to rain-fed India. Discuss** — ridge-to-valley logic → IWMP/PMKSY/Atal Bhujal → community models (APFAMGS, Neeru-Meeru, Ralegan Siddhi/Hiware Bazar) → limits (equity, maintenance).
3. **"Land degradation is a silent emergency." Analyse** causes → soil-reclamation toolkit → schemes (Soil Health Card, watershed, LDN-2030 target).
4. **Characterise the soils of Andhra Pradesh and suggest conservation/reclamation measures** (a repeat APPSC-style question) — red/alluvial/regur/saline map → erosion & delta-salinity problems → gypsum/drainage/watershed/micro-irrigation remedies.
