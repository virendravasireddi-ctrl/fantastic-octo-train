## 0. Framing

Fossil fuels and minerals are the **non-renewable, one-time endowment** (File 01). This file covers **coal, petroleum, natural gas** (energy minerals), **metallic & non-metallic minerals**, the **conservation & policy arc**, and **mining in AP and India** — with the environmental cost of extraction as the through-line.

## PART A — FOSSIL FUELS

## 1. Coal — "the black diamond" ⭐

- **Origin:** carbonised plant matter of the **Carboniferous & Gondwana** ages; ranks by carbon content: **Peat → Lignite → Bituminous → Anthracite** (anthracite = highest carbon/best quality, rare in India) ⭐.
- **India's coal:** **~5th-largest reserves in the world (~378 billion tonnes)**; India is the **2nd-largest coal producer & consumer**; domestic output crossed **1 billion tonnes (FY25)** yet India still **imports 200+ mt** (coking coal for steel). Most Indian coal = **Gondwana (~99%), bituminous, high-ash**.
- **Coalfields:** **Damodar valley (Jharia, Raniganj, Bokaro — Jharkhand/WB), Son-Mahanadi (Korba, Talcher), Godavari valley** ⭐. **Coal India Limited (CIL)** dominates; **commercial coal mining opened to private sector (2020)**.
- **Environmental cost:** ~**70% of India's electricity generated from coal** ⭐ (capacity ≠ generation trap — File 01); CO₂, fly-ash, acid-mine drainage, land degradation, black-lung; **just-transition** challenge for coal districts.

### 1.1 AP/Godavari coal
- **Singareni Collieries (SCCL)** works the **Godavari Valley coalfield** (now largely in Telangana post-2014 — Kothagudem, Ramagundam); residual AP coal is minor. Cite Singareni as the region's coal story.

## 2. Petroleum (crude oil) ⭐

- **Origin:** marine organic matter in **sedimentary basins** ("cap rock–reservoir–source rock" anticline trap).
- **India's dependence ⭐⭐:** imports **~85–88% of crude** — the single biggest strategic & current-account vulnerability; crude import bill ≈ $130–140 bn.
- **Basins/fields:** **Mumbai High (offshore, ONGC — India's biggest)**, **Assam (Digboi — India's oldest, 1889; Naharkatiya)**, **Gujarat (Ankleshwar, Cambay)**, **Rajasthan (Barmer — Cairn/Mangala)**, **Krishna–Godavari (KG) basin (AP)** ⭐.
- **Refining:** capacity **~257 MMTPA (4th-largest globally)**; **Jamnagar (Reliance) = world's largest refining complex**; **HPCL Visakh refinery (AP)** expanded to **15 MMTPA**.
- **Strategic Petroleum Reserves (SPR):** **Visakhapatnam (AP) was India's FIRST SPR (1.33 MMT)** ⭐, plus Mangaluru & Padur.

## 3. Natural gas ⭐

- **Cleanest fossil fuel**; India targets raising gas to **15% of the energy mix by 2030 (from ~6%)** — the "gas-based economy" push.
- **Sources:** **KG basin (RIL KG-D6, ONGC KG-DWN-98/2 — first oil Jan 2024)** ⭐, Mumbai High, Assam, Tripura, imported **LNG** (Dahej, Hazira terminals).
- **Uses:** power, fertiliser (feedstock), **City Gas Distribution (PNG/CNG)**; **PNGRB** regulates; **HELP/OALP (2016)** licensing regime replaced NELP.
- **Unconventional:** **Coal-Bed Methane (CBM), shale gas, gas hydrates (KG basin has huge hydrate potential)** ⭐.

### 3.1 Krishna–Godavari (KG) basin — the AP hydrocarbon crown ⭐⭐
The **KG basin (offshore Bay of Bengal, off AP coast)** is India's premier deep-water oil-and-gas province — **RIL KG-D6** gas and **ONGC's KG-DWN-98/2 (first oil Jan 2024)**; also India's biggest **gas-hydrate** prospect. **The single most important AP energy-resource fact.**

## PART B — MINERAL RESOURCES

## 4. Classifying minerals

| Class | Examples |
|---|---|
| **Metallic — Ferrous** | Iron ore, manganese, chromite, nickel, cobalt |
| **Metallic — Non-ferrous** | Copper, bauxite (aluminium), lead, zinc, gold, silver |
| **Non-metallic** | Limestone, mica, gypsum, dolomite, phosphate, barytes |
| **Energy/atomic** | Coal, uranium, thorium (monazite), lignite |

- **India's mineral belts:** the **"mineral heartland" = Chhota Nagpur plateau (Jharkhand–Odisha–Chhattisgarh–WB)** ⭐; **North-Eastern Peninsular belt** (iron/coal), **South-Western (Karnataka iron/gold), Rajasthan (non-ferrous)**.
- **India's global ranks (approx.):** **2nd in coal, 2nd–4th in iron ore, top in mica, 2nd in barytes**; **import-dependent for copper, nickel, cobalt, lithium, rare earths (the "critical minerals")** ⭐.

## 5. Depletion of metals/minerals & Conservation Policy ⭐ (direct syllabus item)

- **The problem:** high-grade ores exhausting; rising import dependence for **critical minerals** that clean-energy/EV/electronics need (lithium, cobalt, nickel, REEs, graphite) — dominated globally by **China**.
- **Conservation & policy toolkit:**
  1. **Recycling / urban mining** (scrap steel, e-waste, battery recycling) & **Extended Producer Responsibility**.
  2. **Substitution & efficient use** (low-grade beneficiation, aluminium for copper).
  3. **Auction-based allocation & revenue-sharing** — see the MMDR arc below.
  4. **Strategic security** — **KABIL (2019)** buys mineral assets abroad; **National Critical Mineral Mission (Jan 2025, ~₹16,300 cr)** ⭐; **lithium find at Reasi, J&K (2023, ~5.9 mt inferred)**.
  5. **Circular economy & inter-generational reinvestment** (DMF).

### 5.1 The MMDR policy arc — learn the chain ⭐⭐
**MMDR Act 1957** → **2015 amendment (compulsory AUCTIONS + District Mineral Foundation (DMF) + National Mineral Exploration Trust (NMET))** → **National Mineral Policy 2019** → **2021 amendment (captive/merchant easing)** → **2023 amendment (critical & deep-seated minerals; private exploration licences; lithium/atomic minerals de-listed for private mining)** → **Offshore Areas Mineral amendment 2023** → **National Critical Mineral Mission (2025)**.
- **DMF / PMKKKY** — a slice of mining royalty is ring-fenced for **development of mining-affected districts & people** ⭐ (the "mining-with-justice" instrument).

## 6. Environmental & social cost of mining

- **Environmental:** deforestation & habitat loss, **overburden dumps & tailings**, air/dust & water pollution, **acid-mine drainage**, subsidence, river siltation, biodiversity loss.
- **Social:** **displacement & rehabilitation**, tribal-rights conflict (Fifth Schedule areas), illegal mining, health (silicosis).
- **Regulation:** **EIA/environmental clearance** (File 07), **Forest Clearance (FC Act 1980)**, **Samatha judgment (1997)** ⭐ — SC barred transfer of tribal land in Scheduled Areas to private miners (an **AP-origin case** — Borra/Eastern Ghats bauxite); **PESA 1996** gram-sabha consent; **sustainable/"responsible" mining, mine-closure & reclamation** norms.

## 7. Mining in Andhra Pradesh & India ⭐⭐ (AP-core)

### 7.1 AP minerals box
| Resource | Fact ⭐ |
|---|---|
| **Barytes — Mangampeta (YSR Kadapa)** | **World's single largest barytes deposit** ⭐⭐ (APMDC-mined) |
| **Uranium — Tummalapalle (YSR Kadapa)** | Among the **world's largest uranium reserves**; UCIL mine — fuels the nuclear cycle ⭐ |
| **Beach sands (Srikakulam–Visakhapatnam–Nellore coast)** | **Monazite (thorium), ilmenite, garnet** — feeds the **3-stage nuclear programme** ⭐ |
| **Bauxite — Eastern Ghats (Visakhapatnam agency)** | Large reserves; mining restricted by **tribal/Fifth-Schedule & Samatha** sensitivities ⭐ |
| **Limestone (Krishna, Kurnool, Kadapa, Guntur)** | Base of AP's large **cement** industry |
| **Granite / dimension stone** | **"Black Galaxy" granite — Chimakurthy (Prakasam)** — world-famous export ⭐ |
| **Diamonds (historic)** | **Kollur (Krishna) — Golconda diamonds (Koh-i-Noor lore)** ⭐ (history-series link) |
| **Mica, dolomite, quartz, feldspar, calcite** | Various pegmatite/basin sources |
| **Hydrocarbons** | **KG basin** (Part A) |

- **APMDC** (AP Mineral Development Corporation) — the state PSU; **AP is a leading state for barytes, granite, limestone, beach-sand minerals**.

### 7.2 India — the mineral-mining picture in one line
Iron ore (**Odisha, Karnataka–Bellary, Chhattisgarh, Jharkhand**), coal (Damodar/Son/Mahanadi), bauxite (**Odisha–Koraput, AP Eastern Ghats**), copper (**Khetri-Rajasthan, MP-Malanjkhand**), gold (**Kolar/Hutti-Karnataka**), mica (Jharkhand–Bihar–Andhra Nellore belt), manganese (Maharashtra–MP), limestone (nationwide). **Illegal mining** (Bellary iron ore) and **critical-mineral import dependence** are the two live policy sores.

## 8. Prelims nuggets

1. Coal rank order: **Peat < Lignite < Bituminous < Anthracite**; India coal reserves **~378 bt (5th)**; **~70% of electricity from coal**.
2. **Digboi (Assam, 1889) = India's oldest oilfield/refinery**; **Mumbai High = biggest**; crude import **~85–88%**.
3. **KG basin (AP) — ONGC 98/2 first oil Jan 2024; RIL KG-D6**; **SPR-1 = Visakhapatnam (1.33 MMT)**; gas target **15% by 2030**.
4. **MMDR 1957 → 2015 (auctions + DMF + NMET) → 2023 (critical minerals + exploration licences)**; **NCMM 2025**; **KABIL 2019**; **lithium — Reasi 2023**.
5. **Mangampeta = world's largest barytes; Tummalapalle = world-class uranium; Black Galaxy granite = Chimakurthy** ⭐.
6. **AP beach sands = monazite/thorium** → 3-stage nuclear programme; **Kollur = Golconda diamonds**.
7. **Samatha judgment (1997)** — bars tribal-land transfer to private miners in Scheduled Areas (AP-origin); **PESA 1996** consent.
8. Mineral heartland = **Chhota Nagpur plateau**; **DMF/PMKKKY** = mining revenue to affected districts.
9. Refining capacity **~257 MMTPA (4th)**; **Jamnagar = world's largest refinery**; **HPCL Visakh = 15 MMTPA**.
10. Unconventional gas: **CBM, shale, gas hydrates (KG basin)**.

## 9. Mains angles

1. **"India's mineral wealth is a curse unless it is mined responsibly." Discuss** — resource-curse/illegal-mining → EIA + FC Act + Samatha/PESA safeguards → DMF reinvestment → mine-closure & reclamation.
2. **Critical minerals are the new oil — assess India's strategy.** import dependence (China) → NCMM/KABIL/auctions + Reasi lithium → recycling/EPR → AP beach-sands & value-addition.
3. **"For Andhra Pradesh, minerals and hydrocarbons are the base of industrialisation." Evaluate** — Mangampeta/Tummalapalle/Black Galaxy/KG basin → cement & nuclear linkages → Eastern-Ghats bauxite dilemma (tribal rights) → sustainable-mining verdict.
4. **Fossil fuels vs. the energy transition.** coal-70%-of-generation reality → import bill (oil/gas) → gas-as-bridge & renewables → just transition for coal districts.
