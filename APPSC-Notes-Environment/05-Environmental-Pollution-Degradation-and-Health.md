## 1. Pollution — the core concept

- **Pollution** = introduction of contaminants (**pollutants**) into the environment at levels that harm health, ecosystems or amenity. A **pollutant** is the agent; classify it as:
  - **By persistence:** **Biodegradable** (sewage, food waste — decompose) vs **Non-biodegradable / persistent** (plastics, DDT, heavy metals, radioactive waste) ⭐.
  - **By behaviour:** **Primary** (emitted directly — SO₂, CO) vs **Secondary** (formed in the environment — **ozone (O₃), PAN, smog**) ⭐.
  - **By source geometry:** **Point** (a chimney, an outfall) vs **Non-point/diffuse** (farm run-off, urban run-off).

### 1.1 Natural vs. Anthropogenic sources ⭐⭐ (direct syllabus item)

| **Natural pollution** | **Anthropogenic (human-made) pollution** |
|---|---|
| Volcanic ash & SO₂, forest fires, dust storms, pollen, sea-salt aerosol, methane from wetlands, radon, natural fluoride/arsenic in groundwater, algal blooms, ozone from lightning | Vehicular & industrial emissions, thermal-power SO₂/NOₓ/fly-ash, sewage & effluents, agro-chemicals (fertiliser/pesticide run-off), plastics & e-waste, mining dust, construction dust, biomass burning (stubble), noise, radioactive & thermal discharge |

- **Key exam point:** natural events *episodically* pollute, but **chronic, large-scale, non-biodegradable pollution is overwhelmingly anthropogenic** — and it is what regulation targets. Some pollutants (fluoride, arsenic, radon, ozone) have **both** natural and human contributions.

## 2. The major kinds of pollution

### 2.1 Air pollution ⭐
- **Key pollutants & health:** **PM2.5 & PM10 (respirable particulates — the #1 killer), SO₂, NOₓ, CO, ground-level O₃, Pb, VOCs**; **CPCB's National Air Quality Index (AQI) — 8 pollutants, 6 categories (Good→Severe)** ⭐.
- **Phenomena:** **smog (London/classic sulphurous vs Los Angeles/photochemical)**, **temperature inversion** trapping pollutants (Delhi winter), acid rain, **stubble burning** (Punjab–Haryana → Delhi NCR).
- **Bhopal Gas Tragedy (Dec 1984 — MIC leak, Union Carbide)** ⭐ — worst industrial disaster; catalysed the **Environment (Protection) Act 1986**.
- **Policy:** **Air Act 1981**, **National Clean Air Programme (NCAP, 2019 — 40% PM reduction by 2026 in target cities)** ⭐, BS-VI fuels (2020), FAME/EV push, **Graded Response Action Plan (Delhi)**, **Commission for Air Quality Management (CAQM)**.

### 2.2 Water pollution ⭐
- **Sources:** untreated **sewage (the largest source in India)**, industrial effluents, agro-chemical run-off (→ **eutrophication** & algal blooms), fecal coliforms; **groundwater — fluoride, arsenic (Bengal/Bihar), nitrate, salinity**.
- **Indicators:** **BOD, COD, DO, coliform count**; thermal pollution (power-plant discharge).
- **Icon cases:** Ganga & Yamuna pollution; **Kolleru (AP)** aquaculture load; **minamata disease (mercury, Japan)** & **itai-itai (cadmium)** — global classics.
- **Policy:** **Water Act 1974**, **Namami Gange**, sewage-treatment (STP) push, **CRZ** for coasts, effluent norms/ZLD for industry.

### 2.3 Soil / land pollution
- Plastics, industrial & mining waste, **fly-ash**, excess agro-chemicals, **e-waste** heavy metals, biomedical waste; leachate from landfills.

### 2.4 Solid & hazardous waste ⭐
- **MSW** (~62+ mt/yr, low segregation), **Swachh Bharat Mission (2014)**; **Plastic Waste Management Rules (single-use plastic ban, 2022) + Extended Producer Responsibility**; **E-Waste (Management) Rules**; **Biomedical Waste Rules (COVID surge)**; **Hazardous & Other Wastes Rules**.

### 2.5 Noise, thermal, radioactive, light, marine & plastic pollution
- **Noise** (Noise Pollution Rules 2000 — day/night limits by zone); **thermal** (power/industry heat to water); **radioactive** (Chernobyl 1986, **Fukushima 2011**); **light pollution**; **marine — oil spills, microplastics, ocean acidification**; **plastic — "Great Pacific Garbage Patch", microplastics in food chain** ⭐.

## 3. Environmental degradation — the broader concept ⭐

- **Degradation** = deterioration of the environment through **depletion of resources + pollution + ecosystem destruction** — wider than pollution alone. Encompasses **deforestation, desertification, soil erosion, biodiversity loss, wetland loss, groundwater depletion, coastal erosion, urban heat islands**.
- **Drivers ("IPAT"):** **Impact = Population × Affluence × Technology** ⭐ — a quotable framework. Add: poverty (both cause & victim), market failure (unpriced externalities), weak governance.
- **Bioaccumulation & Biomagnification ⭐:** persistent toxins (DDT, mercury, PCBs) **concentrate up the food chain** — top predators (and humans) get the highest dose; the mechanism behind *Silent Spring*.

## 4. Correlation between Health & Environment ⭐⭐ (direct syllabus item)

- **Core idea — "Environmental health":** a large share of the disease burden is **environmentally mediated**. The **WHO estimates ~a quarter of the global disease burden** is attributable to modifiable environmental factors; India carries one of the world's heaviest **air-pollution** health burdens.
- **Pathways (learn the pollutant → disease map):**

| Environmental factor | Health effect |
|---|---|
| **Air (PM2.5, SO₂, NOₓ, O₃)** | Respiratory (asthma, COPD, lung cancer), cardiovascular disease, low birth-weight; **household air pollution from biomass cooking** (a huge rural burden — the **Ujjwala LPG** rationale) ⭐ |
| **Unsafe water & sanitation** | Diarrhoea, cholera, typhoid, hepatitis, **fluorosis (dental/skeletal — endemic in AP/Rayalaseema, Nalgonda) & arsenicosis** ⭐ |
| **Vector habitats** | Malaria, dengue, chikungunya, filariasis (linked to stagnant water, urbanisation, warming) |
| **Chemical exposure** | Heavy-metal poisoning (lead → IQ loss; mercury → Minamata), pesticide poisoning, cancers |
| **Climate change** | Heat-stroke, expanded vector range, malnutrition (crop failure), disaster injuries, mental health ⭐ |
| **Noise** | Hypertension, hearing loss, stress |

- **Concepts to quote:** **"One Health"** (human + animal + ecosystem health integrated — zoonoses, AMR, pandemics) ⭐; **Environmental Justice** (pollution burden falls disproportionately on the poor — File 06 links to climate justice); **DALYs** (disability-adjusted life-years) as the measure.
- **Bhopal & Minamata** are the set-piece human-cost cases. **Fluorosis in Rayalaseema/erstwhile Nalgonda** is the **AP-specific** environment–health exhibit ⭐.

## 5. Institutions & law (pollution-control frame)

- **CPCB (1974)** + **State PCBs — Andhra Pradesh Pollution Control Board (APPCB)** ⭐ (APPSC has directly asked about APPCB's role, incl. **post-disaster pollution management**); **MoEFCC**; **NGT (2010)**; **Environment (Protection) Act 1986** umbrella + **Water Act 1974**, **Air Act 1981**, waste-management rules; **Polluter Pays & Precautionary principles** (read into law by the SC — *Vellore Citizens Forum, 1996*) ⭐; **Public Trust Doctrine** (*M.C. Mehta*).
