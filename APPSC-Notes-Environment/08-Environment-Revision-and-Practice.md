## 3. "Firsts, largest & superlatives" ⭐

- **First EIA law** — NEPA, USA (1969); **first global environment summit** — Stockholm (1972).
- **Umbrella environmental law (India)** — **Environment (Protection) Act, 1986**.
- **Most successful environmental treaty** — **Montreal Protocol (ozone)**.
- **India** — **2nd-largest fish producer**, **world's largest shrimp exporter**, **largest groundwater user**, **3rd-largest CO₂ emitter (but low per-capita)**.
- **Largest forest area (state)** — **Madhya Pradesh**; forest cover **21.76%** (ISFR 2023).
- **World's largest barytes deposit** — **Mangampeta, YSR Kadapa (AP)**; world-class **uranium — Tummalapalle (AP)**.
- **AP** — **largest fish-producing state; ~70% of India's shrimp exports; India's first SPR (Visakhapatnam)**.
- **India = 1st country with a National Agroforestry Policy (2014)**.
- **Tsunami warning** — **INCOIS, Hyderabad (ITEWC, 2007)**; **cyclone naming** — IMD (RSMC New Delhi).

## 5. Twenty MCQs (key at the end) ⭐

1. Forest cover of India per ISFR 2023 is about — (a) 24.6% (b) **21.76%** (c) 19.4% (d) 33%.
2. "Renewable but exhaustible" best describes — (a) solar (b) wind (c) **groundwater** (d) tidal.
3. The Environment (Protection) Act was enacted in the wake of — (a) Chipko (b) **Bhopal disaster** (c) Stockholm (d) Rio.
4. "Our Common Future" (1987) is associated with — (a) IPCC (b) **Brundtland Commission** (c) Club of Rome (d) UNEP.
5. Compulsory mineral auctions were introduced by the — (a) MMDR 1957 (b) **MMDR Amendment 2015** (c) NMP 2019 (d) MMDR 2023.
6. World's largest single barytes deposit is at — (a) Tummalapalle (b) **Mangampeta** (c) Chimakurthy (d) Kollur.
7. The National Agroforestry Policy was adopted by India in — (a) 2006 (b) **2014** (c) 2019 (d) 2020.
8. Gypsum is used to reclaim — (a) acidic (b) **alkaline/sodic** (c) waterlogged (d) laterite soils.
9. India's tsunami early-warning centre is at — (a) New Delhi (b) Chennai (c) **Hyderabad (INCOIS)** (d) Vizag.
10. Seismic Zone V includes all EXCEPT — (a) Kutch (b) NE India (c) Himalayas (d) **coastal Andhra plains**.
11. The most successful environmental treaty is the — (a) Kyoto (b) Paris (c) **Montreal** (d) Basel.
12. India's "Panchamrit" pledge was announced at — (a) Paris (b) **Glasgow (COP-26)** (c) Dubai (d) Sharm el-Sheikh.
13. CBDR-RC is a principle of the — (a) CBD (b) **UNFCCC** (c) CITES (d) Ramsar.
14. Category-A projects under EIA 2006 are appraised by — (a) SEIAA (b) **MoEFCC/EAC** (c) CPCB (d) NGT.
15. India is the world's ___ largest fish producer — (a) 1st (b) **2nd** (c) 3rd (d) 4th.
16. Biomagnification is best illustrated by — (a) CO₂ (b) **DDT/mercury** (c) ozone (d) SO₂.
17. The NAPCC (2008) has how many missions — (a) 5 (b) **8** (c) 10 (d) 12.
18. "Regur" soil is also called — (a) laterite (b) alluvial (c) **black cotton soil** (d) red soil.
19. Red Sanders, a smuggling-prone endemic tree, is found in — (a) Sundarbans (b) **Seshachalam hills (AP)** (c) Nilgiris (d) Aravallis.
20. The nodal agency for landslide hazard mapping in India is — (a) IMD (b) NCS (c) **GSI** (d) INCOIS.

**Key:** 1-b, 2-c, 3-b, 4-b, 5-b, 6-b, 7-b, 8-b, 9-c, 10-d, 11-c, 12-b, 13-b, 14-b, 15-b, 16-b, 17-b, 18-c, 19-b, 20-c.

## 6. Twelve Mains answer-skeletons ⭐

1. **Development vs. environment — a false binary?** → poles → sustainable-development synthesis → India's twin duty → EIA/SDG instruments.
2. **Renewable ≠ inexhaustible** → groundwater/soil/fisheries → sustainable-yield policy.
3. **Conservation of metals & minerals as inter-generational equity** → recycle/substitute/reinvest (DMF) → critical-minerals security (NCMM/KABIL).
4. **Social forestry — good idea, flawed execution** → four components → eucalyptus critique → JFM/FRA correction (AP's VSS).
5. **Andhra Pradesh as India's aqua-capital — triumph with an asterisk** → export dominance → Kolleru/mangrove/groundwater cost → sustainable roadmap.
6. **Water is India's binding constraint** → 4%-freshwater/18%-people → groundwater over-draft → demand-side reform → AP Godavari-to-Rayalaseema engineering.
7. **Factors responsible for environmental pollution** (2020 PYQ) → natural vs anthropogenic → four media → IPAT drivers → policy.
8. **Environment & public health are two sides of one coin** → pollutant→disease map → air/water(fluorosis-AP)/climate → One Health.
9. **Concept & importance of Sustainable Development** (2020 PYQ) → Brundtland → 3 pillars → Stockholm-to-SDGs → possibilities vs challenges.
10. **Climate change is science; climate justice is politics** → IPCC → differentiated impacts → CBDR/loss-&-damage → India/LiFE.
11. **What is EIA? Evaluate & improve it** (2023 PYQ) → framework/stages → critique → reforms (independent authority, SEA, PARIVESH).
12. **Vulnerability of AP to cyclones & floods** (2025 PYQ) → Bay-of-Bengal + delta coast → surge/flood mechanics → district patterns → IMD/NCRMP/mangroves/evacuation.

## 7. Last-mile checklist ⭐

- [ ] Say **21.76% forest cover (ISFR 2023); 33% NFP-1988 target; MP largest**.
- [ ] Distinguish **renewable-but-exhaustible** and **recyclable (metals) vs non-recyclable (fossil fuels)**.
- [ ] Chain the **MMDR arc (1957→2015 auctions/DMF→2023 critical minerals)**; name **Mangampeta/Tummalapalle/Black Galaxy/KG basin**.
- [ ] Water stats: **4% freshwater/18% people; ~1,123 BCM; water-stressed <1,700 m³; groundwater 62% of irrigation**.
- [ ] Watershed = **ridge-to-valley**; schemes **PMKSY/Atal Bhujal/Jal Jeevan**; AP **Polavaram/Pattiseema/Neeru-Meeru/APFAMGS**.
- [ ] Soils: **alluvial/regur/red/laterite/saline**; reclamation **gypsum(alkaline)/lime(acidic)/drainage(saline)**.
- [ ] Pollution: **natural vs anthropogenic; primary vs secondary; biomagnification; APPCB; Polluter-Pays/Precautionary**.
- [ ] Sustainable development: **Brundtland 1987; 3 pillars; Stockholm→Rio→SDGs (17, 2015)**.
- [ ] Climate: **UNFCCC 1992→Kyoto 1997→Paris 2015; Panchamrit; net-zero 2070; 50% non-fossil met 2025; NAPCC 8 missions**.
- [ ] Climate justice: **CBDR-RC, historical responsibility, loss & damage, LiFE; SC M.K. Ranjitsinh 2024**.
- [ ] EIA: **NEPA 1969; EP Act 1986→1994→2006; Cat A/B; 8 stages; critique + reforms**.
- [ ] Disasters: **DM Act 2005/NDMA; Sendai 2015–30; IMD (cyclones), NCS (quakes, Zones II–V), GSI (landslides), INCOIS-Hyderabad (tsunami)**; AP = **multi-hazard**.
