## PART A — SUSTAINABLE DEVELOPMENT

## 1. Concept & definition ⭐⭐

- **The classic definition (Brundtland Commission, *Our Common Future*, 1987):** *"Development that meets the needs of the present without compromising the ability of future generations to meet their own needs."* ⭐⭐ — memorise verbatim; the twin ideas are **needs** (esp. of the poor) and **limits** (of the environment/technology).
- **The three pillars ("triple bottom line"):** **Economic + Social + Environmental** sustainability — often drawn as three overlapping circles or nested (economy inside society inside biosphere) ⭐.
- **Core principles:** **inter-generational equity** (fairness across time), **intra-generational equity** (fairness within this generation), **precautionary principle**, **polluter-pays**, **common-but-differentiated responsibility (CBDR)**.

## 2. The global journey — conferences & the SDGs ⭐⭐

| Year | Milestone | Outcome |
|---|---|---|
| **1972** | **Stockholm Conference** (UN Conf. on Human Environment) ⭐ | First global environment summit → created **UNEP** (HQ Nairobi); **World Environment Day, June 5** |
| **1987** | **Brundtland Report** *Our Common Future* | Defined "sustainable development" |
| **1992** | **Rio Earth Summit (UNCED)** ⭐ | **Agenda 21**; opened **UNFCCC, CBD, UNCCD**; Rio principles (CBDR, precaution) |
| **2000–2015** | **Millennium Development Goals (MDGs)** — 8 goals | Poverty/health/education focus |
| **2002 / 2012** | Johannesburg (Rio+10) / **Rio+20** | "The Future We Want"; launched SDG process |
| **2015** | **UN 2030 Agenda — Sustainable Development Goals** ⭐⭐ | **17 SDGs, 169 targets** (2016–2030); "leave no one behind" |
| **2015** | **Paris Agreement (COP-21)** | Climate treaty (Part B) |

- **The 17 SDGs (know the frame):** SDG-1 No Poverty … SDG-6 Clean Water … SDG-7 Affordable Clean Energy … SDG-11 Sustainable Cities … SDG-12 Responsible Consumption … SDG-13 Climate Action … SDG-14 Life Below Water … SDG-15 Life on Land … SDG-17 Partnerships. **India's Voluntary National Reviews; NITI Aayog SDG India Index** (states ranked — a good federal/AP data hook) ⭐.

## 3. Sustainable development — possibilities & challenges (the syllabus phrasing) ⭐

**Possibilities / opportunities:**
- **Decoupling** growth from resource/emissions (renewables at grid parity, EVs, green hydrogen, circular economy).
- **Green jobs & new industries** (solar, wind, recycling, eco-tourism, organic farming).
- **Technology leapfrogging** (India skipping to solar/digital), **natural-capital & ecosystem-service accounting**, **green finance** (green bonds, ESG).
- **India's assets:** young workforce, high solar potential, **LiFE — "Lifestyle for Environment" (India's global campaign for mindful consumption, 2021)** ⭐, traditional low-footprint practices.

**Challenges / constraints:**
- **The growth–equity–environment trilemma** — a poor, populous country needs energy/steel/cement first.
- **Cost & finance gap**, technology dependence, **jobs-vs-environment** trade-offs (coal districts), weak institutional capacity & enforcement.
- **Global free-riding** (climate is a commons — File 01), unmet **climate-finance** promises, consumption-driven demand, population & urbanisation pressure.
- **Measurement:** GDP ignores depletion — need **Green GDP / Genuine Progress Indicator / Gross Environmental Product** (Uttarakhand's GEP pilot).

## PART B — CLIMATE CHANGE & ITS EFFECTS ON THE WORLD

## 4. The science in brief ⭐

- **Greenhouse effect** — GHGs (**CO₂, CH₄, N₂O, water vapour, O₃, F-gases/HFCs**) trap outgoing infrared → warming. **CO₂ = largest contributor; methane = far more potent per molecule** (short-lived).
- **Global warming:** ~**1.1–1.2°C above pre-industrial** already; the **IPCC (Intergovernmental Panel on Climate Change; set up 1988 by WMO+UNEP)** is the authority — its **Sixth Assessment Report (AR6, 2021–23)** states warming is **"unequivocally" human-caused** ⭐.
- **Carbon budget & thresholds:** the **1.5°C vs 2°C** guardrails; tipping points (ice-sheet collapse, AMOC slowdown, Amazon dieback, permafrost methane).

## 5. Effects on the world ⭐⭐

| Domain | Impact |
|---|---|
| **Temperature** | More frequent/intense heatwaves; warmest years on record clustering |
| **Cryosphere** | Glacier & ice-sheet melt (Himalaya "third pole"), **GLOFs** (glacial-lake outburst floods), Arctic sea-ice loss |
| **Oceans** | **Sea-level rise** (thermal expansion + melt) → coastal & island inundation; **ocean acidification & warming → coral bleaching**; marine-stock shifts |
| **Water cycle** | Erratic monsoon, more extreme rainfall & floods **and** droughts; shrinking dry-season river flow |
| **Weather extremes** | Stronger cyclones, cloudbursts, wildfires |
| **Biosphere** | Species range shifts & extinctions, phenology mismatch, ecosystem collapse |
| **Human systems** | Food & water insecurity, health (File 05), migration ("climate refugees"), economic loss, conflict |

- **India's specific vulnerability ⭐:** long **densely-populated coastline** (sea-level rise; **AP/Bay-of-Bengal cyclones** — File 07), **Himalayan glacier** dependence, **monsoon-dependent agriculture**, heat stress; India is among the **most climate-vulnerable large economies** despite low per-capita emissions — the moral basis of its climate-justice argument.

## 6. Global & Indian climate policy ⭐⭐

| Instrument | Note |
|---|---|
| **UNFCCC (1992)** | Parent convention; **CBDR-RC** principle; annual **COP** meetings |
| **Kyoto Protocol (1997)** | Binding cuts on **developed (Annex-I)** countries only; carbon markets/CDM |
| **Paris Agreement (2015, COP-21)** ⭐⭐ | Hold warming **"well below 2°C, pursue 1.5°C"**; **bottom-up NDCs**; net-zero mid-century; $100 bn/yr climate-finance pledge; **Global Stocktake** |
| **COP-26 Glasgow (2021)** | India's **"Panchamrit"** pledge ⭐ |
| **COP-28 Dubai (2023)** | First **Global Stocktake**; "transitioning away from fossil fuels"; **Loss & Damage Fund** operationalised ⭐ |
| **Montreal Protocol (1987) + Kigali Amendment (2016)** | Ozone layer — phase out CFCs, then **HFCs** (climate co-benefit); the **most successful** environmental treaty ⭐ |
| **IPCC** | Science assessments (AR6) |

**India's climate commitments — "Panchamrit" (Glasgow 2021) & updated NDC (2022):** ⭐⭐
1. **Non-fossil electricity capacity → 500 GW by 2030.**
2. **50% of electric capacity from non-fossil sources by 2030** — *achieved in 2025, five years early* ⭐.
3. **Reduce projected carbon emissions by 1 billion tonnes by 2030.**
4. **Reduce emissions intensity of GDP by 45% (vs 2005) by 2030.**
5. **Net-zero by 2070.** ⭐

- **Domestic architecture:** **NAPCC (2008) — 8 missions** (Solar, Enhanced Energy Efficiency, Sustainable Habitat, Water, Sustaining Himalayan Ecosystem, **Green India**, Sustainable Agriculture, Strategic Knowledge) + **State Action Plans on Climate Change (SAPCC — incl. Andhra Pradesh)** ⭐; **International Solar Alliance (India–France, 2015; HQ Gurugram)**, **CDRI (Coalition for Disaster-Resilient Infrastructure, 2019)**, **National Green Hydrogen Mission (2023)**, **LiFE campaign**, **carbon-credit trading scheme (2023)**, **sovereign green bonds**.

## PART C — CLIMATE JUSTICE

## 7. Climate justice — a global phenomenon ⭐⭐ (direct syllabus item)

- **The idea:** climate change is not just a scientific/economic problem but an **ethical & equity** one — *"those who did least to cause it suffer most, and are least able to adapt."* ⭐ Climate justice links the atmosphere-as-commons to **human rights**.
- **Three dimensions:**
  1. **Between nations (historical/North–South):** the **Global North** built its wealth on cumulative emissions; the **Global South** bears the worst impacts → grounds for **CBDR-RC**, **climate finance ($100 bn/yr pledge, now the "New Collective Quantified Goal")**, **technology transfer**, and the **Loss & Damage Fund (COP-27 agreed, COP-28 operationalised)** ⭐.
  2. **Within nations (intra-generational):** the poor, women, tribals, coastal & farming communities are most exposed and least insured → overlaps with **environmental justice** (File 05).
  3. **Across generations (inter-generational):** today's emissions burden tomorrow's citizens → youth movements (**Fridays for Future / Greta Thunberg**), **climate litigation** (rights-of-future-generations cases worldwide).
- **India's stance ⭐:** champions climate justice — **low per-capita & historical emissions**, demands **"carbon space" / equitable burden-sharing, finance & technology** from developed nations, and frames **LiFE** as a demand-side justice message ("the rich must consume less"). PM's phrase: developed nations must show **"climate finance and climate justice."**
- **Key equity concepts to quote:** **historical responsibility, per-capita entitlement, "common but differentiated responsibilities and respective capabilities (CBDR-RC)", just transition, loss & damage, polluter-pays.**
- **Indian judicial angle ⭐:** the Supreme Court in *M.K. Ranjitsinh v. Union of India (2024)* recognised a **"right to be free from the adverse effects of climate change"** under Articles 21 & 14 — a citable domestic climate-justice landmark.
