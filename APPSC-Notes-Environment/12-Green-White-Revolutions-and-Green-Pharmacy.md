## 2. Green Revolution ⭐⭐

- **What:** the sharp **rise in foodgrain (esp. wheat) output from the mid-1960s** through modern inputs; made India **self-sufficient in foodgrains** and ended "ship-to-mouth" food imports.
- **Architects:** **Norman Borlaug — "Father of the Green Revolution" (Nobel Peace Prize 1970)**; **Dr M.S. Swaminathan — "Father of the Green Revolution in India"** ⭐; enabled by **C. Subramaniam** (Agriculture Minister).
- **The input package ("seed–water–chemical" technology):** **High-Yielding Variety (HYV) seeds** (dwarf wheat; **IR8 "miracle rice"**) + **assured irrigation** + **chemical fertilisers** + **pesticides** + **mechanisation (tractors, tube-wells)** + **credit & MSP/procurement**.
- **Core region:** **Punjab, Haryana, western Uttar Pradesh** (wheat belt); later spread.
- **Positive impacts:** food security, higher farm incomes, buffer stocks (PDS), agro-industry growth.
- **Negative / environmental impacts ⭐⭐ (the key exam angle):**
  - **Groundwater depletion** (paddy in Punjab), **waterlogging & salinity** from canal over-irrigation.
  - **Soil degradation** — nutrient mining, loss of organic matter, **micronutrient deficiency**.
  - **Chemical pollution** — fertiliser run-off (eutrophication), pesticide residues & **biomagnification** (File 05).
  - **Loss of agro-biodiversity** — monoculture displaced traditional varieties.
  - **Regional & social inequality** — benefited irrigated, large farmers; widened disparities; farmer **debt & distress**.
- **The corrective — "Evergreen Revolution" (Swaminathan) ⭐:** raising productivity **"in perpetuity without ecological harm"** — via **organic/natural farming, precision agriculture, integrated nutrient/pest management, micro-irrigation, climate-resilient & bio-fortified crops**. Also **"Second/Rainbow Green Revolution"** targeting **eastern India** (BGREI) and pulses/oilseeds.
- **AP link ⭐:** the **Godavari–Krishna deltas ("Rice Bowl of the South")** were prime Green-Revolution beneficiaries; AP now pioneers **Andhra Pradesh Community-Managed Natural Farming (APCNF / ZBNF)** — a globally cited **agro-ecological, chemical-free** model (the Evergreen idea in practice) ⭐.

## 3. White Revolution (Operation Flood) ⭐⭐

- **What:** the programme that made India **self-sufficient in milk and the world's LARGEST milk producer** (~a quarter of global output) ⭐ — turning India from a milk-deficit importer into #1.
- **Operation Flood (1970–1996)** — the world's largest dairy-development programme, run by the **National Dairy Development Board (NDDB)**; **three phases**.
- **Architect: Dr Verghese Kurien — "Father of the White Revolution / Milkman of India"** ⭐, chairman of NDDB; **National Milk Day = 26 November** (his birthday).
- **The "Anand Pattern" ⭐:** a **three-tier cooperative** (village society → district union → state federation) that links the small producer directly to the urban market, cutting out middlemen — pioneered by **Amul (Gujarat Co-operative Milk Marketing Federation, Anand)**.
- **Impacts:** rural incomes & **women's empowerment** (dairying is largely women's work), nutrition, a vast cooperative movement; **sustainability angle** — dairy pressures on fodder/water and **methane** emissions (livestock) are the modern caveat.
- **AP link:** strong dairy cooperatives & private dairies (**Heritage, Sangam, Dodla**); AP among India's major milk-producing states.

## 4. Green Pharmacy ⭐⭐ (direct syllabus item)

**Two complementary meanings — learn both:**

**(A) Medicinal plants / herbal & traditional medicine ("nature's pharmacy") ⭐:**
- **Green Pharmacy = medicines derived from plants, herbs and natural sources** — the science and practice of **plant-based / herbal medicine**, drawing on **Ayurveda, Siddha, Unani and folk/tribal knowledge**. India is a **mega-biodiversity, medicinal-plant powerhouse** — ~**7,000+ (some estimates ~20,000) plants of medicinal value** used across systems ⭐.
- **Institutions & schemes:** **Ministry of AYUSH; National Medicinal Plants Board (NMPB, 2000); ICMR/CSIR; National AYUSH Mission; Traditional Knowledge Digital Library (TKDL)** ⭐ — the database that **prevents biopiracy** by documenting prior art.
- **Biopiracy & Access-Benefit-Sharing (link to Biodiversity Act, File 11):** landmark cases — **Neem (EU patent revoked), Turmeric (US patent revoked), Basmati (RiceTec)** ⭐ — India's wins against wrongful patents; the **Biological Diversity Act 2002 (+ 2023 amendment)** governs bioprospecting & benefit-sharing.
- **Sustainability caveat:** **over-harvesting of wild medicinal plants → threatens species** (e.g., **Red Sanders of AP's Seshachalam — CITES-listed, smuggled for cosmetics/medicine** ⭐); the remedy is **cultivation, sustainable harvesting & tribal benefit-sharing**.

**(B) Green chemistry in pharmaceuticals ("eco-friendly pharma") ⭐:**
- **Designing pharmaceutical products & processes that reduce or eliminate hazardous substances and waste** — applying the **12 Principles of Green Chemistry (Anastas & Warner, 1998)**: prevent waste, atom economy, safer solvents, energy efficiency, renewable feedstocks, biodegradable products.
- **Why it matters:** the pharma industry is a big source of **toxic effluent, solvent waste and "pharmaceutical pollutants" (drug residues, antibiotics → AMR)** in rivers; green pharmacy cuts this at source — an **environmental-health** measure (File 05) and a **circular-economy** practice.

**AP link ⭐:** the **Eastern Ghats, Nallamala & Seshachalam** forests are rich in medicinal flora with deep **tribal ethno-botanical knowledge**; **Red Sanders** is the signature (and threatened) medicinal/aromatic species; AP hosts a large **bulk-drug/pharma industry (Visakhapatnam–Hyderabad corridor)** where **green-chemistry effluent control** is a live concern.

## 5. Prelims nuggets

1. **Green Revolution — Borlaug (world, Nobel 1970) / M.S. Swaminathan (India)**; inputs = **HYV + irrigation + fertiliser + pesticide + mechanisation**; belt = **Punjab-Haryana-W.UP**.
2. Green-Revolution costs: **groundwater depletion, soil salinity, agro-chemical pollution, agro-biodiversity & equity loss**; corrective = **Evergreen Revolution (Swaminathan)**.
3. **White Revolution = Operation Flood (1970–96), NDDB, Verghese Kurien**; India = **world's largest milk producer**; **Anand Pattern (Amul) 3-tier cooperative**; **National Milk Day 26 Nov**.
4. **Rainbow revolutions: Green (food), White (milk), Blue (fish), Yellow (oilseed), Pink (prawn/meat), Silver (eggs), Grey (fertiliser)**.
5. **Green Pharmacy (A) = plant/herbal medicine (Ayurveda); India ~7,000–20,000 medicinal plants; NMPB (2000), AYUSH, TKDL**.
6. **Biopiracy wins: Neem, Turmeric, Basmati**; governed by **Biological Diversity Act 2002/2023 (ABS)**.
7. **Green Pharmacy (B) = green chemistry in pharma (12 Principles, Anastas & Warner 1998)** → less toxic effluent/solvent waste.
8. **AP: rice-bowl deltas; APCNF/ZBNF natural farming; Red Sanders (medicinal, CITES); pharma corridor**.
9. **Blue Revolution = AP #1 (File 02)**; links to Green/White as the "rainbow."
10. **TKDL prevents biopiracy** by documenting traditional knowledge.

## 6. Mains angles

1. **"The Green Revolution fed India but starved its soil and water." Critically examine** — achievement (food security) → environmental & social costs → **Evergreen/natural-farming** corrective (AP's APCNF) → verdict.
2. **"Operation Flood is a model of inclusive, sustainable rural development." Discuss** — Anand cooperative pattern → incomes, women, nutrition, #1 milk producer → sustainability caveats (fodder/water/methane).
3. **"Green Pharmacy links India's biodiversity, tradition and green chemistry." Elaborate** — medicinal-plant wealth + AYUSH/NMPB/TKDL + anti-biopiracy (Neem/Turmeric) → green-chemistry pharma effluent control → sustainability (Red Sanders) → AP dimension.
4. **"India needs a Second Green Revolution — but a greener one." Argue** — regional (eastern India) + ecological (natural farming, micro-irrigation, bio-fortification, climate-resilient crops) + linking with Blue/White for nutrition security.
