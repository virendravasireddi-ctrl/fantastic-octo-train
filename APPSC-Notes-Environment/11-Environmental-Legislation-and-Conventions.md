## 1. International environmental law — the basics ⭐

- **Nature:** mostly **"soft law"** — Multilateral Environmental Agreements (**MEAs**), declarations, conventions & protocols — enforced by **state consent, reporting & peer pressure**, not a world court.
- **Guiding principles (quote these):** **Sustainable development; Common But Differentiated Responsibilities & Respective Capabilities (CBDR-RC); Precautionary principle; Polluter-Pays; Inter- & Intra-generational equity; Public participation; Common heritage of mankind.**
- **Structure:** a **framework Convention** sets goals (e.g., UNFCCC, Vienna) and later **Protocols/Amendments** add binding detail (e.g., Kyoto/Paris, Montreal/Kigali).

## 2. The key global conventions ⭐⭐

### 2.1 Montreal Protocol (1987)
- Under the **Vienna Convention (1985)**; phases out **Ozone-Depleting Substances (CFCs, halons, HCFCs)**. **Universally ratified; the most successful MEA.** **Kigali Amendment (2016)** adds **HFC** phase-down (a climate co-benefit). India ratified Kigali (2021), phasing HFCs.

### 2.2 UNFCCC (1992) — the climate framework
- Adopted at the **Rio Earth Summit**; objective = stabilise GHGs; enshrines **CBDR-RC**; **annual COP** meetings. Parent of Kyoto & Paris.

### 2.3 Kyoto Protocol (1997)
- First **binding** emission cuts — on **developed (Annex-I) countries only**; two commitment periods (2008–12, 2013–20 via **Doha Amendment**); **market mechanisms — CDM, Joint Implementation, Emissions Trading**. India = **non-Annex-I** (no binding cut; hosted **CDM** projects).

### 2.4 COP-21 / Paris Agreement (2015) ⭐⭐
- Legally binding **framework**, universal (all countries). Goal: hold warming **"well below 2°C, pursue 1.5°C"**; **bottom-up Nationally Determined Contributions (NDCs)**; **Global Stocktake** (every 5 yrs); **$100 bn/yr climate finance** pledge (now **NCQG**); net-zero mid-century. India's NDC/**Panchamrit** in File 06. In force **2016**.

### 2.5 CITES (1973)
- **Convention on International Trade in Endangered Species of Wild Fauna & Flora** — regulates/bans **trade** in listed species via **Appendices I (no commercial trade), II (regulated), III**. India's **Red Sanders (AP)** and many species are CITES-listed ⭐; implemented via the **Wildlife (Protection) Act**.

### 2.6 Convention on Biological Diversity (CBD, 1992) & the "biodiversity" treaties
- **CBD (Rio 1992)** — 3 objectives: **conservation, sustainable use, fair & equitable benefit-sharing (ABS)**. Protocols: **Cartagena (2000 — biosafety/LMOs)**, **Nagoya (2010 — ABS)**; targets: Aichi (2011–20) → **Kunming-Montreal Global Biodiversity Framework (2022 — "30×30")** ⭐.

## 3. Indian environmental legislation ⭐⭐

### 3.1 Environment (Protection) Act, 1986 — the "umbrella" law ⭐
- Enacted **after the Bhopal disaster (1984)** under **Article 253** (to implement Stockholm-1972 decisions); gives the **Centre sweeping powers** to protect & improve the environment. It is the **enabling parent** for **EIA Notification (2006), Coastal Regulation Zone (CRZ), and the waste-management rules** (File 09). Provides for **penalties** and central standards.

### 3.2 Forest (Conservation) Act, 1980 — now Van (Sanrakshan Evam Samvardhan) Adhiniyam ⭐
- **Restricts diversion of forest land** to non-forest use without **Central government approval**; mandates **compensatory afforestation** (funded via **CAMPA Act 2016**). **2023 amendment (renamed)** narrowed the Act's applicability (exemptions for strategic/linear projects near borders) — criticised for dilution; supports the **net-zero-2070 carbon-sink** goal.

### 3.3 Wildlife (Protection) Act, 1972 ⭐
- Protects **wild animals, birds & plants**; creates **National Parks, Wildlife Sanctuaries, Conservation & Community Reserves**; **six Schedules** listing species by protection level (the **2022 amendment** rationalised Schedules to **four** and aligned with **CITES**); statutory bodies — **National Board for Wildlife (NBWL), CZA, Wildlife Crime Control Bureau**; basis of **Project Tiger (1973)/NTCA & Project Elephant**.

### 3.4 Biological Diversity Act, 2002 — and the "Biodiversity Bill" ⭐⭐
- Implements the **CBD**; three-tier structure — **National Biodiversity Authority (NBA, Chennai) → State Biodiversity Boards (SBB) → Biodiversity Management Committees (BMC)** at local level (which maintain **People's Biodiversity Registers**) ⭐.
- Regulates **access to biological resources & associated traditional knowledge** and ensures **Access & Benefit-Sharing (ABS)** — the anti-**biopiracy** law (see Neem/Turmeric/Basmati cases, File 12).
- **The "Biodiversity Bill of India"** = the **Biological Diversity (Amendment) Bill 2021 → Biological Diversity (Amendment) Act, 2023** ⭐ — eases compliance for the **AYUSH/traditional-medicine & cultivated-medicinal-plants** sector, decriminalises offences (civil penalties), and encourages Indian-system-of-medicine research — **welcomed by industry, criticised by conservationists** for diluting ABS. (Ties directly to **Green Pharmacy**, File 12.)

### 3.5 Pollution-control & tribunal laws (recap)
- **Water Act 1974** (→ **CPCB/SPCB**), **Air Act 1981**, **National Green Tribunal Act 2010** (fast-track environmental justice), plus the constitutional hooks **Art. 48A & 51A(g)** and the **Art. 21** right to a clean environment (File 01).

## 4. Sustainable Development Goals (SDGs) — the legislation/policy anchor ⭐
- **UN 2030 Agenda (2015): 17 SDGs, 169 targets** (2016–2030), successor to the **MDGs**; "leave no one behind." Environment-core goals: **6 (water), 7 (clean energy), 11 (cities), 12 (consumption), 13 (climate), 14 (oceans), 15 (land)**. India tracks progress via **NITI Aayog's SDG India Index** (states/UTs ranked — an AP data hook). (Full treatment in File 06.)

## 5. National Disaster Management — policy & initiatives ⭐⭐ (direct syllabus item)

**Clear up the "2016" ambiguity ⭐:**
- **Disaster Management Act, 2005** — the legal backbone → **NDMA (PM-chaired), SDMA (CM), DDMA (Collector); NDRF; NIDM; NDRF/SDRF funds**.
- **National Policy on Disaster Management (NPDM), 2009** — the **policy** framework (prevention, mitigation, preparedness, holistic approach).
- **National Disaster Management Plan (NDMP), 2016** ⭐⭐ — **India's FIRST national plan** for DM, and the **first national plan globally aligned with the Sendai Framework (2015–30)**; covers all phases and the Sendai priorities; **revised in 2019** (added a **Social Inclusion** chapter — gender, SC/ST, elderly, children, persons with disabilities). *(The syllabus's "National Disaster Management Policy 2016" almost certainly means this **NDMP 2016**.)*

**Disaster-management initiatives in India ⭐:**
- **PM's 10-Point Agenda on DRR (2016)**; **CDRI — Coalition for Disaster-Resilient Infrastructure (2019, India-led)** ⭐; **NCRMP** (cyclone), **National Landslide/Earthquake risk programmes**; early-warning systems — **IMD, INCOIS (tsunami), CWC (floods), GSI (landslides)** (File 07); **Aapda Mitra** (community volunteers), **SDRF/NDRF**, mock drills, **school safety & building-code (BIS) enforcement**; **India's leadership at Sendai/UN**; state plans (**AP State Disaster Management Authority — APSDMA**).

## 6. Prelims nuggets

1. **Montreal 1987 (ozone, ODS) + Kigali 2016 (HFCs)**; **Vienna Convention 1985** = parent.
2. **UNFCCC 1992 (Rio) → Kyoto 1997 (Annex-I binding, CDM) → Paris 2015/COP-21 (NDCs, well-below-2°C)**.
3. **CITES 1973** — Appendices I/II/III (Red Sanders listed); implemented via **WPA 1972**.
4. **CBD 1992** — conserve/sustainable-use/ABS; **Cartagena 2000, Nagoya 2010, Kunming-Montreal GBF 2022 (30×30)**.
5. **Ramsar 1971 (wetlands), Basel 1989 (hazardous waste), Stockholm 2001 (POPs), Minamata 2013 (mercury), UNCCD 1994**.
6. **EP Act 1986 = umbrella (post-Bhopal, Art. 253)** → EIA/CRZ/waste rules.
7. **FC Act 1980 (renamed 2023) → compensatory afforestation/CAMPA 2016; WPA 1972 → NBWL/Project Tiger; NGT 2010**.
8. **Biological Diversity Act 2002 → NBA (Chennai)/SBB/BMC + People's Biodiversity Register**; **Amendment Act 2023 = the "Biodiversity Bill"** (eases AYUSH/ABS).
9. **SDGs 2015 — 17 goals, 169 targets, 2016–30; NITI Aayog SDG India Index**.
10. **DM Act 2005; NPDM 2009 (policy); NDMP 2016 (first plan, Sendai-aligned, revised 2019); CDRI 2019; APSDMA**.

## 7. Mains angles

1. **"International environmental law works by consensus, not coercion — is that enough?"** soft-law/MEA nature → CBDR/precaution/polluter-pays → Montreal success vs Kyoto/Paris struggle → enforcement gaps.
2. **"India has a robust environmental legal framework but weak enforcement." Examine** — EP Act 1986 umbrella + FC/WPA/BD Acts + NGT → implementation deficits (EIA dilution, FCA-2023 & BD-2023 amendments debate) → reforms.
3. **"The Biological Diversity (Amendment) Act 2023 balances industry and conservation — or tilts the scales?"** ABS/anti-biopiracy purpose → 2023 eases AYUSH & cultivated plants → benefits (traditional-medicine growth, Green Pharmacy) vs conservationist critique.
4. **"From relief to resilience — trace India's disaster-management policy evolution"** DM Act 2005 → NPDM 2009 → NDMP 2016/2019 (Sendai) → CDRI & early-warning initiatives → gaps (last-mile, building codes) → AP multi-hazard context.
