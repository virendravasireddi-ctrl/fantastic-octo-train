## 2. Twenty MCQs (key at the end) ⭐

1. Flue-gas desulphurisation controls — (a) PM (b) **SO₂** (c) CO (d) noise.
2. Residential-zone noise limit (day) under the 2000 Rules is — (a) 45 (b) **55** (c) 65 (d) 75 dB.
3. The top of the waste-management hierarchy is — (a) recycle (b) recover (c) **reduce/refuse** (d) landfill.
4. Single-use plastic was banned in India in — (a) 2016 (b) 2019 (c) **2022** (d) 2024.
5. The ozone hole is most severe over — (a) Arctic (b) **Antarctica** (c) equator (d) Himalaya.
6. Ozone concentration is measured in — (a) ppm (b) **Dobson Units** (c) decibels (d) pH.
7. Acid rain is defined as precipitation with pH below — (a) 7 (b) 6.5 (c) **5.6** (d) 4.
8. The Taj Trapezium Zone case is associated with — (a) noise (b) **acid rain/air pollution** (c) plastic (d) water.
9. The largest single contributor to the greenhouse effect (anthropogenic) is — (a) CH₄ (b) **CO₂** (c) N₂O (d) O₃.
10. ISRO's public geo-portal for thematic environmental data is — (a) Aarogya (b) **Bhuvan** (c) SAFAR (d) Aaykar.
11. The "umbrella" environmental law of India is the — (a) Water Act 1974 (b) Air Act 1981 (c) **EP Act 1986** (d) WPA 1972.
12. The National Biodiversity Authority is headquartered at — (a) Delhi (b) **Chennai** (c) Hyderabad (d) Dehradun.
13. CITES regulates — (a) hazardous waste (b) **trade in endangered species** (c) wetlands (d) POPs.
14. The Kyoto Protocol imposed binding cuts on — (a) all nations (b) **Annex-I (developed) only** (c) developing only (d) none.
15. India's FIRST national disaster-management plan was released in — (a) 2005 (b) 2009 (c) **2016** (d) 2019.
16. The "Father of the White Revolution" is — (a) Swaminathan (b) **Verghese Kurien** (c) Borlaug (d) Sam Pitroda.
17. "IR8" is associated with the — (a) White (b) **Green** (c) Blue (d) Yellow Revolution.
18. The Anand Pattern is a — (a) two-tier (b) **three-tier cooperative** (c) private (d) corporate model.
19. The database that prevents biopiracy of Indian traditional knowledge is — (a) Bhuvan (b) **TKDL** (c) NBA (d) CoWIN.
20. The "Biodiversity Bill of India" recently became the — (a) BD Act 2002 (b) **BD (Amendment) Act 2023** (c) FC Act 2023 (d) WPA 2022.

## 3. Mains answer-skeletons ⭐

1. **Sources, impacts & control of pollution (air/water/soil/noise)** → source→impact→control matrix → health link → regulatory frame.
2. **Solid waste management in India** → types → impacts → hierarchy/3Rs/EPR → composting/WtE/landfill → SWM Rules 2016 & SBM → circular economy.
3. **Soil & coastal erosion — remedies** → erosion types/causes → agronomic-mechanical-vegetative (soil) + hard/soft/bio-shield/CRZ-ICZM (coast) → AP (Uppada, Coringa).
4. **Ozone success vs climate struggle** → why Montreal worked → why climate lags → lessons.
5. **Acid rain — a transboundary problem** → SO₂/NOₓ chemistry → lake/forest/monument effects → control + treaties.
6. **Global warming impacts on world & India** → mechanism → impact matrix → India/AP vulnerability → mitigation+adaptation.
7. **Role of IT in environment & health** → RS/GIS/AI monitoring + early warning + e-health → green-IT/e-waste caveat.
8. **International environmental law — consensus vs coercion** → MEA/soft-law → principles → Montreal vs Kyoto/Paris → enforcement.
9. **India's environmental legislation — strong law, weak enforcement** → EP Act umbrella + FC/WPA/BD Acts + NGT → gaps & amendments debate.
10. **Disaster-management policy evolution** → DM Act 2005 → NPDM 2009 → NDMP 2016/2019 (Sendai) → CDRI/early-warning → gaps.
11. **Green Revolution — fed India, strained ecology** → gains → costs → Evergreen/natural-farming corrective (APCNF).
12. **Green Pharmacy — biodiversity, tradition & green chemistry** → medicinal plants/AYUSH/TKDL/anti-biopiracy → green-chemistry pharma → sustainability (Red Sanders).
