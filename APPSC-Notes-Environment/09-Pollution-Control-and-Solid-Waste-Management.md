## 1. Air pollution — source · impact · control ⭐⭐

| | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Sources** | **Vehicular** (CO, NOₓ, HC, PM, Pb); **industrial/thermal** (SO₂, NOₓ, fly-ash); **domestic biomass** (indoor smoke); **construction & road dust**; **stubble/biomass burning**; brick kilns; DG sets; natural (dust, volcanic) |
| **Key pollutants** | **PM2.5 & PM10, SO₂, NOₓ, CO, ground-level O₃, Pb, VOCs, NH₃** — the 8 in the **AQI** |
| **Impacts** | Respiratory (asthma, COPD, lung cancer), cardiovascular disease, low birth-weight; **acid rain, smog, ozone-hole (File 10)**; reduced crop yield & visibility; monument corrosion (**Taj**); climate forcing |
| **Control — the toolkit** | **Source:** cleaner fuel (**BS-VI, 2020**; CNG/LPG-**Ujjwala**; EVs-**FAME**), process change, fuel desulphurisation. **End-of-pipe:** **Electrostatic Precipitators (ESP), bag filters, cyclone separators (for PM); scrubbers/FGD (for SO₂); catalytic converters (vehicles)**. **Planning:** taller stacks, green belts, zoning, public transport/metro. **Regulatory:** **Air Act 1981, NAAQS, NCAP (2019 — 40% PM cut), GRAP (Delhi), CAQM** |

## 2. Water pollution — source · impact · control ⭐⭐

| | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Sources** | **Domestic sewage (largest in India)**; industrial effluents (heavy metals, dyes, acids); **agricultural run-off (fertiliser/pesticide → eutrophication)**; oil spills; thermal discharge; religious/immersion waste; mining drainage |
| **Indicators** | **BOD, COD ↑ / DO ↓, coliform count, turbidity, TDS**; groundwater — **fluoride, arsenic, nitrate, salinity** |
| **Impacts** | Waterborne disease (cholera, typhoid, diarrhoea, **fluorosis/arsenicosis**), **eutrophication & algal blooms → fish kills**, biomagnification (Hg-**Minamata**, Cd-**itai-itai**), dead rivers/wetlands, unfit drinking water |
| **Control** | **Sewage Treatment Plants (STPs)** — primary/secondary/tertiary; **Effluent Treatment Plants (ETP) + Zero Liquid Discharge (ZLD)** for industry; **Common ETPs** for clusters; constructed wetlands/phytoremediation; solid-waste & catchment management; **Water Act 1974, CPCB/APPCB norms, Namami Gange, CRZ**; **Polluter-Pays** |

## 3. Soil (land) pollution — source · impact · control

- **Sources:** industrial & mining waste, **fly-ash**, excess agro-chemicals, plastics & **e-waste heavy metals**, landfill **leachate**, sewage sludge, oil/chemical spills.
- **Impacts:** loss of fertility, food-chain contamination (heavy metals), groundwater pollution (leachate), reduced soil biota, health hazards.
- **Control:** **bioremediation & phytoremediation**, scientific landfills with liners/leachate collection, **integrated nutrient & pest management (INM/IPM), organic/bio-fertilisers**, e-waste & hazardous-waste rules, contaminated-site remediation, reduced agro-chemical use.

## 4. Noise pollution ⭐

- **Sources:** traffic, industry, construction, DG sets, aircraft/railways, loudspeakers, festivals, honking.
- **Measured** in **decibels (dB / dB(A))**; a **logarithmic** scale — safe limit ~**≤ 85 dB** for prolonged exposure.
- **Impacts:** hearing loss, **hypertension & cardiovascular stress**, sleep disturbance, reduced concentration, annoyance; affects wildlife (marine mammals from sonar/ships).
- **CPCB ambient standards (Noise Pollution Rules, 2000) — day (6 am–10 pm)/night dB:** **Industrial 75/70, Commercial 65/55, Residential 55/45, Silence zone 50/40** ⭐ (silence zone = ~100 m around hospitals, schools, courts).
- **Control:** source silencing & maintenance, **acoustic barriers/enclosures**, green belts, zoning & timing rules, ban on loud horns/firecracker limits, personal protective equipment, awareness.

## 5. Solid Waste Management (SWM) ⭐⭐ (direct syllabus item)

### 5.1 Types of solid waste
| Type | Examples |
|---|---|
| **Municipal Solid Waste (MSW)** | Household/commercial refuse — **organic (biodegradable), recyclables, inert** |
| **Industrial** | Process waste, sludge, ash |
| **Hazardous** | Toxic, flammable, corrosive, reactive wastes |
| **Biomedical** | Hospital waste — sharps, pathological, pharmaceutical (surged in **COVID**) |
| **E-waste** | Discarded electronics — heavy metals + recoverable metals ("urban mining") |
| **Plastic** | Single-use plastics, packaging |
| **Construction & Demolition (C&D)** | Rubble, concrete |
| **Agricultural** | Crop residue (stubble), animal waste |

### 5.2 Impacts of solid waste
- **Environmental:** landfill **leachate** → soil/groundwater pollution; **methane** (a potent GHG) & fires; ocean & drain **plastic** choking, microplastics; air pollution from open burning.
- **Health/social:** disease vectors (flies, rats), respiratory illness, **informal ragpicker** occupational hazard, aesthetic/urban blight, drain blockage → **urban flooding**.

### 5.3 The management hierarchy & methods ⭐
- **Waste hierarchy (best → worst):** **Refuse → Reduce → Reuse → Recycle → Recover (energy) → Dispose (landfill)** ⭐; the **"3 Rs" (Reduce, Reuse, Recycle)** are the exam core — extended to **5 Rs / 6 Rs** (Refuse, Repair, Rot).
- **Segregation at source** (wet/dry/hazardous — the foundation of everything).
- **Treatment/disposal methods:**
  - **Biodegradable:** **composting** & **vermicomposting**, **bio-methanation/biogas** (→ CBG).
  - **Combustible:** **incineration** (waste reduction, but emissions), **Waste-to-Energy (WtE)** plants, **Refuse-Derived Fuel (RDF)**.
  - **Residual:** **sanitary/engineered landfill** (lined, gas & leachate capture).
  - **Recyclables:** **Material Recovery Facilities (MRF)**; **e-waste & metal recovery**.
- **Recycling & reuse — why it matters:** conserves virgin resources & energy, cuts emissions & landfill, creates a **circular economy** (File 01), supports livelihoods; the key enabler is **Extended Producer Responsibility (EPR)** — the producer funds take-back/recycling.

### 5.4 Policy & schemes ⭐
- **Swachh Bharat Mission (2014; SBM-Urban 2.0)** — sanitation + scientific SWM; **Solid Waste Management Rules 2016** (mandatory segregation, user fees, EPR); **Plastic Waste Management Rules (single-use plastic ban July 2022) + Plastic EPR**; **E-Waste (Management) Rules 2022**; **Bio-Medical Waste Rules 2016**; **C&D Waste Rules 2016**; **Hazardous & Other Wastes Rules 2016**; **GOBARdhan** (bio-gas), **waste-to-wealth**, **Star-rating of garbage-free cities**.
- **AP:** **Jagananna/urban "Clap" (Clean-Andhra-Pradesh) programme**, door-to-door segregated collection & WtE efforts (Guntur, Visakhapatnam) as state exhibits.

## 6. Remedial measures for Soil Erosion ⭐ (direct syllabus item)

- **Types:** sheet, rill, **gully/ravine** (Chambal badlands), stream-bank, wind erosion; triggers = deforestation, overgrazing, faulty tillage, slope cultivation, mining.
- **Remedies (learn by group):**
  - **Agronomic:** **contour ploughing, strip cropping, crop rotation, cover/green-manure crops, mulching**.
  - **Mechanical:** **contour bunds, terracing, gully plugs/check dams, staggered trenches, gabions**.
  - **Vegetative:** **afforestation & reforestation, shelterbelts/windbreaks, grassed waterways, sand-dune stabilisation**.
  - **Watershed treatment (File 04):** the **ridge-to-valley** integrated package.
  - **Management:** controlled grazing, ban on shifting cultivation, **soil-health-card**-guided practices.

## 7. Remedial measures for Coastal Erosion ⭐ (direct syllabus item)

- **Causes:** wave/current & storm-surge action, **cyclones**, sea-level rise (climate), sand mining, port/harbour structures disrupting **littoral drift**, dam-trapped sediment, mangrove/dune loss.
- **Remedies:**
  - **Hard engineering:** **sea walls, groynes, breakwaters, revetments, gabions, offshore reefs** (protect but can shift erosion downdrift).
  - **Soft engineering:** **beach nourishment (sand replenishment), dune restoration**.
  - **Bio-shields (best long-term):** **mangrove & casuarina/shelterbelt plantation** (**Coringa, AP**) ⭐, coral/seagrass protection.
  - **Regulatory & planning:** **Coastal Regulation Zone (CRZ) & set-back lines, Integrated Coastal Zone Management (ICZM), Coastal Vulnerability Index mapping (INCOIS/NCCR)**, managed retreat.
- **AP angle ⭐:** **AP's long, low-lying delta coast (~974 km traditional / 1,053 km revised)** faces active erosion at **Uppada (East Godavari) — a classic Indian coastal-erosion hotspot** ⭐, addressed via geo-tube sea walls + ICZM + mangrove revival.
