## 1. The global environmental issues — the big picture

The **"planetary" problems** that cross national borders and need global cooperation: **ozone depletion, acid rain, global warming/climate change, biodiversity loss, desertification, transboundary pollution, marine plastic & ocean acidification, e-waste**. They share three features — **global commons, diffuse causes & effects, long time-lags** — which is why they need **treaties** (File 11), not just national laws.

## 2. Ozone layer depletion ⭐⭐

- **The "good" ozone:** the **stratospheric ozone layer (~15–35 km up)** absorbs harmful **solar ultraviolet (UV-B) radiation** — Earth's "sunscreen." (Contrast: **ground-level ozone is a *pollutant*** — the "good up high, bad nearby" rule ⭐.) Measured in **Dobson Units (DU)**.
- **The mechanism of depletion ⭐:** **Ozone-Depleting Substances (ODS)** — **chlorofluorocarbons (CFCs), halons, carbon tetrachloride, methyl chloroform, methyl bromide, HCFCs** — rise to the stratosphere, are broken by UV to release **chlorine/bromine**, which **catalytically destroy ozone** (one Cl atom destroys thousands of O₃ molecules).
- **The "Ozone Hole"** — severe seasonal thinning over **Antarctica** (spring), driven by **polar stratospheric clouds**; discovered **1985 (Farman et al.)** ⭐.
- **Effects:** ↑ UV-B → **skin cancer, cataracts, immune suppression** in humans; damage to **crops, phytoplankton (base of marine food web)** and materials.
- **Response — the success story:** **Vienna Convention (1985) → Montreal Protocol (1987) → Kigali Amendment (2016, phasing down HFCs)** — the **most successful environmental treaty**; the ozone layer is now **healing (projected recovery by ~2040–2066)** ⭐. **World Ozone Day — 16 September.** (Treaty detail in File 11.)

## 3. Acid rain ⭐

- **What:** precipitation with **pH below 5.6** (normal rain is mildly acidic ~5.6 from dissolved CO₂). Caused when **SO₂ and NOₓ** (from **fossil-fuel burning, thermal power, vehicles, smelters**) react with water/oxygen to form **sulphuric & nitric acid** ⭐.
- **Forms:** **wet deposition** (acid rain/snow/fog) and **dry deposition** (acidic particles/gases).
- **Effects:** **acidifies lakes & rivers → fish kills**; **forest dieback ("Waldsterben")** & **soil nutrient leaching (+ aluminium toxicity)**; **corrosion of buildings & monuments** — the classic Indian exhibit is the **Taj Mahal** ("marble cancer"), which led to the **Taj Trapezium Zone** and the SC's **M.C. Mehta (1996)** order to shift/clean polluting industries (Mathura refinery) ⭐; a **transboundary** problem (emissions in one country, deposition in another).
- **Control:** **flue-gas desulphurisation (scrubbers), low-sulphur fuels, catalytic converters, cleaner energy, emission caps/cap-and-trade, liming of acidified lakes**.

## 4. Global warming & its impacts ⭐⭐

- **Mechanism (the greenhouse effect):** GHGs — **CO₂ (largest share), methane (CH₄), nitrous oxide (N₂O), water vapour, ozone, F-gases/HFCs** — trap outgoing infrared → surface warming. **CO₂ from fossil fuels & deforestation is the dominant driver**; methane (livestock, paddy, landfills) is far more potent per molecule but short-lived.
- **State of warming:** ~**1.1–1.2°C above pre-industrial**; **IPCC AR6 (2021–23)** — "unequivocally human-caused"; guardrails **1.5°C / 2°C** (File 06).
- **Impacts on the world ⭐ (source of many Mains sub-questions):**

| Domain | Impact |
|---|---|
| **Heat** | More/intense **heatwaves**; record temperatures |
| **Cryosphere** | Glacier & ice-sheet melt ("third pole"), **GLOFs**, Arctic ice loss |
| **Oceans** | **Sea-level rise → coastal/island inundation**; **acidification & warming → coral bleaching**; stock shifts |
| **Water cycle** | Erratic monsoon, **more extreme floods *and* droughts** |
| **Weather** | Stronger **cyclones**, cloudbursts, wildfires |
| **Biosphere** | Species range shifts, extinctions, ecosystem collapse |
| **Human systems** | Food & water insecurity, **health (heat, vectors, malnutrition)**, migration, economic loss, conflict |

- **Impacts on India/AP ⭐:** threatened **long coast & deltas (sea-level rise; AP/Bay-of-Bengal cyclones), Himalayan glacier-fed rivers, monsoon-dependent farming, heat stress**; India among the **most climate-vulnerable large economies** despite low per-capita emissions → the climate-justice basis (File 06).
- **Response:** **mitigation** (renewables, EVs, green hydrogen, forests, energy efficiency) + **adaptation** (climate-resilient crops/infra, early warning, CDRI); policy in File 06.

## 5. Role of Information Technology in Environment & Human Health ⭐⭐ (direct syllabus item)

**IT as an environmental tool — "you can't manage what you can't measure":**

| Technology | Environmental application |
|---|---|
| **Remote Sensing (RS)** — **IRS/RISAT/Cartosat satellites** | Forest cover (**FSI/ISFR uses satellite imagery**), land-use/land-cover, wasteland & wetland atlases, glacier & coastline monitoring, crop & drought assessment |
| **GIS (Geographic Information Systems)** | Spatial analysis & mapping — hazard/vulnerability zoning, watershed & EIA planning, biodiversity mapping |
| **GPS / GNSS (NavIC)** | Field data, asset tracking, precision agriculture |
| **ISRO / NRSC + Bhuvan geo-portal** ⭐ | India's RS backbone — **Bhuvan** hosts thematic environmental data |
| **Sensors & IoT / real-time monitoring** | **Continuous Ambient Air Quality Monitoring Stations (CAAQMS), SAFAR, water-quality sensors, CPCB online monitoring** |
| **Modelling, AI & Big Data** | Climate & weather models, pollution forecasting, species-distribution modelling, disaster simulation |
| **Early-warning systems** | **IMD (cyclones via INSAT/Doppler radar), INCOIS (tsunami), CWC (floods), GSI (landslides)** — IT-driven (File 07) |

**IT & human health ("e-health / digital health"):**
- **Telemedicine (eSanjeevani), health MIS, disease surveillance (IDSP/IHIP), epidemic modelling (COVID), Aarogya Setu/CoWIN, wearables & remote diagnostics, AI diagnostics, GIS mapping of disease & vector spread** — early warning of health-environment risks (heatwaves, air-quality health advisories via AQI apps).

**The caveat (balance point) ⭐:** IT itself has an **environmental footprint** — **e-waste** (File 09), **energy-hungry data centres**, mining for electronics (critical minerals, File 03). So the answer is **"green IT"** (energy-efficient hardware, EPR, recycling) alongside **"IT for green."**

- **AP link:** ISRO's **NRSC (Shadnagar)** & **RRSC-Central** serve the region; AP uses RS/GIS for **crop, drought (Rayalaseema), cyclone and coastal-erosion monitoring**, plus e-health (village clinics) and the **Real-Time Governance (RTG)** data model.

## 6. Prelims nuggets

1. **Stratospheric ozone (15–35 km) blocks UV-B; measured in Dobson Units**; **"good up high, bad nearby."**
2. **ODS = CFCs, halons, CTC, methyl bromide, HCFCs**; catalytic **Cl/Br** destruction; **ozone hole = Antarctica (discovered 1985)**; **Ozone Day 16 Sept**.
3. Ozone effects: **skin cancer, cataracts, immune suppression, crop & phytoplankton damage**; treaty = **Montreal 1987 + Kigali 2016**.
4. **Acid rain: pH < 5.6; SO₂ + NOₓ → H₂SO₄ + HNO₃**; effects = **lakes/fish, forests (Waldsterben), soil, monuments (Taj → Taj Trapezium Zone, M.C. Mehta 1996)**.
5. Acid-rain control: **FGD/scrubbers, low-S fuel, catalytic converters, liming lakes**.
6. **GHGs: CO₂ (largest), CH₄, N₂O, HFCs**; warming ~**1.1–1.2°C**; **IPCC AR6**; guardrails **1.5/2°C**.
7. Warming impacts: **sea-level rise, glacier melt/GLOF, coral bleaching, extreme floods+droughts, stronger cyclones, health/migration**.
8. IT tools: **Remote Sensing (IRS), GIS, GPS/NavIC, ISRO/NRSC, Bhuvan, CAAQMS/SAFAR, IMD/INCOIS/CWC/GSI**.
9. **e-health: eSanjeevani (telemedicine), IDSP/IHIP surveillance, CoWIN, AQI apps**.
10. **Green IT caveat: e-waste + data-centre energy** → EPR & efficiency.

## 7. Mains angles

1. **"The ozone success and the climate struggle — why one treaty worked and the other lags."** ozone: few substitutable chemicals, clear science, **Montreal** → healed; climate: pervasive fossil economy, equity conflict → contrast lessons.
2. **"Acid rain shows pollution respects no borders." Discuss** — SO₂/NOₓ chemistry → transboundary effects → Taj/monument case → control tech + treaties.
3. **"Global warming is the defining challenge of the century." Assess its impacts on the world and India** — mechanism → impact matrix → India/AP vulnerability → mitigation+adaptation (link File 06).
4. **"Information Technology is both a tool for and a burden on the environment." Examine** — RS/GIS/AI for monitoring, early warning & e-health → the e-waste/energy footprint → "green IT + IT for green."
