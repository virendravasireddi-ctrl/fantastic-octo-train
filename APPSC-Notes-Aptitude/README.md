# APPSC Notes — Prelims Paper-II (General Aptitude) · **Section A: General Mental and Psychological Abilities**

**Target exam:** APPSC **Group-I Screening Test (Prelims), Paper-II — General Aptitude**. The same section, almost verbatim, is Section-A of **Group-II Prelims** and reappears in Group-III/IV aptitude papers, so this series doubles for all of them.

---

## 1. The paper at a glance

| Item | Group-I Prelims Paper-II |
|---|---|
| Name | **General Aptitude** |
| Questions | **120** objective (MCQ) |
| Marks | **120** (1 mark each) |
| Duration | **120 minutes** → **exactly 60 seconds per question** |
| Medium | English & Telugu (bilingual booklet) |
| Negative marking | **⅓ mark deducted per wrong answer** (per the current notification's general instructions — confirm in your year's notification) |
| Nature | **Qualifying-cum-ranking**: Prelims marks decide who reaches Mains; they do **not** carry into the final merit list |
| Section A share | **General Mental and Psychological Abilities — roughly half the paper (≈55–60 questions)** |

> ⭐ **The single most important consequence of the table above:** a mark here costs the same as a mark in History or Polity, but is *far* more reliably earnable — aptitude answers are **derivable**, not remembered. A candidate who is average in GS and 90%+ in Paper-II clears the screening. Treat Section A as your **score-guarantee section**.

### 1.1 Syllabus text (Section A)

> **A. General Mental and Psychological Abilities** — *Logical reasoning and analytical ability; number series; coding–decoding; problems related to relations; shapes and their sub-sections; Venn diagrams; problems based on clocks, calendar and age; number system and order of magnitude; ratio, proportion and variation; central tendencies — mean, median, mode including weighted mean; power and exponent, square, square root, cube root, H.C.F. and L.C.M.; percentage, simple and compound interest, profit and loss; time and work, time and distance, speed and distance; area and perimeter of simple geometrical shapes, volume and surface area of sphere, cone, cylinder, cubes and cuboids; data interpretation and data sufficiency; decision making and problem solving; interpersonal, communication and psychological abilities.*

⚠️ **Source note.** `psc.ap.gov.in` is not reachable from this environment, so the wording above is reconstructed from the notification as reproduced by exam portals and cross-checked across several of them. The **topic list is reliable**; the exact sentence-breaks may differ. Download the official Paper-II syllabus PDF from the APPSC website once and diff it against this list — takes five minutes, removes all doubt.

### 1.2 Level

Class **X standard** arithmetic, plus reasoning that is **untimed-easy but timed-hard**. APPSC does not ask exotic mathematics; it asks ordinary mathematics *fast*, and it asks reasoning *carefully worded*. Almost every mark lost in this section is lost to (a) the clock or (b) misreading, never to the difficulty of the concept.

## 5. Exam-craft for this section

- **Three-pass attempt.** Pass 1 (≈35 min): every question you can finish in <45 s — series, coding, relations, direction, simple percentage, formula-recall mensuration. Pass 2 (≈50 min): DI sets, work/speed, Venn-numerical, clocks-calendars. Pass 3 (rest): puzzles, painted cubes, anything you flagged. **Never** start the paper with a DI set.
- **The 90-second rule.** If a question is not falling in 90 seconds, mark and leave. In a 60-second-average paper, one 4-minute question costs you three easy ones.
- **Negative marking maths.** With −⅓, a blind guess among 4 options has expected value 0. But eliminating just **one** option makes guessing positive (+1/9 per question), and eliminating two makes it clearly worth it (+1/3). So: **never guess blind, always guess after one elimination.**
- **Options are data.** Aptitude MCQs can be *reverse-solved*: substitute options, check divisibility/parity/unit digit, or estimate the order of magnitude and discard. Several files below teach this explicitly — it is often faster than the "proper" method.
- **Approximate first.** DI and percentage questions are usually decided by a rough estimate; exact arithmetic is only needed when two options are close.
- **Rough work discipline.** Number your rough work by question number. Most silly errors in this section come from picking up the wrong intermediate value.
- **Bilingual booklet caution.** If the Telugu and English versions of a question read differently, the **English version is normally treated as authoritative** — but if a question is genuinely ambiguous, flag it and move on; APPSC has deleted defective questions in past keys.
