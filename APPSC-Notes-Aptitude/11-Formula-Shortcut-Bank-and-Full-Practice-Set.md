# PART I — THE FORMULA BANK

## 1. Number system
- Factors of N = aᵖ·bᑫ → **(p+1)(q+1)**; perfect square ⇒ **odd** number of factors.
- **HCF × LCM = product (two numbers only).** HCF of fractions = **HCF(num)/LCM(den)**; LCM of fractions = **LCM(num)/HCF(den)**.
- Greatest number dividing x, y, z leaving **the same unknown** remainder = **HCF of the differences**.
- Least number leaving remainder r with x, y, z = **LCM + r**.
- **Unit-digit cycles:** period 4 for 2, 3, 7, 8; period 2 for 4, 9; period 1 for 0, 1, 5, 6. Exponent ÷ 4; remainder 0 ⇒ 4th term.
- Perfect squares **never end in 2, 3, 7, 8**.
- Divisibility: 4 → last two digits; 8 → last three; 11 → alternating digit-sum difference.

## 2. Ratio, percentage, profit, interest
- Successive change: **a + b + ab/100**.
- ±x% both ways ⇒ net **loss x²/100 %**.
- A is x% more than B ⇒ B is **x/(100+x) × 100 %** less than A.
- **Profit% on CP; discount% on MP.** MP = CP(100+P)/(100−D).
- Two articles at the same SP with ±x% ⇒ **loss x²/100 %**.
- False weight gain% = **Error/(True − Error) × 100**.
- **SI = PRT/100**; sum becomes n times in T years ⇒ **R = 100(n−1)/T**.
- **CI: A = P(1 + R/100)ⁿ**; half-yearly ⇒ R/2, 2n. **CI − SI (2 yr) = P(R/100)²**; (3 yr) = PR²(300+R)/10⁶.
- **Rule of 72** — doubling years ≈ 72/R.
- Partnership share ∝ **capital × time**.

## 3. Averages and central tendency
- Average of consecutive numbers = **(first + last)/2**; first n naturals = (n+1)/2.
- New member: value = **old average + (n+1) × rise**. Replacement: difference = **n × change**.
- Combined average = **(n₁A₁ + n₂A₂)/(n₁ + n₂)**.
- **AM ≥ GM ≥ HM**; equal distances ⇒ average speed = **2xy/(x+y)**.
- **Mode = 3 Median − 2 Mean.** Positive skew: **Mode < Median < Mean**.
- Alligation: **cheaper : dearer = (d − m) : (m − c)**.
- Replacement: remaining = **x[(x − y)/x]ⁿ**.
- Lower **coefficient of variation (σ/x̄)** = more consistent.

## 4. Time, work, speed
- Together = **ab/(a+b)** days. Chain rule: **M₁D₁H₁/W₁ = M₂D₂H₂/W₂**.
- Efficiency ∝ 1/time; wages ∝ work done.
- Leak rate = 1/fill − 1/actual.
- **km/h × 5/18 = m/s.**
- Fixed distance ⇒ speeds a : b ⇒ times **b : a**.
- Relative speed: **u + v** (opposite), **u − v** (same).
- Pole ⇒ train length; platform ⇒ **train + platform**.
- Boat: **b = (down + up)/2, s = (down − up)/2**.
- Circular track, same start: **LCM of lap times**.

## 5. Mensuration
- Cube **a³, 6a², a√3** · Cuboid **lbh, 2(lb+bh+hl), √(l²+b²+h²)**
- Cylinder **πr²h, 2πrh, 2πr(r+h)** · Cone **⅓πr²h, πrl, πr(r+l)**, l = √(r²+h²)
- Sphere **(4/3)πr³, 4πr²** · Hemisphere **(2/3)πr³, 3πr²** (curved 2πr²)
- Equilateral **(√3/4)a²**, height (√3/2)a · Rhombus **½d₁d₂** · Trapezium **½(a+b)h**
- Circle **πr², 2πr**; sector **(θ/360)πr²**
- Polygon: diagonals **n(n−3)/2**; interior angle (n−2)180/n; exterior sum **360°**
- **Linear ×k ⇒ area ×k² ⇒ volume ×k³**
- Cone : hemisphere : cylinder (same r, h) = **1 : 2 : 3**

## 6. Reasoning
- Alphabet: **AFKPUZ** = 1, 6, 11, 16, 21, 26; **opposite letter = 27 − n**.
- Ranking: total = **left + right − 1**; bottom rank = n − r + 1.
- Direction distance = √(ΔNS² + ΔEW²); **morning shadow points west**.
- Grid: squares **n(n+1)(2n+1)/6**; rectangles **[n(n+1)/2]²**; m×n grid → [m(m+1)/2][n(n+1)/2].
- Painted cube: **3 faces = 8 · 2 faces = 12(n−2) · 1 face = 6(n−2)² · 0 faces = (n−2)³**.
- Dice: opposite faces are those **never seen together**; standard die pairs sum to **7**.
- Mirror image reverses **order**; water image reverses **vertically**. Mirror-safe letters: **A H I M O T U V W X Y**.
- Sets: **n(A∪B) = n(A)+n(B)−n(A∩B)**; three-set adds the triple back. **Exactly two = Σpairs − 3c; at least two = Σpairs − 2c.** Min overlap = n(A)+n(B)−Total.
- Syllogism conversions: **A → I, E → E, I → I, O → nothing**.

## 7. Clocks and calendars
- **Angle = |30H − 5.5M|**; relative speed 5.5°/min; coincidence every **65 5/11 min**.
- Per day: coincide **22**, opposite **22**, right angle **44**.
- Coincidence between H and H+1 = **60H/11 min past H**.
- Mirror clock = **11:60 − time**.
- Odd days: ordinary **1**, leap **2**, 100 yr **5**, 200 yr **3**, 300 yr **1**, **400 yr 0**.
- Month codes **3 0 3 2 3 2 3 3 2 3 2 3** (Feb = 1 in a leap year); **0 = Sunday**.
- Century year is a leap year only if divisible by **400**.

## 8. Psychological abilities
- **IQ = (MA/CA) × 100**; aptitude = potential, achievement = attained, attitude = disposition (**cognitive + affective + behavioural**).
- EI: **Salovey & Mayer (1990)**, **Goleman (1995)** — self-awareness, self-regulation, motivation, empathy, social skills.
- **Simon — satisficing/bounded rationality; Lindblom — incrementalism; Etzioni — mixed scanning; Janis — groupthink.**
- Communication: sender → encode → message → channel → decode → receiver → **feedback**; **grapevine = informal**.
- Situational rule stack: **legality → verify facts → act within competence → immediate relief + systemic fix → impartiality → proportionality**. Never evade, never break a rule, never punish without enquiry.

# PART III — EXAM-DAY STRATEGY

1. **Three passes.** Pass 1 (35 min) — everything under 45 seconds. Pass 2 (50 min) — DI sets, work/speed, Venn-numerical, clocks. Pass 3 (rest) — puzzles and flagged questions.
2. **The 90-second rule.** Not falling in 90 seconds ⇒ mark and move.
3. **Negative marking (−⅓).** Blind guess = zero expected value. **One elimination makes guessing profitable**; two make it clearly worth it. So: eliminate, then guess. Never leave a question where you have ruled out even one option.
4. **Reverse-solve.** Substitute options; test parity, unit digit, divisibility, order of magnitude.
5. **Read the last line of the question first** in long word problems — it tells you what to compute, so you read the data with a purpose.
6. **Units discipline.** Circle every unit in DI and mensuration before computing.
7. **Rough work by question number** — most silly errors are transcription errors.
8. **Puzzle sets are all-or-nothing.** Time-box them; a 5-question seating set that resists you for four minutes is a trap, not an opportunity.
9. **Don't chase the hard question you almost solved.** Sunk cost is the biggest single scorer-killer in a 120-question, 120-minute paper.
10. **Last 5 minutes:** stop solving; use them to transfer any un-shaded answers and to guess (after elimination) on everything left.

### Notes on the tricky ones

- **Q3** — "same remainder, value unknown" ⇒ HCF of the differences 70, 105, 175 = 35.
- **Q5** — N = 840k + 3; 840 ≡ 3 (mod 9), so 3k + 3 ≡ 0 (mod 9) ⇒ k = 2 ⇒ N = 1683 (÷9 = 187 ✔).
- **Q8** — 1.20 × 0.75 = 0.90, i.e. a 10% fall. The successive formula gives 20 − 25 − 5 = −10.
- **Q18** — first six + last six counts the sixth number twice: 294 + 312 − 550 = 56.
- **Q27** — relative speed 100 km/h = 250/9 m/s; 200 ÷ (250/9) = 7.2 s.
- **Q29** — harmonic mean 2(40)(60)/100 = 48, not 50.
- **Q34** — the longest rod is the space diagonal √(64 + 36 + 25) = √125 = 5√5 ≈ 11.18 m. ⚠️ Option (c) 11 m is the trap for anyone who estimates carelessly.
- **Q45** — "my grandfather's only son" = the speaker's father, so the lady is his sister.
- **Q54** — minimum overlap = 50 + 40 − 80 = 10.
- **Q55** — the flowers that fade quickly need not be roses. No conclusion.
