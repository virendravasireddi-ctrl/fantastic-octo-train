## Exam-craft notes

- **Prelims/Screening:** APPSC repeatedly asks *thinker–book/definition*, *office–function* (samaharta, mir bakhshi, faujdar), *reform–year–viceroy* matches. Every such pair in these notes is **bolded** — harvest them into flashcards.
- **Mains:** answers score when they (a) open with a crisp definition or periodised thesis line, (b) give 2–3 *specific* institutions or provisions as evidence (an office, a statute, a commission), (c) close with continuity into present-day administration — ideally an **AP tie-in** (Munro and the Ceded Districts, Madras Service Commission 1929 → APPSC, Spandana grievance redressal, village secretariats).
- **Spellings:** exam-standard anglicised forms are used (Kautilya, mansabdari, Ripon) — no diacritics.
- **Disputed "facts":** where scholarship differs (date/authorship of the Arthashastra, exact year mansabdari began), the notes flag it — write "tradition holds…" / "generally dated…" in Mains.
