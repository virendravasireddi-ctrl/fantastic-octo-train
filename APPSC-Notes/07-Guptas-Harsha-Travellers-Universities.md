## 1. The Guptas (c. 320–550 CE) — the "Classical Age"

### 1.1 The kings ⭐

| Ruler | The facts that score |
|---|---|
| **Chandragupta I** (c. 319/320–335) | First **maharajadhiraja**; **Licchavi marriage (Kumaradevi — celebrated on coins)**; **GUPTA ERA 319-20 CE** |
| **SAMUDRAGUPTA** (c. 335–375) | **ALLAHABAD PRASHASTI by HARISHENA** (on an Ashokan pillar!) ⭐: **Aryavarta — 9 kings uprooted & ANNEXED; Dakshinapatha — 12 kings captured & RELEASED (dharma-vijaya**, incl. Vishnugopa of Kanchi); frontier & foreign homage (Shakas, Kushanas, Sri Lanka); revived **ashvamedha**; **veena-playing coins**; V. A. Smith's "**Indian Napoleon**" |
| **CHANDRAGUPTA II "Vikramaditya"** (c. 375–415) | **Destroyed the Western Kshatrapas (Shakas)** → won Malwa-Gujarat-Kathiawar and the **west-coast ports**; **Vakataka marriage diplomacy (daughter PRABHAVATIGUPTA to Rudrasena II)**; **MEHRAULI IRON PILLAR** (rustless metallurgy); **navaratna incl. KALIDASA**; **FA-HIEN visited** ⭐ |
| **Kumaragupta I** (c. 415–455) | **Founded NALANDA** ⭐; Karttikeya worshipper; Huna cloud gathers |
| **SKANDAGUPTA** (c. 455–467) | **Repelled the HUNAS** (Bhitari pillar); **Junagadh inscription — repaired the Sudarshana lake yet again** (the Chandragupta-Maurya→Rudradaman→Skandagupta thread ⭐); war-strained treasury → **debased gold** |
| Decline (to c. 550) | Huna blows (Toramana, Mihirakula), weak successors, rising feudatories (Maukharis, Maitrakas, later Guptas), land-grant decentralisation |

### 1.2 Evidence
**Prashastis** (Allahabad; Bhitari; Junagadh), the **finest gold dinaras of ancient India** (debasement = the decline index), **ERAN inscription 510 CE — the FIRST epigraphic SATI** ⭐, **Fa-Hien** (external check), and the literature-science corpus itself.

### 1.3 "Golden Age" — the balance sheet ⭐⭐ (the set-piece debate)
**FOR:** political reunification of the north; **Sanskrit renaissance** — **KALIDASA** (*Abhijnanashakuntalam, Meghaduta, Raghuvamsha*), Vishakhadatta (*Mudrarakshasa*), Panchatantra, **Amarakosha**; **science — ARYABHATA (Aryabhatiya, 499 CE: earth's rotation, place-value, pi, true cause of eclipses)**, Varahamihira (*Brihatsamhita*), the maturing **decimal place-value & ZERO** ⭐; medicine (Vagbhata) and the rustless pillar; **art — the first structural temples (DEOGARH Dashavatara, Bhitargaon brick), AJANTA murals (Vakataka-Gupta), the SARNATH BUDDHA** (the "Gupta idiom" — the classical benchmark), Sultanganj bronze; **Puranic temple Hinduism** flowers (Guptas = **parama-bhagavatas**, Garuda emblem) yet tolerant (Nalanda founded by them).

**AGAINST — "golden for whom?":** **sati enters the record (Eran 510)**; caste hardens, women's status falls (child marriage, widow restrictions); **untouchability** (Fa-Hien's Chandala passage); and **the feudal turn** — proliferating **agrahara/brahmadeya grants with immunities**, **declining towns, coinage and trade** (R. S. Sharma), administration through **samantas**, provinces (**bhukti/uparika**) → districts (**vishaya/vishayapati**) with strong local-guild self-government (Damodarpur plates) — **far less centralised than the Mauryan state**.

**Verdict line:** *culturally and scientifically a genuine zenith; socially a Brahmanical consolidation with declining status for women and lower castes; administratively the birth of the decentralised land-grant order.*

## 2. Harsha & Kanauj (7th c. → 1194)

- **Setting:** post-Gupta fragments — Maukharis (Kanauj), Later Guptas (Magadha), **Maitrakas (Valabhi)**, **Pushyabhutis (Thaneshvar)**, and the hostile **SHASHANKA of Gauda** (killed Rajyavardhana).
- **HARSHA (606–647; the Harsha era):** avenged brother & brother-in-law, rescued sister **Rajyashri**, united Thaneshvar + Kanauj, **capital KANAUJ**; loose hegemony over the Gangetic north (ally: Bhaskaravarman of Kamarupa) — **but CHECKED ON THE NARMADA by the Chalukya PULAKESHIN II (c. 618-19) — AIHOLE INSCRIPTION of Ravikirti** ⭐: a great *northern*, not pan-Indian, emperor.
- **Sources & court:** **BANABHATTA's HARSHACHARITA — the first Sanskrit historical biography** ⭐ (+ *Kadambari*); **XUANZANG (Hiuen-Tsang)** the eyewitness; **Harsha himself a royal author — *Ratnavali, Priyadarshika, NAGANANDA***.
- **Rule:** deepening **feudalism** — samantas, officials **paid in land, not cash**; revenue mild (~1/6); income split four ways; the **quinquennial PRAYAGA assembly** (gave away the treasury) and the Kanauj assembly; eclectic patron (Shiva, Surya, Buddha; Nalanda). Died heirless → usurper Arjuna beaten by **Wang Xuance's** Sino-Tibetan force (a curiosity worth one line).
- **Kanauj after Harsha — "the imperial city":** Yashovarman (patron of Bhavabhuti) → the **TRIPARTITE STRUGGLE (Pratiharas vs Palas vs Rashtrakutas, 8th–9th c.)** ⭐ won by the **Pratiharas (Mihira Bhoja)** → Gahadavalas (Jayachandra) → fell to **Muhammad Ghori, 1194** — the doorway to §2's Sultanate world ([File 10](10-Delhi-Sultanate.md)).

## 3. Foreign travellers — the consolidated source set-piece ⭐⭐ (2023 PYQ)

| Stream | Traveller | When/whose reign | Work | Payload |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Greek | Skylax (for Darius); **Ktesias** (fables — the cautionary baseline); Alexander's historians (**Nearchus** et al., via Arrian) | 6th–4th c. BCE | — | First notices; the **chronological sheet-anchor** |
| Greek | **MEGASTHENES** (Seleucid envoy to Chandragupta Maurya) | c. 300 BCE | ***Indica*** (fragments in Strabo/Arrian/Diodorus) | Pataliputra's six committees; the **"seven castes" misreading**; "no slavery/famine" claims |
| Roman | Anonymous merchant; **Pliny**; **Ptolemy** | 1st–2nd c. CE | ***Periplus of the Erythraean Sea***; *Natural History*; *Geography* | **Indo-Roman trade manual** (ports, monsoon/Hippalus); Rome's **gold drain**; the gazetteer |
| Chinese | **FA-HIEN** | 399–414, **Chandragupta II** | *Fo-kwo-ki* | Mild Gupta rule, **~1/6 revenue, fines not mutilation**, charity hospitals; **earliest foreign notice of untouchability (Chandalas)** ⭐; never names the king (Buddhist lens!) |
| Chinese | **XUANZANG (Hiuen-Tsang)** — "Prince of Pilgrims" | 630–645, **HARSHA** | *Si-yu-ki* | Nalanda under **Shilabhadra**; Prayaga & Kanauj assemblies; **roads LESS safe — he was robbed** (the post-Gupta slide); rigid varna |
| Chinese | **I-TSING (Yijing)** | 671–695, post-Harsha | *Record of the Buddhist Religion…* | **Monastery-university administration**; ~10 years at Nalanda; the **sea route via Srivijaya** |

**The method (the real exam payload):** foreign accounts excel as (1) **chronological anchors**, (2) **trade evidence**, (3) **an outsider's notice of what insiders normalise (caste, untouchability, sati)** — and fail on motive and internal politics (**pilgrims over-report Buddhism; Megasthenes misreads varna; Ktesias invents**). **Name the traveller, his date, his purpose; cross-check against inscriptions and coins.**

## 4. Early educational institutions ⭐

| Institution | Patron/region | Character | End |
|---|---|---|---|
| **TAKSHASHILA** | pre-Buddhist, Gandhara | Earliest (6th c. BCE) — a **cluster of star teachers, NOT a residential university** — Panini, Kautilya, Jivaka | NW invasions |
| **NALANDA** ⭐ | **Kumaragupta I**; Harsha & Palas expanded | Apex **Mahayana** university — ~10,000 students, **gate-debate entrance**, **~100–200 endowed villages**, the **Dharmaganja** library; teachers Shilabhadra, Dignaga, Dharmakirti, Atisha | **burned by BAKHTIYAR KHALJI c. 1193** ⭐ |
| **VIKRAMASHILA** | **Dharmapala (Pala)** | Tantra/Vajrayana & logic; **ATISHA** (shaper of Tibetan Buddhism) | Turks, c. 1200 |
| **VALABHI** | Maitrakas, Gujarat | **Hinayana + secular studies** (law, administration); **I-tsing ranks it with Nalanda** | declined 8th c. |
| Odantapuri · **Somapura** · Jagaddala | Palas | Somapura (Paharpur) — the largest vihara (UNESCO) | Turks |
| **KANCHI ghatika** | Pallavas | "Nalanda of the south" — Brahmanical college | — |
| **ANDHRA: NAGARJUNAKONDA & AMARAVATI** ⭐ | Ikshvaku/Satavahana | The Krishna-valley monastic universities — Andhra's seat in this map (03 §5, 06 §1.5) | — |

- **The arc:** oral **gurukula**/agrahara (decentralised, Brahmanical) → **residential mahaviharas funded by land grants** (the same grant economy driving "feudalism") → **international hub** (students from China, Korea, SE Asia).
- **The clincher:** our evidence IS the pilgrims (the two syllabus items are one coin); and the **Turkish destruction of the centralised universities helped end Indian Buddhism, while decentralised Brahmanical learning survived** — the structural contrast to close any answer.

## 5. Prelims nuggets

1. **Gupta era 319-20 CE (Chandragupta I + Licchavi Kumaradevi)**.
2. **Allahabad prashasti — HARISHENA — Samudragupta**; north annexed vs south released (**dharma-vijaya**); "Indian Napoleon" — **V. A. Smith**; veena coins.
3. **Chandragupta II:** Shakas crushed; **Prabhavatigupta** → Vakatakas; **Mehrauli iron pillar**; **Fa-Hien**; navaratna-Kalidasa.
4. **Nalanda — founded by KUMARAGUPTA I**; Skandagupta vs **Hunas**; **Sudarshana lake thread** (Maurya→Rudradaman→Skandagupta).
5. **Aryabhatiya 499 CE**; zero & decimal place-value = the Gupta-age gift; **Eran 510 CE = first sati inscription** ⭐.
6. **Deogarh Dashavatara + Bhitargaon** = first structural temples; **Sarnath Buddha** = Gupta idiom; Ajanta murals = Vakataka-Gupta.
7. Gupta admin ladder: **bhukti (uparika) → vishaya (vishayapati)**; agrahara grants; samantas.
8. **Harsha 606–647; Banabhatta's Harshacharita = first Sanskrit biography; Harsha wrote Nagananda/Ratnavali**.
9. **Pulakeshin II checked Harsha on the Narmada — AIHOLE inscription (Ravikirti)** ⭐.
10. **Tripartite Struggle for KANAUJ: Pratiharas-Palas-Rashtrakutas** (Pratiharas won — Mihira Bhoja); 1194 — Ghori takes Kanauj.
11. Traveller-ruler pairs: **Fa-Hien–Chandragupta II; Xuanzang–Harsha; I-tsing–Nalanda/Srivijaya** ⭐; Megasthenes — six committees, "seven castes".
12. **Periplus** = trade manual; **Pliny** = gold-drain complaint; Hippalus = monsoon winds.
13. Universities: **Nalanda-Kumaragupta/Bakhtiyar 1193; Vikramashila-Dharmapala-Atisha; Valabhi-Maitraka; Takshashila = not residential**; Kanchi ghatika; **Nagarjunakonda-Amaravati (AP)** ⭐.
14. Fa-Hien's **Chandala** passage = earliest foreign notice of untouchability.

## 6. Mains angles

1. **"The Gupta age was a golden age — but golden for whom?"** §1.3 both sides; verdict line; anchor every claim to a source (prashasti/coin/Eran/Fa-Hien).
2. **"Socio-religious life and administration of early India from the Chinese travellers" (2023 PYQ verbatim).** Fa-Hien→Gupta mildness; Xuanzang→Harsha's feudal welfare + declining road-safety; I-tsing→university administration; close with the Buddhist-lens source-criticism — that's the real question.
3. **"Harsha was the last great emperor of the north." Examine.** Achievements + Aihole check + heirless collapse → the loose-samanta character explains both the rise and the dissolution; Kanauj's later career as the contested prize.
4. **"Why did the Buddhist universities perish while Brahmanical learning survived?"** Centralised land-grant-funded mahaviharas vs decentralised gurukula/agrahara/matha network — institutional geography decided survival.
