# 24 · Economic Geography IV — Industries: Agro, Mineral, Forest, Fuel & Man-power Based (India and AP)

⭐⭐ The syllabus phrase is **"industries (agro, mineral, forest, fuel and man-power)"** — a raw-material/input classification. 2025 PYQ: *"Highlight the industrial importance and major manufacturing sectors of Andhra Pradesh"* — §7 is that answer. Every industry below gets an **AP anchor**.

---

## 1. Classification and location logic (open any industry answer with this)

- **By raw material:** agro-based, mineral-based, forest-based, pastoral/animal-based, marine-based.
- **By input dominance:** **fuel/power-based** (smelting, refining) vs **man-power/labour-based** (garments, assembly) vs **footloose** (light, market-oriented — electronics, watches).
- Other bases: role (basic vs consumer), ownership (public/private/joint/cooperative), size (MSME vs large).
- **Location theory in one line:** Weber's least-cost triangle — industries gravitate to raw material (weight-losing: sugar, cement), market (weight-gaining: bakery, assembly), cheap power (aluminium), labour, or **ports (break-of-bulk: Visakhapatnam refinery-steel-fertiliser)** ⭐.

## 2. Agro-based industries

### 2.1 India essentials

- **Cotton textiles** — India's oldest modern industry (**first successful mill: Bombay, 1854**); "Manchester of India" Ahmedabad, "of the South" **Coimbatore**; distribution shifted toward cotton tracts + market metros; largest industrial employer chain (farm → gin → spin → weave → garment).
- **Jute** — **first mill Rishra (1855)**, Hugli belt monopoly (water, humid climate, Kolkata port); competition from Bangladesh & synthetics.
- **Sugar** — classic **weight-losing, raw-material-tied** industry (sucrose inversion → mills within cane belts); **the southward shift** ⭐ classic Mains point: tropical south gives higher sucrose + longer crushing season + cooperative model (Maharashtra) vs UP's sub-tropical belt.
- Others: edible oils, dairy (Operation Flood — File 40), rice milling, food processing (PM Kisan SAMPADA; mega food parks).

### 2.2 AP's agro-industrial map ⭐ (this row of facts wins Part-B answers)

| Industry | AP anchor |
|---|---|
| Rice milling | Deltas — India's biggest milling belts; **Kakinada rice-export gateway** |
| Aqua feed & processing | **Bhimavaram (W. Godavari)** aqua cluster; Vizag seafood export units |
| Tobacco | **ILTD/ITC leaf operations, Chirala (Bapatla)**; Virginia-tobacco processing, Guntur–Prakasam ⭐ |
| Chilli & spice | Guntur cold-store + oleoresin/export units |

| Textiles-apparel | Guntur spinning; **Brandix Apparel City (Achutapuram SEZ, Anakapalli)** — women-employing export garments ⭐ |

## 3. Forest-based industries

- **Paper & pulp:** first successful mill Ballygunge (1870); raw-material ladder — bamboo → hardwoods/eucalyptus/**subabul-casuarina farm pulpwood** → bagasse & wastepaper; water-intensive, effluent-heavy.
  - **AP: Andhra Paper (Rajahmundry, est. 1964)** on the Godavari ⭐; fed by the **Prakasam–Guntur–Godavari farm-forestry belt** (File 40); AP among India's leading paper states. *(Trap: ITC Bhadrachalam & Sirpur mills are in Telangana.)*
- Plywood & panel (peninsular hardwoods), matches (Sivakasi, TN), **lac** (Jharkhand–Chhattisgarh), sports goods (Jalandhar–Meerut), beedi rolling (tendu leaf — agency NTFP link), bamboo products (National Bamboo Mission), sandal & **red-sanders value chains** (state-auctioned — File 40).

## 4. Mineral-based industries

### 4.1 Iron & steel (India #2 crude-steel producer, ~140+ mt)

| Plant | Collaboration hook |
|---|---|
| TISCO Jamshedpur (1907) | First integrated (private) |
| Bhilai & Bokaro | **Soviet** ⭐ |
| Rourkela | **German** |
| Durgapur | **British** |
| **VISAKHAPATNAM STEEL PLANT (RINL, commissioned 1992)** | **India's first shore-based integrated plant** ⭐⭐; born of the 1966 **"Visakha ukku — Andhrula hakku"** agitation (32 police-firing deaths; Tenneti Viswanadham's leadership; plant sanctioned 1970); **only major integrated plant without captive iron-ore mines** (Bailadila ore) — the root of its losses; 2021 strategic-sale move → protests → **Jan 2025 ₹11,440 cr revival package, strategic sale shelved (verify latest)** ⭐ |

- Private giants: JSW Vijayanagar, AM/NS Hazira — and now an **ArcelorMittal–Nippon Steel integrated plant announced at Anakapalli (AP, 2024-25 — verify progress)** ⭐.

### 4.2 Cement, aluminium, ferro-alloys

- **Cement:** raw-material-tied to limestone; India world #2. **AP is a top-3 cement state** ⭐ — plants strung along the Palnadu (Macherla–Dachepalli), **Nandyal**, **Kadapa (Yerraguntla–Kamalapuram)** and **Tadipatri (Anantapur)** limestone belts, plus coastal grinding units; cheap AP limestone + coal via ports.
- **Aluminium:** power-hungry (smelting ≈ power-based); NALCO (Odisha) nearest giant; **AP's Anrak smelter (Makavarapalem, Anakapalli) built but stalled — bauxite linkage cancelled after the agency-mining freeze** ⭐ (Samata backdrop).
- **Ferro-alloys:** **Vizianagaram cluster (Garividi–Kothavalasa)** on the old Chipurupalle manganese field + port power ⭐.

## 5. Fuel-based industries (refining, petrochemicals, fertilisers, power)

- **Refining:** ~23 refineries; **HPCL Visakh Refinery (1957, Caltex origins; nationalised 1976) — the east coast's veteran refinery, expanded to ~15 mtpa under the Visakh Modernisation Project** ⭐⭐; a BPCL greenfield refinery-petrochemical complex in coastal AP has been **proposed** (verify status).
- **Gas economy:** **KG-basin gas lands at Kakinada (Gadimoga/onshore terminals)** → gas-based power (Vemagiri belt — many plants stranded when KG output fell ⭐ cautionary point), city gas, and the **Kakinada–Bharuch East-West pipeline (~1,400 km)**.
- **Fertilisers:** **Coromandel International — Visakhapatnam & Kakinada plants (Kakinada among India's largest private phosphatic units)** ⭐; Nagarjuna Fertilizers (Kakinada, gas-based urea).
- **Power map of AP** (match-pairs):
  - Thermal — **Dr Narla Tatarao TPS (Ibrahimpatnam, NTR dist)**, **NTPC Simhadri (first coastal NTPC, Anakapalli)**, **Sri Damodaram Sanjeevaiah TPS (Krishnapatnam, Nellore)**, RTPP Muddanur (Kadapa).
  - Hydel — Srisailam right-bank (770 MW, AP), Nagarjuna Sagar, Upper & Lower Sileru, **Machkund (AP–Odisha joint project)** ⭐; **Polavaram 960 MW under construction**.
  - Renewables — **Kurnool (Orvakal) & NP Kunta ultra-mega solar parks (~1 GW each)** ⭐, Anantapur–Kadapa wind corridor (AP a top-5 wind state), **Pinnapuram (Kurnool) integrated renewable + pumped-storage — billed the world's largest single-site IRESP (under commissioning)** ⭐, **NTPC green-hydrogen hub, Pudimadaka (announced)**; **Kovvada (Srikakulam) nuclear park proposed**.
- **Analytical line:** AP is power-surplus with a renewables tilt — its pitch to industry is "port + power + land," the classic fuel-based location trio.

## 6. Man-power (labour) based & footloose industries

- **Concept:** where labour cost/skill outweighs material pull — garments, leather, gems & jewellery (Surat), beedi, match works, electronics **assembly**; increasingly *skill-based footloose* services (IT) behave the same way.
- **AP examples:** Brandix apparel (Achutapuram), leather & footwear parks (Krishnapatnam belt), **electronics assembly** —
  - **Sri City (Tirupati dist)** ⭐⭐ — integrated SEZ/DTZ hosting **Mondelez (its largest India chocolate plant), Alstom (metro-coach exports), Isuzu, Kellogg's, Colgate, Blue Star, Daikin**, plus electronics units; an **LG Electronics plant is under construction (announced 2025)**.
  - **Tirupati EMC (Renigunta–Yerpedu)** mobile-handset cluster; **Kopparthy (Kadapa)** mega industrial node; **AMTZ Visakhapatnam — India's first integrated medical-devices manufacturing zone** ⭐⭐.
  - IT/ITeS & data: Vizag IT push; a **major AI data-centre campus at Visakhapatnam announced (2025 — verify)**.
- **Employment logic for Mains:** after farm labour, AP's surplus workforce is absorbed by construction, garments, aqua processing and assembly lines — labour-based industrialisation is the state's demographic-dividend play.

## 7. Andhra Pradesh's industrial structure ⭐⭐ (2025 PYQ answer in one section)

1. **Endowment logic:** 2nd-longest coastline + 1 major & many non-major ports + KG hydrocarbons + limestone/barytes + agri-marine surpluses + power surplus → **port-led, resource-processing industrialisation**.
2. **Major manufacturing sectors (name ~8 with places):** steel & metallurgy (RINL, ferro-alloys); refining-petrochemicals (HPCL Visakh, Kakinada gas hub); **pharma & bulk drugs — Jawaharlal Nehru Pharma City (Parawada), Pydibhimavaram cluster (Aurobindo, Laurus), Nakkapalli bulk-drug park (one of three national parks), AMTZ med-tech** ⭐; cement (Palnadu–Nandyal–Kadapa–Tadipatri); agro & marine processing (rice, aqua, mango pulp, edible oil, tobacco); textiles-apparel (Brandix); **automobiles — KIA Motors, Penukonda (Sri Sathya Sai), AP's largest FDI** ⭐⭐ with an auto-component belt, ATC/Yokohama off-highway tyres (Achutapuram); electronics (Sri City, Tirupati, Kopparthy); shipbuilding & defence — **Hindustan Shipyard (est. 1941; first ship 'Jala Usha', 1948)** ⭐, naval Ship Building Centre (SSBN programme), BEL Machilipatnam (opto-electronics), BDL Visakhapatnam; space — **SDSC-SHAR, Sriharikota (Tirupati dist): India's spaceport is in AP** ⭐⭐.
3. **PSU roll-call (Vizag = PSU capital):** RINL, HPCL, HSL, BHEL-HPVP (ex-BHPV), NTPC Simhadri, **Dredging Corporation of India (HQ Visakhapatnam)** ⭐, Eastern Naval Command, NSTL/DRDO.
4. **Corridors & parks:** **Visakhapatnam–Chennai Industrial Corridor (VCIC — ADB-backed, India's first coastal corridor, part of the East Coast Economic Corridor)** with nodes at Visakhapatnam, Machilipatnam, Donakonda, Srikalahasti–Yerpedu ⭐; **Chennai–Bengaluru IC (Krishnapatnam node)**; **Hyderabad–Bengaluru IC (Orvakal node)**; SEZs — **VSEZ (1989, one of India's oldest EPZs)**, APSEZ Achutapuram, Sri City, Kakinada SEZ; an approved **NIMZ in Prakasam**; MSME parks programme.
5. **The NCERT hook:** the **Visakhapatnam–Guntur belt is one of India's eight major industrial regions** ⭐⭐ (steel-refinery-fertiliser-ship at Vizag + Vijayawada–Guntur agro-commerce) — quote this in any AP-industry answer.
6. **Constraints (balance the answer):** post-bifurcation loss of Hyderabad's industrial-services base (Part-B overlap), no metropolitan anchor yet, capital & credit gaps, DISCOM finances, land & R&R friction, RINL's ore-less economics, export concentration (pharma/shrimp tariff shocks).
7. **Trajectory:** Industrial Development Policy 2024-29, big-ticket announcements (AM/NS steel, LG, data centres, green hydrogen) — cite as "announced; verify progress."
