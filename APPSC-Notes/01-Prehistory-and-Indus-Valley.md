## 0. The method rule (carries through all of §1)

**The type of evidence dictates the kind of claim you may make.** Archaeology gives material culture, rarely names or motives; inscriptions give an official self-image; texts give ideals and later redactions. **Name your source and its limit — you have half-written a good answer.** Pre-history = the past **before writing**, so everything here is archaeological inference; India has artefacts, not early hominin fossils — **a record of cultures, not creatures**.

## 1. The prehistoric periodisation ⭐ (learn cold)

| Age | Span (approx.) | Technology | Economy | Type-sites |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Palaeolithic** | c. 2 mn – 10,000 BCE | Hand-axes/cleavers (Lower), flakes (Middle), blades-burins (Upper) | Hunting-gathering | **Bhimbetka** (MP), Hunsgi, **Attirampakkam** (TN — Acheulian pushed to ~1.5 mn yrs), Soan valley, **Kurnool caves (AP)** |
| **Mesolithic** | c. 10,000–6,000 BCE | **Microliths** | + first **domestication** (Bagor, Adamgarh); first burials (Sarai Nahar Rai) | Bagor, Langhnaj, Bhimbetka |
| **Neolithic** | c. 7,000–1,000 BCE (staggered) | **Polished tools; food PRODUCTION** (Gordon Childe's "Neolithic Revolution") | Farming, settled villages, pottery | **MEHRGARH** (c. 7000 BCE — subcontinent's first farming village, ancestral to Harappa) ⭐; **Burzahom** (pit-dwellings, dog-with-master burial); Chirand (bone tools); Koldihwa (early rice); southern **ash-mounds** (Piklihal, Utnur — cattle pastoralism) |
| **Chalcolithic** | c. 3,000–1,000 BCE | Copper + stone; painted pottery | Village farming | Ahar-Banas, Malwa, **Jorwe** (Daimabad — bronze hoard, southernmost Harappan-linked; **Inamgaon** — best-excavated village) |

- **Robert Bruce Foote, 1863, Pallavaram** — India's first recognised Palaeolithic tool ⭐.
- **The stage-not-date rule:** ages are technological stages, not calendar boxes — the south's Neolithic is contemporary with the north-west's Bronze Age cities.
- **Dating methods (source-note):** relative — stratigraphy, typology; absolute — radiocarbon (fixed the Harappan mature phase), potassium-argon, thermoluminescence, now **ancient DNA (Rakhigarhi)**. Dates are ranges, not points.

### 1.1 AP prehistoric box ⭐ (localise every prehistory answer)

| Site | Why it matters |
|---|---|
| **Kurnool caves (Billasurgam)** | Palaeolithic cave sequence with **bone tools** — explored from Foote's era |
| **Jwalapuram (Kurnool)** ⭐ | Tool layers **below and above the Toba super-eruption ash (~74,000 BP)** — evidence in the global debate on human continuity through the Toba event |
| Southern Neolithic fringe | Ash-mound cattle-keeping culture of the Rayalaseema–Karnataka border (Utnur type) |
| **Deccan megaliths** | Iron-age **burial megaliths** (dolmens, cairns, cists) across Rayalaseema/coastal Andhra — the bridge from prehistory to the Satavahana age |

## 2. The Indus Valley (Harappan) Civilization

**Claim to fame:** the subcontinent's **first urbanisation** — Bronze-Age, literate (script unread), city-based, and the **largest ancient civilization by area** (Sutkagendor ↔ Alamgirpur; Manda ↔ Daimabad).

### 2.1 Chronology & discovery
- Early (c. 3300–2600 BCE) → **Mature (c. 2600–1900 BCE)** → Late/de-urbanisation (1900–1300 BCE).
- **Harappa 1921 — Daya Ram Sahni; Mohenjo-daro 1922 — R. D. Banerji; under John Marshall (ASI)** ⭐.
- Majority of sites on the dry **Ghaggar-Hakra ("Sarasvati")**; "Harappan" (type-site naming) is the safe convention.

### 2.2 Key sites (name with a reason)

| Site | Signature finds |
|---|---|
| **Mohenjo-daro** | **Great Bath**, Great Granary, "Priest-King", bronze **"Dancing Girl"** (lost-wax), **Pashupati seal** |
| **Harappa** | Granaries, cemetery R-37, workmen's quarters |
| **Dholavira** | Three-part city, **water reservoirs**, **10-sign signboard**, stadium |
| **Lothal** | **Dockyard (tidal)**, bead factory, fire-altars |
| **Kalibangan** | **Earliest ploughed field**, fire-altars, earthquake evidence |
| **Rakhigarhi** | **Largest site in India**; aDNA studies |
| Chanhudaro / Surkotada | No citadel (bead town) / debated **horse bones** |

### 2.3 Urbanism — the analytical core (what Mains tests)
- **Planning:** grid streets; **citadel (west) + lower town (east)**; kiln-fired **bricks in 4:2:1 ratio** across 1,000+ km — the strongest proof of shared standards.
- **Water & sanitation** — the true genius: covered drains, wells, Great Bath, Dholavira's reservoirs. No Bronze-Age peer matched it.
- **Economy:** wheat-barley + world's earliest **cotton**; standardised **chert weights** (binary/decimal); seals & goods in Mesopotamia — which called the partner "**Meluhha**" ⭐; carnelian beads, faience; **iron unknown**.
- **Script:** **undeciphered**, ~400+ signs, **boustrophedon** — so no names, gods, or political history can be read (the defining limitation).
- **Religion (inferred):** Mother-Goddess figurines, **Pashupati/"proto-Shiva" seal** (Marshall's reading, disputed), pipal & animal veneration, possible fire-cult. **No temples, no royal tombs.**
- **Polity — the puzzle:** no palaces, kings, or armies depicted → readings range from priestly oligarchy to merchant order to stateless heterarchy. Honest line: *uniformity implies coordination; its political form is unknown because the script is unread.*
- **Bronze-Age checklist (examiner-bait):** HAD bronze, cotton, wheel, weights, writing; **LACKED iron, the horse (debated), the true arch, coined money**.

### 2.4 Decline — a menu, not a cause
1. **Climate/river:** weakening monsoon; **drying of the Ghaggar-Hakra** (current favourite). 2. Floods/tectonics (Mohenjo-daro; Kalibangan). 3. Ecology (deforestation for brick-kilns, salinity). 4. **"Aryan invasion/massacre" (Wheeler) — discredited** (skeletons are of different periods). 5. Trade collapse → **de-urbanisation, not extinction** — rural Late-Harappan life continued (Cemetery H, Jhukar, Rangpur). **Conclude multi-causal.**

### 2.5 The two PYQ set-pieces ⭐ (asked verbatim)
- **"Origin and expansion" (2025):** origin = **indigenous and evolutionary** — Mehrgarh (7000 BCE) → Early Harappan (Kot Diji, Amri, Sothi-Siswal, Kalibangan-I) → Mature c. 2600 BCE (not a Mesopotamian import); expansion = **~1.5 mn sq km, 1,500+ sites**, driven by agrarian surplus + trade + resource procurement (Khetri copper, Gujarat carnelian, Afghan lapis), glued by standardisation (bricks, weights, seals).
- **"Survival and continuity into the present" (2023):** *transformation, not disappearance* — argue (i) archaeological continuity (Late-Harappan successors) and (ii) living survivals: **Pashupati→Shiva/Nandi; Mother-Goddess→grama-devata worship (the Deccan's village goddesses!); pipal & naga shrines; Great Bath→temple tanks & ghat bathing; swastika; bangles & sindur in the parting; carnelian beads→Khambhat industry; cotton; bullock cart; cross-furrow ploughing.** Close with the caveat: script unread → continuity is **argued inference, never proof**, and the theme is politically loaded.

## 3. Prelims nuggets

1. **Foote, 1863, Pallavaram** — first Indian Palaeolithic tool; Bhimbetka — richest rock-shelter/rock-art site.
2. **Microlith = Mesolithic**; earliest domestication — **Adamgarh, Bagor**.
3. **Mehrgarh (c. 7000 BCE)** — first farming village; **Burzahom** — pit-dwellings; **Koldihwa** — early rice.
4. **Jwalapuram (Kurnool)** — Toba-ash palaeolithic site ⭐; Kurnool/Billasurgam caves — bone tools.
5. **Daimabad** — southernmost Harappan-linked site (bronze hoard); **Inamgaon** — best-dug Chalcolithic village.
6. **Harappa 1921 (Sahni); Mohenjo-daro 1922 (Banerji); Marshall** supervising.
7. Mature phase **2600–1900 BCE**; bricks **4:2:1**; script **undeciphered, boustrophedon**.
8. Match pairs: **Dholavira-reservoirs/signboard; Lothal-dockyard; Kalibangan-ploughed field/fire-altars; Rakhigarhi-largest (India); Surkotada-horse bones (debated); Chanhudaro-no citadel**.
9. **Meluhha** = Mesopotamia's name for the Harappan trade partner.
10. Harappans lacked **iron, true arch, coined money**; had world's earliest **cotton**.
11. Pashupati seal = Marshall's "proto-Shiva" (contested); Dancing Girl = **lost-wax bronze**.
12. Wheeler's "massacre" decline thesis — **debunked**; Ghaggar-Hakra drying — current favourite.

## 4. Mains angles

1. **"Critically explain the salient features of Harappan urban planning" (2020 PYQ).** Grid + citadel/lower town → bricks 4:2:1 → drainage/water engineering (Dholavira, Great Bath) → weights/standardisation → close: uniformity without visible kingship — the "faceless" civilization.
2. **"Origin and expansion of the IVC" (2025 PYQ).** Use §2.5 frame: indigenous Mehrgarh-rooted origin → maturation 2600 BCE → widest Bronze-Age spread → integration by standardisation and trade; qualify with regional domains.
3. **"Survival and continuity of Harappan culture" (2023 PYQ).** §2.5 table in prose + source caveat — examiners reward the honesty.
4. **"Indus vs Vedic civilization — a comparative perspective."** Urban vs rural; bronze vs iron (later); script vs oral; trade vs pastoral; goddess-pipal vs fire-sacrifice; then the continuity threads that connect them (File 02 §Aryan debate).
