## 1. Distribution and density — India

- **Uneven in the extreme:** the North Indian Plain + deltas + coasts pack most people; **UP most populous (19.98 cr)**, Sikkim least; **Bihar the densest state (1,106)**, **Arunachal the sparsest (17)**; NCT Delhi ~11,300.
- **Controls of distribution (use as answer frame):** physical (water, alluvium, level land, climate) → historical (early agrarian hearths — the same deltas that fed File 39) → economic (industry-ports-mines) → political (capitals). **Physiological density** (per unit arable land) is the sharper measure — India's is far higher than arithmetic density.
- Growth history: **1921 = the "Great Divide"** (last census with absolute decline) ⭐; 1951 "small divide"; peak growth 1961–81; steady deceleration since — India is in the **late-expanding stage (Stage 3) of demographic transition**, with **TFR 2.0 (NFHS-5)** already below replacement.

## 2. Distribution and density — Andhra Pradesh ⭐⭐ (2025 PYQ (a) first half)

- **The demographic core is the coastal plain:** the Godavari–Krishna delta belt (East & West Godavari, Krishna, Guntur) plus the NH-16 corridor holds the bulk of the population on a fraction of the area — the census map mirrors the canal map (File 18).
- **Density gradient (2011, old districts):** **Krishna the densest (~520)**, deltas 430–520 → coastal average well above state mean → **Rayalaseema sparse (Kadapa ~190, Anantapur ~213, Prakasam ~193)** → **agency tracts sparsest** (hill terrain, forest, podu economy).
- **Largest / smallest (2011):** **East Godavari largest (51.5 lakh)** ⭐; Vizianagaram smallest (23.4 lakh); **Anantapur largest by area**.
- **Why the pattern:** assured delta irrigation vs rain-shadow scarcity (File 18's rainfall map is also the population map); ports-industry on the coast; drought out-migration from Rayalaseema (File 45).
- **Growth:** 9.21% (2001–11) with delta districts growing slowest (E. Godavari ~5%) — AP's transition came **before** it grew rich ⭐ (the Mains hook for ageing, below).
- *District caveat:* census data belong to the **13-district frame**; mention the **2022 reorganisation into 26 districts** (incl. tribal-majority **Alluri Sitharama Raju & Parvathipuram Manyam**) when interpreting spatial answers.

## 3. Age structure and the demographic dividend ⭐⭐ (2023 PYQ (a) — full kit)

- **Definition:** the growth bonus available when the **working-age (15–59) share rises** relative to dependants, as fertility falls — a **one-time, time-bound window**, India's roughly **2005-06 → 2055-56** (UNFPA).
- **India's numbers:** working-age ≈ **63–65%** and still rising; median age ~28–29 (vs China/US ~38, Japan ~48) — "the world's youngest big economy."
- **It is conditional, not automatic (the "critically" part):**
  1. **Education & skills** — learning-poverty (ASER) and only ~5% formally skilled ⭐; NEP 2020/Skill India as responses.
  2. **Health & nutrition** — stunting/anaemia (NFHS-5) shrink the effective workforce.
  3. **Jobs** — the dividend is cashed only via productive employment (structural shift out of farm work); gig/informality caveat.
  4. **Female LFPR** — rose from ~23% (2017-18) to ~41% (PLFS 2023-24) ⭐ — the single biggest lever.
  5. Else: **"demographic disaster"** — unemployment-driven unrest; Economic Survey 2018-19 ("India's Demography at 2040") flagged the policy runway.
- **The federal twist ⭐ (scoring point):** the window is **staggered by state** — the south (Kerala, TN, **AP**) is ageing first while UP-Bihar stay young → interstate migration (File 45) becomes the market that transfers the dividend; delimitation/fiscal debates follow.
- **Ageing:** India Ageing Report 2023 — 60+ share **10.5% (2022) → 20.8% (2050)**; **AP ages faster than India** (TFR 1.7, out-migration of youth) — "AP must build its Kerala-style care economy before it builds its Gujarat-style factories" is a serviceable closing line.

## 4. Sex composition

- **India:** 943 (2011), recovering from 933 (2001); **child sex ratio 919 — the alarm number** (kept falling despite overall rise) ⭐; extremes: **Kerala 1,084** vs **Haryana 879**; causes — son preference, sex-selective abortion (PCPNDT Act 1994), differential care; **Beti Bachao Beti Padhao (2015)**; NFHS-5 sex ratio at birth shows improvement (verify latest SRS).
- **AP: 997 — among the best big-state ratios** ⭐⭐; child ratio ~940 (better than national but below the overall — vigilance point); drivers: less son-preference culture, female work participation (**AP's female work-participation rate ~36% vs India ~25.5** ⭐ — ties to the farm-labour economy of File 39).
- One-line analysis for Mains: a good overall sex ratio can coexist with a slipping child ratio — always cite both.
