## 1. Ashoka and the Dhamma ⭐⭐

### 1.1 Kalinga, the hinge (c. 261 BCE)
- **Rock Edict XIII** — Ashoka's own confession: **100,000 slain, 150,000 deported**; remorse (*anushochana*) → **Digvijaya → Dhamma-vijaya**. The single most-cited passage in ancient Indian history.
- Personal faith: Buddhist **upasaka** (Bhabru/Bairat edict addresses the Sangha). **But state policy = Dhamma, not sectarian Buddhism.**

### 1.2 The edicts — the source base
- **14 Major Rock Edicts** (frontier/peninsular sites — Girnar, Dhauli, Jaugada, Shahbazgarhi, Kalsi, Sopara, **Yerragudi**), **Minor Rock Edicts**, **7 Pillar Edicts** (Ganga heartland), the two **Kalinga/Separate Edicts** (Dhauli, Jaugada), the **Rummindei/Lumbini pillar** (pilgrimage; tax cut to 1/8).
- **Languages/scripts:** Prakrit in **Brahmi**; **Kharoshthi** (north-west, Shahbazgarhi-Mansehra); **Greek & Aramaic (Kandahar)** — a ruler addressing subjects in their own tongues.
- He is "**Devanampiya Piyadasi**"; the **Maski and Gujarra** minor edicts name him "**Asoka**" ⭐; **deciphered by James Prinsep, 1837** ⭐ — the first Indian ruler we hear in his own voice.

### 1.3 What Dhamma WAS
A **non-sectarian civic-ethical code** to hold a plural empire together: ahimsa & restraint on slaughter (RE I); **welfare — hospitals for men & animals, wells, trees, medicinal herbs (RE II)**; **Dhamma-mahamatras (RE V)**; respect to elders, Brahmins & shramanas, kindness to servants and the poor; **tolerance — "honour another's sect and you exalt your own" (RE XII)**; Dhamma-yatras replacing hunts; "all men are my children" (Separate Edicts). **The essential distinction: personal Buddhism ≠ state Dhamma.**

### 1.4 Assessment (give both sides)
- Greatness: ethical-paternal kingship, tolerance as policy, Buddhism made a world religion (missions — Mahinda & Sanghamitra to Sri Lanka), and the Republic's symbols (Sarnath capital, Dharmachakra).
- Critique: did pacifism weaken the state (Haraprasad Sastri's Brahmanical-reaction thesis)? **Overstated** — he kept the army and death penalty, and RE XIII warns the forest tribes. Verdict: decline owed more to **over-centralisation + weak successors** than to Dhamma.

### 1.5 Ashoka in Andhra ⭐ (APPSC localiser)
- **Yerragudi (Erragudi, Kurnool):** a full set of **Major Rock Edicts PLUS a Minor Rock Edict** — the empire's deep-south proclamation point.
- **Rajula-Mandagiri (Kurnool):** Minor Rock Edict.
- **RE XIII counts the ANDHRAS among peoples within the imperial fold**; the southern province ran from **Suvarnagiri** (near the Karnataka border zone). Cross-link: the Mauryan-Andhra box in the PubAd series ([Kautilya file](../APPSC-Notes-PubAd/02-Kautilya-Arthashastra-Administrative-Ideas.md)).

## 2. Mauryan administration (the Arthashastra state)

*Deep dive with modern parallels: [PubAd File 02](../APPSC-Notes-PubAd/02-Kautilya-Arthashastra-Administrative-Ideas.md). The history-paper skeleton:*

- **Centralised monarchy** + mantri-parishad; paternal despotism ("all men are my children").
- **Provinces (~4–5) under princes (kumaras):** **Taxila** (NW), **Ujjain** (W), **Suvarnagiri** (S), **Tosali** (Kalinga), centre **Pataliputra**.
- Officialdom: amatyas, mahamatras (incl. **Dhamma-mahamatras**), **adhyakshas** (superintendents — a near-command economy), **rajukas** (district revenue-justice), yuktas, pradeshikas; **gudha-purushas** (espionage).
- **Megasthenes on Pataliputra:** **six committees of five (30 officials)** — crafts, foreigners, births-deaths, trade, manufactures, sales-tax; his "**seven castes**" is a famous **misreading** (occupational groups read as castes) — quote it as the caution on foreign sources; army of six boards (Pliny's numbers colossal).
- **Economy:** land revenue (**bhaga**, ~1/4–1/6) the mainstay; state monopolies (mines, salt); **sita** (crown) farms; **Sudarshana lake at Girnar** (built under Chandragupta by Pushyagupta; repaired by Rudradaman & again by Skandagupta — the rare thread across seven centuries ⭐).
- **Decline (multi-causal):** weak successors & partition; top-heavy personality-dependent centralisation; fiscal strain (debased punch-marks); Brahmanical reaction (cite, qualify); provincial revolts; exposed north-west. *Best line: a centralised empire dependent on personality could not survive weak successors.*

## 3. Mauryan art & architecture ⭐ (the court-vs-popular contrast)

| | Court (imperial) art | Popular (folk) art |
|---|---|---|
| Works | **Monolithic pillars** (Chunar sandstone, **"Mauryan polish"**), **Sarnath Lion Capital** (State Emblem; its wheel = the flag's Ashoka Chakra), Rampurva bull; **80-pillared hall, Pataliputra** (Megasthenes; Fa-Hien thought it spirits' work); **Barabar & Nagarjuni caves** ⭐ — **India's FIRST rock-cut caves, dedicated by Ashoka/Dasharatha to the AJIVIKAS** (ecumenism in granite; Lomas Rishi facade imitates wooden caitya arch → ancestor of Karle-Ajanta) | **Yaksha-yakshi colossi** — **Didarganj Yakshi** (chamara-bearer), Parkham Yaksha — folk fertility cults; mass **terracottas**; **NBPW** pottery; **punch-marked coins**; stupas begun (traditionally 84,000; **Sanchi's core is Mauryan but the carved gateways are later — foundation ≠ fabric**) |

- **Foreign-influence debate:** bell-lotus capital & polish recall **Achaemenid Persepolis**; but pillars are **monolithic and free-standing** (Persian ones drum-built, roof-bearing) and the symbols (lotus, cakra, the animals) are Indian. **Verdict: idiom borrowed, meaning Indian** (Niharranjan Ray).

## 4. Prelims nuggets

1. Chandragupta beat **Seleucus c. 305 BCE** — 500 elephants; **Megasthenes → Indica** (fragments only).
2. **Bindusara = Amitraghata**; linked to the **Ajivikas**; asked Antiochus for wine, figs and a Sophist.
3. Kalinga war **c. 261 BCE — RE XIII** (100,000 slain); Bhabru edict — Buddhist confession.
4. **Maski & Gujarra name "Asoka"**; decipherment — **Prinsep, 1837**; title **Devanampiya Piyadasi**.
5. Scripts: Brahmi; **Kharoshthi (NW)**; **Greek-Aramaic at Kandahar** ⭐.
6. **RE II** welfare; **RE V** Dhamma-mahamatras; **RE XII** tolerance; **RE XIII** Kalinga + Andhras named.
7. ⭐ **Yerragudi (Kurnool) — Major + Minor edicts; Rajula-Mandagiri (Kurnool) — Minor edict**.
8. Provincial capitals: **Taxila, Ujjain, Suvarnagiri, Tosali**.
9. Megasthenes: **six committees of five**; "seven castes" = misreading.
10. **Sudarshana lake**: built Chandragupta-era → repaired Rudradaman → Skandagupta.
11. **Barabar caves — first rock-cut, for the Ajivikas**; Sarnath capital = State Emblem; "Mauryan polish".
12. **Didarganj Yakshi** = popular tradition; Sanchi gateways = post-Mauryan.
13. **185 BCE — Brihadratha killed by Pushyamitra Shunga** at an army review.

## 5. Mains angles

1. **"Similarities and differences between Buddhism and Ashoka's Dhamma; their impact" (2012 PYQ).** Overlap (ahimsa, ethics) vs the deliberate ecumenism (RE XII; Barabar-to-Ajivikas as the clincher); impact — welfare kingship, Buddhism's globalisation, the Republic's symbols.
2. **"Evaluate Mauryan administration from the Arthashastra, the Indica and the edicts."** Play the three sources against each other (prescriptive vs external vs contemporary) — the source-triangle IS the answer; skeleton from §2.
3. **"Distinguish the court art and popular art of the Mauryas" / "Persian vs indigenous elements".** §3 table + idiom-vs-meaning verdict.
4. **"Why did the Mauryan empire decline within fifty years of Ashoka?"** Multi-causal menu; lead with structural over-centralisation; treat the pacifism thesis as overstated.
