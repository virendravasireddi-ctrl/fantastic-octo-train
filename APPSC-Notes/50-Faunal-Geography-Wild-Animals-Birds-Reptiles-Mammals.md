## 2. Mammals ⭐ (~430 species in India)

### 2.1 India — the species you must be able to place

| Mammal | Note / stronghold |
|---|---|
| **Bengal tiger** | National animal; MP = "Tiger State"; **NSTR (AP) = largest tiger reserve by area** ⭐ |
| **Asiatic lion** | **Gir (Gujarat) — the only wild population**; ~600–900 (rising; verify latest census) |
| **Asian elephant** | Keystone; Project Elephant 1992; AP habitat = **Koundinya (Chittoor)** |
| **One-horned rhino** | **Kaziranga (Assam)** — conservation success |
| **Snow leopard** | High Himalaya; Project Snow Leopard |
| **Gaur (Indian bison)** | **World's largest wild bovine**; Nallamala & Eastern Ghats |
| **Nilgai** | India's largest antelope |
| **Blackbuck** | **AP's state animal (Krishna jinka)**; **Rollapadu (AP)** ⭐ |
| **Lion-tailed macaque, Nilgiri tahr** | Western Ghats endemics (not AP) |
| **Dhole (wild dog), sloth bear, leopard, striped hyena, Indian wolf** | Widespread; all in AP's forests/scrub |
| **Gangetic dolphin** | National aquatic animal; **dugong** in Gulf of Mannar |

### 2.2 Andhra Pradesh mammals

- **Big cats & carnivores:** tiger & leopard (**Nallamala/NSTR**), **dhole, sloth bear, striped hyena, jungle cat, rusty-spotted cat**; **Indian wolf** on the Rayalaseema grasslands ⭐.
- **Herbivores:** chital (spotted deer), sambar, **blackbuck (Rollapadu)**, chinkara, four-horned antelope (chousingha), wild boar, mouse deer (Indian chevrotain), gaur.
- **Elephants:** migratory herds in **Koundinya WLS (Chittoor)** — AP's only resident elephant tract (from TN–Karnataka) ⭐.
- **Wetland/coastal mammals:** **FISHING CAT — the Coringa mangrove flagship** ⭐⭐; smooth-coated otter (Godavari); **Indian pangolin** (heavily trafficked); slender loris (Seshachalam scrub) ⭐.

## 3. Birds ⭐⭐ (~1,350 species in India; AP is a flyway state)

### 3.1 India headline species

- **Indian peafowl** (national bird); **Great Indian Bustard (CR — Rollapadu, AP!)** ⭐⭐; **Sarus crane** — world's tallest flying bird; **Himalayan monal, hornbills, flamingos**; migratory Siberian/Central-Asian waterfowl on the Central Asian Flyway.

### 3.2 Andhra Pradesh — a birdwatcher's state (build AP answers on this) ⭐⭐

| Bird | Site (district) | Hook |
|---|---|---|
| **Great Indian Bustard** | **Rollapadu WLS (Kurnool)** | CR; India's flagship grassland bird; <150 nationally ⭐⭐ |
| **Jerdon's courser** (*kalivi kodi*) | **Sri Lankamalleswara WLS (Kadapa)** | AP-**endemic**, nocturnal, **rediscovered 1986** ⭐⭐ |
| **Spot-billed pelican & painted stork** | **Nelapattu (Nellore), Telineelapuram–Naupada (Srikakulam), Uppalapadu (Guntur), Kolleru–Atapaka** | Major **pelicanries/heronries**; Uppalapadu is a village-protected haven ⭐ |
| **Greater & lesser flamingo, migratory waterfowl** | **Pulicat lagoon (Nellore)** | 2nd-largest brackish lagoon; flamingo festival |
| **Bar-headed geese & migrants** | Kolleru, Kondakarla Ava | Central Asian Flyway stopovers |

- **State bird:** **rose-ringed parakeet (rama chiluka)** ⭐.

## 4. Reptiles ⭐ (the syllabus names them explicitly)

### 4.1 India

- **"Big Four" venomous snakes (most snakebite deaths):** **spectacled cobra, common krait, Russell's viper, saw-scaled viper** ⭐⭐.
- **King cobra** — world's longest venomous snake; **Indian rock python**; **gharial (CR — Chambal)**, **mugger (marsh) crocodile**, **saltwater/estuarine crocodile** (largest living reptile); **star tortoise**, monitor lizards; **sea turtles — olive ridley (mass "arribada" nesting, Gahirmatha Odisha)**, plus green, hawksbill, leatherback.

### 4.2 Andhra Pradesh

- **GOLDEN GECKO (*Calodactylodes aureus*) — an Eastern-Ghats endemic** (Tirumala–Nallamala rock faces), WPA Schedule-I ⭐⭐ (prime trick pair).
- **Crocodiles:** **mugger and estuarine crocodile in Krishna WLS & Coringa** ⭐; **olive ridley turtles nest on AP beaches (Godavari–Krishna coast, near Hope & Sacramento island spits)** ⭐ — a state hatchery-and-release programme runs each season.
- Snakes: cobra, krait, Russell's & saw-scaled vipers, rock python; **Indian star tortoise** (trafficking hotspot); monitor lizards; freshwater softshell turtles in the deltas.

## 5. Amphibians & fish (brief — for completeness)

- **Amphibians:** India ~450 species, high Western-Ghats endemism (**purple/pig-nosed frog**); the Eastern Ghats hold lesser-known endemic frogs (ongoing discoveries in the AP agency).
- **Fish:** the syllabus's "fishery" economy is File 40; ecologically, note **freshwater biodiversity of the Godavari–Krishna** and estuarine fish of Kolleru/Pulicat; **mahseer** as an indicator sport-fish; invasive African catfish & tilapia are a Kolleru management issue ⭐.

## 6. AP flagship & endemic fauna — one-look revision table ⭐⭐

| Group | Flagship / endemic | Where |
|---|---|---|
| Mammal | **Tiger** (source pop.), **fishing cat**, slender loris, Indian pangolin | NSTR/Nallamala; Coringa; Seshachalam |
| Grassland | **Blackbuck, Indian wolf, Great Indian Bustard** | **Rollapadu (Kurnool)** |
| Bird (endemic) | **Jerdon's courser** | **Lankamala (Kadapa)** |
| Waterbirds | Spot-billed pelican, painted stork, flamingo | Nelapattu, Telineelapuram, Uppalapadu, Kolleru, Pulicat |
| Reptile (endemic) | **Golden gecko** | Eastern Ghats (Tirumala, Nallamala) |
| Marine reptile | **Olive ridley turtle** | Godavari–Krishna coast |
| Elephant | Migratory herds | **Koundinya (Chittoor)** |

**Mains line:** AP's fauna is defined by three habitats — the **Nallamala–Eastern Ghats forest** (tiger, gaur, loris, golden gecko), the **Rayalaseema grassland-scrub** (bustard, blackbuck, wolf), and the **deltaic wetland-coast** (fishing cat, pelicans, flamingos, crocodiles, olive ridleys) — a spread that, lacking hotspot status (File 49), is protected chiefly through its sanctuaries (File 52).
