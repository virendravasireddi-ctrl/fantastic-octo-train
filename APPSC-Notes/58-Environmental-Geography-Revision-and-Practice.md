## 1. Concept & convention one-liners (memorise) ⭐⭐

- **Risk = Hazard × Vulnerability ÷ Capacity**; hazard ≠ disaster (needs vulnerability).
- **Seismic waves:** P (fastest, longitudinal) · S (transverse, no liquids) · L (surface, most damaging); **magnitude (Richter/Moment) ≠ intensity (Mercalli)**.
- **BIS seismic zones II–V** (V highest = NE/Kutch/Kashmir); **AP mostly Zone II, Ongole-Bapatla coast Zone III**.
- **Cloudburst = >100 mm/hr** over a small area (Himalayan).
- **Tropical cyclone needs SST >26.5 °C + Coriolis**; **Bay of Bengal ≈ 4× the Arabian Sea**; **IMD = 1 of 6 RSMCs**, names for a 13-country panel.
- **Drought types:** meteorological → hydrological → agricultural → socio-economic; **IMD "deficient" = 26–50% below normal**.
- **ENSO:** **El Niño → weak monsoon/drought; La Niña → good monsoon/more cyclones; +IOD aids monsoon**.
- **DM Act 2005:** NDMA (PM) · SDMA (CM) · DDMA (Collector) · NDRF (2006) · NIDM; **Sendai Framework 2015–2030**.
- **Sustainable Development = Brundtland 1987, "Our Common Future"**; **SDGs 2015 — 17 goals, 2030 Agenda**; milestones **Stockholm 1972 → Rio 1992 → Rio+20 2012 → SDGs 2015**.
- **EIA:** EPA 1986 → **EIA Notification 2006**; Category A (centre) / B (state); steps screening→scoping→baseline→prediction→EMP→public hearing→appraisal→clearance→monitoring.
- **Pollution law:** **Water Act 1974 (CPCB/SPCB) · Air Act 1981 · EPA 1986 · NGT 2010**; **Panchamrit & net-zero 2070** (COP26, 2021).

## 2. AP hazard & environment flashcards ⭐⭐

- **Cyclones:** **Diviseema 1977 (~10,000 dead)**, Hudhud 2014 (Vizag), Titli 2018 (Srikakulam), Michaung 2023 (Bapatla) — evacuation model = near-zero recent casualties.
- **Floods:** **2009 Krishna–Tungabhadra (Kurnool submerged, Mantralayam)**; Budameru–Vijayawada 2024; Michaung deltas.
- **Drought:** **Rayalaseema — double rain-shadow; Anantapur ≈ 550 mm, India's 2nd-driest district**; consequences — agrarian distress, groundwater collapse, fluorosis, out-migration.
- **Pollution/industrial disaster:** **LG Polymers styrene-gas leak, Visakhapatnam, 7 May 2020 (~12 deaths; NGT ₹50 cr)**; Visakhapatnam = CEPI critically-polluted cluster; pharma effluent (Parawada, Pydibhimavaram); Kolleru aquaculture pollution.
- **Institutions:** **APSDMA** (cyclone preparedness), **APPCB** (consents, monitoring, post-disaster pollution), Doppler radars (Visakhapatnam, Machilipatnam), NCRMP cyclone shelters, Coringa mangrove buffer.

## 3. Fifteen MCQs (key at end)

1. Risk in disaster studies is best expressed as — (a) hazard + vulnerability (b) **hazard × vulnerability ÷ capacity** (c) hazard ÷ capacity (d) vulnerability × capacity
2. The most damaging seismic waves at the surface are — (a) P-waves (b) S-waves (c) **L (surface) waves** (d) body waves
3. Andhra Pradesh lies mostly in seismic — (a) Zone V (b) Zone IV (c) **Zone II** (d) Zone I
4. A cloudburst is rainfall of at least — (a) 50 mm/hr (b) **100 mm/hr** (c) 200 mm/day (d) 100 mm/day
5. Tropical cyclones do not form near the equator because of the absence of — (a) warm water (b) humidity (c) **Coriolis force** (d) low pressure
6. India's worst cyclone by death toll on the AP coast was — (a) Hudhud 2014 (b) **Diviseema 1977** (c) Titli 2018 (d) Michaung 2023
7. Which phase typically weakens the SW monsoon? — (a) La Niña (b) **El Niño** (c) positive IOD (d) MJO wet phase
8. India's 2nd-driest district, in Rayalaseema, is — (a) Kurnool (b) Kadapa (c) **Anantapur** (d) Chittoor
9. "Deficient" rainfall per IMD means a departure from normal of — (a) 0 to –10% (b) –11 to –25% (c) **–26 to –50%** (d) below –60%
10. Under the DM Act 2005, the SDMA is chaired by the — (a) Governor (b) **Chief Minister** (c) Chief Secretary (d) Collector
11. The global disaster-risk-reduction framework for 2015–2030 is the — (a) Hyogo (b) Yokohama (c) **Sendai** (d) Kyoto Framework
12. "Our Common Future" (1987), which defined sustainable development, is the — (a) Stockholm Declaration (b) **Brundtland Report** (c) Agenda 21 (d) Rio Declaration
13. EIA in India derives its legal force from the — (a) Water Act 1974 (b) Air Act 1981 (c) **Environment (Protection) Act 1986** (d) Forest Act 1980
14. The 2020 industrial-pollution disaster at Visakhapatnam involved a leak of — (a) methyl isocyanate (b) **styrene** (c) chlorine (d) ammonia
15. The Central & State Pollution Control Boards were first created by the — (a) Air Act 1981 (b) **Water Act 1974** (c) EPA 1986 (d) NGT Act 2010

## 4. Mains answer skeletons (all item-15 PYQs + the cross-listed hazard question)

1. **Factors responsible for environmental pollution (2020a):** types (air/water/soil/noise/others) → driver-wise factors (population-urbanisation, industry-fossil fuel, transport, agrochemicals, deforestation, mining, plastic/e-waste, weak enforcement) → effects (health, acid rain, eutrophication, climate) → framework (Acts, NGT, NCAP) + AP (Vizag CEPI, LG Polymers) close. *(File 57 §1)*
2. **Concept & importance of Sustainable Development (2020b):** Brundtland 1987 definition → three pillars + principles → milestones Stockholm→Rio→SDGs 2015 → importance (limits, security, equity, resilience) → India's Panchamrit/net-zero 2070 + AP portfolio. *(File 57 §4)*
3. **EIA — meaning, evaluation, suggestions (2023a):** define + EPA 1986/2006 Notification + A/B categories + process steps → criticism (post-facto, weak baseline/consultation, no cumulative view, Draft 2020) → suggestions (independent authority, real public participation, strategic/cumulative EIA, strict monitoring, SIA). *(File 57 §3)*
4. **Drought — definition, types, drought-prone areas of India (2023b):** define → four types (meteorological→hydrological→agricultural→socio-economic; IMD thresholds) → prone belt (Rayalaseema, Marathwada, Bundelkhand, Kutch, W. Rajasthan, interior Karnataka, Kalahandi) → DPAP/watershed response. *(File 56 §2)*
5. **Disaster vulnerability of Rayalaseema (drought) & socio-economic consequences (2025a):** double rain-shadow + hard-rock aquifers + Anantapur ~550 mm → consequences (agrarian distress, groundwater collapse, fluorosis, out-migration, fodder/water scarcity) → responses (lift schemes, watersheds, micro-irrigation, millets/APCNF, solar) + evaluation → structural-not-episodic close. *(File 56 §2.3)*
6. **Role of APPCB in post-disaster pollution management (2025b):** APPCB mandate (consents, standards, monitoring, enforcement) → post-disaster secondary pollution (flood-water contamination, debris/hazardous waste, spills, water-quality testing, plant-accident response — LG Polymers template) → gaps & way forward (CAAQMS, manpower, APSDMA/NDRF coordination, prevention over cure). *(File 57 §2)*
7. **AP cyclone & flood vulnerability — causes, patterns, impacts (2025 Q14b, cross-listed):** Bay cyclone alley + flat delta + surge → landmark storms (1977→2023) & recurrent Godavari-Krishna floods (2009 Kurnool) → impacts → the evacuation-model success + mitigation (shelters, mangroves, Polavaram cushion, drainage) → climate-proofing close. *(File 56 §1 + File 55 §3)*
8. *(Predicted)* **Globalization and India's environment — opportunities and threats:** LPG 1991 → clean-tech/finance/awareness vs pollution-havens/extraction/supply-chain carbon → AP value chains (shrimp, pharma, Sri City) & shock exposure → sustainability as the hedge. *(File 57 §5)*
