## 1. Physiographic divisions of AP

### 1.1 The Coastal Plains (Circar coast + southern coast)

- Stretch **Srikakulam → Tirupati/Nellore** between Eastern Ghats and the Bay; widest in the **Krishna–Godavari twin-delta** tract, narrow near Visakhapatnam (Ghats approach the sea).
- **Godavari–Krishna deltas = "Rice Bowl of India/Andhra"** ⭐ — canal irrigation since Sir Arthur Cotton (below); coconut-paddy-aquaculture landscape.
- Coastal features: spits and bars, **Kolleru lake** (deltaic depression between the two deltas), **Pulicat lagoon** with **Sriharikota** barrier island (**SDSC-SHAR/ISRO**) ⭐, mangrove fringes (**Coringa**), beach-ridge plains; ports — **Visakhapatnam (natural harbour; Dolphin's Nose)**, Kakinada, Krishnapatnam, Machilipatnam (historic), Bhavanapadu/Mulapeta (building).
- Erosion hotspot: **Uppada coast** (Kakinada); cyclone-surge belt (Diviseema 1977 tragedy).

### 1.2 The Eastern Ghats and eastern hills

- **Discontinuous** ranges trending NE–SW, cut by the Godavari (Papi hills gorge) and Krishna; higher, forested northern blocks; drier southern blocks.
- **Northern block (Vizag agency):** Arma Konda, **Galikonda (~1,643 m)**, Chintapalli–Paderu–**Araku** highlands (coffee!), **Borra caves** (limestone, on Gosthani river) ⭐, **Ananthagiri** hills; **Maredumilli** forests (ASR district); **Papi hills (Papikonda)** on the Godavari.
- **Central-southern ranges (west of the plains, north→south):**
  - **Nallamala** — between Krishna and Penna; **Srisailam** (Jyotirlinga) plateau; Chenchu tribal homeland; **largest compact forest block of AP**.
  - **Velikonda** — eastern outer ridge (Nellore–Prakasam edge).
  - **Palakonda** and **Seshachalam** — Kadapa–Tirupati; **Tirumala hills** (Venkateswara temple); **RED SANDERS endemic belt** ⭐⭐; **Talakona falls** (AP's highest, ~82 m) in Seshachalam.
  - **Erramala (Yerramala)** — Kurnool–Nandyal; **Lankamala** (Jerdon's courser habitat), **Nagari hills** (Chittoor–Tirupati edge).
- **Horsley Hills (~1,265 m, Madanapalle/Annamayya district)** — "Andhra Ooty"; **Gandikota gorge of the Penna — "Grand Canyon of India"** ⭐.

### 1.3 Rayalaseema plateau / western pediplain

- Western Anantapur–Kurnool–Kadapa–Chittoor uplands (300–600 m) — the AP fringe of the **Deccan plateau**: granite-gneiss pediplains with tors and inselbergs, thin red soils, **rain-shadow semi-arid** climate → tanks + wells + drought; groundnut country.
- Note the basin lowlands within: Kunderu valley (Nandyal), Penna trough (Kadapa), Chittoor's Palar-Ponnai valleys.

## 2. Climate and climatic change in AP

| Element | Facts |
|---|---|
| Type | **Tropical monsoon**; hot summers (Apr–Jun), mild winters |
| Rainfall | State average **~940–970 mm**; **SW monsoon (Jun–Sep) gives ~60–65%**, **NE monsoon (Oct–Dec) ~25–30%** — NE monsoon is **crucial for south-coastal AP (Nellore–Tirupati) and Rayalaseema** ⭐ |
| Gradient | Rainfall decreases coast→interior: Srikakulam–Vizag ~1,000–1,100 mm; deltas ~1,000; **Rayalaseema ~550–700 mm — Anantapur the driest district (~550 mm; among India's driest outside the Thar)** ⭐⭐ |
| Temperature | Coastal humidity high; interior extremes — Rentachintala (Palnadu) and Tirupati-Vidapanakal belt record **46–48 °C+**; heat-wave state (2015: 1,700+ deaths) |
| Cyclones | AP coast is a **cyclone alley** of the Bay: **Diviseema (Nov 1977, ~10,000 deaths)**, 1990 Machilipatnam, **HUDHUD (12 Oct 2014 — devastated Visakhapatnam)** ⭐, Titli 2018 (Srikakulam), Michaung 2023 (floods, Tirupati-Nellore); storm surge + delta flooding are the classic hazards |
| Climate change | Rising heat-wave days, erratic monsoon, sea-level rise & salinity in the deltas, stronger cyclones; responses — **AP State Action Plan on Climate Change**, cyclone shelters + early warning (a post-1977/Hudhud success story), mangrove restoration (Coringa), solar push (Kurnool ultra-mega solar park among world's largest) ⭐ |

## 3. Soils of AP ⭐ (percentages approximate — quote as "about")

| Soil | Share | Where in AP | Crops |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Red soils** | **~64–66% (dominant)** | Rayalaseema, uplands of north-coastal & agency AP | Groundnut, millets, horticulture (with drip) |
| **Black (regur)** | **~20–25%** | **Krishna–Guntur–Palnadu, Prakasam, Kurnool, western Kadapa–Anantapur patches** | **Cotton, chillies (Guntur!), tobacco (Prakasam/Guntur "Virginia belt")** |
| **Alluvial (deltaic)** | ~5–10% | **Godavari–Krishna deltas, Penna delta, river valleys** | Paddy, sugarcane, banana, coconut |
| **Laterite/lateritic** | small patches | Visakhapatnam–Srikakulam uplands, parts of agency | Cashew, mango |
| **Coastal sandy** | narrow belt | Shore ridge plains | Casuarina, coconut |
| **Saline–alkaline (chowdu)** | patches | Delta margins, canal commands, Kolleru fringe | Needs gypsum/drainage; aquaculture conversion |

Problems: erosion on Rayalaseema slopes, salinity/waterlogging in canal deltas, fluoride in groundwater (notably erstwhile Prakasam/Guntur, Anantapur belts) ⭐.

## 4. Rivers of AP ("water" and "streams" in the syllabus)

### 4.1 Godavari — the "Dakshina Ganga" ⭐⭐

- Enters AP from Telangana; flows through the **Papikonda gorge**; splits at Rajahmundry into **Gautami & Vasishta** (major distributaries; also Vainateya, Vriddha Gautami) → **delta at Antarvedi (Sagara Sangamam)**; **Yanam** enclave sits in the delta.
- AP tributaries: **Sabari (with Sileru** — hydel cascade), Kinnerasani (TS border side).
- **Sir Arthur Cotton's DOWLESWARAM ANICUT/BARRAGE (1852)** turned the delta from famine tract to granary — Cotton is venerated in the deltas ⭐⭐; barrage rebuilt (1970–82) as **Sir Arthur Cotton Barrage**.
- **POLAVARAM (Indira Sagar) Project** ⭐⭐: multipurpose **national project** on the Godavari (Eluru/ASR districts): irrigation to Krishna–Godavari–Vizag zones, 960 MW hydel, drinking water, **right-main-canal link Godavari→Krishna (to Prakasam Barrage)** — the first operating river-link idea realised earlier via **Pattiseema lift (2015)**; issues — **R&R of submergence villages, Odisha/Chhattisgarh objections, diaphragm-wall damage in 2020 floods**.

### 4.2 Krishna ⭐⭐

- Enters AP at **Srisailam** reach after Karnataka–Telangana; gorge through Nallamala; **delta head at Vijayawada (Prakasam Barrage)**; joins the Bay at **Hamsaladeevi**; delta distributaries after barrage: three main canal systems (Eluru, Bandar, Ryves…) — quote simply as "Krishna delta canals (1855)".
- Tributaries relevant to AP: **Tungabhadra** (joins at **Sangameswaram/Kudali near Alampur–Kurnool border**), **Handri** (Kurnool town), Munneru/Muniyeru, Paleru (TS side).
- Major structures on the AP reach: **Srisailam (Neelam Sanjiva Reddy Sagar)** — 512 m long, 145 m high, **hydel-oriented (right-bank 770 MW AP; left-bank 900 MW TS underground)**; **Nagarjuna Sagar** (AP–TS boundary; world's largest masonry dam; Right canal = **Jawahar** to Guntur–Prakasam; Left = **Lal Bahadur** to TS+Krishna); **Pulichintala (K. L. Rao Sagar)** — flood-cushion & delta stabiliser; **PRAKASAM BARRAGE, Vijayawada (original anicut 1852–55 by Cotton's plan; present barrage 1957)**.
- **Krishna water disputes:** Bachawat Tribunal (KWDT-I, 1976 — 811 TMC to combined AP) → Brijesh Kumar (KWDT-II) → post-bifurcation AP–TS sharing (ad-hoc 66:34), **Krishna River Management Board (KRMB)**; upstream Almatti effects ⭐ (recurring Mains point).

### 4.3 Penna (Pinakini) and the southern rivers ⭐

- **Penna**: rises **Nandi Hills (Karnataka)**; through Anantapur (Penna Ahobilam) → Kadapa (**Gandikota gorge**, Mylavaram) → Nellore → sea near **Utukuru/Nellore**; length ~597 km; chronically water-short ("river of sand") yet the lifeline of Rayalaseema–Nellore.
- Tributaries: right — **Chitravati, Papagni, Cheyyeru (Bahuda+Punchha)**; left — **Jayamangali, Kunderu (Kurnool–Nandyal valley), Sagileru**.
- Structures: **Somasila** (Nellore; the Penna's main reservoir) → feeds **Kandaleru** (for **Telugu Ganga to Chennai**) ⭐; **Mylavaram, Gandikota** (GNSS-linked), **Sangam & Nellore barrages** (modernised 2022–23); **Penna delta canals** (Nellore anicut, 1850s-era Cotton-school works).
- Other south AP rivers: **Swarnamukhi** (Srikalahasti–Tirupati), **Araniar, Kalangi**, **Kandaleru**, **Gundlakamma** (**largest of AP's minor rivers**, rises in Nallamala, Prakasam; Gundlakamma reservoir) ⭐, Paleru, Musi, Manneru (Prakasam–Nellore streams).

### 4.4 North-coastal rivers (AP–Odisha set) ⭐

| River | Course | Project |
|---|---|---|
| **Vamsadhara** | Odisha → **Srikakulam** (sea at Kalingapatnam) | **Gotta barrage**; Neradi barrage (interstate tribunal with Odisha) |
| **Nagavali (Langulya)** | Odisha → Srikakulam (Thotapalli regulator in Vizianagaram) | **Thotapalli barrage**; Narayanapuram anicut; **Madduvalasa** (on Suvarnamukhi trib.); **Janjhavati rubber dam — India's first rubber dam** ⭐ (Vizianagaram) |
| **Champavathi, Gosthani** | Vizianagaram–Visakhapatnam (Gosthani rises near **Borra caves**) | **Tatipudi, Raiwada** reservoirs; Gosthani supplies Vizag |
| **Sarada, Varaha, Tandava, Yeleru** | Visakhapatnam–Anakapalli–Kakinada belt | **Yeleru (Eleru) reservoir** — irrigation + Vizag industrial water; Tandava reservoir; Konam |
| **Bahuda, Mahendratanaya** | Srikakulam (Odisha border; Mahendragiri flank) | minor schemes |

### 4.5 Waterfalls, lakes and springs (AP micro-bank)

- Falls: **Talakona (~82 m — highest in AP, Seshachalam)**, **Ethipothala** (Chandravanka stream near Nagarjuna Sagar, Palnadu), **Katiki** (Gosthani, Araku), Chaparai (Maredumilli), Ubbalamadugu/Tada (Chittoor–Tirupati), Kaigal (Koundinya).
- Lakes: **Kolleru — one of India's largest freshwater lakes** (between the deltas; **Ramsar site 2002; Atapaka bird sanctuary**; +5 ft contour encroachment issue ⭐); **Pulicat — India's 2nd largest brackish lagoon** (flamingos; Ramsar-proposed); **Cumbum tank** (Prakasam) — one of Asia's oldest working man-made tanks (Gajapati/Vijayanagara era — File 07 tie-in!).
- Hot springs: none notable; instead quote **Belum caves** (longest cave system in the Indian plains, Nandyal) with File 14 geology.

### 4.6 Water resources & irrigation stock-take (AP)

- Dependable water: AP's shares — **Krishna ~512 TMC (of KWDT-I 811), Godavari ~large but under-used**, Penna fully stretched; groundwater stress in Rayalaseema (over-exploited mandals, fluoride).
- **Lift-irrigation era schemes for Rayalaseema:** **Handri–Neeva Sujala Sravanthi (HNSS — Srisailam→Anantapur–Chittoor)**, **Galeru–Nagari (GNSS — Srisailam flood water → Gandikota → Chittoor)**, **Telugu Ganga (Krishna+Penna → Somasila–Kandaleru → also 15 TMC drinking water to Chennai)** ⭐, **Veligonda (Nallamala Sagar — tunnel from Srisailam foreshore for Prakasam–Nellore–Kadapa)**, **KC Canal (Kurnool–Cuddapah, 1863–70 — one of India's oldest canals, from Sunkesula on Tungabhadra)** ⭐, **Tungabhadra HLC/LLC + Rajolibanda (RDS)** shared with Karnataka/Telangana.
- North-AP counterparts: **Vamsadhara & Thotapalli projects, Uttarandhra Sujala Sravanthi (Godavari water via Polavaram left canal to Vizag–Srikakulam)**.
- Tanks: ~40,000 minor-irrigation tanks — the historic **golusukattu** legacy (Files 06/08 link); **"Neeru–Chettu"/Jalayagnam-era programmes**; **APWALTA 2002** (water-land-trees act) for regulation.

## 5. Last-mile checklist (File 13)

1. Three physiographic divisions + one marker each (deltas / Arma Konda / pediplain-tors).
2. Hill-range → district → special: Nallamala-Srisailam-Chenchus; Seshachalam-red sanders; Papikonda-Godavari gorge; Horsley Hills; Gandikota.

7. Kolleru (freshwater, Ramsar, Atapaka) vs Pulicat (brackish, Sriharikota).
