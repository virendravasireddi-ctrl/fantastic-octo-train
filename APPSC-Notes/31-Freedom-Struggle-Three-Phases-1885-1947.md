## 0. Frame

The standard periodisation (write it as your opening line in any "phases" answer): **Phase I, 1885–1905 — the MODERATE era** (constitutional petitioning by an educated elite); **Phase II, 1905–1920 — the EXTREMIST/militant era** (Swadeshi, boycott, revolutionary terrorism, Home Rule — politics reaches the towns); **Phase III, 1920–1947 — the GANDHIAN mass era** (three all-India satyagraha waves + constitutional interludes — politics reaches the villages). Each phase kept the gains of the last: the Moderates built the platform and the economic critique, the Extremists the passion and the techniques, Gandhi the mass army. *(2025 Q5a = Phase I; 2025 Q5b = Phase III — both direct hits. Leader biographies are in File 30; this file gives the connected narrative.)*

## 1. Phase I — the Moderates, 1885–1905 ⭐⭐⭐ (2025 Q5a)

- **Who:** Dadabhai Naoroji ("Grand Old Man of India" ⭐), Pherozeshah Mehta, W.C. Bonnerjee, Surendranath Banerjea ("Indian Burke"), **Gopal Krishna GOKHALE** (Gandhi's guru; **Servants of India Society 1905** ⭐), Justice Ranade, Dinshaw Wacha; British allies — Hume, Wedderburn; **British Committee of the INC & the journal *India* (1889–90)**; Naoroji thrice Congress President and **MP in the British Commons (1892, Liberal, Finsbury)** ⭐.
- **Method:** the "3 Ps" — prayer, petition, protest; memorials, council speeches, press; faith in **British justice** ("un-British rule" — the point was to make Britain live up to itself).
- **Demands:** expansion & Indianisation of legislative councils; **simultaneous ICS exams in India** ⭐; separation of judiciary from executive; cut army expenditure & Home Charges; end the drain; protect Indian industry; repeal the Arms & Vernacular Press Acts; spread of education.
- **Achievements (the "credit" paragraph):**
  1. **The economic critique of colonialism** ⭐⭐ — Naoroji's drain theory (*Poverty and Un-British Rule in India*, 1901), **R.C. Dutt's *Economic History***, Wacha — nationalism's intellectual foundation; "no other colonial movement produced so complete an economic indictment."
  2. **Indian Councils Act 1892** — indirect election, budget discussion (their one legislative fruit).
  3. Forced the **Welby Commission** on Indian expenditure (Naoroji, Wacha deposed).
  4. Built an **all-India political platform, trained a generation**, spread democratic ideas — "the seed-time of nationalism."
- **Critique (the "limits" paragraph):** narrow social base ("a microscopic minority" — Dufferin's sneer); mendicancy (Tilak: "political mendicancy"); no mass contact by design (fear of the "unlettered mob"); achievements meagre against growing repression → the young turned Extremist after **Curzon** (1899–1905: Calcutta Corporation Act 1899, Universities Act 1904, Official Secrets Act, **Partition of Bengal 1905**).
- **Verdict line for 2025 Q5a:** they did not win concessions, they **created Indian nationalism** — platform, ideology (economic nationalism), and the constitutional habit the Republic inherited.

## 2. Phase II — Extremism, Revolution & Home Rule, 1905–1920 ⭐⭐⭐

### 2.1 Swadeshi/Vandemataram & the Extremists (1905–08)
Full treatment File 30 §2 — hold the chain: **Partition 16 Oct 1905 → boycott-swadeshi-national education-samitis → Lal-Bal-Pal & Aurobindo → Swaraj goal (Calcutta 1906, Naoroji) → SURAT SPLIT 1907 → repression (Tilak to Mandalay 1908) → Morley-Minto 1909 (separate electorates ⭐) → annulment 1911**. Extremist creed: swaraj as birthright, **self-reliance (atma-shakti)**, mass politics, sacrifice — Aurobindo's "New Lahore-programme" of passive resistance.

### 2.2 Revolutionary terrorism — first wave ⭐⭐ (match-pairs)
| Organisation / event | Names to hold |
|---|---|
| **Anushilan Samiti (1902) / Jugantar** (Bengal) | Barindra Ghosh, Bhupendranath Datta; **Muzaffarpur bomb 1908 — Khudiram Bose & Prafulla Chaki** (hanged/suicide — Khudiram at 18 ⭐); **Alipore Conspiracy Case** (Aurobindo acquitted — C.R. Das defending ⭐) |
| **Mitra Mela → ABHINAV BHARAT (1904)** — Maharashtra | **V.D. Savarkar** (transported 1911, Cellular Jail); earlier the **Chapekar brothers shot Rand (Poona, 1897)** — the first political assassinations |
| **India House, London** | Shyamji Krishna Varma; **Madan Lal Dhingra shot Curzon Wylie (1909)** |
| **Delhi conspiracy 1912** | Rash Behari Bose & Basanta Biswas — bomb on Hardinge |
| **GHADAR PARTY, San Francisco, 1913** ⭐ | **Lala Har Dayal**, Sohan Singh Bhakna; paper *Ghadar*; **KOMAGATA MARU incident 1914** ⭐ (Budge Budge firing) → 1915 planned rising betrayed; **Kartar Singh Sarabha hanged at 19** (Bhagat Singh's hero) |
| **Berlin Committee & Kabul provisional govt 1915** | Raja Mahendra Pratap (President), Barkatullah, Obaidullah Sindhi |

### 2.3 War, Home Rule & the hinge year 1916–1920
- **WWI:** India's men & money in Britain's war → expectation of reward; **Defence of India Act 1915** repression instead.
- **HOME RULE LEAGUES 1916 (Tilak — April; Besant — September)** — File 30 §3; **LUCKNOW 1916** ⭐⭐: Congress reunited (Moderate + Extremist) **and** the **Congress-League Pact** (separate electorates conceded — unity at a price).
- **Montagu Declaration (20 Aug 1917)** → **Government of India Act 1919: DYARCHY in the provinces** ⭐ (transferred vs reserved subjects; bicameral centre; extended separate electorates — Sikhs etc.); Congress verdict: "inadequate, unsatisfactory, disappointing."
- **1919 — the breaking point:** **Rowlatt Act (Feb)** → Gandhi's first all-India satyagraha → **JALLIANWALA BAGH 13 April 1919** ⭐ (Dyer; **Hunter Commission** whitewash; **Tagore renounced his knighthood**; Sankaran Nair quit the Viceroy's Council) → **Khilafat wrong** (Sèvres) → the stage set for Non-Cooperation. **Tilak died 1 Aug 1920** — the very day NCM was launched: the torch passed to Gandhi ⭐.

## 3. Phase III — the Gandhian era, 1920–1947 ⭐⭐⭐ (2025 Q5b)

The movement now breathes in a rhythm ⭐ — **Struggle → Truce → Struggle (S-T-S)**: mass movement, withdrawal, constructive work/council politics, next bigger wave.

| Sub-phase | Content to hold |
|---|---|
| **NCM + Khilafat 1920–22** | Nagpur 1920 constitution (linguistic PCCs ⭐, 4-anna membership); boycotts, Tilak Swaraj Fund, charkha; peaks — Prince of Wales boycott 1921; **withdrawn after CHAURI CHAURA 5 Feb 1922** (File 30 §5) |
| **Swarajist interlude 1923–27** | **C.R. Das & Motilal Nehru — Swaraj Party (1923)** ⭐: "wreck the councils from within" (pro-changers) vs no-changers (Patel, Prasad, Rajaji — constructive work); Das's Calcutta Corporation; deadlock after Das's death 1925 |
| **Simon to Lahore 1927–29** | **SIMON COMMISSION 1927 — all-white → "Simon Go Back"** ⭐; lathi-charge death of **Lala Lajpat Rai (1928)**; **NEHRU REPORT 1928** (Motilal — Dominion status, joint electorates; **Jinnah's "Fourteen Points" 1929** in reply ⭐); Bardoli 1928; **LAHORE CONGRESS Dec 1929 (Jawaharlal presiding) — PURNA SWARAJ; first Independence Day 26 Jan 1930** ⭐⭐ |
| **CDM 1930–34** | Dandi → Gandhi-Irwin → 2nd RTC failure → resumption & Communal Award/Poona Pact 1932 (File 30 §5); **Karachi 1931 — Fundamental Rights & National Economic Programme resolution** ⭐ (Patel presiding, written by Nehru) |
| **1935 Act & ministries 1935–39** | **GoI Act 1935**: provincial autonomy replaced dyarchy, dyarchy moved to the centre, federal scheme stillborn ("a machine with strong brakes and no engine"); **1937 elections — Congress ministries in 7→8 provinces**; **resigned Oct-Nov 1939** (war without consultation) — League's "**Deliverance Day**" 22 Dec 1939 ⭐ |
| **War years 1939–45** | **August Offer 1940**; **Individual Satyagraha 1940–41** (Vinoba first); **CRIPPS MISSION 1942** ("post-dated cheque") → **QUIT INDIA Aug 1942** (File 30 §6); Gandhi's 21-day fast 1943; **Bengal Famine 1943** ⭐; C.R. formula & Gandhi-Jinnah talks 1944; **Bose & the INA** (File 30 §7.1) |
| **Endgame 1945–47** | **Simla Conference & Wavell Plan 1945** (wrecked on Jinnah's veto); **INA trials → RIN revolt Feb 1946**; 1945–46 elections (League sweeps Muslim seats ⭐); **Cabinet Mission 1946 → Partition & freedom** — File 33 |

- **The second revolutionary wave (1920s–30s) ⭐⭐:** **HRA 1924 (Kanpur — Bismil, Ashfaqullah; KAKORI 1925)** → **HSRA 1928 (Delhi, Ferozeshah Kotla — "Socialist" added; Chandrashekhar AZAD)**; **Saunders shooting 1928** (Lajpat Rai avenged); **Assembly bomb 8 April 1929 (Bhagat Singh & B.K. Dutt — "to make the deaf hear")**; jail hunger strike (**Jatin Das died, 64th day** ⭐); **Bhagat Singh, Rajguru, Sukhdev hanged 23 March 1931** ⭐⭐ (Shaheed Diwas); Azad fell at Alfred Park, Feb 1931; **CHITTAGONG ARMOURY RAID 1930 — Surya Sen ("Masterda")**, Kalpana Datta, **Pritilata Waddedar** ⭐.
- **2025 Q5b skeleton (Gandhi's movements 1920–47):** NCM (aims-programme-spread-withdrawal) → CDM (salt as symbol; women's & business participation; Gandhi-Irwin; why it declined) → QIM (spontaneity, underground, parallel govts, verdict) → thread: each wave wider and deeper (towns 1920 → villages 1930 → leaderless people 1942); close with the S-T-S strategy and the mass legitimacy that made 1947 inevitable.

## 4. Andhra tie-ins ⭐

1. Phase-wise Andhra mirror (File 24): Vandemataram 1907 (Phase II) → Home Rule's strongest field → Chirala-Perala, Palnadu, Pedanandipadu 1921–22, **Alluri 1922–24** → Salt satyagraha & Quit India in the coastal districts.
2. **Nagpur 1920 linguistic PCCs → separate ANDHRA PCC (1917/1920)** — the administrative seed of the Andhra movement (File 25).
3. **Pattabhi Sitaramayya** — the Andhra historian of the Congress (*History of the Indian National Congress*) and Tripuri 1939 protagonist.
4. **Duggirala Gopalakrishnaiah's Chirala-Perala "Ramanagara"** — the NCM's most creative local experiment (File 24).
