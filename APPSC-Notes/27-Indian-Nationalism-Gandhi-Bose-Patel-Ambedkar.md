## 0. Frame

National consciousness (File 26 §3) organised itself in **1885** as the Indian National Congress, passed through **Moderate petitioning → Extremist mass agitation (Vandemataram/Swadeshi) → Home Rule → the Gandhian mass phase (Satyagraha: NCM → CDM → Quit India)**, while a parallel **anti-caste plebeian stream (Phule → Narayana Guru → Periyar → Ambedkar)** demanded that *swaraj* include social democracy. Hold five biographies to answer-depth: **Gandhi, Bose, Patel, Ambedkar, Periyar** — 2020 Q4b (Ambedkar) and 2025 Q4b (safety valve) came straight from this file.

## 1. The Indian National Congress (1885) ⭐⭐⭐

- **Founded 28 Dec 1885, Gokuldas Tejpal Sanskrit College, Bombay** — **72 delegates**; convener **Allan Octavian HUME** (retired ICS); first president **W.C. BONNERJEE** ⭐; (venue shifted from Poona due to plague). Early presidents: Naoroji (1886, '93, 1906), Badruddin Tyabji (1887 — first Muslim), George Yule (1888 — first Englishman); **first woman — Annie Besant 1917**; **first Indian woman — Sarojini Naidu 1925** ⭐.
- **"SAFETY VALVE" theory ⭐⭐ (2025 Q4b):** claim — Hume founded the INC (with Dufferin's blessing) to channel post-1857 educated discontent harmlessly ("saving the Raj from revolution"); source — William Wedderburn's 1913 biography of Hume; **Lala Lajpat Rai** used it to attack Moderates; **extreme left (R.P. Dutt)** made it conspiracy. **Modern verdict (Bipan Chandra):** a **myth** — no "Dufferin letter" evidence; the Congress was the culmination of pre-existing associations and political awakening; at best Hume was a **"lightning conductor"** ⭐ — Indians used a sympathetic Englishman as cover against official repression. *Skeleton: state theory → origins/users → evidence against → lightning-conductor formula → conclude: INC = product of nationalism, not of official design.*
- **Moderate phase 1885–1905** (2025 Q5a overlap): prayer-petition-protest ("3 Ps"); demands — council reform, ICS exams in India, drain-military spending cuts, separation of judiciary-executive; achievements — **economic critique of colonialism (Naoroji-Dutt drain theory) ⭐**, Indian Councils Act 1892, a national platform & training; limits — narrow base, "political mendicancy" (Tilak's jibe).

## 2. Vandemataram / Swadeshi movement (1905–08) ⭐⭐

- **Partition of Bengal — Curzon, announced July, in force 16 Oct 1905** ("Bengal divided, nationalism doubled"); observed as **Raksha Bandhan day (Tagore)**; *Amar Sonar Bangla* written.
- Programme: **boycott of foreign cloth, swadeshi enterprise** (mills, Bengal Chemicals — P.C. Ray; **National Council of Education & Bengal National College 1906 — Aurobindo principal** ⭐), samitis, vernacular songs and festivals (Tilak's Ganapati-Shivaji model).
- **Extremists (Lal-Bal-Pal + Aurobindo)** took command; **Congress Calcutta 1906 (Naoroji presiding) declared "SWARAJ" the goal** ⭐; **SURAT SPLIT 1907** (Moderates vs Extremists; reunited Lucknow 1916); repression → **Tilak's 1908 Mandalay sentence**; revolutionary terrorism's first wave (Khudiram Bose; Alipore case — Aurobindo).
- Fallout: **Muslim League founded Dhaka 1906**; **Morley-Minto 1909 — SEPARATE ELECTORATES** ⭐⭐ (the "Minto legacy"); Partition **annulled 1911** (Delhi durbar; capital shifted to Delhi).
- **In Andhra the Swadeshi echo = "VANDEMATARAM MOVEMENT" (1907)** ⭐⭐ — Bipin Chandra Pal's coastal tour, **Harisarvottama Rao**, Kotappakonda firing, national schools (File 39 — the exam expects you to join the all-India and Andhra halves).

## 3. Home Rule Movement (1916–18) ⭐⭐

| | **Tilak's League** | **Besant's League** |
|---|---|---|
| Launched | **April 1916, Belgaum/Poona** | **September 1916, Adyar (Madras)** |
| Area | Maharashtra (ex-Bombay city), Karnataka, CP, Berar | rest of India **incl. ANDHRA ⭐** (strongest recruitment) |
| Slogans | **"Swaraj is my birthright and I shall have it"** ⭐ | papers **New India, Commonweal**; George Arundale organising |

- Model: Irish Home Rule; demand — self-government within the Empire, **war-time agitation without violence**; **Besant's internment (June 1917)** electrified the country → **Montagu Declaration, 20 Aug 1917** ("responsible government" as goal) ⭐ and **Besant INC President 1917**. Lucknow Pact 1916 (Congress–League; Tilak-Jinnah; Congress conceded separate electorates ⭐). The Leagues built the mass network **Gandhi inherited**.

## 4. The anti-caste / Self-Respect stream ⭐⭐⭐

### 4.1 JYOTIBA PHULE (1827–90) — the pioneer
- Books: ***Gulamgiri* (Slavery, 1873)** ⭐, *Sarvajanik Satya Dharma*; inverted the Aryan myth — brahminism as conquest ideology; hailed **Shivaji as "kulwadi-bhushan"** and dedicated Gulamgiri to the American anti-slavery movement.
- Work: **first girls' school in Poona (1848, wife SAVITRIBAI PHULE teaching — first Indian woman teacher ⭐)**; schools for "untouchables"; widow home & infanticide prevention; opened his **water tank to all castes**; **SATYASHODHAK SAMAJ (24 Sept 1873)** — truth-seeking, no priest, no intermediary; title **"MAHATMA" conferred 1888** ⭐. Ambedkar counted Phule among his **three gurus (Buddha, Kabir, Phule)** ⭐.

### 4.2 SREE NARAYANA GURU (1856–1928) — Kerala
- Ezhava sage; **Aruvippuram consecration 1888** ⭐ — installed a Shiva idol himself ("My Shiva, not a Brahmin's"); **SNDP Yogam 1903** (with Dr Palpu & poet Kumaran Asan).
- Watchwords: **"ONE CASTE, ONE RELIGION, ONE GOD FOR MAN"** ⭐⭐; "whatever the religion, it suffices if man improves"; "no caste" (jati venda). Backdrop of Vaikom Satyagraha (1924–25, temple-road entry; Gandhi & Periyar both involved ⭐ — Periyar = "Vaikom Virar/hero").

### 4.3 PERIYAR E.V. RAMASAMY (1879–1973) & the SELF-RESPECT MOVEMENT ⭐⭐
- Congressman till **1925** (quit over the Cheranmadevi gurukulam's caste discrimination & communal-representation resolution); Vaikom hero 1924.
- **SELF-RESPECT MOVEMENT, 1925** ⭐⭐: human dignity before everything; **self-respect marriages** (no priest, no ritual — later legalised in Madras, 1967) ⭐; women's rights (property, divorce, against child marriage & devadasi system); rationalism — against superstition, priestcraft and God-centred authority ("no god; the one who created god is a fool…" — his radical edge); journals ***Kudi Arasu* (1925)**, *Viduthalai*.
- Trajectory: took over the **JUSTICE PARTY (1938–39) → DRAVIDAR KAZHAGAM (1944)** ⭐ — social movement, not electoral (DMK split off 1949 — Annadurai); anti-Hindi agitations (1937–40); demanded Dravida Nadu. Title **"Periyar" (the Great One) given by women's conference 1938** ⭐; "Vaikom hero"; the southern counterpart of Phule — link all three for a "social democracy before political democracy" answer (ties to Ambedkar §7 and the Andhra Non-Brahmin/Justice material, File 39 ⭐).

## 5. GANDHI & SATYAGRAHA ⭐⭐⭐

- **South Africa 1893–1914:** satyagraha invented (Natal; Tolstoy Farm; Transvaal marches); returned **9 Jan 1915** ⭐ (now Pravasi Bharatiya Divas); guru — **Gokhale** ("one year's tour with ears open, mouth shut"); Sabarmati Ashram 1915/17.
- **SATYAGRAHA — the doctrine:** truth-force; resistance to wrong **without hatred and without violence** (ahimsa as active love, not passivity); methods — non-cooperation, civil disobedience, fasting, constructive programme; the satyagrahi **accepts suffering & punishment** — converting, not coercing, the opponent; ends do not justify means ⭐ (quote: "an eye for an eye makes the whole world blind").
- **The apprenticeship trio ⭐⭐:** **CHAMPARAN 1917** (Bihar, tinkathia indigo — first civil disobedience; Rajendra Prasad joined) → **AHMEDABAD MILL STRIKE 1918** (first hunger strike; 35% wage award) → **KHEDA 1918** (Gujarat, revenue suspension — first non-cooperation; **Patel emerges ⭐**).
- **Rowlatt Satyagraha 1919** (first all-India) → **JALLIANWALA BAGH, 13 April 1919 (Dyer; Baisakhi)** ⭐ → Khilafat issue → **NON-COOPERATION MOVEMENT 1920–22**: surrender of titles, boycott of schools-courts-councils-foreign cloth; charkha & Tilak Swaraj Fund; "Swaraj in one year"; **called off after CHAURI CHAURA, 5 Feb 1922** ⭐ (means over ends — the defining Gandhian act).
- **CIVIL DISOBEDIENCE 1930–34:** 11 demands → **DANDI MARCH, 12 March–6 April 1930 (Sabarmati → Dandi, 78 marchers, 240 miles) — breaking the SALT LAW** ⭐⭐; Dharasana (Sarojini Naidu); NWFP — **Khan Abdul Ghaffar Khan's Khudai Khidmatgars**; **Gandhi-Irwin Pact (5 Mar 1931)** → Second Round Table Conference (1931, Gandhi attends — failure) → resumed 1932.
- **Communal Award & POONA PACT (Sept 1932)** ⭐⭐ — Gandhi's Yeravada fast against **separate electorates for Depressed Classes**; settlement with **Ambedkar**: joint electorates with **reserved seats (148, up from 71)** — the crucial Gandhi–Ambedkar hinge (see §7).
- Also hold: Harijan campaign & Wardha constructive programme; **Individual Satyagraha 1940–41 (Vinoba first, Nehru second)** ⭐.

## 6. QUIT INDIA MOVEMENT (August Kranti), 1942 ⭐⭐⭐

- **Context:** WWII reverses (fall of Singapore-Rangoon), **failure of CRIPPS MISSION (March–April 1942** — "a post-dated cheque"; Dominion status later, partition seed via provincial opt-out) ⭐.
- **8 Aug 1942 — "Quit India" resolution, Gowalia Tank (August Kranti Maidan), Bombay**; Gandhi's mantra: **"DO OR DIE" (karo ya maro)** ⭐⭐. 9 Aug: entire leadership arrested (Gandhi → Aga Khan Palace; Working Committee → Ahmednagar Fort).
- **Leaderless mass upsurge:** students-peasants-workers; attacks on communications & symbols of the Raj; **underground network — Jayaprakash Narayan (Hazaribagh escape), Aruna Asaf Ali (hoisted the flag at Gowalia Tank ⭐), Ram Manohar Lohia, Achyut Patwardhan, Usha Mehta's CONGRESS RADIO** ⭐; **parallel governments (prati sarkars)** — **Ballia (Chittu Pandey, first)**, **Tamluk (Jatiya Sarkar; Matangini Hazra, 73, shot with the flag ⭐)**, **SATARA (Nana Patil — longest, till 1944)**.
- Repression: ~10,000 dead (unofficial), 90,000+ arrests; aloof/opposed — **Muslim League ("Divide and Quit"), CPI ("people's war" line), Hindu Mahasabha; Ambedkar in the Viceroy's Council** — but **1942 proved Britain could hold India no longer**: the psychological point of no return ⭐ (2020 Q5b balance: failed as revolt, decisive as verdict).

## 7. The big four — leader dossiers ⭐⭐⭐

### 7.1 SUBHASH CHANDRA BOSE (1897–1945) — "Netaji"
- Resigned ICS (1921); Bengal radical (Deshbandhu C.R. Das's lieutenant; Calcutta Corporation CEO); with Nehru, the Congress left — **Independence for India League**; INC President **Haripura 1938** (National Planning Committee ⭐) and **TRIPURI 1939 — defeated Gandhi's candidate Pattabhi Sitaramayya ("Pattabhi's defeat is my defeat" — Gandhi ⭐⭐, the Andhra link, File 39)**; forced out → **FORWARD BLOC (May 1939)**.
- **The Great Escape (Jan 1941)** Calcutta → Kabul → Berlin (Free India Centre, Azad Hind Radio) → submarine to Singapore (1943): took over the **INDIAN NATIONAL ARMY (Azad Hind Fauj — founded from POWs by Mohan Singh 1942 on Rash Behari Bose's platform)** ⭐; **Provisional Government of Free India (Arzi Hukumat-e-Azad Hind), Singapore, 21 Oct 1943** (recognised by 9 Axis states; Andamans renamed **Shaheed & Swaraj**).
- Slogans: **"Give me blood, I will give you freedom"; "Jai Hind"; "Delhi Chalo"** ⭐; women's **Rani Jhansi Regiment (Lakshmi Sahgal)**; INA at **Imphal-Kohima 1944 (flag at Moirang)** — retreat with Japan's collapse; reported air-crash death, Taipei, **18 Aug 1945**.
- **Post-war detonation:** **INA TRIALS, Red Fort, Nov 1945 (Shah Nawaz Khan, Prem Sahgal, Gurbaksh Dhillon; defended by Bhulabhai Desai-Nehru)** → nationwide upsurge → fed the **RIN ratings' revolt, Feb 1946** ⭐ — Bose's real victory: the loyalty of the armed forces cracked.

### 7.2 SARDAR VALLABHBHAI PATEL (1875–1950) — "Iron Man / Bismarck of India"
- Gandhi's general: **Kheda 1918**, Nagpur flag satyagraha 1923, **BARDOLI 1928** ⭐⭐ — the no-revenue satyagraha (22% hike rolled back) where the **women of Bardoli gave him "SARDAR"**; INC President **Karachi 1931** (Fundamental Rights resolution session); the organisation man of the CDM & 1937 ministries; Ahmednagar prisoner 1942–45.
- **1947–50:** Home, States & Information Minister; with **V.P. Menon** (and Mountbatten's weight) executed the **INTEGRATION OF 560+ PRINCELY STATES** ⭐⭐ — instruments of accession + **privy purses**; "Junagadh (plebiscite 1948), HYDERABAD (Operation POLO — 'police action', 13–17 Sept 1948 ⭐ — the Nizam & Razakars; Telangana's entry into India, cross-ref File 40), Kashmir (accession 26 Oct 1947)"; a "bloodless revolution" completed by Nov 1949 (merger & democratisation).
- Also: steadied the services — **"IRON FRAME"; called the "patron saint of the civil services"** ⭐ (defended ICS→IAS in the Constituent Assembly); first Deputy PM; **Statue of Unity; National Unity Day 31 Oct**; Bharat Ratna 1991 (posthumous).

### 7.3 DR B.R. AMBEDKAR (1891–1956) ⭐⭐⭐ (2020 Q4b)
- **Education against odds:** Mahar caste; Columbia (PhD; guru **John Dewey**) & LSE (DSc — *The Problem of the Rupee*); Bar-at-Law — the most educated Indian of his generation.
- **For the untouchables (2020 Q4b core):**
  1. Organisation & press — **Bahishkrit Hitakarini Sabha 1924** ("educate, agitate, organise" ⭐); papers ***Mooknayak* 1920, *Bahishkrit Bharat* 1927, *Janata***.
  2. Direct action — **MAHAD SATYAGRAHA 1927** (Chavadar tank water; **burned the Manusmriti, 25 Dec 1927** ⭐); **KALARAM TEMPLE entry, Nashik 1930**.
  3. Political safeguards — Depressed Classes' voice at **all three Round Table Conferences** ⭐; Communal Award → **POONA PACT 1932** (reserved seats compromise with Gandhi); **Independent Labour Party 1936; Scheduled Castes Federation 1942**.
  4. Ideas — ***Annihilation of Caste* (1936)** ⭐ (the undelivered Jat-Pat-Todak address); "caste is not a division of labour, it is a division of labourers"; *Who Were the Shudras?*; **conversion as liberation — embraced BUDDHISM with ~5 lakh followers, Nagpur, 14 Oct 1956** ⭐ (*The Buddha and His Dhamma*; Navayana).
- **For labour (the 2020 Q4b second limb ⭐):** as **Labour Member, Viceroy's Executive Council 1942–46** — reduction of factory hours **(8-hour day)**, dearness allowance, **maternity benefit**, employment exchanges, tripartite labour machinery, skilled-worker training; ILP fought khoti & mahar-vatan servitude in Bombay; water-power policy (Damodar/Hirakud groundwork).
- **Nation-builder:** **Chairman, DRAFTING COMMITTEE of the Constitution (29 Aug 1947)** — "chief architect" ⭐⭐; Art 17 (untouchability abolished), Art 32 ("heart and soul of the Constitution" ⭐); first **Law Minister** — resigned 1951 over the stalled **HINDU CODE BILL** (women's property & marriage rights — his second great cause); "grammar of anarchy" & the warning: political democracy without **social and economic democracy** is unstable ⭐ (26 Nov 1949 speech — quote it to close any Ambedkar answer). Bharat Ratna **1990**; **Mahaparinirvan Diwas 6 Dec**.

### 7.4 GANDHI one-paragraph verdict (for "role of Gandhi" questions)
Turned a lawyers' Congress into a **mass movement** (peasants, women, workers, "Harijans" inside the fold); gave a **technique (satyagraha)** that matched Indian conditions — moral, cheap, participatory, controllable; three national waves (NCM 1920–22, CDM 1930–34, QIM 1942) each deepened the reach of nationalism; the **constructive programme** (khadi, Hindu-Muslim unity, untouchability removal, village uplift) made freedom a social project; limits — Chauri Chaura withdrawals, failures on Partition and full Dalit reconciliation — yet **independence came through the mass legitimacy he built**; assassinated **30 Jan 1948 (Godse)**.

## 8. Andhra tie-ins ⭐ (Part-B multipliers)

1. **Vandemataram Movement 1907** = Andhra's Swadeshi (File 39); **Besant's Home Rule** ran strongest in Andhra.
2. Gandhian waves in Andhra: **Chirala-Perala, Palnadu forest satyagraha, Pedanandipadu no-tax (1921–22); Alluri's Rampa 1922–24; Salt satyagraha at Dandi's Andhra twins (Mypadu etc.); Quit India in coastal towns** (File 39).
3. **Pattabhi Sitaramayya's Tripuri defeat 1939** — the Bose link every examiner loves.
4. **Operation Polo 1948** (Patel) → Hyderabad State → the 1956 merger story (File 40).
5. Ambedkar ↔ Andhra's **Adi-Andhra movement (1917, Bhagya Reddy Varma)** (File 38).

## 9. Prelims nuggets

1. **INC — 28 Dec 1885, Bombay, 72 delegates, Hume + W.C. Bonnerjee**; safety-valve source = **Wedderburn 1913**; counter = **"lightning conductor" (Lajpat Rai's phrase, Bipan Chandra's verdict)**.
