## 4. Mains skeletons (10)

1. **Company rule transformed Andhra's land and society (Files 21).** Circars→Ceded acquisition; zamindari vs ryotwari; poligar destruction; famine-deindustrialisation vs Cotton's deltas — verdict.
2. **Munro–Mackenzie–Brown–Cotton (2023-style).** 3–4 sentences each + Andhra memorials; thesis: "the Raj's best men governed, mapped, read and watered Andhra."
3. **Missionaries and education (2025).** Missions table → schools/girls/Dalits/print/medicine → critique → verdict.
4. **Social awakening's factors (2025).** Education, press, missionary challenge, Brahmo-Theosophy, infrastructure, Veeresalingam's agency.
5. **Veeresalingam critically (2020).** Achievements → limits → "one-man renaissance".
6. **Gurajada + Gidugu (2023).** Literature as reform; vyavaharika movement's war on grandhika.
7. **Library movement (2020).** 1914 Vijayawada → Association/journal → functions → 1948 Act; Ayyanki-Gadicherla-Nageswara Rao.
8. **Justice Party's formation (2020).** Five circumstances → office record (Communal G.O., HRE Act, suffrage) → legacy.
9. **Alluri and Rampa (2023).** Causes-course-end-memory; subaltern rising with a nationalist head.
10. **1950s formation of AP (2020) / Gentlemen's Agreement audit (2025) / causes & impact of bifurcation.** Sreeramulu → 1953 → SRC vs merger → Agreement's breach → 1969/1972 → 371-D → 2001–14 → 2 June 2014 balance-sheet.
