## 1. Geological framework of India

Geological Survey of India's classic **four-fold division of Indian stratigraphy** ⭐:

| Division | Era | Rock systems | One-line identity |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Archaean** | Pre-Cambrian (oldest) | Archaean gneisses/granites; **Dharwar system** | Foundation ("basement complex"); **azoic (no fossils)**, crystalline, metallic minerals |
| **Purana** | Proterozoic | **Cuddapah** & **Vindhyan** systems | Old sedimentaries on the shield; building stone, diamonds, barytes |
| **Dravidian** | Palaeozoic (Cambrian–Carboniferous) | Mostly in extra-Peninsular (Himalayan) region | Marine sediments; limestone, slate |
| **Aryan** | Upper Carboniferous → Recent | **Gondwana**, Deccan Trap, Tertiary, Quaternary | Coal, lava plateau, petroleum, alluvium |

### 1.1 Rock systems in detail

- **Archaean gneisses & granites** (~two-thirds of the Peninsula's basement): Bundelkhand gneiss (oldest dated rocks), Bellary gneiss, **Nilgiri charnockites**; also in Aravalis and as Himalayan core.
- **Dharwar system** — first **metamorphosed sedimentary** rocks (schists); type area Dharwar–Shimoga (Karnataka); highly **metalliferous**: **gold (Kolar, Hutti), iron, manganese, chromite, copper**; equivalents — Aravali series, **Khondalite/Charnockite belts of the Eastern Ghats** ⭐ (AP link, File 22).
- **Cuddapah system** (named after **Kadapa, AP** ⭐): quartzites, slates, **limestones**; famous for **barytes, asbestos, Cuddapah slabs**; **no fossils**; classic basin = crescent-shaped **Cuddapah basin** (File 22).
- **Vindhyan system:** vast sandstone-shale-limestone cover; **red sandstone** (Red Fort, Sanchi, Fatehpur Sikri); **diamond-bearing conglomerates — Panna (MP) and the Golconda/Krishna-valley diamonds** ⭐; building-stone belt of India.
- **Gondwana system** (Upper Carboniferous–Jurassic; fluvio-lacustrine rift basins along Damodar, Mahanadi, **Godavari valleys**): holds **~98% of India's coal**; plant fossils (Glossopteris); the name comes from the **Gond land** — and the system underpins **Singareni coal of the Godavari valley** ⭐.
- **Deccan Trap** (Cretaceous–Eocene): **fissure-eruption flood basalts**, ~5 lakh sq km; step-like ("trap") relief; weathers to **black regur soil**; quarried as road metal; **Rajahmundry Traps** — outliers near the Godavari delta ⭐.
- **Tertiary system:** Himalayan uplift phase; **Nummulitic limestone**; **petroleum** of Assam–Gujarat–Mumbai High–**KG basin**; **lignite of Neyveli**; Shiwalik molasse with vertebrate fossils.
- **Quaternary:** Indo-Gangetic **alluvium** (Bhangar/Khadar), coastal and deltaic deposits.

### 1.2 Rocks — quick classification (2-marker bank)

- **Igneous:** granite (plutonic; Deccan tors, building/monument stone), basalt (volcanic; traps), dolerite dykes, **kimberlite pipes — the diamond host (Panna; Wajrakarur, AP ⭐)**.
- **Sedimentary:** sandstone, shale, **limestone (cement!)**, conglomerate, coal (organic), laterite (residual duricrust).
- **Metamorphic:** granite→**gneiss**, shale→**slate** (Markapur ⭐)→schist, limestone→**marble** (Makrana), sandstone→**quartzite**, coal→graphite; **khondalite & charnockite** — Eastern Ghats signatures.

## 2. Mineral resources of India

Framework: minerals = **metallic (ferrous / non-ferrous), non-metallic, energy (fuel)**. India's mineral heartland = the **Chotanagpur–Odisha–Chhattisgarh belt**; the **Dharwar rocks** supply most metals.

### 2.1 Metallic — ferrous group

| Mineral | Ores/grades | Chief producers & fields |
|---|---|---|
| **Iron ore** | **Haematite** (better grade, Peninsular), **magnetite** (highest Fe, Karnataka–Kerala–TN) | **Odisha (No. 1)** — Barbil-Joda; Chhattisgarh — **Bailadila** (exports via Vizag ⭐); Jharkhand — Singhbhum/Noamundi; Karnataka — Kudremukh, Ballari-Hosapete |
| **Manganese** | ferro-alloys, dry cells | **MP–Maharashtra (Balaghat–Nagpur)**, Odisha; **AP's Kodur–Garividi belt (Vizianagaram–Srikakulam)** ⭐ — among India's earliest-worked |
| **Chromite** | stainless steel | **Odisha ~98% (Sukinda valley)** |
| Nickel, cobalt, tungsten | strategic | Sukinda (Ni, laterites), Degana (W, Rajasthan) |

### 2.2 Metallic — non-ferrous group

| Mineral | Chief sources | Note |
|---|---|---|
| **Bauxite** (→ aluminium) | **Odisha No. 1 (Koraput–Panchpatmali, NALCO)**, Gujarat, Jharkhand; **East-coast bauxite of the AP–Odisha Eastern Ghats** ⭐ (undeveloped in AP) | Laterite-capped plateaus |
| **Copper** | **Khetri (Raj), Malanjkhand (MP — largest open pit), Singhbhum (Jhk)** | India deficit → imports |
| **Lead–zinc** | **Rampura-Agucha, Zawar (Raj)** — Hindustan Zinc | Galena–sphalerite |
| **Gold** | **Kolar (closed), Hutti (working, Karnataka)**; **Ramagiri (Anantapur, AP)** ⭐ | Dharwar schist belts |
| **Silver** | By-product Zawar/Agucha | Raj leads |

### 2.3 Non-metallic & industrial minerals

- **Mica** (electrical insulator): **Jharkhand (Koderma–Giridih "mica belt"), Rajasthan, AP (Gudur, Nellore) ⭐** — India historically world No. 1 in sheet mica.
- **Limestone**: cement/flux — MP, Rajasthan, Chhattisgarh, **AP–Telangana Cuddapah/Kurnool belts ⭐**.
- **Barytes**: **AP's Mangampeta = world's largest single deposit ⭐⭐** (drilling mud) — India ≈ world's top producer thanks to AP alone.
- **Asbestos**: **Pulivendula (Kadapa, AP)** ⭐; Rajasthan.
- **Dolomite, magnesite, kyanite–sillimanite** (refractories): Odisha/TN/Uttarakhand (magnesite — Salem, Almora).
- **Diamonds**: **Panna (MP)** — only organised mine; historic **Golconda diamonds from Krishna gravels (Kollur — Koh-i-noor)** ⭐; kimberlites of **Wajrakarur (Anantapur)**.
- **Gypsum** (Raj), **rock phosphate** (Jhamarkotra), **graphite** (Odisha, Arunachal).
- **Atomic minerals**: **uranium — Jaduguda (Jharkhand), TUMMALAPALLE (Kadapa, AP — among the world's largest reserves)** ⭐⭐; **thorium — monazite of Kerala/Odisha/AP beach sands** ⭐; beach placers also give **ilmenite–rutile (titanium), garnet, zircon** (Kerala, TN, Odisha, **Srikakulam–Kakinada coast**).

### 2.4 Energy minerals (one-liners)

- **Coal**: Gondwana fields ~98% — **Jharia (coking), Raniganj (oldest, 1774), Bokaro, Korba, Talcher, Singrauli, SINGARENI (Godavari valley — the Peninsular south's only major coalfield)** ⭐; Tertiary coal (NE), **lignite — Neyveli (TN)**.
- **Petroleum/natural gas**: **Digboi (oldest, 1889), Mumbai High (largest offshore), Gujarat (Ankleshwar), Barmer (Raj), KRISHNA–GODAVARI (KG) BASIN — on/offshore AP** ⭐⭐ (Ravva, KG-D6), Cauvery basin.

### 2.5 Policy pegs

- **MMDR Act 1957** (amended 2015 — **auctions, District Mineral Foundation (DMF), PMKKKY**; 2021, 2023 — critical-mineral list, private exploration), National Mineral Policy 2019, **KABIL** for critical minerals (lithium etc.; Reasi lithium find, J&K, inferred 2023).

## 3. Metals, clays and construction materials (the "small minerals" the syllabus names)

### 3.1 Metals — smelting geography (match pairs)

- **Steel plants:** Bhilai (Chhattisgarh), Rourkela, Bokaro, Durgapur, Burnpur (all east), **Visakhapatnam (RINL — the only major shore-based steel plant; "Vizag Steel — Pride of Andhra", ore from Bailadila)** ⭐⭐.
- **Aluminium:** NALCO Angul, BALCO Korba, Hindalco Renukoot, Vedanta Jharsuguda.
- **Copper:** Khetri; **zinc:** Chanderiya; **gold refining:** Hutti.

### 3.2 Clays ⭐ (APPSC names them explicitly)

| Clay | Nature/use | Where |
|---|---|---|
| **Kaolin (china clay)** | White, from feldspar decay; **ceramics, paper coating** | Kerala (Kundara), WB, Rajasthan; **AP — Rajahmundry area/East Godavari** |
| **Ball clay** | Plastic, fine; whiteware/sanitaryware | **AP (Godavari districts — major producer)**, Rajasthan (Bikaner) |
| **Fire clay** | Refractory bricks | Jharia/Raniganj coal measures |
| **Fuller's earth (multani mitti)** | Absorbent, bleaching | Rajasthan, Bikaner; AP patches |
| **Bentonite** | Drilling mud, foundry | Gujarat (Kachchh), Rajasthan |
| Common/brick clay | Bricks, tiles | Alluvial belts everywhere |

### 3.3 Construction materials

- **Granite**: India a top exporter — **AP (Chimakurthy "Black Galaxy", Prakasam ⭐⭐), Telangana, Karnataka, TN**.
- **Marble**: **Makrana (Taj Mahal)**, Rajsamand (Raj); Ambaji (Guj).
- **Sandstone**: Vindhyan belt — Red Fort/Agra Fort stone; **Kota stone** (flaggy limestone, Rajasthan).
- **Slate**: **Markapur (Prakasam, AP — writing slates)** ⭐, Kund (Haryana), HP.
- **Limestone flags**: **Cuddapah slabs & Betamcherla "Napa" slabs (AP)** ⭐; Shahabad stone (Karnataka).
- **Sand & aggregates**: riverbed sand (regulated — Sustainable Sand Mining Guidelines 2016; M-sand substitution), laterite blocks (Konkan-Kerala walls), lime (**Piduguralla, AP — "lime city"** ⭐), cement (India = **world No. 2 producer**; plants ride the limestone belts incl. AP's Krishna–Guntur–Kurnool–Kadapa).

## 4. Reservoirs and dams (multipurpose projects — Nehru's "temples of modern India")

### 4.1 All-India superlatives ⭐ (MCQ bank)

| Project | River/state | Claim to fame |
|---|---|---|
| **Tehri** | Bhagirathi, Uttarakhand | **Highest dam in India (~260 m, earth-rockfill)** |
| **Bhakra (Gobind Sagar)** | Satluj, HP-Punjab | Highest **concrete gravity** dam (226 m); Nangal companion |
| **Hirakud** | Mahanadi, Odisha | **Longest dam in India (~25.8 km with dykes)** |
| **NAGARJUNA SAGAR** | Krishna, **AP–Telangana border** | **World's largest masonry dam**; canals — Right = **Jawahar**, Left = **Lal Bahadur** ⭐⭐ (detail File 21) |
| **Sardar Sarovar** | Narmada, Gujarat | Terminal Narmada dam; Narmada Bachao Andolan debates |
| **Indira Sagar** | Narmada, MP | **Largest reservoir by live capacity** |
| **Govind Ballabh Pant Sagar (Rihand)** | Rihand, UP | Among the largest artificial lakes (area) |
| **Idukki** | Periyar, Kerala | **Arch dam** in a gorge |
| **Mettur (Stanley)** | Kaveri, TN | Oldest big post-1930s dam (1934) |
| **Krishnaraja Sagara** | Kaveri, Karnataka | Visvesvaraya; Brindavan gardens |
| **Kallanai (Grand Anicut)** | Kaveri | **Oldest functioning — Karikala Chola, c. 2nd century CE** |
| **Farakka Barrage** | Ganga, WB | Hooghly flushing; Indo-Bangladesh treaty 1996 |
| **Tungabhadra Dam (Pampa Sagar)** | Tungabhadra, Hospet (Karnataka) | Joint KA–AP interest; HLC/LLC canals serve **Rayalaseema** ⭐ |

- Other names to recognise: DVC dams (Maithon, Panchet, Tilaiya — India's first multipurpose project, 1948, on Damodar), Kosi barrage, Ukai (Tapti), Gandhi Sagar–Rana Pratap Sagar–Jawahar Sagar (Chambal cascade), **Polavaram (national project, AP — File 21)** ⭐⭐, **Srisailam** (AP ⭐), Almatti & Narayanpur (upper Krishna, KA — inflow disputes with AP!), Koyna (hydel, MH), Salal/Baglihar/Kishanganga (J&K — IWT flashpoints).
- Dam institutions: **Central Water Commission**, **Dam Safety Act 2021**, National Register of Large Dams (~6,000+ large dams; India 3rd after China, USA).
- Purposes: irrigation, hydro-power, flood moderation, municipal/industrial water, fisheries, navigation — with costs: submergence & displacement (Narmada, Polavaram R&R ⭐), siltation (Tungabhadra lost ~30% capacity), seismicity (Koyna 1967).

## 5. Last-mile checklist (File 20)

1. Four-fold stratigraphy (Archaean–Purana–Dravidian–Aryan) with one mineral each: Dharwar-gold, Cuddapah-barytes, Vindhyan-diamond, Gondwana-coal, Tertiary-oil.
2. Kimberlite = diamond host; Wajrakarur; Kollur/Koh-i-noor from Krishna gravels.
3. Iron-ore states in order; Bailadila→Vizag export line; RINL shore-based steel.
4. Mangampeta barytes & Tummalapalle uranium = **world-class AP deposits** (asked repeatedly).
5. Clays: kaolin vs ball clay vs fuller's earth — use + one location each; AP's Godavari ball clay.
6. Dam superlatives: highest (Tehri), longest (Hirakud), largest masonry (Nagarjuna Sagar), arch (Idukki), oldest (Kallanai).
7. MMDR 2015 = auctions + DMF; Dam Safety Act 2021.
