# 16 · The Vijayanagara Empire (1336–1646) — Socio-Cultural Conditions, Literature, Fine Arts

## 0. Frame — four dynasties, one city

| Dynasty | Years | Landmark rulers |
|---|---|---|
| **Sangama** | 1336–1485 | Harihara I & Bukka I (founders, 1336, blessed by **Vidyaranya** — origins debated: Kampili/Kakatiya-officer traditions); **Devaraya I** (Tungabhadra dam-aqueduct; **Nicolo Conti** visited c. 1420); **DEVARAYA II "Gajabetekara"** (Muslim archers recruited, **Quran placed beside the throne** for their homage — Ferishta; **Abdur Razzaq's** embassy 1443; Srinatha's kanakabhishekam) |
| **Saluva** | 1486–1505 | Saluva Narasimha (first usurpation; horse-trade focus) |
| **Tuluva** | 1505–1570 | **KRISHNADEVARAYA (1509–29)**, Achyutadevaraya (**Paes & Nuniz** wrote under these two), Sadashiva under regent **"Aliya" Rama Raya** — **TALIKOTA (Rakkasa-Tangadi), 23 Jan 1565** — Rama Raya beheaded by Hussain Nizam Shah; Hampi sacked |
| **Aravidu** | 1570–1646 | Tirumala (capital → **PENUKONDA**), Venkata II (→ **CHANDRAGIRI**; recovery, Jesuit presence); **1639: Madras site granted to the English** by Damarla Venkatadri Nayaka under Venkata III (from Chandragiri ⭐ AP fact); last Sri Ranga III — kingdom dissolved into Nayaka states by 1646 |

**Krishnadevaraya's decade to memorise:** Ummattur rebellion crushed (1510–12) → **Gajapati war 1512–19** (Udayagiri-Kondavidu-Potnuru pillar; marriage-peace, Krishna border) → **Raichur 1520** vs Ismail Adil Shah (Portuguese musketeers under Christovao de Figueiredo helped) → Gulbarga raid, briefly restoring a Bahmani puppet — hence his startling title "**Yavana-rajya-sthapanacharya** (establisher of the Yavana kingdom)" ⭐. Titles: **Andhra Bhoja, Sahiti-Samarangana-Sarvabhauma, Kannada Rajya Rama Ramana, Mooru Rayara Ganda**. Babur's memoir ranks him the most powerful Indian king of the day.

## 1. Administration & economy (asked every cycle)

- **NAYANKARA system:** territory assigned to **amaranayakas** who maintain fixed troops & remit tribute — Kakatiya seed grown into the empire's skeleton; nayakas kept agents (*sthanapatis*) at court; long-run effect — nayakas became hereditary → the successor Nayaka states.
- **AYAGAR system:** every village served by **12 hereditary functionaries** (karanam/accountant, reddy/headman, talari/watchman, purohit, smith, carpenter, potter, washerman, barber…) paid in tax-free land — the classic "village republic" machinery of the south ⭐.
- Revenue: land tax (*shist*) graded by crop/soil (nominally up to 1/6); plus professional, house, marriage taxes; customs at ports; **Athavana** = land-revenue department; currency — gold **VARAHA (pagoda/hun)**, fanam fractions.
- **Horse monopoly diplomacy:** Arab, then **Portuguese** (post-1510 Goa) horse imports bought en bloc — cavalry as imported technology; Vijayanagara–Portuguese entente vs the sultanates.
- Irrigation as royal duty: Devaraya I's **Tungabhadra dam & 24-km aqueduct** (Nuniz), tank chains (Anantaraja Sagara etc. in Rayalaseema), temple-linked tanks.

## 2. Society & religion — through the travellers' eyes ⭐

- **Abdur Razzaq (1443):** "The city is such that eye has not seen nor ear heard of any place resembling it upon the whole earth"; bazaar-and-jewel economy; **Mahanavami (Dasara)** spectacle.
- **Domingo Paes (c. 1520):** Hampi "as large as Rome and very beautiful… the best-provided city in the world"; KDR "the most feared and perfect king"; temple women (devadasis) of great wealth; Mahanavami wrestling, processions.
- **Fernao Nuniz (c. 1535):** nayaka obligations; **sati** among warrior families; palace staffed by **women — guards, wrestlers, accountants, astrologers** ⭐ (women in professions fact).
- **Duarte Barbosa (c. 1516):** *"The king allows such freedom that every man may come and go and live according to his own creed, without suffering any annoyance"* — the religious-freedom quotation to memorise verbatim.
- Religion: Sangamas signed "**Sri Virupaksha**" (Shaiva); Tuluva era tilts Vaishnava (**Vitthala, Tirumala Venkateshwara**); **Madhva Vyasatirtha** as KDR's honoured rajaguru; **Bukka's 1368 arbitration** between Jains & Srivaishnavas ("one community — same protection") ⭐; Muslims in the army with mosques (Devaraya II); Virashaivas strong in trade-towns.
- **Tirumala's imperial rise:** KDR's seven recorded visits; **copper portrait statues of KDR with queens Tirumala Devi & Chinna Devi still stand at Tirumala** ⭐; crown-jewel gifts inscribed; Tallapaka family's sankirtana institution (Annamayya's line) flourished under the empire — the making of modern Tirupati.

## 3. Literature — a four-language empire

### 3.1 Telugu: the PRABANDHA golden age
- **KDR's own *AMUKTAMALYADA* (Vishnuchittiyamu)** — on Andal/Goda's love of Ranganatha, dedicated to **Venkateshwara**; its **rajaniti section** (statecraft advice: trust ministers, guard ports, settle forests) is quotable in GS answers ⭐; ranked among the **Pancha Mahakavyas** of Telugu.
- **ASHTADIGGAJAS** (the eight elephants of the **Bhuvana Vijayamu** hall — traditional list):
| Poet | Signature work |
|---|---|
| **Allasani Peddana — "Andhra Kavita Pitamaha"** | ***Manucharitramu*** (Svarochisha Manu Sambhava) — the model prabandha |
| **Nandi Timmana ("Mukku Timmana")** | *Parijatapaharanamu* |
| **Dhurjati** | *Kalahasti Mahatmyamu*; **Sri Kalahastishvara Satakamu** (AP shrine ⭐) |
| **Madayyagari Mallana** | *Rajasekhara Charitramu* |
| **Ayyalaraju Ramabhadra** | *Ramabhyudayamu* |
| **Pingali Surana** | *Raghavapandaviyamu* (dvyarthi — simultaneous Ramayana+Bharata!), *Kalapurnodayamu* (proto-novelistic) |
| **Ramarajabhushana (Bhattumurti)** | *Vasucharitramu* (musical slesha), *Narasabhupaliyamu* |
| **Tenali Ramakrishna** | *Panduranga Mahatmyamu* (+ the wit-legends) |
- Achyuta/Aravidu continuations; note the list is a later tradition — flag "traditional" in Mains.

### 3.2 Sanskrit
- **Vedic renaissance:** **SAYANA's bhashyas on the Vedas** (with brother Madhava — identified by tradition with **Vidyaranya**) composed under Bukka I–Harihara II ⭐ ("the Vedas were saved and expounded under Vijayanagara" — classic line); Madhava's *Parashara-Madhaviya*, *Sarvadarshanasangraha*.
- **Women Sanskrit poets:** **GANGADEVI (wife of Kumara Kampana) — *MADHURAVIJAYAM*** on the conquest of the Madurai Sultanate (c. 1370) ⭐; **TIRUMALAMBA — *Varadambika Parinayam*** (Achyuta's court) — two women epic-poets = guaranteed question.
- KDR's Sanskrit: *Jambavati Kalyanam* (play), *Madalasa Charita* etc.

### 3.3 Kannada & Tamil
- **Kannada:** **Kumara Vyasa's *Gadugina Bharata* (Karnata-Bharata-Kathamanjari)** — Devaraya II era; Chamarasa's *Prabhulinga Leele*; **HARIDASA movement — Purandaradasa & Kanakadasa** (Kanaka's *Ramadhanya Charite* — a grain-allegory of caste ⭐).
- **Tamil:** Kumara Kampana's conquest **restored Srirangam worship** (Madhuravijayam's climax); **Arunagirinathar's *Tiruppugazh*** under Devaraya II; Tamil Vaishnava-Shaiva scholarship patronised through the Nayaka viceroys.

## 4. Art, architecture, fine arts

### 4.1 Vijayanagara style (Hampi = **UNESCO 1986**)
- Signature elements: towering **RAYAGOPURAMS**; **KALYANA-MANDAPAS** (marriage halls); **composite/animated pillars** (rearing yalis, horse-riders); **musical pillars**; granite base + brick-stucco superstructure; monoliths.
- Hampi checklist: **Vitthala temple** (the **STONE CHARIOT**, musical pillars — the "₹50-note temple"), **Virupaksha** (living shrine), **Hazara Rama** (royal chapel — continuous **Ramayana friezes**), Krishna temple (Udayagiri Balakrishna installed after 1514), monoliths — **Lakshmi-Narasimha (1528), Kadalekalu & Sasivekalu Ganeshas, Badavilinga**.
- **Indo-Islamic secular royal architecture inside a Hindu capital ⭐:** **LOTUS MAHAL, ELEPHANT STABLES**, Queen's Bath, domed watch-towers + **MAHANAVAMI DIBBA** platform and the great stepped tank — composite culture in stone (top Mains evidence).

### 4.2 The AP circuit (name-drop these — home-state marks)
- **LEPAKSHI Veerabhadra temple** (1530s, built by **Virupanna**, Penukonda treasurer, under Achyutaraya): India-famous **MURALS** (the giant Veerabhadra ceiling panel — among India's largest), the **hanging pillar**, the colossal **monolithic Nandi** and Naga-linga — ⭐ Sri Sathya Sai dist., AP's showpiece.
- **Tirumala** (KDR statues & gifts), **Srikalahasti** (the great gopuram credited to KDR, 1516), **Tadipatri** (Bugga Ramalingeshvara, Chintala Venkataramana — chariot-shrine & Ramayana reliefs), **Ahobilam & Srisailam** mandapas, forts: **PENUKONDA (Gagan Mahal), CHANDRAGIRI (Raja Mahal), GANDIKOTA ("grand canyon" fort, Pemmasani nayakas), UDAYAGIRI**.

### 4.3 Music & dance
- **PURANDARADASA (1484–1564) — "Karnataka Sangita Pitamaha"**: invented the graded teaching method (Mayamalavagowla scale, sarali-jantai exercises); lakhs of devaranamas attributed ⭐.
- **RAMAMATYA — *SVARAMELAKALANIDHI* (1550, at Rama Raya's behest)** — the mela (scale-classification) treatise that reset Carnatic theory ⭐.
- **Tallapaka corpus** (Annamayya → sons/grandsons) — 14,000+ sankirtanas engraved on **copper plates at Tirumala**; the padam-kirtana stream that feeds Kshetrayya and the Trinity.
- Temple dance (devadasi/sadir) at imperial scale; **Yakshagana**'s growth in the Telugu-Kannada country; Kuchipudi's deeper roots (its royal charter comes under Golconda — next file).

## 5. After 1565 — Aravidu twilight and the NAYAKA successor states ("contemporary kingdoms" for the Qutb Shahi era)

| State | Facts that score |
|---|---|
| **Madurai Nayakas** (1529–1736) | Founder Vishwanatha; **TIRUMALA NAYAKA (1623–59)** — Meenakshi temple's **Pudu Mandapam**, unfinished Raya-gopuram, **Thirumalai Nayakkar Mahal** (Indo-Saracenic stucco palace), Teppakulam; Tamil+Telugu court |
| **THANJAVUR Nayakas** (1532–1673) | ⭐ **Telugu literature's southern capital**: poet-king **RAGHUNATHA NAYAKA** (*Valmiki Charitramu*; the **Sangita Sudha** music treatise stands in his name with minister **GOVINDA DIKSHITA**); Govinda's son **VENKATAMAKHIN — *CHATURDANDI PRAKASHIKA* (c. 1660): the 72-MELAKARTA scheme** — Carnatic music's periodic table ⭐; court poets **Chemakura Venkata Kavi** (*Vijayavilasamu*, *Sarangadhara Charitramu*) and the **women scholars Ramabhadramba & Madhuravani**; **Kshetrayya of Movva** sang his Muvva-Gopala padams here (and at Golconda); last king **Vijayaraghava** (yakshagana author) fell 1673; **Bhagavata Mela** dance-drama seeded at Melattur; Marathas under Venkoji/Ekoji took Thanjavur 1676 |
| **Gingee Nayakas** | fort-lords of the Carnatic; later Maratha-Mughal prize |
| **Keladi/Ikkeri Nayakas** | **Shivappa Nayaka's "sist"** — a model scientific land-revenue settlement ⭐; Queen **Chennamma** sheltered Rajaram (1689) |
| **Mysore Wodeyars** | Raja Wodeyar took Srirangapatna (1610); Chikkadevaraja's secretariat (*athara kacheri*) — the future Mysore state |

**The point to argue:** Talikota destroyed a city, not a civilisation — the empire's culture **migrated south with its nayakas**, making the 17th-century Kaveri delta the new home of **Telugu letters and Carnatic music**.

## 6. Prelims nuggets

1. 1336 foundation — Harihara+Bukka+Vidyaranya; Sangama-Saluva-Tuluva-Aravidu sequence.
2. Travellers in order: **Conti (Devaraya I) → Razzaq (Devaraya II, 1443) → Barbosa (c. 1516) → Paes (KDR) → Nuniz (Achyuta)** ⭐ match them!
3. Devaraya II: Gajabetekara; Muslim archers; Quran by the throne.
4. KDR: Raichur 1520; Potnuru pillar; Amuktamalyada→Venkateshwara; Andhra Bhoja; Yavana-rajya-sthapanacharya.
5. Ashtadiggajas: Peddana (Manucharitram, "Andhra Kavita Pitamaha") + the table above.
6. Sayana's Veda-bhashya; Gangadevi's Madhuravijayam; Tirumalamba's Varadambika Parinayam.
7. Hampi: stone chariot (Vitthala), Hazara Rama friezes, Lotus Mahal & Elephant Stables (Indo-Islamic), Mahanavami Dibba; UNESCO 1986.
8. **Lepakshi** — Virupanna, Achyutaraya era; murals, hanging pillar, monolithic Nandi.
9. Ayagars = 12 village servants; amaranayaka = military assignee; varaha = gold pagoda.
10. Svaramelakalanidhi 1550 (Ramamatya, for Rama Raya); Purandaradasa = Sangita Pitamaha; Chaturdandi Prakashika 1660 (Venkatamakhin, Thanjavur) = 72 melakartas.
11. Talikota/Rakkasa-Tangadi, 23 Jan 1565 — four-sultanate confederacy; Rama Raya killed.
12. Aravidu capitals: Penukonda → Chandragiri → Vellore; **Madras granted 1639 from Chandragiri**.
13. Bukka's 1368 Jain-Srivaishnava decree; Bukka's embassy to Ming China (1374).

## 7. Mains angles

1. **"Vijayanagara was a war-state that financed a renaissance."** Nayankara-horse-fort machinery on one side; Sayana's Vedas, prabandha poetry, Hampi and temple-endowment economy on the other — argue they were one system (plunder-protected patronage).
2. **"Reconstruct Vijayanagara society from foreign accounts."** Razzaq-Paes-Nuniz-Barbosa quotes (§2) + their limits (court-centric, bazaar-centric evidence).
3. **"KDR deserves the title Andhra Bhoja."** Own authorship (Amuktamalyada + Sanskrit plays), Ashtadiggaja patronage, Tirumala-Srikalahasti-Tadipatri building, Telugu as the empire's court-literary language though the base was Kannada country — a bilingual empire argument.
4. **"Hampi's ruins document a composite culture, not a besieged one."** Lotus Mahal/Elephant stables + Muslim soldiery + Portuguese trade + Deccani dress at court (Paes) — before 1565's confessional reading is challenged.
5. **"Talikota scattered, not destroyed, Vijayanagara's culture."** Aravidu survival at Penukonda-Chandragiri (AP!), Nayaka courts as arks (Thanjavur's Telugu-Carnatic bloom), 1639 Madras grant as the era's unexpected legacy.
