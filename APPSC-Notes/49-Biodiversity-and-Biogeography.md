## 1. Biodiversity — concept and India's standing

- **Three levels (Prelims):** **genetic** (variation within a species — landraces, breeds), **species** (richness of species), **ecosystem** (variety of habitats). Values: ecological (ecosystem services), economic (bioprospecting, NTFP — File 40), scientific, ethical, aesthetic.
- **India = 1 of 17 "megadiverse" countries** ⭐⭐ — ~**2.4% of world land area** but ~**7–8% of recorded species**; ~**1,00,000+ animal** species (ZSI) and ~**45,000+ plant** species (BSI); 4 of the 36 global **biodiversity hotspots**.
- Custodians: **Zoological Survey of India (ZSI, 1916, Kolkata)** and **Botanical Survey of India (BSI, 1890, Kolkata)** ⭐.

## 2. Zoogeographic realms — the global frame

- **Alfred Russel Wallace = "father of zoogeography"** ⭐; the **Wallace Line** (Bali–Lombok) divides the **Oriental** and **Australian** realms.
- Six land realms: **Palaearctic, Nearctic, Neotropical, Afrotropical (Ethiopian), Oriental, Australian**.
- **India lies mainly in the ORIENTAL realm**, with the **Himalaya belonging to the Palaearctic** — a transition/overlap that makes the subcontinent unusually rich ⭐.

## 3. Phytogeographic regions of India

Commonly grouped into ~**8 floristic regions**: **Western Himalayan, Eastern Himalayan, Indus plain (arid), Gangetic plain, Assam/North-East, Deccan, Malabar (Western Ghats) and the Andaman & Nicobar**. **Andhra Pradesh sits in the Deccan region** with a distinct **Eastern Ghats** floristic character (§6).

## 4. Biogeographic classification of India (Rodgers & Panwar, WII, 1988) ⭐⭐

The exam-standard scheme: **10 biogeographic zones, 27 provinces** —

| # | Zone | # | Zone |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Trans-Himalaya** | 6 | **Deccan Peninsula** |
| 2 | Himalaya | 7 | Gangetic Plain |
| 3 | Desert (Thar, Kutch) | 8 | **Coasts** |
| 4 | Semi-Arid | 9 | North-East India |
| 5 | Western Ghats | 10 | Islands |

- **Andhra Pradesh falls in Zone 6 (Deccan Peninsula — Eastern Ghats & Deccan-South provinces) and Zone 8 (Coasts — East-coast province)** ⭐⭐ — this locational line is the ideal opener for any AP biodiversity answer.

## 5. Biodiversity hotspots — the "AP trick" ⭐

- A **hotspot** (Norman Myers) = ≥1,500 endemic vascular plants **and** ≥70% of primary habitat already lost. **India has four:** **(1) Himalaya, (2) Indo-Burma, (3) Western Ghats–Sri Lanka, (4) Sundaland (Nicobar Islands)**.
- **Andhra Pradesh is NOT inside any of the four hotspots** ⭐⭐ (the Eastern Ghats, though biodiversity-rich and endemic, are **not** a designated hotspot) — a favourite trap; the WG hotspot lies to AP's west, in Karnataka/Kerala/TN. Use this to argue that **AP's Eastern-Ghats endemism (red sanders, Cycas beddomei, Jerdon's courser, golden gecko) is under-protected precisely because it lacks hotspot status.**

## 6. The Eastern Ghats — AP's biodiversity signature

- **Discontinuous, older and drier** than the Western Ghats; broken by the Godavari, Krishna and Penna; the northern blocks (Vizag agency — Papikonda, Maredumilli) are **semi-evergreen & moist-deciduous**, the southern blocks (Nallamala, Seshachalam, Lankamala) **dry-deciduous to scrub** (File 20 §2.2).
- **Endemism pockets** — **Seshachalam–Veligonda** (red sanders, *Cycas beddomei*, *Syzygium alternifolium*, slender loris, golden gecko), **Nallamala** (Chenchu-managed forests, tiger source), **Lankamala** (Jerdon's courser). These make AP a **centre of Eastern-Ghats endemism** despite the no-hotspot status.

## 7. IUCN Red List — categories and India's threatened species ⭐

- **Nine categories:** **EX** (extinct), **EW** (extinct in wild), **CR** (critically endangered), **EN** (endangered), **VU** (vulnerable), **NT** (near threatened), **LC** (least concern), **DD** (data deficient), **NE** (not evaluated). *(WPA 1972 "schedules" are the legal list; IUCN is the scientific status — don't confuse them.)*
- **Some India CR flags:** Great Indian Bustard (**Rollapadu, AP** ⭐), gharial, Jerdon's courser (**Lankamala, AP** ⭐), Bengal florican, white-rumped & other vultures, pygmy hog, Kashmir stag (hangul), Namdapha flying squirrel.
- **AP-relevant threatened taxa:** GIB & Jerdon's courser (CR), red sanders (EN/near-threatened, CITES), *Cycas beddomei* (CITES Appendix I), fishing cat (VU), olive ridley turtle (VU), Indian pangolin, smooth-coated otter.

## 8. AP biodiversity profile — snapshot (extends File 20)

| Element | Detail |
|---|---|
| Biogeographic zones | **Deccan Peninsula (6) + Coasts (8)**; Eastern Ghats spine |
| Forest cover | ~18% of state (ISFR — File 20 §1.3); dry-deciduous dominant |
| Signature ecosystems | Eastern-Ghats hill forests, Nallamala plateau forest, **Coringa mangroves**, **Kolleru** freshwater lake, **Pulicat** lagoon, olive-ridley nesting beaches |
| Endemics | Red sanders, *Cycas beddomei*, *Syzygium alternifolium*; Jerdon's courser, golden gecko |
| Institutions | **AP Biodiversity Board** (under BD Act 2002); AP Forest Dept, GCC (File 40); Seshachalam Biosphere Reserve (2010) |
| State symbols | Animal **blackbuck**, bird **rose-ringed parakeet**, tree **neem**, flower **jasmine** ⭐ |
