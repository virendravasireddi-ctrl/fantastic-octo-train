## 1. Socio-cultural & religious conditions in Andhradesa, 1000–1565 ⭐⭐⭐

- **Society:** the **cultivator-warrior castes mature** — Reddy, Velama, Kamma clans as nayaka-gentry (Kakatiya service nobility → the 14th-c. successor kingdoms, File 15) — but flag the **anachronism warning (Cynthia Talbot)** ⭐⭐: in the 12th–13th c. these were still **fluid status-and-occupation identities**, hardening into fixed caste blocs only much later (partly colonial-census work) — never project them back onto Vengi; ruling houses **manufactured Kshatriya genealogies in prasastis** (Kakatiyas' contested varna → the openly **agrarian-caste sovereign Reddis** — "status mobility achieved through arms and epigraphy" ⭐); Brahmins split **VAIDIKA vs NIYOGI** (the secular record-keeping Brahmins — a distinction born in exactly this milieu ⭐); merchants — the **Komati/Arya Vaishya** with the **VASAVI KANYAKA Penugonda legend** ⭐; artisans — Padmasali weavers, the **Visvabrahmana/Panchanamvaru** claiming priestly rank; **left-hand/right-hand (idangai-valangai) moieties** whose recorded fights were over **honours and insignia, not class** ⭐; **VIPRA VINODULU** (Brahmin entertainers), devadasi institution at the great shrines; women — the **paradox to write both ways** ⭐: property via *stridhana*, autonomous royal donors, a queen-regnant (Rudramadevi, File 14) — yet sati-stones (mahasati), tightening widow norms and the devadasi institution: **"elite-conditional female agency, not general advance"**; beneath all, recorded **dasa-dasi bondage** and agrestic labour; the **12th-c. VEERASHAIVA upheaval** as a plebeian-artisan surge (below).
- **Religion — the sequence to hold ⭐⭐:**
  1. **Shaiva ascendancy** — Kalamukha-Pashupata mathas (Srisailam, Draksharama) under late Chalukyas-early Kakatiyas → **VEERASHAIVISM (Basava's wave from Kalyani)**: anti-caste, anti-ritual, temple-and-matha network; its Telugu voice **PALKURIKI SOMANATHA** (§2); **★ ARADHYA SHAIVISM — the distinctively ANDHRA synthesis** ⭐⭐: Telugu **Brahmin** Veerashaivas who took the *ishtalinga* but **kept Vedic ritual, the sacred thread and caste** — Andhra domesticated Karnataka's radical movement into a caste-conserving, Veda-affirming form (fountainhead **Mallikarjuna Panditaradhya**, 12th c., *Sivatattvasaramu* ⭐ — hence Somanatha's *Panditaradhya Charitra*); Kakatiya state Shaivism (Ganapatideva-Rudramadevi era; **Pasupati "Golaki matha"** chain ⭐); **Srisailam** as the pan-Indian node — at once **Jyotirlinga + Shakti Pitha (Bhramaramba)** ⭐ and the Reddis' flagship project (Veerasiromandapam, the ghat steps — File 15).
  2. **Vaishnava rise** — **Ramanuja's Srivaishnavism** radiating from the Tamil country: **Visistadvaita** (souls & matter as the *body* of Narayana), salvation by bhakti and **prapatti (total surrender)** ⭐, and **ubhaya-Vedanta** — Sanskrit sruti + the Tamil *Divya Prabandham* on equal authority (a vernacular-devotional charge Telugu bhakti inherited ⭐); Andhra anchors **Tirumala, Ahobilam, Simhachalam, SRIKURMAM** (whose "converted by Ramanuja c. 1130" tradition is **contested** — the site also carries **Madhva Dvaita** inscriptions of **Narahari Tirtha** ⭐ — a model source-critical caveat); Tirupati's ascent; by Vijayanagara times the **Tirumala-centred bhakti economy** (Annamayya) and the Rayas' tilt to **Vitthala-Venkateswara** (KDR's *Amuktamalyada* is itself a Srivaishnava manifesto ⭐).
  3. **Syncretic & folk layer** — village goddesses, hero-stones (Palnadu's **Veeracharitra cult** ⭐), Dattatreya-Sufi overlaps after 1323; early Islam in Andhra via the Sultanate shock (File 14's Warangal falls) and the Bahmani frontier (File 17).
- **Temple as institution** ⭐: land-bank (devadana), school (Vedic + music-dance), irrigation trustee (Kakatiya tanks tied to shrines), employer, and the stage on which castes negotiated rank — quote for any "temple in medieval Andhra society" question.

## 2. Telugu literature — antiquity, origin, growth ⭐⭐⭐ (the relay table)

| Stage | Poet & work | The line to write |
|---|---|---|
| **Adikavi** | **NANNAYA** (c. 1051, Rajahmundry) — *Andhra Mahabharatamu* (Adi, Sabha, part-Aranya) | began the **marga/champu** (Sanskrit-heavy) tradition; "Vaganusasanudu" ⭐ |
| **The desi revolt** | **PALKURIKI SOMANATHA** (c. 1200) — ***Basava Puranamu*, *Panditaradhya Charitra*** in **DWIPADA** ⭐⭐ | Veerashaiva, deliberately in "the Telugu of the people" — the counter-current to Nannaya |
| Court Shaiva | **Nanne Choda** — *Kumarasambhavamu*; **Baddena** — ***Sumati Satakam*** (attributed ⭐) | the **sataka** form is born — Telugu's aphoristic vehicle (to Vemana §6) |
| Desi epic | **GONA BUDDHA REDDI** (Kakatiya era) — ***Ranganatha Ramayanamu*** — the **FIRST TELUGU RAMAYANA, in dwipada** ⭐⭐ | the desi metre claims the second epic (pair with Somanatha in the marga-desi answer) |
| **Kavitraya II** | **TIKKANA Somayaji** (13th c., **Nellore court of Manumasiddhi** ⭐⭐) — 15 parvas of the Mahabharata; *Nirvachanottara Ramayanamu* | "Ubhaya-kavi-mitra" (friend of both Sanskrit & Telugu; Hari-Hara synthesis ⭐); brokered Manumasiddhi's restoration via **Ganapatideva** (File 14 link) |
| **Kavitraya III** | **ERRANA/Yerrapragada** (14th c., court of **PROLAYA VEMA REDDI** ⭐) — completed the Aranya parva; *Nrisimhapuranamu*, *Harivamsamu* | the **KAVITRAYA = Nannaya-Tikkana-Errana**, a 300-year relay on one Mahabharata ⭐⭐ (2025 Part-B favourite) |
| **The Srinatha age** (Reddi courts, File 15) | **SRINATHA "Kavisarvabhauma"** ⭐⭐ — *Sringara Naishadhamu*, *Palnati Veeracharitra* (dwipada), *Kridabhiramamu* (attrib.), *Haravilasamu*; honoured with **kanakabhisheka** at Vijayanagara | salaried **vidyadhikari** (minister of learning) of **Peda Komati Vema Reddi** ⭐ — the salaried court-poet is itself proof of Telugu's arrival as a language of power; his brother-in-law's contrast — |
| | **POTANA** — ***Andhra Maha Bhagavatamu*** ⭐⭐ — refused to dedicate it to a king ("dedicated to Rama") | bhakti against patronage — the moral exemplum of Telugu letters |
| Tallapaka | **ANNAMAYYA (Annamacharya)** ⭐⭐ — ~32,000 **sankirtanas** to Venkateswara; wife **Timmakka (*Subhadra Kalyanam*) — first Telugu poetess** ⭐ | temple-lyric stream; copper-plate corpus at Tirumala |
| **The golden age** | **KRISHNADEVARAYA (1509–29)** — ***AMUKTAMALYADA*** ⭐⭐ (Telugu, on Andal; the raja-niti canto) + the **ASHTADIGGAJAS** of the **Bhuvana Vijayamu** hall | "**Desa bhashalandu Telugu lessa**" ⭐ (attributed to KDR) — Telugu as imperial language |

- **ASHTADIGGAJAS (drill all eight ⭐⭐):** **Allasani Peddana — "Andhra-kavita-pitamaha", *Manucharitramu*** ⭐; **Nandi Timmana — *Parijatapaharanamu***; **Dhurjati — *Kalahasti Mahatmyamu*** (later the *Kalahastiswara Satakam* voice); **Madayyagari Mallana**; **Ayyalaraju Ramabhadra**; **Pingali Surana — *Raghavapandaviyamu*** (double-epic tour de force ⭐), *Kalapurnodayamu*; **Ramarajabhushana (Bhattumurti) — *Vasucharitramu***; **Tenali Ramakrishna — *Panduranga Mahatmyamu*** ⭐ (and the folk "Vikata-kavi" legend).
- **Prabandha** = the age's form: single-hero, ornate, srngara-heavy — literature's move from translation to invention ⭐ (2020 Q7b "momentous literary movement" = exactly this + File 16).

## 5. Qutb Shahi contribution to Andhra history & culture ⭐⭐⭐ (compressed from File 17)

- **State & economy:** Golconda sultanate (1518–1687) reunified Telugu country north of the Krishna; **diamond economy (Kollur)**, Machilipatnam's boom (the European magnet — File 22); tank & tax administration through **Telugu niyogi record-keepers** ⭐.
- **Telugu patronage — the surprise the exam loves ⭐⭐:** **IBRAHIM QUTB SHAH = "MALKIBHARAMA"** of Telugu poets (raised at Vijayanagara ⭐); court poets **Addanki Gangadhara, Kandukuri Rudrakavi, PONNAGANTI TELAGANARYA — *Yayaticharitramu*, the first all-native (achcha) Telugu kavya** ⭐⭐ (dedicated to a Muslim noble!); **Kancherla Gopanna "RAMADASU"** — Bhadrachalam temple & kirtanas under **Abul Hasan TANA SHAH** ⭐ (with Akkanna-Madanna's ministry); **Tana Shah's 1678 grant of KUCHIPUDI village to the Bhagavatulu dancers** ⭐⭐ — a Muslim king endowing a Hindu dance village (the classic composite-culture fact); **Kshetrayya's padams** (Movva) circulating in the same world.
- **Urban-architectural:** **Hyderabad founded 1591 (Muhammad Quli Qutb Shah) — CHARMINAR, Char Kaman**; Golconda fort acoustics & water-system; **Qutb Shahi tombs**; **Hussain Sagar (1562)**; **DAKHNI** language-literature (Muhammad Quli — the first sahib-e-diwan Urdu poet ⭐) and the Telugu-Persian bilingual chancery ⭐.
- **Verdict line:** the Qutb Shahis were **"Telugu sultans"** — Persianate kings running a Telugu agrarian state, proof that Andhra's composite culture predates modern secularism (close every Q on them with this).

## 6. Regional literature — PRAJA KAVI VEMANA & others ⭐⭐

- **VEMANA** (dates disputed — 15th–17th c.; Rayalaseema, Reddy-family tradition; "write *c. 17th c., disputed*"): the **people's poet** — thousands of **ATAVELADI-metre satakam verses** ending in the makuta **"VISHWADABHIRAMA VINURA VEMA"** ⭐⭐.
  - **Content:** frontal attack on **caste ("What is one's caste when the soul is one?"), idol-ritualism, pilgrimage-commerce, priestcraft, and hypocrisy**; yogic-Siddha vein (Nirguna current — pairs with Kabir ⭐, File 12); homely imagery (dogs, pots, palmyra) — philosophy in kitchen Telugu.
  - **Transmission:** oral centuries → **C.P. BROWN collected, edited & translated the *Verses of Vemana* (1829)** ⭐⭐ — the Company-era rescue that made Vemana print-canon (File 36's Brown dossier — the perfect cross-link; 2023 Q8b).
- **The wider popular shelf:** **sataka tradition** (Sumati, **Dasarathi Satakam — Ramadasu**, Kalahastiswara — Dhurjati; Bhaskara) — the moral pocket-literature of Telugu homes ⭐; **MOLLA — the potter-caste poetess's *Ramayanamu*** ⭐ (the essay [`Molla-and-Telugu-Authors.md`](../Molla-and-Telugu-Authors.md) is the full argument — caste, gender & the vernacular counter-current); **Annamayya-Kshetrayya-Ramadasu** = the lyric trinity of temple & home; folk dwipada, Burrakatha ancestry, Palnadu-Katamaraju oral epics (File 38's subaltern culture takes the story forward).

## 8. Prelims nuggets

1. **Kavitraya = Nannaya (c. 1051, Rajahmundry) + Tikkana (Nellore, Manumasiddhi) + Errana (Prolaya Vema Reddi).**
2. **Somanatha — Basava Puranamu (dwipada); Gona Buddha Reddi — Ranganatha Ramayanamu (first Telugu Ramayana); Srinatha — kanakabhisheka, vidyadhikari of Peda Komati Vema; Potana — Bhagavatamu (no royal dedication); Annamayya ~32,000 sankirtanas; Timmakka — first poetess.**
2a. **Aradhya Shaivism — Mallikarjuna Panditaradhya (Sivatattvasaramu): Veerashaiva devotion + Vedic ritual & caste retained — Andhra's signature sect; Srisailam = Jyotirlinga + Shakti Pitha.**
3. **Ashtadiggajas: Peddana (Manucharitramu, "Andhra-kavita-pitamaha"), Timmana (Parijatapaharanamu), Dhurjati (Kalahasti), Surana (Raghavapandaviyamu), Bhattumurti (Vasucharitramu), Tenali Ramakrishna (Panduranga Mahatmyamu), Mallana, Ramabhadra; KDR — Amuktamalyada.**
4. **Ramappa 1213 — UNESCO 2021; Thousand Pillar 1163; Nrittaratnavali — Jayapa Senani; Perini; kirti-torana = Telangana emblem.**
5. **Lepakshi — murals, hanging pillar, Nandi; Hampi UNESCO 1986; Gandikota "Grand Canyon of India".**
6. **Ibrahim Qutb Shah = Malkibharama; Telaganarya — Yayaticharitramu (achcha Telugu); Kuchipudi grant — Tana Shah 1678; Ramadasu — Bhadrachalam; Hyderabad-Charminar 1591; Hussain Sagar 1562.**
7. **Vemana — Ataveladi, "Vishwadabhirama Vinura Vema", C.P. Brown 1829; Molla — potter poetess; Dasarathi Satakam — Ramadasu.**
