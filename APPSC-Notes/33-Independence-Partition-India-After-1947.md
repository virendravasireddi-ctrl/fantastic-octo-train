## 0. Frame

1945–47 compressed a decade into two years: **INA trials → RIN revolt → Cabinet Mission → Direct Action → Mountbatten → 15 August 1947 with Partition as its price.** The new state then performed five founding feats in one decade — **rehabilitating ~8 million refugees, integrating 560+ states, writing the Constitution, reorganising states on language, and launching planned development with a non-aligned foreign policy.** *(2020 Q5a — Cabinet Mission — is a direct hit; the states-integration half doubles with Patel, File 30 §7.2, and the linguistic half with Files 25.)*

---

## 1. The endgame, 1945–47 ⭐⭐⭐

- **Why Britain left (enumerate):** WWII exhaustion & Labour victory (Attlee, July 1945); the **1942 verdict** (File 30 §6); **INA trials' nationwide upsurge + RIN revolt Feb 1946** ⭐ — armed forces' loyalty cracked; 1945–46 elections polarised (Congress ~90% general seats, **League swept Muslim seats** — its Pakistan mandate ⭐); US-international pressure; administrative fatigue ("the steel frame rusted").
- **Simla Conference (June–July 1945, Wavell Plan):** executive council parity; wrecked on **Jinnah's claim to nominate all Muslims** ⭐.

### 1.1 CABINET MISSION, March–June 1946 ⭐⭐⭐ (2020 Q5a skeleton)
- **Members: Pethick-Lawrence (SoS), Stafford Cripps, A.V. Alexander** ⭐ (Attlee's "three Magi"; Wavell associated).
- **Rejected Pakistan** (both the full six-province and truncated versions — "neither a workable solution nor in the interest of the communities").
- **The Plan (16 May 1946):** a **three-tier union** — (1) **Union of India** with only **foreign affairs, defence, communications**; (2) **GROUPING of provinces** ⭐⭐ — Section **A** (Hindu-majority), **B** (Punjab-Sind-NWFP-Baluchistan), **C** (Bengal-Assam) writing group constitutions; (3) provinces with residuary powers, **opt-out review after 10 years**; a **Constituent Assembly of 389** (292 British-Indian, elected by provincial assemblies via communal blocs; 93 states) ⭐; an **Interim Government**.
- **Fate:** both parties "accepted" reading grouping oppositely (League — compulsory; Congress/Nehru's 10 July press conference — free to modify) → **League withdrew (27 July) → DIRECT ACTION DAY 16 Aug 1946 (Great Calcutta Killings → Noakhali → Bihar)** ⭐; **Interim Government 2 Sept 1946 (Nehru; League joined Oct to obstruct — Liaquat's 1947 "poor-man's budget")**; **Constituent Assembly first met 9 Dec 1946 — League boycotted** ⭐.
- **Assessment line:** the last chance for a united India — federal, decentralised, with the seeds (grouping, compulsory sections, a 10-year secession review) of its own failure; its Constituent Assembly, minus the League, wrote free India's Constitution.

### 1.2 Partition & freedom
- **Attlee's statement, 20 Feb 1947** — withdrawal by **June 1948**; Mountbatten Viceroy (March 1947).
- **MOUNTBATTEN / 3 JUNE PLAN 1947** ⭐⭐: partition conceded; Punjab & Bengal assemblies to vote division; **referendums in NWFP & Sylhet**; Sind's assembly to opt; princely paramountcy to **lapse**; date advanced to **15 AUGUST 1947**. Congress acceptance — why: the riot chain, League obstruction inside the interim government, "a smaller India with a strong centre over a moth-eaten united India"; Gandhi's anguish (spent 15 Aug fasting in Calcutta ⭐).
- **INDIAN INDEPENDENCE ACT, 18 July 1947** ⭐: two Dominions from 15 Aug; **RADCLIFFE boundary commissions** (award published **17 Aug** — after freedom ⭐); Jinnah G-G of Pakistan, **Mountbatten of India**; **"Tryst with Destiny"** at midnight.

## 2. India after Independence — rehabilitation after Partition ⭐⭐

- **The human cost:** ~**8–10 million** cross-migrations (Punjab's near-total two-way transfer; Bengal's continuing trickle into the 1950s ⭐ — the key East-West contrast); deaths estimated 2–10 lakh+; ~**75,000 women abducted** — the **Abducted Persons (Recovery & Restoration) Act 1949** (Mridula Sarabhai's recovery work ⭐).
- **Machinery:** **Ministry of Relief & Rehabilitation (K.C. Neogy, then Mohanlal Saksena)**; camps (**Kurukshetra's ~3 lakh** ⭐); land allotment against evacuee property (Punjab's graded cuts — **Tarlok Singh's "standard acre"** ⭐); urban resettlement — **Faridabad, Nilokheri (S.K. Dey's "Mazdoor Manzil")** ⭐, Delhi's Lajpat/Rajendra Nagars, Ulhasnagar; Displaced Persons (Compensation & Rehabilitation) Act 1954; Dandakaranya scheme (1958) for Bengal refugees.
- **NEHRU-LIAQUAT PACT, April 1950** ⭐ — minority protection & migration rights after the 1950 East-Bengal riots (S.P. Mookerjee & Neogy resigned against it → Jana Sangh 1951 ⭐).
- **Gandhi's last mission:** Calcutta fast (Sept 1947 — "the one-man boundary force", Mountbatten), Delhi fast (Jan 1948 — including the ₹55-crore transfer issue); **assassinated 30 Jan 1948**.

## 3. Integration of the Indian States ⭐⭐⭐ (with File 30 §7.2)

- **Problem:** 560+ states, paramountcy lapsing 15 Aug 1947; Travancore-Hyderabad-Bhopal flirting with independence; **States Department (5 July 1947) — PATEL + V.P. MENON** ⭐.
- **Instruments:** **Instrument of Accession** (defence-external affairs-communications only — the Cabinet-Mission trio ⭐) + **Standstill Agreement**; appeals to patriotism + **privy purses**; all but three in by 15 Aug 1947.
- **The three hold-outs:** **JUNAGADH** (Muslim Nawab, Hindu majority → acceded to Pakistan → plebiscite Feb 1948, ~99% for India); **HYDERABAD** (Nizam + **Razakars (Qasim Razvi)** + Telangana rising → **Operation POLO, 13–17 Sept 1948** ⭐⭐); **KASHMIR** (Hindu Maharaja Hari Singh, Muslim majority; tribal invasion 22 Oct 1947 → **accession 26 Oct 1947** → Art 370's special status; UN reference 1 Jan 1948).
- **Phase II — merger & democratisation (1948–49):** small states merged into provinces or unions (PEPSU, Saurashtra, Rajasthan…), rulers pensioned as Rajpramukhs; French pockets (**Pondicherry, de facto 1954**) and **GOA — Operation Vijay, Dec 1961** ⭐ completed the map. "A bloodless revolution" — the biggest consolidation of India since the Mauryas (standard closing line).

## 4. The Indian Constitution ⭐⭐

- **Constituent Assembly:** first met **9 Dec 1946** (Sachchidananda Sinha interim chair) → **Rajendra Prasad President (11 Dec)**; **Objectives Resolution — Nehru, 13 Dec 1946** ⭐ (→ the Preamble); after Partition, membership 299; **Drafting Committee 29 Aug 1947 — AMBEDKAR chairman** (File 30 §7.3; B.N. Rau constitutional adviser ⭐); **2 years 11 months 18 days**; **adopted 26 Nov 1949** (Constitution Day), **in force 26 JANUARY 1950** ⭐ (the Purna Swaraj anniversary — File 31); Andhra members to name — **Pattabhi, N.G. Ranga, Durgabai Deshmukh** ⭐.
- **Features (one-line each):** longest written constitution; parliamentary system; quasi-federal ("federal with a strong unitary bias"); **Fundamental Rights (III) + DPSP (IV — Irish)** ⭐; universal adult franchise from day one (the bravest gamble); independent judiciary & judicial review; secular state; single citizenship; **Art 17** (untouchability abolished), **Art 370**, **Art 32** ("heart and soul"). Sources mnemonic: UK-parliamentary, US-FRs/judicial review, Ireland-DPSP, Canada-federation with strong centre, Australia-concurrent list, USSR-fundamental duties (1976), GoI Act 1935 — the administrative skeleton (~2/3) ⭐.

## 5. Linguistic reorganisation of states ⭐⭐⭐ (the Andhra-centred story — File 25 in brief)

- Congress's own promise (Nagpur 1920 linguistic PCCs) vs post-Partition caution: **DHAR COMMISSION 1948** (against language) → **JVP COMMITTEE 1949 (Jawaharlal-Vallabhbhai-Pattabhi ⭐)** — deferred it, but left the Andhra door ajar.
- **POTTI SRIRAMULU's 58-day fast → death 15 Dec 1952** ⭐⭐ → **ANDHRA STATE, 1 OCT 1953 (Kurnool capital; Prakasam CM)** — the first linguistic state and the dam-burst.
- **FAZL ALI COMMISSION (SRC) 1953–55 (with K.M. Panikkar, H.N. Kunzru)** → **STATES REORGANISATION ACT 1956** ⭐: 14 states + 6 UTs; **ANDHRA PRADESH 1 NOV 1956** (Telangana merged under the **Gentlemen's Agreement** ⭐ — File 25); language, but "not the sole" criterion; Art 371 safeguards.
- **After 1956:** Bombay bifurcated — **Maharashtra & Gujarat 1960** (Samyukta Maharashtra & Mahagujarat movements ⭐); Nagaland 1963; **Punjab-Haryana 1966 (Shah Commission; Punjabi Suba — Fateh Singh)**; NE reorganisation 1972; Sikkim 1975; Mizoram-Arunachal-Goa 1987; **Chhattisgarh-Uttarakhand-Jharkhand 2000; TELANGANA 2 JUNE 2014** ⭐ (the wheel came full circle — File 25). Verdict: linguistic states **strengthened, not balkanised**, the Union (standard Mains close).

## 6. Economic policies after Independence ⭐⭐

- **Framework choice:** **mixed economy** — Nehruvian socialism + private sector (intellectual roots: Karachi 1931, **National Planning Committee 1938 (Bose-Nehru)**, **Bombay Plan 1944** ⭐).
- **Industrial Policy Resolutions:** **1948** (mixed-economy charter) → **1956 (the "economic constitution" ⭐⭐** — Schedule A/B/C, state commanding heights, aligned to the **socialist pattern of society — Avadi resolution 1955** ⭐).
- **Planning:** **Planning Commission 15 March 1950** (Nehru chair); **Five-Year Plans — I (1951–56, agriculture-Harrod-Domar), II (1956–61, MAHALANOBIS heavy-industry model ⭐⭐ — steel plants: Bhilai-USSR, Rourkela-W.Germany, Durgapur-UK ⭐), III, plan holiday 1966–69**; NITI Aayog replaced the PC in 2015 (one line only).
- **Agrarian:** **zamindari abolition (first wave, 1950s)** ⭐, tenancy reform & ceilings (patchy), Bhoodan (Vinoba 1951 — Pochampally, Telangana ⭐); community development 1952; **GREEN REVOLUTION (mid-1960s — C. Subramaniam, M.S. Swaminathan, Borlaug; HYV wheat, Punjab-Haryana-W.UP)** ⭐⭐ — food self-sufficiency at the cost of regional-crop imbalance.
- **Deepening state control:** **bank nationalisation 19 July 1969 (14 banks; 6 more 1980)** ⭐, insurance (LIC 1956, GIC 1972), coal; MRTP 1969, FERA 1973 — the licence-permit raj; **1966 devaluation**; then the arc to **1991 — BoP crisis → LPG reforms (Narasimha Rao-Manmohan Singh: delicensing, trade opening, rupee devaluation)** ⭐ — close any economic-policy answer with this arc: state-led foundation (1950s) → stagnation & control (1965–80) → liberalisation (1991).

## 7. Foreign policy initiatives ⭐⭐

- **NON-ALIGNMENT** ⭐⭐ — independent judgement, not neutrality (Nehru: "we are not neutral on questions of right and wrong"); institutional arc: **Asian Relations Conference, Delhi 1947 → Indonesia conference 1949 → BANDUNG Afro-Asian Conference 1955 → FIRST NAM SUMMIT, BELGRADE 1961 (Nehru-Tito-Nasser + Sukarno, Nkrumah)** ⭐.
- **PANCHSHEEL — 29 April 1954** ⭐⭐ (preamble of the India-China Tibet trade agreement): mutual respect for sovereignty & territorial integrity, non-aggression, non-interference, equality & mutual benefit, peaceful coexistence; "Hindi-Chini bhai-bhai" era.
- **Peace-broker roles:** Korea (NNRC chair — **General Thimayya**; V.K. Krishna Menon at the UN ⭐), Indo-China 1954 (International Control Commission chair), Suez 1956 protest; decolonisation & anti-apartheid advocacy (first UN complaint against South Africa, 1946 ⭐); Commonwealth membership as a republic (**London Declaration 1949** ⭐ — the formula India invented).
- **The hard tests:** **CHINA 1962** (border war — NEFA-Ladakh; the great NAM/defence-policy shock ⭐; Menon's exit); **Pakistan 1965 → TASHKENT DECLARATION Jan 1966 (Shastri-Ayub, Kosygin; Shastri died there ⭐)**; **1971 — INDO-SOVIET TREATY (Aug) + BANGLADESH war (Dec) → SHIMLA AGREEMENT 1972 (Indira-Bhutto: bilateralism, LoC)** ⭐⭐; **POKHRAN-I "Smiling Buddha" 18 May 1974** (peaceful nuclear explosive) → Pokhran-II 1998; IPKF-Sri Lanka 1987–90; **Look East 1992 → Act East 2014** (one line each).
- **Assessment line:** non-alignment maximised a weak new state's autonomy and voice; 1962 exposed its military naïveté; 1971 showed its flexibility (treaty diplomacy); the economic reforms of 1991 refounded it as multi-alignment.
