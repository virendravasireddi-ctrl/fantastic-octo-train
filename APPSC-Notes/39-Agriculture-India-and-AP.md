## 1. Determinants, farming types, seasons

- **Determinants:** physical (temperature, rainfall, soil — see Files 16 & 18), **institutional** (holding size, tenancy, credit), infrastructural (irrigation, power, roads, storage), technological (seeds, fertiliser, mechanisation).
- **Types of farming:** subsistence vs commercial; intensive; plantation (tea/coffee/rubber); dryland vs wetland; mixed; **shifting cultivation — "podu" in the AP agency tracts** ⭐ (jhum in the NE).
- **Cropping seasons:**

| Season | Sown → harvested | Typical crops |
|---|---|---|
| **Kharif** | SW-monsoon onset (Jun–Jul) → Sep–Oct | Rice, maize, jowar-bajra, cotton, groundnut, tur, soybean |
| **Rabi** | Oct–Dec → Mar–Apr | Wheat, barley, gram, mustard; **rabi/"dalwa" paddy and maize in the AP deltas** ⭐ |
| **Zaid** | summer interlude | Melons, cucumbers, fodder, vegetables |

## 3. The Green Revolution ⭐⭐ (2020 PYQ — full answer kit)

**Context:** 1950s food deficits → **PL-480 imports**; back-to-back droughts **1965–66** + wars → "ship-to-mouth" crisis → new agricultural strategy (C. Subramaniam; **Norman Borlaug** CIMMYT wheat; **M.S. Swaminathan**).

### 3.1 Components (the package — name all of them, not just seeds)

1. **HYV seeds:** Mexican dwarf wheats (**Lerma Rojo, Sonora-64** → Indian **Kalyan Sona, Sonalika**); rice **IR-8** (IRRI "miracle rice") → **Jaya, Ratna**; **HYVP kharif 1966**.
2. **Assured irrigation** (tubewell boom) + **chemical fertilisers + pesticides** — the seed-fertiliser-water package.
3. **Extension pilots:** **IADP 1960 "package programme" — pilot districts included WEST GODAVARI (AP)** ⭐⭐ (with Thanjavur, Ludhiana etc.); IAAP 1964.
4. **Institutions & prices:** **Agricultural Prices Commission (→CACP) and FCI, both 1965** ⭐ — MSP + assured procurement + buffer stock; rural credit (cooperatives → NABARD 1982); land consolidation (Punjab-Haryana).
5. **Research network:** ICAR system, state agricultural universities (AP: **ANGRAU**, Bapatla/Maruteru stations).

### 3.2 Impacts — argue both sides

- **Positive:** foodgrains ~82 mt (1960-61) → ~130 mt (1980s) → **self-sufficiency & end of PL-480 dependence**; wheat more than doubled within a decade of 1965; buffer stocks enabled **PDS**; farm incomes & wage employment rose in GR belts; forex saved.
- **Negative (the "critically analyse" half):**
  - **Regional inequity** — confined to Punjab–Haryana–W. UP (wheat) and deltaic pockets; eastern & rainfed India bypassed → later **BGREI** (Bringing GR to Eastern India).
  - **Crop inequity** — wheat-rice bias squeezed **pulses, oilseeds, millets** (import dependence in edible oils persists).
  - **Class inequity** — capital-intensive package favoured large farmers; tenant displacement.
  - **Ecological cost** — groundwater mining, soil fatigue & salinity, monoculture, pesticide residues, (later) stubble burning; power-subsidy trap.
  - Nutritional paradox: calories secured, protein/micronutrients lagged.
- **Close (evaluation):** a production triumph but not a distribution or sustainability triumph → Swaminathan's **"Evergreen Revolution"**, rainbow revolutions, millets & natural farming as course-correction. **AP tie-in:** IADP West Godavari, Maruteru's Swarna, and today **APCNF (natural farming)** as the post-GR turn ⭐.

## 4. Irrigation and agro-climatic zones

### 4.1 India

- ≈ **52–55% of GCA irrigated**; source mix: **wells + tubewells ≈ 62%**, canals ≈ 24%, **tanks ≈ 2–3%** (declining) ⭐.
- Programmes: PMKSY (**"Har Khet Ko Pani", "Per Drop More Crop"** — micro-irrigation), AIBP, watershed development; groundwater — Atal Bhujal Yojana.
- **15 agro-climatic zones (Planning Commission, 1989)** — AP falls in **X. Southern Plateau & Hills** and **XI. East Coast Plains & Hills** ⭐.

### 4.2 Andhra Pradesh

- Roughly **half the sown area irrigated**: **canals ~1/3 (deltas** — Godavari anicut 1852, Krishna anicut 1855, Prakasam Barrage; File 18), **wells ~half (Rayalaseema/uplands)**, **tanks declining (~7%)** — AP's tank legacy (Kakatiya-era chains) is a Mains flourish.
- **ANGRAU agro-climatic zones of AP (≈7)** ⭐: North-coastal; Godavari; Krishna; Southern; **Scarce-rainfall (Rayalaseema)**; High-altitude & tribal (agency); (+ central/black-soil sub-zones in some schemes — verify the board's preferred list).
- **AP Micro-Irrigation Project (APMIP, 2003) — India's first dedicated state micro-irrigation project** ⭐; AP is a national leader in drip/sprinkler coverage (drives the horticulture surge).

## 5. AP agriculture and climate change ⭐⭐ (2025 PYQ — input-wise kit)

**Background line:** an agrarian, dualistic state — assured-canal deltas vs rainfed Rayalaseema (Anantapur ~550 mm, India's second-driest pocket); agriculture & allied ≈ 36% GSVA; allied (livestock+fisheries) contributes nearly **half of agricultural GVA** — a cushion field crops lack.

| Input | Climate-change stress | Evidence / response |
|---|---|---|
| **Rainfall** | Erratic SW-monsoon onset & withdrawal; more intense bursts; NE-monsoon variability hits Nellore–Rayalaseema | Michaung (2023) delta floods; recurring Rayalaseema droughts → watershed missions, millets, **APCNF natural farming — won the 2024 Gulbenkian Prize for Humanity** ⭐⭐ |
| **Temperature** | Heat waves (2015: 1,700+ deaths); night-warming cuts rabi paddy/maize grain-filling; livestock & poultry mortality | Heat-action plans; adjusted sowing windows; heat-tolerant ANGRAU varieties |
| **Irrigation water** | Krishna inflow deficits (upstream use + drought years) vs Godavari flood years; groundwater stress in Rayalaseema; **salinity ingress in deltas** | Polavaram (national project), lift schemes (HNSS, Veligonda), micro-irrigation, tank restoration; mangrove buffers (Coringa) |
| **Power** | Pumping demand rises with heat/drought; free farm power = fiscal + grid stress | **9-hour assured day-time farm power via dedicated solar capacity** ⭐; feeder solarisation; Kurnool/NP Kunta solar parks |
| **Cross-cutting** | Cyclone frequency/intensity on the coast; pest shifts (fall armyworm) | Crop insurance (PMFBY/state free-insurance variants), **e-crop enrolment via village-level Rythu (farmer) service centres** ⭐, cyclone shelters + early warning (a recognised AP success) |

**Close for Mains:** AP's answer = *diversify* (horticulture, aqua, livestock) + *de-risk* (insurance, RBK-style last-mile institutions, natural farming) + *water-proof* (Polavaram, micro-irrigation, solar power). *(Scheme names have been renamed after the 2024 government change — Rythu Bharosa/Annadata Sukhibhava etc.; verify current nomenclature.)*
