⭐ The plant-kingdom file — syllabus words **"trees and plants."** India has ~45,000+ plant species (BSI). Forest-type *classification* and ISFR figures are in **File 20**; phytogeographic regions in **File 49** — this file covers the **trees, endemic & threatened plants, grasslands, aquatic/mangrove/marine flora and medicinal plants**, with the AP endemic-flora set that pairs with the fauna of File 50.

## 1. National plant symbols ⭐

- National **tree** — **Banyan** (*Ficus benghalensis*); National **flower** — **Lotus**; National **fruit** — **Mango**. **AP state tree — NEEM; state flower — JASMINE (malle)** ⭐ (contrast with the national symbols in Prelims).

## 2. Trees of ecological & economic importance (place each) ⭐

| Tree | Forest type / zone | Note |
|---|---|---|
| **Teak (*Tectona grandis*)** | Tropical **moist/dry deciduous** | Premier timber; **Nallamala, Godavari agency (AP)**; "Adilabad/Godavari teak" repute |
| **Sal (*Shorea robusta*)** | Moist deciduous | Central & NE India; **northern AP agency fringes** |
| **Sandalwood (*Santalum album*)** | Southern dry deciduous | Karnataka-heartland; scattered in Rayalaseema |
| **RED SANDERS (*Pterocarpus santalinus*)** | Dry-deciduous **Eastern-Ghats endemic** | **Endemic to Seshachalam–Veligonda–Palakonda–Lankamala (AP)**; CITES-listed; smuggling wars (RSASTF) ⭐⭐ |
| **Bamboo** ("green gold") | Moist forests, plantations | NTFP + industry (File 40, 42); National Bamboo Mission; de-notified as "tree" (2017) |
| **Rosewood, mahogany, ebony** | Tropical evergreen | W. Ghats/NE (not AP) — trick options |
| **Terminalia, mahua, tendu, neem, tamarind, palmyra** | Dry deciduous / scrub | AP's everyday economic flora; tendu = beedi leaf (NTFP) |
| **Casuarina, subabul, eucalyptus** | Coastal/farm-forestry | **Prakasam–Godavari pulpwood belt** (File 40, 42); casuarina = cyclone shelterbelt |

## 3. Endemic & threatened plants — India and AP ⭐⭐

- **India:** ~**28% of plant species are endemic**; hotspots (Western Ghats, NE) hold most; threatened plants tracked in Red Data Books (BSI). Examples: *Nepenthes khasiana* (NE pitcher plant), many Western-Ghats *Impatiens*.
- **Andhra Pradesh — Eastern-Ghats endemics (the AP-specific set):**
  - **Red sanders (*Pterocarpus santalinus*)** — the celebrity endemic (above).
  - ***Cycas beddomei*** — a **cycad endemic to the Tirumala/Seshachalam hills**, **CITES Appendix I** ⭐⭐ (India's only Appendix-I cycad).
  - ***Syzygium alternifolium*** (*adavi jam / mogi*) — endemic tree of Seshachalam; ***Pterocarpus***, ***Terminalia pallida*** (endemic), ***Shorea***; ***Boswellia***; rare medicinal ***Decalepis hamiltonii*** (nannari/swallow-root) ⭐.
  - These make Seshachalam a **plant-endemism centre** — the botanical case for its **Biosphere Reserve status (2010)** despite AP's no-hotspot position (File 49).

## 4. Grasslands & their flora

- India's grasslands (often "wastelands" in revenue records — a policy problem ⭐) include the **Deccan/Rayalaseema semi-arid grasslands** critical for the **Great Indian Bustard, blackbuck and Indian wolf** (File 50) — **Rollapadu** is the AP exemplar.
- Flora: *Cymbopogon* (lemongrass), *Themeda, Aristida, Sehima-Dichanthium* covers; acacia-neem-palmyra savanna; overgrazing and conversion are the threats — grassland conservation is under-institutionalised (no "grassland park" category).

## 5. Wetland, mangrove & marine flora ⭐

- **Mangroves (halophytes):** **Coringa (Godavari delta)** — *Avicennia, Rhizophora, Sonneratia, Bruguiera, Ceriops*; adaptations = **pneumatophores (breathing roots), stilt roots, vivipary, salt glands** ⭐; ecosystem services = cyclone-surge buffer, fish nursery, carbon (blue carbon). AP mangrove cover ~404 sq km — **4th in India** (File 20).
- **Freshwater/wetland flora:** **Kolleru** (India's largest freshwater lake — Ramsar 2002) with reed-beds and (invasive) water hyacinth/African weed problems; lotus, *Typha*, *Ipomoea*.
- **Marine/coastal flora:** **seagrass beds** (dugong/turtle forage), **seaweeds/algae** (emerging economy — seaweed mission), and **casuarina shelterbelts** on the shore ridge.

## 6. Medicinal, aromatic & sacred flora

- India = a global medicinal-plant repository (Ayurveda/AYUSH); **National Medicinal Plants Board**; examples — *Rauvolfia* (sarpagandha), *Withania* (ashwagandha), *Andrographis* (nela vemu — Rayalaseema), *Gymnema* (podapatri), *Commiphora, Aegle* (bael). **Gum karaya** (*Sterculia urens*) is an AP-agency speciality NTFP (File 40) ⭐.
- **Sacred groves** — community-protected forest patches (in-situ conservation, File 52); AP's temple-hill forests (Tirumala, Srisailam, Ahobilam) function similarly ⭐.
- Crop/agro flora (rice varieties, chillies, oil palm, Araku coffee) → **File 39**.

## 7. AP floral profile — one-look table ⭐

| Category | AP examples |
|---|---|
| Dominant forest | **Tropical dry-deciduous** (teak-terminalia-bamboo); semi-evergreen in NE Ghats (File 20) |
| Endemic trees/plants | **Red sanders, *Cycas beddomei*, *Syzygium alternifolium*, *Terminalia pallida*, *Decalepis*** |
| Grassland flora | Rayalaseema savanna (bustard-blackbuck habitat, Rollapadu) |
| Wetland/mangrove | **Coringa** mangroves; **Kolleru** reed-beds; seagrass |
| Economic flora | Teak, bamboo, tendu, casuarina-subabul pulpwood, palmyra, gum karaya |
| Medicinal | Nela vemu, nannari, ashwagandha; temple-hill sacred groves |
| State symbols | Tree **neem**; flower **jasmine** |

**Mains line:** AP's flora mirrors its rainfall map (File 18) — evergreen-to-deciduous on the wet northern Ghats, thorn-scrub-and-grassland in rain-shadow Rayalaseema, mangroves on the deltas — and its endemism, concentrated in Seshachalam, is a hotspot-grade asset that hotspot maps miss.
