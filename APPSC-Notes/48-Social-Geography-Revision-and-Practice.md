## 3. Concept-and-author one-liners

- **Ravenstein** — laws of migration; **Lee** — push-pull + intervening obstacles.
- **1921** — Great Divide of Indian demography; **demographic dividend window ≈ 2005–2055** (UNFPA).
- **Risley (1915)/GUHA (six races)** — colonial/foundational racial classifications; census dropped race after 1931.
- **Census town** = 5,000 + 75% male non-farm workers + 400/km²; statutory town = notified civic body.
- **Wet-point settlement** — village at water source (tanks of Rayalaseema).
- Telugu = **4th largest** Indian language, largest Dravidian, **Classical (2008)**; Savara/Gutob-Gadaba = **Austroasiatic islands** in AP's agency.
