## 1. The "Making of Andhra" thesis ⭐⭐⭐ (the synthesis paragraph that lifts any Part-B answer)

**One-line thesis:** *Under the Eastern Chalukyas an imported Deccan lineage became a deltaic agrarian monarchy and, in financing the Brahmanical temple order, the samanta chiefdoms and the vernacular literary culture, laid down "Andhra" — a formation the Kakatiyas made sovereign, the Reddis made classical, and Vijayanagara-Golconda carried to the zenith.*

Four transformations, read together:

1. **Political** ⭐⭐ — the Vengi viceroyalty (624) **decomposed into its samantas** after the Danarnava crisis (973–99); the **Kakatiyas re-aggregated** those pieces into the first sovereign Telugu state (File 06); the Sultanate broke it (1323); the **Musunuri reconquista (c. 1336)** legitimised a new self-made warrior class; the **Reddis & Recherla Velamas** rebuilt sovereignty on that basis — **kingship migrated from birth to arms, land-restoration and Brahmin patronage** (the Reddis: an openly agrarian-caste sovereign house ⭐).
2. **Economic** ⭐⭐ — **agrahara/brahmadeya + devadana grants** as instruments of expansion (Kulke's **integrative model** ⭐: Brahmin settlements inserted to convert forest into taxable wet-rice land, the grant an instrument of expansion, not a symptom of decay); the **Kakatiya tank revolution** (Pakala, Laknavaram, Ramappa cheruvu; the ayagar village-service system); the **temple as total institution** — landholder, employer, bank, school; **protected trade** (Motupalli Abhaya Sasanam, 1244–45; Nanadesi-Ayyavole guilds, gadyana/varaha coinage); the **nayankara logic in embryo** among the Musunuri-Reddi-Velama chiefs, flowering under Vijayanagara (File 08).
3. **Religious** ⭐⭐ — the heterodoxies extinguished (endowment flowed to Brahmins and temples, not sanghas — Files 35 §5); a **competitive temple-bhakti order** installed; Andhra's signature move — **radical Karnataka Veerashaivism domesticated into caste-conserving, Veda-affirming ARADHYA Shaivism** (File 37 §1), beside a socially-widening Srivaishnavism; Islam a permanent factor after 1323.
4. **Linguistic** ⭐⭐⭐ — the through-line: Telugu rose **from the boundary-clauses of Vengi's bilingual plates (fact & locality) → verse in stone (Pandaranga 848) → courtly epic (Nannaya 1051) → the desi counter-current (Somanatha, Gona Buddha Reddi) → the Reddi courts' salaried classicism (Srinatha) → the Vijayanagara zenith (Amuktamalyada, Ashtadiggajas)** — always along the **MARGA-DESI axis** ⭐⭐ (Sanskritised pan-Indic register vs indigenous dwipada impulse) first set up under Vengi.

**Evidence discipline (write one line of it):** the record is skewed — **copper-plate grants show donative elite transactions, not peasants**; a grant proves an intention and a claim, **not** a demographic fact; **foundation ≠ fabric** (Pancharamas); genealogical prasastis manufacture status. Sources: charters + stone inscriptions (hero-stones, sati-stones) + literature (*Kridabhiramamu*'s Warangal streets) + foreign checks (**Xuanzang 639, Marco Polo 1290s**).

## 2. Precision flags — the examiner-trap table ⭐⭐⭐ (drill before every Part-B paper)

| Claim you'll be tempted to write | Status |
|---|---|
| Earliest Telugu inscription is from Vengi | ❌ **Kalamalla (Renati Cholas, c. 575)**; Vengi has the earliest *literary* Telugu in stone (Pandaranga, 848) |
| Undavalli caves = Eastern Chalukya | ❌ mainly **VISHNUKUNDIN** (File 35) |
| Nannaya translated the whole Mahabharata | ❌ **2½ parvas** (Adi, Sabha, part-Aranya); the "curse" story of the unfinished Aranya = literary tradition, flag it |
| Nannaya wrote the *Andhra Sabda Chintamani* | ⚠️ traditional attribution, **seriously disputed** |
| Errana completed the Aranya parva at a Reddi court | ✔ under **Prolaya Vema Reddi** — the Kavitraya spans Chalukya → Reddi courts ⭐ |
| Pancharama temples as we see them are Eastern Chalukyan | ⚠️ **foundations** yes, standing **fabric heavily later** |
| Eastern Chalukyas were purely Shaiva | ❌ Jain & Vaishnava endowments throughout (Ayyana Mahadevi, Amma II, Vimaladitya — File 36) |
| Kamma-Reddy-Velama caste blocs existed at Vengi | ❌ anachronistic — fluid status-identities till late-medieval (Talbot) |
| Kakatiyas were sovereign from their origin | ❌ feudatories until **Prola II / Rudradeva** |
| Rudramadevi was a regent | ❌ queen-**REGNANT**, ruled as "Rudradeva Maharaja", died in battle vs Ambadeva (Chandupatla 1289) |
| Motupalli Abhaya Sasanam is a religious grant | ❌ **trade-protection** charter (Ganapatideva) |
| Reddis were Kshatriyas | ❌ agrarian-caste sovereigns — the endpoint of medieval status mobility |
| Ramanuja's persecutor "Krimikantha" = Kulottunga I | ⚠️ traditional identification, **disputed** |
| Srikurmam "converted by Ramanuja" | ⚠️ contested — carries **Madhva (Narahari Tirtha)** epigraphy too |
| Rajahmundry named after Rajaraja Narendra | ✔ from his title **Rajamahendra** |
| Amaravati school = Gandhara's contemporary imitator | ❌ an independent third school, native limestone idiom (File 00F) |
