## 1. Vegetation classification — India

### 1.1 The five-type (NCERT) classification ⭐ (Prelims workhorse)

| Type | Rainfall | Traits | Trees | Where |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Tropical evergreen (rain forest)** | >200 cm | Dense, layered, **no fixed leaf-shedding season** | Rosewood, mahogany, ebony, rubber | W. Ghats windward, NE India, A&N |
| **Tropical deciduous (monsoon forest)** — **India's most widespread** | 70–200 cm | Shed leaves in dry season; **moist** (100–200) & **dry** (70–100) sub-types | **TEAK (moist), SAL; sandalwood, terminalia** | Peninsular fringe, middle Ganga; **most of AP's forest is (dry) deciduous** ⭐ |
| **Thorn & scrub** | <70 cm | Xerophytic — long roots, thick bark, thorns | **Acacias (babul), palas, euphorbia, cacti** | NW India + **rain-shadow Deccan incl. Rayalaseema** ⭐ |
| **Montane** | altitude-zoned | Wet temperate (1000–2000 m), coniferous (1500–3000 m), alpine | Oaks-chestnuts; **pine, deodar, silver fir**; birch-rhododendron | Himalaya; Nilgiri **sholas** (temperate patches in tropics) |
| **Mangrove (tidal/littoral)** | deltaic | Stilt/breathing roots (pneumatophores), salt-tolerant | **Sundari (Sundarbans!)**, Avicennia, Rhizophora | Ganga-Brahmaputra, Mahanadi, **Godavari–Krishna (Coringa, AP — 2nd/3rd largest mangrove tract)** ⭐ |

### 1.2 Champion & Seth (1968) — the scientific classification

**16 type-groups** (~221 sub-types) — remember the ladder: tropical **wet evergreen → semi-evergreen → moist deciduous → littoral & swamp → dry deciduous → thorn → dry evergreen**, then subtropical (broad-leaved hill, pine, dry evergreen), temperate (montane wet, Himalayan moist, Himalayan dry), and alpine (sub-alpine, moist alpine scrub, dry alpine scrub).
- **Tropical dry deciduous** is India's (and AP's) largest single group ⭐.
- **Tropical dry evergreen** — a curiosity found on the **TN–south-AP coast** (Nellore–Tirupati belt!) ⭐ favourite trick question.

### 1.3 Forest survey numbers (ISFR 2023 — verify latest edition before exam)

- Forest + tree cover ≈ **25.17% of India** (forest cover ~21.76%); **Madhya Pradesh** — largest forest area; **Mizoram** — highest %; **Lakshadweep/A&N** top UT %.
- **Mangroves ~4,992 sq km** — West Bengal > Gujarat > A&N; **AP ~404 sq km (4th)** ⭐.
- **AP: recorded forest area ~37,258 sq km (~22.9% of the state); actual forest cover ~29,800 sq km (~18.3%)** — below the 33% national-policy goal; AP posted **gains in recent ISFRs** (plantations, mangrove restoration).
- National Forest Policy **1988** — 33% cover goal, ecological balance + people's needs; **FRA 2006** (community/individual forest rights — agency-area relevance ⭐), **CAMPA/Green India Mission**, Godavarman case (forest definition).

## 2. Mountains, hills and their forests (syllabus: "Plateau forests, Hill forests")

### 2.1 India snapshot

- **Hill forests (Himalayan/montane):** altitudinal zonation — sal/mixed deciduous (foothills) → pine (chir) → **deodar-oak-fir-spruce** → birch-rhododendron krummholz → alpine meadows (**bugyals**); economically: timber, resin, medicinal herbs; fragile — landslides, fires.
- **Plateau forests:** dry/moist deciduous cover on Chotanagpur, Vindhya-Satpura, Dandakaranya and the Deccan — **teak, sal, tendu (bidi leaf), mahua, bamboo**; the tribal-forest economy (minor forest produce) and also the mining-forest conflict zone ⭐ (GS overlap).
- **Western Ghats** — evergreen/shola-grassland mosaic, biodiversity hotspot; **Eastern Ghats** — drier deciduous, fragmented, less protected (AP's share below).

### 2.2 AP's hills and their forests ⭐⭐ (build answers around this table)

| Hill tract | Forest type | Signature flora | Signature fauna/notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| **North Eastern Ghats (Araku–Paderu–Chintapalli–Maredumilli, ASR/Vizag)** | **Semi-evergreen & moist deciduous "hill forests"** — AP's lushest | Coffee-pepper plantations under forest, bamboo, jackfruit, wild mango | **Papikonda NP** (Godavari gorge), hornbills; shifting-cultivation (podu) issue; Borra-Araku ecotourism |
| **NALLAMALA (Palnadu–Prakasam–Nandyal–Kurnool)** | **Dry deciduous plateau forest** — largest compact block | Teak, terminalia, bamboo, **red sanders on southern fringes** | **NAGARJUNASAGAR–SRISAILAM TIGER RESERVE (NSTR) — INDIA'S LARGEST TIGER RESERVE** ⭐⭐; **Chenchu** PVTG co-existence; **Gundla Brahmeswaram WLS**; Srisailam–Ahobilam temple forests |
| **SESHACHALAM–Veligonda–Palakonda–Lankamala (Tirupati–Kadapa–Nellore)** | Dry deciduous–scrub with **endemic pockets** | **RED SANDERS (Pterocarpus santalinus) — endemic, CITES-listed, smuggling wars** ⭐⭐; sandalwood patches | **Seshachalam Biosphere Reserve (2010 — AP's first)**; **Sri Venkateswara NP & WLS**; **Sri Lankamalleswara WLS — JERDON'S COURSER (rediscovered 1986; critically endangered)** ⭐; slender loris |
| **Erramala–Kurnool plateau, Rayalaseema pediplain** | **Thorn-scrub ("plateau" dry forests)** | Acacia-neem-palmyra; fodder scarcity | **ROLLAPADU WLS (Kurnool) — GREAT INDIAN BUSTARD** ⭐⭐; blackbuck; lesser florican records |
| **Coastal belt & deltas** | **Littoral/mangrove + dry evergreen patches** | Avicennia-Rhizophora; casuarina shelterbelts; palmyra ("state tree of coastal identity" — actual state tree is neem) | **CORINGA WLS** (mangroves; **fishing cat** ⭐), **Krishna WLS** (estuarine crocodiles), Kolleru-Atapaka pelicanry, Pulicat flamingos, **Nelapattu (pelicans), Telineelapuram–Naupada (Srikakulam — pelicans/storks)**; **Uppalapadu (Guntur)** village bird haven |

### 2.3 AP protected-area roll call ⭐ (match-pair bank)

- **National Parks (3):** **Papikonda** (Godavari gorge), **Sri Venkateswara** (Tirupati), **Rajiv Gandhi/Rameswaram (Kadapa — small)**.
- **Tiger Reserve:** **NSTR** (largest in India; core Gundla Brahmeswaram).
- **Elephant Reserve:** **Koundinya WLS (Chittoor — AP's only elephant habitat**, migrants from TN-Karnataka) ⭐.
- **Biosphere Reserve:** **Seshachalam (2010)**.
- **Bird sanctuaries:** Kolleru–Atapaka, Nelapattu, Pulicat, Telineelapuram, Uppalapadu (community).
- **Others:** Kambalakonda (Vizag), Kondakarla Ava (lake, Anakapalli), Papikonda buffer, Sri Penusila Narasimha (Nellore), Koundinya, Gundla Brahmeswaram, Krishna, Coringa.
- **Ramsar sites in AP: Kolleru (2002)** — watch for new additions (verify).
- **State symbols (post-2014 AP):** animal — **blackbuck (krishna jinka)**; bird — **rose-ringed parakeet (rama chiluka)**; tree — **NEEM**; flower — **jasmine (malle)** ⭐.

### 2.4 Flora & fauna — species one-liners (Prelims darts)

- **Red sanders** — endemic to Seshachalam–Veligonda; heartwood export bans; Operation-style anti-smuggling task force (RSASTF).
- **Jerdon's courser** — nocturnal bird, Lankamala scrub, rediscovered **1986**; Telugu *kalivi kodi*.
- **Great Indian Bustard** — Rollapadu; critically endangered (<150 nationally).
- **Fishing cat** — Coringa mangroves flagship; **Olive Ridley turtles** nest on AP beaches (Sacramento–Hope island spits).
- **Chenchus** (NSTR), **Khonds/Konda Doras** (khondalite name-source!), **Yanadis** (Nellore) — forest-dwelling communities; FRA link.
- India frame: 4 hotspots (Himalaya, Indo-Burma, W. Ghats–Sri Lanka, Sundaland-Nicobar); Project Tiger 1973 (AP entry: NSTR 1983); Project Elephant 1992; cheetah reintroduction 2022 (Kuno — outside AP).

### 3.2 Mains answer skeletons (10–15 marks)

1. **"The Godavari–Krishna deltas are the gift of engineering as much as of the rivers." Discuss.** — Thesis: natural delta + Cotton's anicuts (1852/55) → assured irrigation → rice bowl; evidence: Dowleswaram, Prakasam barrage, canal net, famine→granary shift; today: Polavaram/Pattiseema linking, salinity & cyclone risks; close: climate-proofing (mangroves, Coringa).
2. **Account for the aridity of Rayalaseema and evaluate the lift-irrigation response.** — Rain-shadow of both monsoon branches + interior location + hard-rock aquifers; droughts (Anantapur driest); response: HNSS, GNSS, Telugu Ganga, Veligonda, KC canal legacy; evaluate: energy cost, tail-end reliability, groundwater recharge alternatives (watersheds, tanks).
3. **AP as "Ratna Garbha": survey the state's mineral wealth and its development dilemmas.** — World-class: barytes, uranium, beach sands, Black Galaxy, KG gas; dilemmas: Samata & agency bauxite, Obulapuram, sand governance, DMF use; close: value-addition (ceramics, cement, steel) + sustainable-mining framing.
4. **Classify AP's natural vegetation and relate each type to relief and rainfall.** — Use §2.2 table: hill semi-evergreen (NE Ghats, >1,100 mm), dry deciduous plateau (Nallamala), scrub (Rayalaseema <700 mm), littoral-mangrove (deltas), dry evergreen (south coast); tie each to a protected area and one species.
5. **Dams as multipurpose "temples": compare Nagarjuna Sagar and Polavaram in objectives and controversies.** — NS: masonry giant, Jawahar/Lal Bahadur canals, power, AP-TS sharing; Polavaram: national project, river-link, 960 MW, R&R + interstate objections; close with KRMB/GRMB governance lesson.
