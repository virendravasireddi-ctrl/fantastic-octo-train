## 0. The pattern

India's **north-west (Khyber-Bolan corridor)** is the permanent invasion door. Persians → Alexander → (post-Mauryan) **Indo-Greeks → Shakas → Pahlavas → Kushanas**, each pushed by the one behind (the ultimate driver: the **Yuezhi-Xiongnu** domino in Central Asia). The story is **cultural fusion more than conquest** — the north-west becomes ancient India's cosmopolitan crossroads.

## 1. The Achaemenid Persians (6th–4th c. BCE)
- **Cyrus**, then **Darius I (c. 518 BCE)** annexed the Indus region — the **"Hindush" satrapy**, the richest of the Persian empire (Herodotus: huge gold tribute).
- **Impact:** the **Kharoshthi script (Aramaic-derived)**; Indo-Persian trade & administrative ideas; likely influence on **Mauryan pillar polish/bell capitals**; opened India's door to Greek knowledge.

## 2. Alexander (327–325 BCE)
- Crossed the Indus 326 BCE; **Ambhi of Taxila submitted**; **battle of the Hydaspes (Jhelum, 326) vs PORUS** — won, reinstated him; army **mutinied at the Beas** (fear of the Nandas); died at Babylon 323.
- **Assessment — militarily marginal, historically catalytic ⭐:** held nothing (conquests absorbed by Chandragupta within a decade), but (a) exposed north-western fragmentation → **eased Mauryan unification**; (b) opened land & sea routes; (c) his companions' accounts = the **"sheet-anchor of ancient Indian chronology"**; (d) seeded the Indo-Greek states → Gandhara art. *Sell the paradox: a failed conquest with vast indirect consequences.*

## 3. The Shungas (185–73 BCE) & Kanvas (73–28 BCE)

| Item | Detail |
|---|---|
| Coup | **Pushyamitra Shunga**, Brahmin senapati, killed **Brihadratha** at an army review (185 BCE); capitals Pataliputra + **Vidisha** (son **Agnimitra** — hero of Kalidasa's *Malavikagnimitram*) |
| Vedic revival | **Two ashvamedhas** — attested by **Patanjali** ("iha Pushyamitram yajayamah") and the **Ayodhya inscription of Dhanadeva** ⭐; the milieu of the **Manusmriti's** codification and Sanskrit's consolidation |
| Frontier | **Indo-Greek advance (Demetrius/Menander) repelled** — grandson **Vasumitra** on the Sindhu; sources: *Yuga Purana*, *Malavikagnimitram*; **Kharavela of Kalinga** (Hathigumpha) also pressed Magadha |
| Art ⭐ | **Bharhut railings & Sanchi gateways-railings** — early Buddhist **narrative relief, still ANICONIC** (Buddha as wheel, footprints, empty throne, bodhi tree) — before the Kushana-age human image |
| The verdict | "**Brahmanical revival, NOT Buddhist pogrom**" — the *Divyavadana's* persecution charge is sectarian; Buddhist art flourished under them. (Loss of patronage ≠ persecution — a transferable line.) |
| Kanvas | **Vasudeva Kanva** murdered the last Shunga Devabhuti; four kings, ~45 years; overthrown by the **Satavahanas** (File 06) |

## 4. The invader relay (2nd c. BCE – 1st c. CE)

| Power | Key names | What they left ⭐ |
|---|---|---|
| **Indo-Greeks** | Demetrius; **MENANDER (Milinda)** — the *Milindapanha* dialogue with Nagasena | **First gold coins; first portrait-and-legend coins** (numismatics as history begins); Gandhara art's seed |
| **Shakas** (Scythians) | Branches; the **Western Kshatrapas of Ujjain** most durable; **RUDRADAMAN I (c. 150 CE)** | **Junagadh/Girnar inscription — the FIRST long polished-SANSKRIT inscription** (Prakrit→Sanskrit epigraphy shift) + Sudarshana lake repair; **Shaka era 78 CE**; *Vikrama Samvat 58 BCE* tied to Vikramaditya-expels-Shakas legend |
| **Parthians (Pahlavas)** | **Gondophernes** | **St Thomas** tradition |
| **Kushanas** (Yuezhi) | below | The greatest of the relay |

## 5. The Kushanas (1st–3rd c. CE)

- **Rise:** one of five **Yuezhi** clans; **Kujula Kadphises** unified them (Bactria-Kabul); **Vima Kadphises** entered NW India — **abundant gold coinage** (Roman-trade gold), styled a **Shiva devotee (Maheshvara)**.
- **KANISHKA (accession conventionally 78 CE = Shaka era):** empire **Central Asia (Tarim) → Afghanistan-Gandhara → the Ganga plain**; capitals **Purushapura (Peshawar) + Mathura**. **"The second Ashoka":** convened the **Fourth Buddhist Council (Kashmir)** → **Mahayana** crystallised and travelled the **Silk Road to China**; court luminaries — **Ashvaghosha** (*Buddhacharita*), **NAGARJUNA** (shunyata — the Andhra link! 03 §5), **Vasumitra**, **CHARAKA** (medicine) ⭐.
- **The two art schools ⭐⭐ (guaranteed contrast):**

| | **Gandhara** | **Mathura** |
|---|---|---|
| Idiom | **Greco-Roman (Hellenistic)** form, Buddhist theme | **Indigenous** Indian |
| Material | Grey schist (later stucco) | **Red sandstone** |
| Buddha | Wavy hair, muscular realism, heavy drapery | Robust, energetic, thin clinging robe, spiritual vigour |
| Note | Among the first anthropomorphic Buddhas | Independently made the first Buddhas too; also Jain & Brahmanical icons |

  Both under Kushana patronage; **the Buddha depicted in HUMAN form for the first time** (earlier art aniconic — contrast Bharhut-Sanchi, §3).
- **Silk Road & syncretism:** controlled the overland route linking China-India-Rome; **Pliny complained of Rome's gold drain**; coin pantheon = **Greek (Helios), Persian (Mithra), Indian (Shiva/Oesho, Buddha)** deities — religious eclecticism as imperial glue; finest **gold coinage** before the Guptas.
- **Decline & legacy:** Huvishka → **Vasudeva** (note the Indianised name — assimilation); fragmented by the 3rd c. before the **Sasanians**; legacy = Mahayana's export, the two art schools & first Buddha images, Silk-Road kingship, gold-coin template for the Guptas, the Shaka era.

## 6. Prelims nuggets

1. **Darius I c. 518 BCE — "Hindush"**, richest satrapy; **Kharoshthi = Aramaic-derived**.
2. **Hydaspes = Jhelum, 326 BCE, vs Porus**; mutiny at the **Beas**; Ambhi of Taxila submitted.
3. Alexander's historians = the **"sheet-anchor of Indian chronology"**.
4. **Pushyamitra's ashvamedha** — attested by **Patanjali + Ayodhya (Dhanadeva) inscription**.
5. **Bharhut & Sanchi = Shunga-age, ANICONIC** narrative relief ⭐.
6. *Malavikagnimitram* — Kalidasa, on **Agnimitra**; *Yuga Purana* — Indo-Greek advance.
7. **Menander = Milinda (Milindapanha, with Nagasena)**; Indo-Greeks = **first portrait/legend & gold coins**.
8. **Rudradaman's Junagadh (c. 150 CE) = first long Sanskrit inscription** + Sudarshana repair ⭐.
9. Eras: **Vikrama Samvat 58 BCE; Shaka era 78 CE** (conventionally Kanishka's accession).
10. **Gondophernes ↔ St Thomas**; Kushanas = **Yuezhi** clan.
11. Kanishka: capitals **Purushapura + Mathura**; **Fourth Council, Kashmir**; court — Ashvaghosha, Nagarjuna, Charaka.
12. **Gandhara = grey schist/Hellenistic; Mathura = red sandstone/indigenous** ⭐; first human Buddha images.
13. Kushana coins' multi-religious pantheon; **Vasudeva** = Indianised late Kushana.

## 7. Mains angles

1. **"Alexander's invasion was militarily insignificant but historically consequential." Examine.** The §2 paradox with its four consequences; close with Chandragupta filling the vacuum.
2. **"The Shunga age was a Brahmanical revival rather than a persecution of Buddhism." Critically examine.** Ashvamedha-Patanjali-Manusmriti milieu VS Bharhut-Sanchi flourishing; the Divyavadana as sectarian; verdict = patronage shifted, art thrived.
3. **"Assess the cultural impact of the foreign invasions (Indo-Greeks to Kushanas)."** Coins-as-history → Sanskrit epigraphy (Rudradaman) → eras → Gandhara-Mathura → Mahayana's Silk-Road export; fusion, not just conquest.
4. **"Kanishka was the second Ashoka." Evaluate.** Parallels (council, missions, patronage-plus-eclecticism) and differences (Silk-Road commercial empire; no Dhamma-style state ethic in his own voice — coins, not edicts).
