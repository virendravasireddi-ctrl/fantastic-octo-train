## 1. Trade and commerce

### 1.1 India's external trade (label years; verify latest)

- **2024-25 (prov.):** merchandise exports ≈ **$437 bn** + services ≈ $385 bn → **total ≈ $820+ bn**; merchandise deficit ≈ $280 bn (services surplus offsets ~half).
- **Top exports:** engineering goods, electronics (fastest riser), petroleum products, gems & jewellery, pharma (India = "pharmacy of the world"), textiles, **marine products (AP's stake ⭐)**, rice (**world's largest rice exporter**).
- **Top imports:** crude oil, gold, electronics, coal, machinery; **partners:** USA (largest export market), **China (largest import source)**, UAE; FTAs — UAE CEPA & Australia ECTA (2022), **EFTA TEPA (2024)**, **UK FTA signed 2025**; US tariff shock (2025) = current-affairs risk theme.
- **Sea routes carry ~95% of trade by volume and ~68–70% by value** ⭐ (the bridge to §1.3); Foreign Trade Policy 2023 ($2-trillion exports by 2030; districts as export hubs).
- **Domestic commerce:** GST (one nation-one market), e-NAM mandis, ONDC, e-commerce boom; **e-NAM + GST + highways = the quiet unification of India's internal market** (good Mains line).

### 1.2 Sagarmala ⭐⭐ (2023 PYQ (a) — full kit)

- **What:** flagship **port-led development** programme, **2015**, Ministry of Ports, Shipping & Waterways; National Perspective Plan 2016; **800+ projects ≈ ₹5.5–5.8 lakh cr**; a **Sagarmala 2.0** phase was announced in 2025 (verify).
- **Five pillars:** (1) **port modernisation & new ports** (incl. Vadhavan major port), (2) **port connectivity** (rail/road/pipeline evacuation), (3) **port-led industrialisation** (CEZs — coastal economic zones), (4) **coastal community development** (fishing harbours, skilling), (5) **coastal shipping & inland waterways** promotion.
- **Why it matters:** logistics cost ~13–14% of GDP → single-digit target; halved major-port turnaround times; shifts freight to cheaper coastal/water modes; **for AP** — VCIC synergy, new fishing harbours, port capacity additions ⭐.
- Companion visions: Maritime India Vision 2030, Maritime Amrit Kaal Vision 2047.

### 1.3 AP's sea-ports and their economic role ⭐⭐ (2023 PYQ (b))

| Port | Status | Hook |
|---|---|---|
| **Visakhapatnam** | AP's only **major port** (opened **1933**) | Natural harbour (Dolphin's Nose); iron-ore-to-Japan legacy; ~80 mt cargo — among India's top major ports; cruise terminal; anchors the steel-refinery-fertiliser complex |
| **Gangavaram** (Anakapalli edge of Vizag) | Non-major (Adani) | **One of India's deepest ports (~21 m draft)** — capesize vessels; serves RINL |
| **Krishnapatnam** (Nellore) | Non-major (Adani) | AP's biggest private port by capacity; coal-thermal + CBIC node |
| **Kakinada** (anchorage + deep-water) | Non-major | **India's largest rice-export gateway** ⭐; fertiliser & edible-oil imports; KG-basin oil-and-gas services hub |
| Ravva | Captive | ONGC/Cairn crude terminal |
| **Machilipatnam, Ramayapatnam (Nellore–Prakasam border), Mulapeta/Bhavanapadu (Srikakulam), Kakinada Gateway** | **Under construction (4 new greenfield ports)** ⭐ | The "a port every ~50 km" strategy — verify commissioning status |

- **Institutional:** **AP Maritime Board (2018)** regulates non-major ports; coastline ~974 km (2nd-longest; ~1,053 km per re-survey).
- **Economic role (the actual answer):** (i) **EXIM gateway** for AP *and* the interior (Chhattisgarh ore, Telangana cargo — Vizag's hinterland spans states); (ii) **industrial anchoring** — break-of-bulk industries cluster at ports (File 24); (iii) **export engine** — seafood (#1 state), rice, granite, barytes, pharma; (iv) **employment-urbanisation** (Vizag's rise); (v) **corridor logic** — VCIC/ECEC ride on the port chain; (vi) **state revenue & logistics competitiveness** (AP a LEADS "Achiever" state).
- **Challenges to balance:** rail-road evacuation gaps, cyclone exposure, dredging costs, inter-port competition (Chennai–Paradip), environmental/fisherfolk concerns.

## 2. Communication

- **Postal:** India Post — **world's largest postal network (~1.65 lakh post offices, ~90% rural)** ⭐; PIN code 1972; India Post Payments Bank (2018) — post office as fintech last mile.
- **Telecom & digital:** world's **2nd-largest telecom market** (~118 cr subscribers; ~97 cr internet users); teledensity ~85%; **5G (2022) — among the world's fastest rollouts**; **BharatNet** — OFC to 2+ lakh gram panchayats; Digital India (2015); **UPI — world's largest real-time payment system** (commerce enabler ⭐).
- **AP angle:** **AP FiberNet (APSFL, 2015-16) — pioneer state-owned fibre-to-home grid** ⭐; e-governance legacy (village secretariats as digital service points); **satellite communication launches from Sriharikota (in AP!)** — a cheeky but valid AP tie-in.
- Mass media: DD/AIR reach, vibrant Telugu press — one line each is enough.

## 3. Road transport

### 3.1 India

- **~66–67 lakh km — world's 2nd-largest road network**; NH ≈ **1.46 lakh km = ~2% of length but ~40% of traffic** ⭐⭐; SH ~1.8 lakh km; rural roads the bulk (**PMGSY 2000** transformed access).
- Programmes: NHDP → **Bharatmala (2017)** — economic corridors + expressways (Delhi–Mumbai); **Golden Quadrilateral + NS–EW corridors**; **PM GatiShakti (2021)** multimodal master-planning; **National Logistics Policy (2022)**; FASTag tolling; road safety (~1.7 lakh deaths/yr — India's grim #1).

### 3.2 Andhra Pradesh ⭐

- **NH-16 (old NH-5)** — the **Kolkata–Chennai GQ leg down coastal AP (Srikakulam→Nellore) is the state's economic spine** ⭐⭐; **NH-44** (India's longest, Srinagar–Kanyakumari) crosses Kurnool–Anantapur; NH-65 (Pune–Machilipatnam), NH-40 (Kurnool–Chittoor), NH-216 (coastal); AP NH length ~8,700+ km.
- **Bengaluru–Chennai Expressway clips Chittoor district** (nearing completion — verify); Amaravati/port connectivity corridors announced.
- **APSRTC:** lineage from the **Nizam's road-transport department (1932)** → corporation **1958** → once in **Guinness Records for the world's largest bus fleet** → **merged into the state government (Jan 2020) — the first RTC absorbed by a government** ⭐⭐; Pallevelugu rural services = access equity.

## 4. Storage and warehousing

- **Why the syllabus lists it:** the gap between record harvests and food security is storage — post-harvest losses ≈ **₹1.5 lakh cr/yr (cereals 4–6%, fruits & vegetables up to ~15%)** ⭐.
- **Agencies:** **FCI (1965)** — central pool, buffer norms, CAP (cover-and-plinth) storage; **CWC (1957)** + **AP State Warehousing Corporation (1958)**; PPP **steel silos**; cold chain — **~8,700 cold storages / ~37–40 mt, ~60% concentrated in UP–WB (potato) and ~75% single-commodity** ⭐ classic imbalance point.
- **Modern instruments:** **WDRA (2007) — negotiable warehouse receipts (e-NWR)** = pledge finance against stored produce ⭐; Agri Infrastructure Fund; mega food parks (**Srini Food Park, Chittoor — among India's first; Godavari Mega Aqua Park, Bhimavaram**) ⭐.
- **AP specifics:** village-level godowns & procurement points attached to the RBK/village-secretariat network (post-2024 renaming — verify); **Guntur's chilli cold-store cluster (among India's densest)**; aqua cold chains; buffer + PDS interface via AP civil supplies.

## 5. "Others" — rail, air, water, pipelines ⭐ (AP superlatives cluster here)

- **Railways:** **South Coast Railway (SCoR) — India's newest zone, HQ VISAKHAPATNAM (announced 2019; being operationalised — verify)** ⭐⭐; the scenic **Kothavalasa–Kirandul (KK) line** hauls Bailadila iron ore to Vizag port through the Araku hills — **Shimiliguda (~996 m) was India's highest broad-gauge station at its opening** ⭐; freight arteries: Howrah–Chennai trunk, **Obulavaripalle–Venkatachalam link to Krishnapatnam port**, Nadikudi–Srikalahasti (under construction); an East-Coast dedicated freight corridor is proposed.
- **Air:** Visakhapatnam (international; **greenfield Bhogapuram international airport under construction — verify opening**) ⭐, Vijayawada (Gannavaram), Tirupati (international), Rajahmundry, Kadapa, **Kurnool-Orvakal (2021, UDAN)**; UDAN = regional connectivity scheme.
- **Water:** **NW-4 (~2,890 km): Krishna & Godavari stretches + the Kakinada–Puducherry canal chain (incl. the historic BUCKINGHAM CANAL)** ⭐⭐ — Phase-1 Muktyala–Vijayawada stretch developed (cement/aggregate barging); coastal shipping under Sagarmala; AP's canal-era commerce (Cotton's anicuts) is a heritage tie-in.
- **Pipelines:** HPCL's **Vizag–Vijayawada–Secunderabad (VVSPL)** product pipeline; **Kakinada–Bharuch East-West gas pipeline (~1,400 km)**; KG-basin gas grid & city-gas networks.
