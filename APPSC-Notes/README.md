## Exam-craft notes

- **Prelims/Screening:** APPSC repeatedly asks *author–work*, *temple–builder*, *inscription–ruler*, *traveller–ruler*, *title–ruler* matches. Every such pair in these notes is **bolded** — harvest them into flashcards.
- **Mains:** answers score when they (a) open with a periodised thesis line, (b) give 2–3 *specific* pieces of evidence (an inscription, a temple, a text), (c) close with significance/legacy — ideally an **Andhra tie-in** (Vengi, Telugu, Warangal 1323, Annamayya, Dakhni).
- **Spellings:** exam-standard anglicised forms are used (Chalukya, Rashtrakuta, Alauddin) — no diacritics.
- **Dates:** early-medieval dates are conventional; where scholarship disputes a "fact" (e.g., Koh-i-noor from Warangal, sitar invented by Amir Khusrau), the notes flag it — write "tradition holds…" in Mains.
