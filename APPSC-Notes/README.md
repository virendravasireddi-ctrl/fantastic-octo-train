# APPSC Notes — History, Culture & Geography of India and Andhra Pradesh

**Target exams:** APPSC Group-I (Mains – Paper-II: History, Culture & Geography of India and AP) and Group-II (Screening/Mains – History, Culture & AP Geography). Also useful for AP-relevant sections of other state exams.

This series now covers **all three parts of Paper-II end-to-end**:

- **Part A & B — History and Culture of India and AP:** Ancient India (Unit 0, files `00A–00H`) → early-medieval to European advent (Units 1–3, files `01–15`) → **Modern History of AP and India** (Units 5–6, files `21–34`) → an **Ancient & Medieval Andhradesa regional deep-dive** (Unit 7, files `35–38`).
- **Part C — Geography of India and AP:** **Physical** features & resources (Unit 4, files `16–20`) → **Economic, Social, Bio- and Environmental** geography (Units 8–11, files `39–58`).

> **Note on file order:** the physical-geography files keep their original numbers `16–20`; the modern-history block occupies `21–38`; the remaining geography continues at `39–58`. So *history* = `00A–15` + `21–38` and *geography* = `16–20` + `39–58`. Read by **Part** using the units below rather than strictly by number.

## Syllabus items covered

> **Ancient India —** *"Pre-Historic Cultures — Indus Valley — Vedic Culture — Mahajanapadas — Jainism & Buddhism — Magadha & the Mauryas — Ashoka Dharma — Foreign Invasions — The Kushans — The Satavahanas, the Sangam Age, the Sungas, the Guptas, the Kanauj and their contributions — Historical accounts of foreign travellers — Early educational institutions."*

> **Medieval & Early-Modern History —** *"The Pallavas, the Badami Chalukyas, the Eastern Chalukyas, the Rashtrakutas, the Kalyani Chalukyas and the Cholas — Delhi Sultanates — Advent of Islam — Bhakti and Sufi movements — Vernacular languages, scripts, literature, fine arts — the Kakatiyas, the Vijayanagaras, the Bahmanis, the Qutub Shahis — the Mughals — Shivaji and the Marathas — Advent of the Europeans."*

> **Modern History —** *"Rise of the East India Company and its hegemony; changes in administration, social and cultural spheres; role of Christian missionaries; socio-religious reform movements; the national movement (1885–1947), its Andhra dimension and the making of Andhra State / Andhra Pradesh (1953, 1956, 2014)."*

> **Geography —** *"Physical Features and Resources of India and AP — Economic geography (agriculture, livestock, mining, industries, trade & transport) — Social & population geography (population, migration, settlements) — Bio-geography (biodiversity, flora, fauna, wildlife conservation) — Climatology, natural hazards, disaster management and environmental geography."*

## Exam-craft notes

- **Prelims/Screening:** APPSC repeatedly asks *author–work*, *temple–builder*, *inscription–ruler*, *traveller–ruler*, *title–ruler* (and in Geography *mineral–district*, *river–project*, *soil–crop*, *species–sanctuary*) matches. Every such pair is **bolded** — harvest them into flashcards.
- **Mains:** answers score when they (a) open with a periodised thesis line (or, in Geography, a locational/spatial frame), (b) give 2–3 *specific* pieces of evidence, (c) close with significance/legacy — ideally an **Andhra tie-in**.
- **Spellings:** exam-standard anglicised forms are used (Chalukya, Rashtrakuta, Alauddin) — no diacritics.
