## 2. India superlatives & frame numbers

- **Megadiverse**: ~2.4% land, ~7–8% of species; **ZSI (1916)** animals, **BSI (1890)** plants.
- **Biogeography**: **10 zones / 27 provinces** (Rodgers-Panwar); realm = **Oriental** (+ Palaearctic Himalaya); **4 hotspots** (Himalaya, Indo-Burma, Western Ghats–Sri Lanka, Sundaland-Nicobar) — **AP in none** ⭐.
- **PAs (approx., verify):** ~**106 national parks**, ~**570+ sanctuaries**, ~**130+ conservation** & ~**220+ community reserves**, **18 biosphere reserves**, **55+ tiger reserves**, **33 elephant reserves**, **80+ Ramsar sites**.
- **Tigers:** 2006→2022 = **1,411 → 1,706 → 2,226 → 2,967 → ~3,682**; India ~**75%** of world's wild tigers; **MP = most tigers**; **NSTR (AP) = largest reserve by area** ⭐.

## 4. AP endemic / flagship species (Prelims darts) ⭐⭐

- **Plants:** **red sanders** (Seshachalam-endemic, CITES), ***Cycas beddomei*** (Tirumala, CITES App-I), ***Syzygium alternifolium***, *Terminalia pallida*, *Decalepis* (nannari).
- **Birds:** **Great Indian Bustard** (Rollapadu, CR), **Jerdon's courser** (Lankamala, endemic, rediscovered **1986**), spot-billed pelican & painted stork (Nelapattu/Telineelapuram/Uppalapadu), flamingos (Pulicat).
- **Mammals:** **fishing cat** (Coringa), slender loris & tiger (Seshachalam/Nallamala), Indian wolf (Rayalaseema), migratory elephants (Koundinya).
- **Reptiles:** **golden gecko** (Eastern-Ghats endemic), mugger & estuarine crocodile (Krishna/Coringa), **olive ridley** (Godavari–Krishna coast).
