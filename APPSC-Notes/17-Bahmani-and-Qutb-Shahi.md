# PART A — THE BAHMANIS

## A1. Frame

- Founded **1347** by **Alauddin Hasan Bahman Shah ("Hasan Gangu", ex-Zafar Khan)** in revolt against Muhammad bin Tughlaq; capital **GULBARGA (Ahsanabad)**, shifted by Ahmad Shah Wali to **BIDAR (Muhammadabad)** c. 1424. Eighteen sultans; dissolved 1490–1527 into the **five Deccan sultanates**.
- Standing geopolitical fact: endless **Raichur-doab wars with Vijayanagara** — punctuated by marriages (Firoz Shah married **Devaraya I's daughter**, 1407) and constant cultural exchange.
- **Golconda enters history here:** Musunuri **Kapaya Nayaka ceded Golconda fort (c. 1364)** to Muhammad Shah I — along with the turquoise throne (**Takht-i-Firoza**) that became the Bahmani coronation seat ⭐.

## A2. Rulers who matter

| Ruler | Points |
|---|---|
| **Muhammad Shah I** (1358–75) | organised the state: **4 TARAFS (provinces)** — Gulbarga, Daulatabad, Berar, Bidar — under tarafdars; wazir-led central ministries |
| **FIROZ SHAH BAHMANI (1397–1422)** | the learned king: polyglot (Ferishta credits him with Kannada/Telugu too), married a Vijayanagara princess, **inducted Deccani Brahmins into revenue service** ⭐, built **Firuzabad** on the Bhima, planned an **observatory near Daulatabad**; beaten at Pangal (1420) → abdication |
| **AHMAD SHAH "WALI"** (1422–36) | moved capital to **Bidar**; patron of the **Nimatullahi Sufis**; savage Warangal-Rachakonda raids yet venerated as a **saint — his urs at Ashtur is still celebrated by Hindus and Muslims together** ⭐ (syncretism fact) |
| **Muhammad III** (1463–82) with **MAHMUD GAWAN** | the Sultanate's zenith through its Persian wazir (below) |

## A3. MAHMUD GAWAN (wazir 1463–81) — a favourite exam biography ⭐

- Iranian merchant-scholar who rose to **Malik-ut-Tujjar** and chief minister; conquered the west coast (**Goa taken from Vijayanagara, 1472**).
- **Reforms:** subdivided 4 tarafs into **8** (cutting governors down); most forts placed under **central (khalisa) control**; **systematic land survey/measurement** of the kingdom; regularised salaries — a proto-Todar Mal programme.
- **The MADRASA at BIDAR (1472):** three storeys, ~100-ft minarets, Persian tile-work, a **3,000-volume library** — the Deccan's great centre of learning (damaged by a gunpowder blast in Aurangzeb's time). Author of *Riyaz-ul-Insha* and *Manazir-ul-Insha* (epistolography).
- Destroyed by the **DECCANI vs AFAQI (old-comer vs newcomer/Persian) factionalism**: executed in 1481 on a **forged letter**; the remorseful sultan died within a year — and the kingdom broke up within a generation. *Factionalism as the Bahmani fatal flaw = standard Mains line.*
- Russian merchant **ATHANASIUS NIKITIN** (in the Deccan 1469–74, *Voyage Beyond Three Seas*) saw Bidar: nobles "overflowing with wealth", the countryside "naked and poor" — the inequality quotation ⭐.

## A4. Bahmani culture
- **Architecture:** **Gulbarga Jami Masjid (1367** — fully covered prayer-court, unique in India; Persian architect tradition), Haft Gumbaz tombs, **Gesudaraz dargah** (File 12 link); **Bidar fort** (Rangin Mahal's tile & mother-of-pearl work), **Ashtur tombs** (Ahmad Shah's painted interior), Gawan's madrasa — Persia transplanted and Indianised.
- **BIDRIWARE** ⭐ — Bidar's zinc-alloy metalcraft with silver inlay (Persian technique naturalised; today a GI craft) — the applied-arts point for this unit.
- **Dakhni** grows from Gulbarga-Bidar Sufi circles (Gesudaraz) toward court literature (blossoms at Bijapur-Golconda).
- Early **gunpowder-artillery** adoption in the Deccan wars (both sides fielded cannon by Raichur 1520).

## A5. The five successor sultanates (1490–1527)

| Sultanate | Founder | One cultural flag |
|---|---|---|
| **Ahmadnagar (Nizam Shahi)** | Malik Ahmad (1490) | *Tarif-i-Husain Shahi* illustrated ms — early **Deccani painting**; Chand Bibi's defence vs Mughals (1595–1600); Malik Ambar's guerrilla & land settlement (post-period) |
| **Bijapur (Adil Shahi)** | Yusuf Adil Khan (1490) | **Ibrahim Adil Shah II's *KITAB-I-NAURAS*** (song-book invoking Saraswati & Ganapati! — "Jagadguru Badshah"), **Gol Gumbaz** (world-class dome, whispering gallery, 1656), **FERISHTA wrote his *Tarikh* (Gulshan-i-Ibrahimi) here** ⭐ |
| **GOLCONDA (QUTB SHAHI)** | Sultan Quli (1518) | Part B below |
| **Berar (Imad Shahi)** | Fathullah Imad-ul-Mulk | absorbed by Ahmadnagar 1574 |
| **Bidar (Barid Shahi)** | Qasim Barid line | absorbed by Bijapur 1619 |

# PART B — THE QUTB SHAHIS OF GOLCONDA (1518–1687) ⭐⭐ APPSC core

## B1. Frame

| Ruler | Years | Memorise |
|---|---|---|
| **Sultan Quli Qutb-ul-Mulk** | 1518–43 | Turkoman (Qara-Qoyunlu descent) governor of Telangana from 1496; independent on the Bahmani collapse; rebuilt **GOLCONDA** (the old Kakatiya "shepherd's-hill" fort ceded in 1364) in granite; murdered at ~90 by son Jamsheed |
| **IBRAHIM QUTB SHAH** | 1550–80 | **"MALKIBHARAMA" of the Telugu poets** ⭐ — spent **7 years' exile at Vijayanagara** under Aliya Rama Raya, returned a Telugu-phile; joined the 1565 Talikota coalition, then absorbed coastal forts (Kondavidu etc.); **Hussain Sagar (1562, by the saint-engineer Hussain Shah Wali)**, Ibrahimpatnam tank, Golconda's great walls |
| **MUHAMMAD QULI QUTB SHAH** | 1580–1612 | **founded HYDERABAD (1591)** on the Musi — planner **Mir Momin Astarabadi** (Isfahan model); **CHARMINAR (1591)**, Char Kaman, Jami Masjid (1597), **Badshahi Ashurkhana (1594)**; the **first substantial DIWAN (collected poems) in Urdu/Dakhni by any poet** ⭐; the **Bhagmati/"Bhagnagar"** story — flag as tradition |
| Muhammad Qutb Shah | 1612–26 | **Mecca Masjid begun** (completed by Aurangzeb, 1694) |
| **ABDULLAH QUTB SHAH** | 1626–72 | **1636 Deed of Submission** to Shah Jahan (tribute, khutba); mother **HAYAT BAKSHI BEGUM ("Ma Saheba")** the dynasty's power-woman ⭐; **Mir Jumla** (diamond-magnate wazir) defected to Aurangzeb → 1656 first Mughal siege; **"Golden Firman" (1632) to the English East India Company** — free trade for 500 pagodas/year ⭐ |
| **ABUL HASAN "TANA SHAH"** | 1672–87 | Sufi-raised last sultan; Brahmin ministers **MADANNA (chief minister, "Surya Prakasha Rao") & AKKANNA** ⭐; **alliance with SHIVAJI (1677** Hyderabad visit — subsidy + joint Karnatak expedition); Madanna-Akkanna murdered (1686) under Mughal pressure; **AURANGZEB'S 8-MONTH SIEGE OF GOLCONDA (Jan–Sep 1687)** — fell by the treachery of Abdullah Pani; the loyal **Abdul Razzaq Lari**, wounded ~70 times, was honoured even by Aurangzeb ⭐; Tana Shah died a prisoner at Daulatabad (1699); Golconda → Mughal Deccan → (1724) Asaf Jahi Hyderabad |

## B2. Socio-cultural conditions — the composite "Hyderabadi" matrix ⭐

- **Persianate court + Telugu country:** Persian/Dakhni at court, **Telugu in revenue & village records**; **Niyogi Brahmin secretariat** rising to the very top (Madanna-Akkanna) — a Muslim state administered substantially in Telugu.
- **Economy of world rank:** **GOLCONDA DIAMONDS — the KOLLUR mine (Krishna bank)**: Koh-i-noor (tradition), Hope, Pitt — **Jean-Baptiste TAVERNIER** (six voyages, 1630s–60s) counted tens of thousands of workers at Kollur ⭐; **MACHILIPATNAM (Masulipatnam)** as the sultanate's world-port — **Dutch factory 1605, ENGLISH FACTORY 1611 (their first on the Coromandel)**, French 1669; exports: diamonds, **kalamkari/chintz, muslins**; the "Masula" surf-boats.
- **Religious accommodation:** Muharram grew into the folk **"PEERLA PANDUGA"** observed by Hindu villagers across Telangana-Rayalaseema ⭐; ashurkhanas endowed; temple grants continued (Bhadrachalam!); saint-cults shared.
- **Women:** Hayat Bakshi Begum — de facto regent across three reigns, builder (Hayat Bakshi mosque, Hayathnagar sarai).

## B3. Language & literature

### Telugu under the Qutb Shahis ⭐ (the "Muslim dynasty patronising Telugu" question)
| Poet | Work | Patron link |
|---|---|---|
| **Addanki Gangadhara** | *Tapati Samvaranopakhyanamu* | **dedicated to Ibrahim Qutb Shah** ("Malkibharama") |
| **Kandukuru Rudrakavi** | *Nirankusopakhyanamu*; *Sugriva Vijayamu* (an early **yakshagana**) | Ibrahim's circle |
| **Ponnaganti Telaganarya** | ***Yayati Charitramu* — the first ACCHA-TELUGU (pure-Telugu, Sanskrit-free) kavya** ⭐ | dedicated to **Amin Khan**, Ibrahim's noble |
| **Kshetrayya of Movva** | Muvva-Gopala **padams** | sang at Abdullah's Golconda (and Thanjavur) |
| **Kancherla Gopanna "BHADRACHALA RAMADASU"** | *Dasarathi Satakam*, Rama **kirtanas** | Madanna's kinsman; tahsildar of Palvancha; **built the Bhadrachalam temple (c. 1674)** from revenue → ~12 years in Golconda prison → released by Tana Shah (the Rama-paying-the-gold "**Ram tanka**" legend — flag as devotional tradition) ⭐⭐ |
| **KUCHIPUDI charter** | — | **Tana Shah granted Kuchipudi village (Krishna dist.) as an agraharam to the Bhagavatulu dance-brahmins (1678)** — the royal deed behind AP's classical dance ⭐⭐ |

### Dakhni & Persian
- **Mullah WAJHI**: *Qutb-Mushtari* (1609, masnavi for Muhammad Quli) and ***SAB RAS* (1636) — the first great Dakhni/Urdu PROSE work** ⭐; **Ghawwasi** (*Saif-ul-Mulk-o-Badi-uj-Jamal*, Dakhni *Tuti-nama*) under Abdullah; **Ibn Nishati** (*Phulban*). Remember the claim: **Urdu poetry's first flowering was in the DECCAN (Golconda-Bijapur), not Delhi**.
- Persian chancellery, histories, and the Shia liturgical culture (marsiyas) of the ashurkhanas.

## B4. Art & architecture — Qutb Shahi style (Persian form + Deccan stucco + Telugu craftsmen)

- **Golconda fort:** acoustic signalling (the Fateh Darwaza clap heard at Bala Hissar), water-lift systems, Ibrahim's ramparts.
- **Hyderabad set:** **CHARMINAR** (1591 — plague-thanksgiving per tradition; four 56-m minarets over a crossroads), Char Kaman, Jami Masjid, **Mecca Masjid** (Qutb Shahi begun, Mughal finished), **Badshahi Ashurkhana** (Persian tile mosaic), **Toli Masjid (1671)**.
- **QUTB SHAHI TOMBS (Ibrahim Bagh necropolis):** all seven-plus sultans under bulbous domes on stucco-laced cubes — the most complete royal necropolis in India; **Hussain Sagar** & tank-garden urbanism.
- **Painting & crafts:** **GOLCONDA school of Deccani miniature** (jewel palette, gold, portraits & ragamalas); **KALAMKARI's two schools patronised through Golconda trade — Machilipatnam-Pedana (block-printed, Persianised export "chintz/palampore") and Srikalahasti (freehand pen, temple narratives)** ⭐⭐; **Warangal durries/carpets**; muslin weaving.

## Prelims nuggets

1. 1347 Hasan Gangu; Gulbarga → Bidar (Ahmad Shah Wali); 4 tarafs (Muhammad I) → 8 (Gawan).
2. Golconda + Takht-i-Firoza ceded by Kapaya Nayaka, c. 1364.
3. Firoz Bahmani: VJN marriage 1407; Brahmins in revenue; Firuzabad; observatory plan.
4. **Gawan: Malik-ut-Tujjar; Goa 1472; Bidar madrasa 1472 (3,000 books); executed 1481 (forged letter; Deccani-Afaqi feud)**; Nikitin's Bidar testimony.
5. Five sultanates: Ahmadnagar-Bijapur-Golconda-Berar-Bidar; Ferishta at Bijapur; Kitab-i-Nauras — Ibrahim Adil Shah II; Gol Gumbaz 1656.
6. Sultan Quli independent 1518; Ibrahim = Malkibharama (7-year Vijayanagara exile).
7. **Hyderabad 1591; Charminar 1591; planner Mir Momin; Muhammad Quli = first Urdu diwan**.
8. Hussain Sagar 1562 (Hussain Shah Wali); Mecca Masjid completed by Aurangzeb 1694.
9. Abdullah: 1636 submission deed; **Golden Firman 1632 to the English**; Masulipatnam factories — Dutch 1605, English 1611.
10. Tavernier & Kollur diamonds; Koh-i-noor tradition.
11. Tana Shah: Madanna & Akkanna; Shivaji pact 1677; fall of Golconda Sep 1687 (Abdullah Pani's treachery; Abdul Razzaq Lari's loyalty).
12. **Ramadasu — Bhadrachalam c. 1674, prison, kirtanas; Kuchipudi agraharam grant 1678**.
13. Telugu at Golconda: Gangadhara (Tapati-Samvarana → Ibrahim), Telaganarya (Yayati Charitram = first accha-Telugu kavya → Amin Khan), Rudrakavi (Sugriva Vijayam yakshagana).
14. Wajhi: Qutb-Mushtari 1609 (verse) + Sab Ras 1636 (first Dakhni prose classic).
15. Bidriware — Bidar; Kalamkari — Machilipatnam (print) vs Srikalahasti (pen).

## Mains angles

1. **"The Bahmani kingdom was the Deccan's bridge and its battlefield."** Persian immigration + Vijayanagara wars; Gawan's reforms vs Deccani-Afaqi rot; verdict — it institutionalised the Indo-Persian Deccan that its five heirs perfected.
2. **"Mahmud Gawan's career explains both the rise and the ruin of the Bahmanis."** Merchant-wazir's reforms/madrasa vs factional execution — one biography as state autopsy.
3. **"The Qutb Shahis were a Telugu dynasty in Persian dress."** Evidence: Malkibharama's poets, accha-Telugu kavya dedicated in a Muslim court, Telugu revenue machinery to Madanna-Akkanna, Bhadrachalam & Kuchipudi grants; counterpoint: Shia-Persianate high culture — synthesis, not either/or.
4. **"Hyderabad (1591) was the Deccan's planned cosmopolis."** Isfahan model, Charminar crossroads, ashurkhana civic religion, diamond-and-chintz world trade via Masulipatnam; close with 1687 and the Asaf Jahi inheritance.
5. **"Account for the growth of Dakhni literature at Golconda-Bijapur before Urdu rose at Delhi."** Sufi vernacularism (Gesudaraz) + court patronage (Muhammad Quli, Wajhi, Ghawwasi) + distance from Persian-purist Delhi.
