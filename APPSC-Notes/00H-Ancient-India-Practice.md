## 1. The source typology (lead every answer with it) ⭐

| Source type | Gives you | Systematic limit |
|---|---|---|
| **Archaeology** (tools, cities, aDNA) | Material culture, chronology, economy | Rarely names, motives, politics |
| **Inscriptions** (edicts, prashastis, grants) | Official self-image, firm dates | Eulogy, donor-bias |
| **Coins** | Chronology, kings, trade, religion | Early issues hard to attribute |
| **Texts** (Veda, canons, Arthashastra, Puranas, Sangam) | Ideals, doctrine, prescription | Later redaction; elite bias |
| **Foreign accounts** | External check, vivid detail | Outsider error & purpose-bias |

## 2. Historiography — schools & set-piece debates

- **Orientalist/colonial** — Jones, **Prinsep** (cracked Brahmi 1837), **Marshall**, V. Smith: recovered the past, but framed India static/despotic ("civilisation from the west" — rejected).
- **Nationalist** — Bhandarkar, Raychaudhuri, Majumdar, Nilakanta Sastri, **K. P. Jayaswal** (*Hindu Polity* — the republics): answered denigration; risk = glorification.
- **Marxist/materialist** — **D. D. Kosambi, R. S. Sharma, Romila Thapar**, Irfan Habib: modes of production; the **iron→surplus→state→varna→heterodoxy** spine and the **land-grant "feudalism"** thesis are theirs.
- **Subaltern/social** — the excluded (women, shudras, tribes); Thapar's lineage-to-state.
- **Indigenist/OIT ("Sarasvati") school** — politically charged; handle with the 00B balance.

**Debates index:** Aryan origins (00B) · Harappan polity/script & decline (00A) · Mauryan art borrowing & Mauryan decline (00D) · Shunga "persecution" (00E) · Satavahana homeland/varna (00F) · the 78 CE era problem (00E) · Gupta golden-age & feudalism (00G) · foreign accounts as evidence (00G).

## 6. Ten Mains questions with skeletons

1. **Harappan urban planning (2020).** Grid + citadel/lower town → 4:2:1 bricks → water-sanitation genius → weights/standardisation → faceless polity caveat.
2. **Origin & expansion of the IVC (2025).** Mehrgarh-rooted indigenous origin → Early-Harappan coalescence → 1.5 mn sq km spread → trade-standardisation glue → regional domains qualification.
3. **Harappan survival & continuity (2023).** Late-Harappan rural continuity + living survivals table → the unread-script caveat and the politicisation warning.
4. **Early vs Later Vedic.** The master table + iron-eastward causal sentence + sacrifice-as-statecraft → Upanishadic bridge.
5. **Causes of Buddhism's emergence (2020) / teachings of Buddha & Mahavira (2025).** Four causes with ahimsa's economic logic; or doctrine cores + contrast table.
6. **Magadhan imperialism.** Five-point convergence + Bimbisara→Nanda run-up + why gana-sanghas lost.
7. **Buddhism vs Ashoka's Dhamma (2012).** Overlap vs deliberate ecumenism (RE XII; Barabar-to-Ajivikas) → impact on society and the state ideal.
8. **Mauryan administration from three sources.** Arthashastra (prescriptive) × Megasthenes (external, garbled) × edicts (contemporary) — the triangulation IS the answer.
9. **Satavahanas: Deccan bridge and Andhra matrix.** Rulers' arc → land-grant seed of feudalism → Roman trade/guilds → Prakrit + Amaravati school → Ikshvaku succession.
10. **The Gupta "golden age — for whom?" / the travellers question (2023).** Balance-sheet with sources; or the three-pilgrim table + Buddhist-lens critique.
