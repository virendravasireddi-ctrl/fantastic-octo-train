## 1. Vedic culture (c. 1500–600 BCE)

**The pivot:** after Harappan de-urbanisation, north India is for a millennium **rural, pastoral-then-agrarian, non-literate**, organised around Sanskrit oral texts and sacrifice. The only substantial source is the **Vedic corpus itself** — literature cross-checked against archaeology.

### 1.1 Sources — and their bias
- **Four Vedas:** **Rigveda** (hymns — oldest), Samaveda (melodies), Yajurveda (formulae), Atharvaveda (spells/folk religion); each layers as **Samhita → Brahmana → Aranyaka → Upanishad**. Around them: **six Vedangas** (shiksha, kalpa, vyakarana, nirukta, chhandas, jyotisha) and four **Upavedas**; the **Gayatri mantra** (to Savitr) is Rigvedic.
- **Oral transmission (shruti)** by and for a priestly elite → we see ritual and the varna worldview in high resolution, the labouring majority barely at all.
- Archaeological correlates: **PGW (Painted Grey Ware)** ≈ Later Vedic doab; OCP/Copper Hoards earlier — **the text-to-pottery equation is inference, flag it**.

### 1.2 Early vs Later Vedic — THE exam table ⭐⭐

| Theme | Early/Rigvedic (c. 1500–1000 BCE) | Later Vedic (c. 1000–600 BCE) |
|---|---|---|
| Geography | **Sapta-Sindhu** (Punjab, Sarasvati) | Eastward to the **Ganga-Yamuna doab** (Kuru-Panchala) |
| Economy | **Pastoral** — cattle = wealth (*gavishti* = war, lit. "search for cows") | **Agrarian** — **iron (krishna-ayas)** + plough → surplus |
| Polity | Tribal **jana**; rajan checked by **Sabha & Samiti** (+ older Vidatha); bali voluntary | Territorial **janapada**; hereditary king magnified by **rajasuya, ashvamedha, vajapeya**; **ratnins**; assemblies decline; bali/bhaga become dues |
| Society | Fluid; varna emerging — **Purushasukta (RV 10.90, latest book)** | **Rigid varna**, Brahmin-Kshatriya supremacy; untouchability germinating; **jati** forming |
| Women | Better: seers **Lopamudra, Ghosha, Apala**; assembly presence | Decline — excluded from assemblies and upanayana |
| Religion | Nature gods — **Indra** (most hymns), **Agni** (2nd), **Varuna (rita — cosmic order)**, Soma; hymn & simple sacrifice; no temples/images | Ritualism triumphant — **Prajapati** rises; costly priest-run yajna; the **Upanishadic reaction** begins (atman-brahman, karma, moksha) |

### 1.3 Mechanisms (facts → analysis)
- **The master sentence: iron + the eastward move turned a cattle-herding tribe into a taxable agrarian kingdom** — iron cleared the forested doab, plough made surplus, surplus fed kings, priests and stratification.
- **Sacrifice as statecraft:** ashvamedha/rajasuya = **legitimation technology** — converting chief into territorial king, consuming surplus, cementing the Brahmin-Kshatriya alliance.
- **Varna hardens from function to birth** — the grievance base the 6th century BCE would mobilise (File 00C).
- Concepts crystallising: **rita → dharma; purusharthas; ashramas; three rinas; samskaras; gotra**.

### 1.4 The Aryan debate (balance = marks)
- Open with: **"Aryan" is a linguistic-cultural category, not a race.**
- Migration case: Indo-European kinship (Sanskrit-Avestan-Greek-Latin), horse & spoked chariot, **Boghazkoy inscription** (c. 1400 BCE, Vedic gods in Anatolia). Indigenist (OIT) case: Sarasvati prominence, continuity claims.
- Measured position: **slow migration + admixture, not invasion**; **Rakhigarhi aDNA** shows steppe-related gene flow in the 2nd millennium BCE *and* deep Harappan continuity. Reject the massacre model; note the debate is politically charged.

## 2. The Mahajanapadas & the Second Urbanisation (c. 600 BCE)

**What changes:** cities return (first since Harappa), **punch-marked silver coins (karshapanas)** appear, writing revives (**Brahmi**; **Kharoshthi** in the north-west), and janapadas consolidate into **16 mahajanapadas** (listed in the Buddhist **Anguttara Nikaya**; also the Jaina Bhagavati Sutra).

- **Material base:** iron intensifies in the middle Ganga (**NBPW** — the lustrous deluxe pottery), burnt-brick houses, ring-wells → surplus → trade → **merchants (shresthins, gahapatis)**. Caution: punch-marked coins carry **symbols, not legends** — they prove a monetised guild economy, not a named king.

| Kasi | Varanasi | Monarchy |
| Anga | Champa | Monarchy (absorbed early) |

### 2.2 The gana-sanghas — collective rule and its limits
- **How they worked:** sovereignty in an **assembly (santhagara)** of Kshatriya family-heads (each a "raja"), voting by **shalaka** (ballot-sticks) with movers and tellers; elected chief presiding. The Buddha admired Vajji concord (**seven aparihaniya-dhammas**).
- **The trap to dodge:** these were **aristocratic oligarchies, not democracies** — women, shudras, dasas excluded.
- **Why they lost:** factionalism (Ajatashatru's minister **Vassakara** split the Licchavis by intrigue), no unified command, weaker fiscal-military concentration than monarchy. Survivors were finally crushed under Samudragupta. (Rehabilitated for moderns by **K. P. Jayaswal's *Hindu Polity***.)

## 3. The rise of Magadha ⭐ (a set-piece answer)

**Why Magadha won** — the classic five-point convergence:
1. **Geography:** Rajagriha ringed by five hills; **Pataliputra** at the Ganga-Son-Gandak confluence — a "water-fort" taxing river trade.
2. **Iron** mines of south Bihar → superior weapons and tools.
3. **Fertile middle Ganga** → surplus → armies.
4. **War-elephants** from eastern forests (noted by the Greeks).
5. **Ruthless, ambitious rulers** — plus river-trade revenue.

**The run-up dynasties:**
- **Haryanka:** **Bimbisara** (c. 544–492 BCE) — conquest (Anga) + marriage alliances (Kosala, Licchavi, Madra); contemporary of the Buddha & Mahavira. **Ajatashatru** (492–460) — annexed Kasi and broke the **Vajji confederacy** (new engines: *rathamusala*, *mahashilakantaka*); fortified Pataligrama. **Udayin** founded **Pataliputra**.
- **Shishunaga:** crushed the last rival, **Avanti**.
- **Nanda:** **Mahapadma Nanda** — low-born (Shudra) "first empire-builder", styled *Ekarat/Sarva-kshatrantaka*; vast army & treasury (the "Gangaridai/Prasii" that scared Alexander's men); **Dhana Nanda's** unpopularity set the stage for Chandragupta (File 00D).

## 4. Prelims nuggets

1. Veda layers: **Samhita → Brahmana → Aranyaka → Upanishad**; Gayatri — **Rigveda (to Savitr)**.
2. **Indra** = most Rigvedic hymns; Agni second; **rita** = cosmic order (Varuna).
3. **Purushasukta — RV 10.90 (latest book)** — first varna statement.
4. Women seers: **Lopamudra, Ghosha, Apala**; assemblies: **Sabha, Samiti, Vidatha**; Later Vedic **ratnins**.
5. **Iron = krishna-ayas**; PGW ≈ Later Vedic (an inference).
6. **Boghazkoy (c. 1400 BCE)** — Vedic gods named in Anatolia; Rakhigarhi aDNA — steppe + Harappan continuity.
7. 16 mahajanapadas — **Anguttara Nikaya**; **Assaka = only trans-Vindhyan, on the Godavari** ⭐.
8. Republics: **Vajji (Vaishali, Licchavis)**, Malla, Kamboja; Shakyas & Koliyas = the Buddha's world.
9. Second urbanisation markers: **NBPW + punch-marked karshapanas + Brahmi/Kharoshthi**.
10. **Vassakara** — Magadhan minister who split the Licchavis; *Hindu Polity* — **K. P. Jayaswal**.
11. Magadha sequence: **Bimbisara → Ajatashatru → Udayin (Pataliputra) → Shishunaga (Avanti) → Mahapadma Nanda**.
12. First rulers' epithets: Bimbisara's policy = conquest + matrimony; Mahapadma = **Shudra "Ekarat"**.
