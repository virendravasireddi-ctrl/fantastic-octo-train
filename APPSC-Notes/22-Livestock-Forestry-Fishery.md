## 1. Livestock

### 1.1 India frame (20th Livestock Census, 2019 — 21st census fieldwork 2024–25, verify results)

| Item | Fact |
|---|---|
| Total livestock | ≈ **536 million** — world's largest cattle+buffalo herd |
| Cattle | ≈ 193 m — **West Bengal > UP > MP** (exotic/crossbred share rising) |
| Buffalo | ≈ 110 m — **UP #1**; India = world's largest buffalo population |
| **Sheep** | ≈ 74 m — **Telangana #1, AP #2** ⭐ (Rayalaseema–Prakasam herding belt) |
| Goat | ≈ 149 m — Rajasthan #1 ("poor man's cow") |
| Poultry | ≈ 852 m — Tamil Nadu & AP lead; commercial belt in the south |
| Milk | ≈ **230–240 mt — India world #1 since 1998 (~24% of world)**; UP #1 state; per-capita availability ~460 g/day |
| Eggs | India among the **world's top-2 producers**; **AP = India's No. 1 egg state (~20%)** ⭐⭐ (Godavari-districts layer belt) |
| Share | Livestock ≈ **30% of agricultural GVA**, ~4.5–5% of total GVA — bigger than many "industries" |

### 1.2 White Revolution & policy

- **Operation Flood (1970–96, three phases)** — **NDDB (Anand, 1965), Dr Verghese Kurien**; the **Amul/Anand pattern**: village cooperative → district union → state federation; milk grid + "flood" of liquid milk to metros. India moved from milk deficit to world #1 ⭐.
- Current: **Rashtriya Gokul Mission** (indigenous breeds, Gokul Grams, sex-sorted semen), National Livestock Mission, **NADCP** (FMD + brucellosis mass vaccination), Animal Husbandry Infrastructure Development Fund, mobile veterinary units, KCC for animal husbandry.

**Mains line:** in drought-prone Rayalaseema, small ruminants + poultry are the real drought insurance; livestock income is counter-cyclical to rainfed cropping.

## 2. Forestry as an economic activity

*(Physical/ecological forest geography → File 20; here the syllabus word is "forestry" = the production economy.)*

- **Contribution:** forestry & logging ≈ 1.3% of GVA (recorded — undervalues fuelwood/fodder/NTFP subsistence use). Products: timber, **fuelwood (still the biggest use by volume)**, bamboo ("green gold"), pulpwood, and **NTFP/Minor Forest Produce** — tendu/beedi leaf, bamboo, tamarind, honey, **gum karaya (AP agency speciality)** ⭐, adda leaf (plates), medicinal plants, hill brooms.
- **Girijan Cooperative Corporation (GCC, est. 1956, HQ Visakhapatnam)** ⭐⭐ — AP's tribal cooperative monopoly for NTFP procurement + fair-price shops in agency areas; markets **Araku coffee**, honey, gum karaya. Pairs with **TRIFED**'s national **MSP-for-MFP scheme** and **Van Dhan Vikas Kendras (2018)**.
- **Joint Forest Management:** AP's **Vana Samrakshana Samithis (VSS)** — community protection-cum-benefit-sharing; podu regularisation via **FRA 2006** titles (agency current affairs ⭐).
- **Farm forestry:** the **subabul–casuarina–eucalyptus pulpwood belt of Prakasam–Guntur–Godavari districts is one of India's largest farm-forestry economies** ⭐ — feeds paper mills (File 24); coastal casuarina doubles as cyclone shelterbelt.
- **Red sanders economics:** endemic Seshachalam timber (File 20) auctioned by the state (e-auctions to East Asia) — a forest-revenue + smuggling-enforcement story (RSASTF).
- **Bamboo:** National Bamboo Mission; bamboo removed from "tree" definition (IFA amendment 2017) to free farm bamboo ⭐.

## 3. Fishery ⭐⭐ (2020 PYQ: "fisheries management and development in India")

### 3.1 Resource base and status

| Item | Fact (label the year, quote "about") |
|---|---|
| Coastline | **7,516.6 km (classic figure); ~11,099 km per the 2023–25 re-survey** ⭐ — cite either with its source |
| EEZ | **2.02 million sq km**; continental shelf ~0.53 m sq km; ~3,470+ marine fishing villages |
| Production | ≈ **175 lakh t (2022-23) → ~184 lakh t (2023-24, prov.)**; **inland ≈ 75% : marine ≈ 25%** (a historic reversal — marine dominated till the 1990s) ⭐ |
| World rank | **2nd-largest fish producer (~8% of global output; some older rankings say 3rd), 2nd in aquaculture**, top exporter of **frozen shrimp** |
| Economy | ≈ 1.1% of GVA, ~6.5–7% of agricultural GVA; ~3 crore livelihoods; "**sunrise sector**" (double-digit growth) |
| Exports | ≈ **US$ 7–8 bn**; frozen shrimp ≈ ⅔ of value; USA & China top markets |

### 3.2 Development — the policy ladder (use as answer spine)

1. **FFDAs (1970s–80s)** — Fish Farmers' Development Agencies popularised **composite carp culture** (catla-rohu-mrigal); BFDAs for brackish water.
2. **Blue Revolution / Neel Kranti Mission (2015-16)** — umbrella restructuring, ₹3,000 cr.
3. **FIDF (2018-19)** — ₹7,522 cr Fisheries & Aquaculture Infrastructure Development Fund.
4. **PMMSY (2020, ₹20,050 cr — largest-ever fisheries scheme)** ⭐⭐ — targets: production 22 mt by 2024-25, exports ₹1 lakh cr, 55 lakh jobs, cut post-harvest loss 25%→~10%; sub-scheme **PM-MKSSY (2024)** for formalisation of micro-enterprises.
5. Enablers: separate **Ministry of Fisheries, AH & Dairying (2019)**, KCC for fishers, insurance (GAIS), sagar mitras, seaweed park (TN), deep-sea fishing vessels.

**Institution match-pairs ⭐:** CMFRI–Kochi (marine research), CIFA–Bhubaneswar (freshwater aquaculture), CIBA–Chennai (brackish), CIFRI–Barrackpore (riverine), **NFDB — Hyderabad (2006)**, **MPEDA — Kochi (export promotion)**, Fishery Survey of India — Mumbai, **Coastal Aquaculture Authority (CAA Act 2005, amended 2023)**.

### 3.3 Management (the half candidates forget)

- **Marine regulation:** state **Marine Fishing Regulation Acts** (AP MFRA 1994) — mesh-size limits, zoning for artisanal vs mechanised craft; **61-day uniform monsoon fishing ban** (east coast Apr 15–Jun 14); turtle-excluder devices (Gahirmatha/olive ridley); MPAs.
- **EEZ & beyond:** deep-sea fishing policy, monitoring-control-surveillance, transponders on vessels; **WTO fisheries-subsidies negotiations** (India defends small-scale fishers) ⭐.
- **Aquaculture regulation:** CAA licensing, antibiotic-residue (MRL) testing — US/EU rejections are the recurring risk; disease surveillance (white-spot, EHP).
- **Quality & welfare:** MPEDA/EIC certification, e-traceability; saving-cum-relief during ban months; harbours & landing-centre hygiene.
- **Challenges checklist:** overfishing & juvenile catch, trawl bycatch, climate change (warming, cyclones, sardine shifts), pollution, credit & middlemen, price crashes, import-country tariff shocks.

### 3.4 Andhra Pradesh — India's aqua powerhouse ⭐⭐ (every AP answer needs 3 of these)

- **No. 1 fish-producing state: ~50+ lakh t ≈ 30% of India's fish** (2022-23); **~70%+ of India's farmed shrimp (L. vannamei)**; fish + shrimp dominate AP's export basket — **AP is India's largest seafood-exporting state (~⅓ of export value)** ⭐⭐.
- **Geography of the boom:** freshwater carp belt around **Kolleru (Bhimavaram–Akiveedu–Undi, W. Godavari/Eluru)** — the "**aqua hub of India**"; brackish shrimp along Krishna–Guntur–Bapatla–Prakasam–Nellore coasts; **hatcheries concentrated at Nellore–Muthukur**; processing-export clusters at Bhimavaram & Visakhapatnam (Vizag among India's top seafood ports).
- **Why AP won:** deltaic water + warm climate, early conversion of salinity-hit paddy to ponds, vannamei adoption after 2009, feed mills, electricity subsidy for aqua, road access to Vizag/Krishnapatnam ports.
- **State institutions:** **AP State Aquaculture Development Authority (APSADA, 2020)** — first of its kind (licensing & zoning); **AP Fisheries University, Pulivendula (2020)** ⭐; "Fish Andhra" retail push; aqua labs at village level.
- **Fishing harbours programme:** phased construction — **Juvvaladinne (Nellore, completed), Nizampatnam (Bapatla), Machilipatnam, Uppada (Kakinada), Budagatlapalem (Srikakulam), Pudimadaka (Anakapalli)** etc., alongside the existing **Visakhapatnam fishing harbour (one of India's largest)** — verify current status list.
- **Risks to flag in Mains:** disease + antibiotic MRL rejections, feed-cost squeeze, **export-market concentration (US tariff shocks of 2025 hit AP shrimp hard)** ⭐, Kolleru lake encroachment vs livelihoods (+5 contour issue), power subsidies' fiscal weight, climate (cyclones, heat-driven pond oxygen crashes).
- **PYQ-ready close:** PMMSY + APSADA + harbours = harvest-to-market infrastructure; diversification (species, markets, domestic consumption) is AP aqua's next frontier.

## 4. One-paragraph synthesis (Mains opener you can reuse)

India's allied sector — livestock, forestry produce and fisheries — now grows faster than crop agriculture and cushions farm incomes against monsoon risk; in AP the pattern is extreme: the state leads India in eggs, fish and shrimp, its NTFP economy sustains the agency tribes through the GCC, and allied activities contribute nearly half of agricultural GVA — making "agriculture policy" in AP inseparable from ponds, poultry sheds and forests.
