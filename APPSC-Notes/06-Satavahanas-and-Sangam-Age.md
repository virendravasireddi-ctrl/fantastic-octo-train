## 1. The Satavahanas (c. 230 BCE / 1st c. BCE – c. 220s CE) ⭐⭐⭐

**For APPSC the single most important ancient dynasty** — the **first great empire of the Deccan**, the **bridge between the Mauryan north and the Tamil south**, and the **founding matrix of Andhra history** (tested in BOTH the all-India and the Andhra halves of the paper: agrarian system 2020, socio-religious conditions 2023, origins of the Andhras 2025).

### 1.1 Quick facts & the debates

| Item | Detail |
|---|---|
| Names | **Puranas: "Andhras/Andhra-bhrityas"; their own inscriptions: "Satavahana"** — the identity question itself is an exam point |
| Founder / capital | **Simuka** (rose as Mauryan power collapsed; per the Puranas overthrew the Kanvas); capital **Pratishthana (Paithan) on the Godavari** |
| Homeland debate | Earliest inscriptions are **western** (Naneghat, Nasik) vs the Puranic **"Andhra"** label and later eastern centres — *unsettled; they ended ruling both* |
| Varna debate | Claimed Brahmin status (**"eka-bamhana"**) yet read as an indigenous Deccan people **Sanskritised into Brahminhood** — manufactured status (the theme the Telugu-country essay carries forward) |

### 1.2 The rulers who matter
- **Satakarni I** — first great expander; his queen **NAGANIKA's NANEGHAT inscription** records **ashvamedha & rajasuya** with huge fees — legitimation by Vedic sacrifice ⭐.
- **HALA** — the poet-king; compiled the **GATHASAPTASHATI (Sattasai)** — 700 Prakrit (Maharashtri) love-lyrics; **Gunadhya's Brihatkatha** (Paishachi, lost) tied to his court.
- **⭐ GAUTAMIPUTRA SATAKARNI (c. 106–130 CE)** — the greatest. The **NASIK PRASHASTI of his mother GAUTAMI BALASHRI**: destroyer of **Shakas, Yavanas, Pahlavas**; crusher of the Kshatrapa **NAHAPANA** — proved physically by the **Jogalthambi hoard of restruck Nahapana coins** ⭐; "eka-brahmana", stopper of **varna-samkara**.
- **Vasishthiputra Pulumavi** — pushed east into the **Krishna-Godavari Andhra country**; **Amaravati/Dhanyakataka** rises as second centre.
- **Yajna Sri Satakarni** — last strong king; **SHIP-TYPE COINS = maritime power** ⭐. Then 3rd-century fragmentation → **Ikshvakus, Abhiras, Chutus, early Pallavas**.
- **The Shaka duel** (the spine of their politics): Nahapana's gains → Gautamiputra's reconquest → **Rudradaman's** counter-recovery (Junagadh boasts of twice defeating, but sparing, a "lord of Dakshinapatha" — a kinsman by marriage). War and matrimony ran together.

### 1.3 State, economy, society, religion
- **Administration:** loosened Mauryan template — provinces (**aharas**) under amatyas; graded feudatory nobility (**raja, maharathi, mahabhoja, mahasenapati**).
- **⭐ THE analytical point: the FIRST royal LAND GRANTS with fiscal-administrative immunities** to Brahmins and Buddhist monks (Nasik, Karle) — **R. S. Sharma's "seed of Indian feudalism"** — the thread that runs to the Gupta agraharas (07) and the medieval grant economy (§2).
- **Economy:** hinge of **Indo-Roman trade** (*Periplus* ports — **Kalyana, Sopara, Bharukachchha/Broach**); Roman gold inflow; **guilds (shrenis) as bankers** — Nasik records cash endowments deposited at fixed interest ⭐; coins mostly **lead and potin**.
- **Society — the metronymic puzzle:** **Gautami-putra, Vasishthi-putra** — naming by the **mother's gotra** = prominence of royal women (Naganika, Balashri as donors), **NOT matriarchy** (succession stayed patrilineal — likely distinguishing sons of a polygamous house).
- **Religion — the double patronage:** **Vedic sacrifices (Naneghat) + the greatest patronage of Buddhist stupas and caves** — no contradiction: **royal legitimation was plural**.
- **Language:** **Prakrit was the official language** (a deliberate contrast with the Sanskrit of their Shaka rivals) — the Deccan's Prakrit golden age.

### 1.4 Art — the Amaravati school ⭐⭐ & the western caves
- **AMARAVATI school** (Krishna valley: **Amaravati, Nagarjunakonda, Ghantasala**; white-green limestone): crowded, slender, sinuous **narrative relief** — the **"third school" of early Indian sculpture beside Gandhara and Mathura** (05 table), and the fountainhead of the south-Indian/Sri Lankan/SE-Asian style. **The Amaravati Mahastupa is Andhra's contribution to world Buddhist art.**
- **Western Deccan rock-cut:** **KARLE** (grandest chaitya), Bhaja, Bedsa, **Nasik (Pandavleni)**, Kanheri, Junnar — guild- and royal-funded (the Barabar idea matured, 04 §3).

### 1.5 The successors box ⭐ (Part-B bridge)
- **IKSHVAKUS of Vijayapuri (Nagarjunakonda), c. 3rd–4th c. CE:** **Chamtamula** (ashvamedha performer — Vedic face) → **Virapurushadatta** (whose **queens and princesses — Chamtisiri et al. — funded the great Buddhist establishment**: the double patronage repeated) ⭐; Nagarjunakonda = monastic metropolis with amphitheatre; tradition places **NAGARJUNA at Sriparvata** (03 §5). The direct Andhra heirs — and the bridge to the Pallava-Vishnukundin-Vengi arc (§2 & the Telugu-country essay).
- Also: Abhiras (west), Chutus (Karnataka), **early Pallavas** (south — reappearing in [File 09](09-South-Indian-Dynasties.md)).

## 2. The Sangam Age (c. 3rd c. BCE – 3rd c. CE)

**Tamilakam's heroic age** — a largely pre-Brahmanical chiefdom society reconstructed mainly from the **oldest literature of any Dravidian language**.

### 2.1 The three crowned kings (mu-vendar) ⭐

| Dynasty | Emblem | Capital | Port | One king to quote |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Cheras** | **Bow** | Vanji (Karur) | **MUZIRIS** (Roman pepper trade) | **Senguttuvan** — *Cilappatikaram* hero; founded the **Pattini (Kannagi) cult** |
| **Cholas** | **Tiger** | Uraiyur | **Puhar (Kaviripattinam)** | **KARIKALA** — battle of Venni; built the **KALLANAI (Grand Anicut)** on the Kaveri — an ancient dam still in use ⭐ |
| **Pandyas** | **Fish** | **Madurai** | Korkai (pearls) | **Nedunjeliyan** — victor of Talaiyalanganam; Sangam patron |

Below them: velir chieftains and the **"seven great patrons" (kadai ezhu vallal)**.

### 2.2 Sources & anchors
- **Sangam corpus:** **Ettuttokai** (eight anthologies) + **Pattuppattu** (ten idylls); grammar **TOLKAPPIYAM** (oldest extant Tamil work); the 18 minor works incl. **TIRUKKURAL of Tiruvalluvar** (strictly post-Sangam); twin epics **Cilappatikaram (Ilango Adigal) & Manimekalai (Sattanar)** — post-Sangam (5th–6th c.); poem classes **akam (love) / puram (war-public)** ⭐.
- **Dating anchors:** **Ashoka's RE II & XIII name the Cholas, Pandyas, Keralaputras, Satiyaputras** as friendly frontier peoples (earliest datable reference); Kharavela's Hathigumpha; **Roman texts (Periplus, Pliny, Ptolemy)**; archaeology — **Roman coin hoards; ARIKAMEDU** (Indo-Roman trading station, Wheeler); **KEELADI** (recent — early Tamil urbanism & Tamil-Brahmi) ⭐.
- **The "Sangam problem":** the three-academies story is **unhistorical**; the corpus is bracketed c. 100 BCE–250 CE by the Ashokan-Roman synchronisms — *say "reconstructed, not exact".*

### 2.3 Analysis
- **Polity = heroic CHIEFDOM, not bureaucratic state** — war, booty, gift (*kodai*) redistribution, bards trading praise for patronage (K. Kailasapathy's reading). Don't describe an empire.
- **The five tinais** (eco-zones organising both landscape and poetics): **kurinji** (hills) – **mullai** (forest) – **marudam** (farmland) – **neital** (coast) – **palai** (wasteland) ⭐.
- **Economy:** overseas trade the engine — pepper, pearls, muslin for **Roman gold**; "**Yavana**" merchants and bodyguards in the poems.
- **Society & religion:** pre-Brahmanical worship — **Murugan (Seyon)**, Mayon (Vishnu), the Pattini cult; **hero-stones (natukal)**; woman poet **Auvaiyar**; harsh widowhood, early sati; the varna order and Sanskrit ritual seeping in from the north → the **Aryan-Dravidian synthesis** that seeds composite south-Indian culture.
- **Language:** the corpus underwrites Tamil's status as a **classical language** with the oldest Dravidian literature.

## 3. Prelims nuggets

1. Puranas call the Satavahanas **"Andhras"**; founder **Simuka**; capital **Pratishthana/Paithan (Godavari)**.
2. **Naneghat — queen NAGANIKA — ashvamedha/rajasuya**; **Nasik prashasti — GAUTAMI BALASHRI** on Gautamiputra ⭐.
3. **Gautamiputra crushed NAHAPANA** — **Jogalthambi restruck-coin hoard** is the proof.
4. **Hala — Gathasaptashati (Prakrit)**; Gunadhya — Brihatkatha (Paishachi).
5. **Yajna Sri — ship coins**; Satavahana coins = **lead/potin**.
6. **FIRST land grants with immunities** (Nasik) → R. S. Sharma's feudalism seed ⭐.
7. Metronymics (Gautamiputra) = mother's-gotra naming, **not matriarchy**.
8. **Amaravati school = third school** (with Gandhara, Mathura); white limestone; **Karle = grandest chaitya**.
9. **Ikshvakus — Vijayapuri/Nagarjunakonda; Chamtamula's ashvamedha; Buddhist queens (Chamtisiri)** ⭐.
10. Sangam emblems: **Chera-bow, Chola-tiger, Pandya-fish**; ports **Muziris-Puhar-Korkai**.
11. **Karikala — Kallanai/Grand Anicut**; Senguttuvan — Pattini cult; **Tolkappiyam = oldest Tamil work**.
12. **Akam vs puram**; five **tinais**; **Arikamedu** (Wheeler) & **Keeladi** excavations.
13. **Ashoka RE II/XIII names the southern kingdoms** — the earliest datable Tamil-country reference.
14. Sattasai/Prakrit (Satavahana court) vs Junagadh/Sanskrit (Shaka court) — the language contrast ⭐.

## 4. Mains angles

1. **"Assess the Satavahana contribution to Deccan polity, economy and culture — and their role as the bridge between north and south."** Rulers' arc → land-grant innovation → Roman trade + guild banking → Prakrit letters + Amaravati art → close as the founding matrix of Andhra history (Part-B double duty).
2. **"Examine the Satavahana land grants as the earliest seeds of Indian feudalism."** Nasik immunities → Gupta agraharas → samanta order (07) → R. S. Sharma's thesis with its critics ("flag, don't worship").
3. **"Socio-religious conditions under the Satavahanas (with special reference to women)" (2023 Part-B PYQ).** Metronymics + queen-donors + varna-samkara anxiety + double patronage — evidence-first.
4. **"Reconstruct Sangam polity, economy and society from its sources, evaluating their reliability."** Corpus + anchors → chiefdom/tinai/trade findings → the Sangam-problem caveat as the closing source-criticism flourish.
