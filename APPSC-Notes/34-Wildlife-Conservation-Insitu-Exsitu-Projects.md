## 1. In-situ conservation (on-site) ⭐⭐

**Definition:** protecting species **in their natural habitat**, so whole ecosystems and evolutionary processes are conserved — the primary strategy.

### 1.1 The protected-area (PA) network (WPA 1972 categories)

| PA type | Feature | India (approx.; verify) | AP |
|---|---|---|---|
| **National Park** | Highest protection; no rights, no grazing | **~106** | **Papikonda, Sri Venkateswara** (+ a third small NP — verify notified list) ⭐ |
| **Wildlife Sanctuary** | Regulated rights permitted | **~570+** | **~13** incl. Rollapadu, Coringa, Krishna, Nelapattu, Koundinya, Lankamala, Gundla Brahmeswaram, Sri Penusila |
| **Conservation Reserve** | Buffer/corridor, govt-owned (2002 amend.) | ~130+ | e.g. community/corridor reserves (verify) |
| **Community Reserve** | Community land, voluntary (2002 amend.) | ~220+ | **Uppalapadu-type** village havens |

### 1.2 Other in-situ instruments

- **Biosphere Reserves (MAB programme)** — core–buffer–transition zoning; India **18** (12 in UNESCO's world network); **AP: Seshachalam (2010)** ⭐.
- **Tiger Reserves (NTCA)** — India **55+**; **NSTR = largest by area** ⭐.
- **Sacred groves** & **temple-hill forests** (Tirumala, Srisailam) — community in-situ conservation.
- **Ramsar wetlands** — India **80+** (verify latest); **AP: Kolleru (2002)** ⭐.

### 1.4 Project Tiger — critical evaluation ⭐⭐ (2020 PYQ (a), on the 2018 report)

- **All-India Tiger Estimation (every 4 yrs):** **2006: 1,411 → 2010: 1,706 → 2014: 2,226 → 2018: 2,967 → 2022: ~3,682** ⭐ (India ~75% of world total; the census methodology won a **Guinness record** for the largest camera-trap survey).
- **Successes:** numbers up 6%/yr; tiger as **umbrella/flagship** protecting whole landscapes; **MEE** (management effectiveness evaluation), CA|TS accreditation; eco-tourism & local livelihoods; source-population model.
- **Concerns (the "critically" half):** **habitat fragmentation & broken corridors**; **man-animal conflict** and retaliatory killing; **poaching & body-part trade**; **prey depletion**; **reserves with few/zero tigers** (uneven distribution — most tigers in a few states); **relocation/rehabilitation** disputes with forest-dwellers (FRA tension); tourism pressure; **skewed attention** to one charismatic species over grasslands/wetlands. **AP note:** NSTR is the largest reserve by area but carries a **modest tiger count (~60–70; verify)** — habitat and prey, not space, are the limiting factors ⭐.

## 2. Ex-situ conservation (off-site) ⭐

**Definition:** conserving components **outside natural habitat** — a complement/insurance, especially for critically endangered species.

- **Zoos & safari parks** — regulated by the **Central Zoo Authority (CZA, 1992)**; captive breeding (gharial, GIB, vultures). **AP:** **Indira Gandhi Zoological Park, Visakhapatnam**; **Sri Venkateswara Zoo, Tirupati**; Nehru Zoological is in Hyderabad (now Telangana) ⭐.
- **Botanical gardens** — **AJC Bose Indian Botanic Garden, Howrah** (BSI); medicinal-plant & seed gardens.
- **Gene/seed/field banks** — **NBPGR (plant genes, Delhi), NBAGR (animal, Karnal), NBFGR (fish, Lucknow)**; the **National Gene Bank**; cryopreservation & tissue culture.
- **Sacred groves** straddle in-/ex-situ; **conservation breeding centres** (e.g. GIB, vulture-breeding programmes).

## 3. Legal & institutional framework ⭐⭐

| Instrument | Year | Point |
|---|---|---|
| **Wildlife (Protection) Act** | **1972** (amended **2022**) | Schedules of protection; **National Board for Wildlife (NBWL)**; the 2022 amendment **rationalised schedules and aligned with CITES** ⭐ |
| **Forest (Conservation) Act** | **1980** (amended/ renamed 2023 — *Van Sanrakshan Evam Samvardhan*) | Curbs diversion of forest land; the **T.N. Godavarman (1996)** "continuing mandamus" widened the **forest definition** ⭐ |
| **Environment (Protection) Act** | 1986 | Umbrella (post-Bhopal); EIA, eco-sensitive zones |
| **Biological Diversity Act** | **2002** (amended **2023**) | **NBA (Chennai)** – **SBBs** – **BMCs**; access-benefit-sharing; **People's Biodiversity Registers** ⭐ |
| Forest Rights Act | 2006 | Community/individual forest rights — the conservation-vs-rights balance |
| National Forest Policy | **1988** | 33% cover goal; ecology + people's needs |

## 4. Forest-conservation strategies in India ⭐⭐ (2023 PYQ (a))

Structure the answer as **protect → afforest → involve people → restore → govern**:
1. **Legal protection:** FCA 1980 (diversion control), WPA 1972 PAs, Godavarman forest definition, eco-sensitive zones.
2. **Afforestation & greening:** **CAMPA** (compensatory afforestation fund), **Green India Mission**, National Afforestation Programme, **Nagar Van (urban forests)**, **National Bamboo Mission**, agroforestry/social & farm forestry (the Prakasam–Godavari pulpwood belt, File 22).
3. **People's participation:** **Joint Forest Management** (AP's **Vana Samrakshana Samithis, VSS** ⭐), **FRA 2006** community rights, **Van Dhan Kendras**, eco-development committees.
4. **Restoration & resilience:** forest-fire management, invasive-species removal, wetland/mangrove restoration (**Coringa**), REDD+ and carbon-sink commitments (**2.5–3 bn tonne CO₂ additional sink NDC goal**) ⭐.
5. **Governance & data:** ISFR biennial monitoring (FSI), MEE, community/People's Biodiversity Registers, DMF funds in mining districts (File 23).

## 5. International conventions ⭐ (match-pairs)

- **CITES (1975)** — trade in endangered species (red sanders, *Cycas beddomei*, star tortoise, pangolin — all AP-relevant ⭐).
- **CMS / Bonn Convention** — migratory species; **India hosted CoP-13 at Gandhinagar (2020)** — mascot "**Gibi**" the Great Indian Bustard ⭐.
- **Ramsar (1971)** — wetlands (AP: **Kolleru 2002**).
- **CBD (1992, Rio)** → **Nagoya Protocol** (ABS), **Cartagena Protocol** (biosafety), **Kunming-Montreal Global Biodiversity Framework (2022) — "30×30"** target ⭐.
- **UNESCO World Heritage (natural):** Western Ghats, Kaziranga, Manas, Keoladeo, Sundarbans, Nanda Devi–Valley of Flowers, Great Himalayan NP (none in AP — a point to note).

## 6. Andhra Pradesh conservation ⭐⭐ (answers 2020b, 2023b, 2025a)

### 6.1 AP protected-area roll call (cross-ref File 20 §2.3)

- **National Parks:** **Papikonda** (Godavari gorge — semi-evergreen; hornbills, tiger, leopard, gaur), **Sri Venkateswara** (Tirupati–Seshachalam — red sanders, slender loris, golden gecko, sambar) (+ verify third NP) ⭐.
- **Tiger Reserve:** **Nagarjunasagar–Srisailam (NSTR) — largest in India by area** (Nallamala; tiger, Chenchu co-residence, Gundla Brahmeswaram core) ⭐⭐.
- **Biosphere Reserve:** **Seshachalam (2010)** — endemism centre.
- **Elephant habitat:** **Koundinya WLS (Chittoor)** ⭐.
- **Key WLS:** **Rollapadu (GIB, blackbuck, wolf), Coringa (mangroves, fishing cat, crocodiles), Krishna (estuarine crocs), Sri Lankamalleswara (Jerdon's courser), Nelapattu & Pulicat (pelicans/flamingos), Gundla Brahmeswaram, Kambalakonda, Sri Penusila Narasimha, Kondakarla Ava**.
- **Ramsar:** **Kolleru (2002)**; **bird community reserves:** Uppalapadu, Telineelapuram.

### 6.2 AP in-situ conservation strategies (2023 PYQ (b))

Use the **PA network + species programmes + community + habitat-restoration** spine, made AP-specific:
- **PAs & tiger reserve** (NSTR, Papikonda, Sri Venkateswara) + **Seshachalam Biosphere Reserve** + **Kolleru Ramsar**.
- **Species recovery:** GIB & blackbuck (Rollapadu), Jerdon's courser habitat (Lankamala), **olive-ridley hatcheries** on Godavari–Krishna beaches, fishing-cat/mangrove programme (Coringa), red-sanders anti-smuggling (RSASTF).
- **Community & tribal:** **VSS (Joint Forest Management)**, **FRA** podu titles, **GCC** NTFP economy (File 22), **Uppalapadu/Telineelapuram** village-protected heronries, ITDA eco-development (File 29).
- **Habitat restoration:** **Coringa mangrove restoration** (cyclone buffer), Kolleru weed & aquaculture-encroachment management (the "+5 contour" issue), grassland (Rollapadu) upkeep.

### 6.3 AP wildlife conservation & biodiversity (2025 PYQ (a))

Frame: **Deccan-Peninsula + Coasts biogeography (File 31) → three habitat suites (forest / grassland-scrub / wetland-coast, File 32) → PA network + Ramsar + biosphere → endemics under no-hotspot status (the AP paradox) → institutions (AP Biodiversity Board, Forest Dept, GCC) → challenges → way forward** (corridor protection, grassland recognition, mangrove blue-carbon, community reserves, curbing wildlife trafficking).

## 7. Challenges & the way forward (balancing paragraph for any answer)

- **Threats:** habitat loss/fragmentation, **man-animal conflict** (elephants in Chittoor, leopards on Seshachalam fringes), poaching & **wildlife trafficking (pangolin, star tortoise, red sanders)**, **Polavaram submergence** of agency forest (File 27), mining pressure (File 19/23), **invasive species** (Kolleru weeds, African catfish), cyclones & sea-level rise (deltas), grassland neglect.
- **Way forward:** corridor & landscape conservation, formal **grassland/scrub protection**, mangrove/**blue-carbon** restoration, community & conservation reserves, strong **CITES enforcement**, People's Biodiversity Registers, eco-tourism revenue-sharing, and reconciling **FRA rights with protection**.
