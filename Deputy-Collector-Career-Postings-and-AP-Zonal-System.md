# Deputy Collector (APPSC Group-I) — How Far From Home Will You Be Posted? · The AP Zonal / Multi-Zonal Cadre System

[Index](APPSC-Notes/README.md) · companion to the Public Administration & Polity notes

⭐⭐ Two-in-one note: it answers the aspirant's real-life worry — *"as a Deputy Collector, will I be posted near my home district or thrown to the other edge of the state?"* — and it is itself a **high-yield Polity/Governance topic** (Art. 371D → Six-Point Formula → Presidential Order → local cadres, and the brand-new **AP Public Employment Order, 2025**).

---

## 0. The short answer (read this first)

**You will always stay inside Andhra Pradesh, and — since the 2025 Presidential Order — inside *one half* of it.**

- APPSC Group-I is a **state service**, not an all-India service. A directly-recruited **Deputy Collector is never posted outside AP** (unlike an IAS officer, who can be sent to any state cadre). So "the other edge of the *country*" never happens.
- **The big change:** Deputy Collector *used to be a **State cadre** post* — meaning the government could post you to **any district in AP**, Srikakulam to Anantapur alike. That is exactly the "thrown to the far edge of the state" fear, and under the old rules it was real.
- Under the **AP Public Employment (Organisation of Local Cadres & Regulation of Direct Recruitment) Order, 2025** (Presidential Order, notified **S.O. 5777(E), 15 Dec 2025**), Deputy Collector has been **re-organised as a MULTI-ZONAL cadre** post. The Deputy-Collector cadre strength is **split between Multi-Zone-I and Multi-Zone-II**, and **95% of direct-recruitment vacancies in a multi-zone are reserved for locals of that multi-zone.**
- **Net effect for you:** if you qualify as a *local* of your multi-zone, your revenue-service career now stays **within your own half of the state (~13 districts)**. A North-Coastal (Uttarandhra) local will **not** be sent to Rayalaseema, and vice-versa — they are in different multi-zones.

**But be honest with yourself about three caveats** (details in §4):
1. A multi-zone is still **half the state** — "neighbouring district / walking distance from home" is *not* guaranteed; you can be posted several hundred km away *within* your half.
2. Revenue officers (RDO/Sub-Collector, and Tahsildars below them) are often **deliberately kept out of their *own* native mandal/town** on vigilance grounds — so "right at home" is sometimes actively avoided.
3. **Promotion lifts you back to State cadre.** Joint Collector, HoD posts and promotion into the **IAS** are State-level — once promoted above the Deputy-Collector cadre you can again be posted anywhere in AP.

## 1. Why this happens — the cadre ladder (lower post = closer to home) ⭐⭐

Under a Presidential Order every civil post is slotted into a **"local area / cadre unit."** The higher the post, the *larger* the area you can be shuffled across:

| Cadre unit | Area you can be posted across | Example posts (Revenue line) | How "home-friendly"? |
|---|---|---|---|
| **District cadre** | **1 district** | Junior Assistant, Senior Asst., Jr. ministerial staff, VRO-type | 🟢 Most local |
| **Zonal cadre** | **1 zone** (≈ 4–5 districts) | **Deputy Tahsildar, Tahsildar** & equivalents (up to first-level gazetted) | 🟢 Very local |
| **Multi-Zonal cadre** | **1 multi-zone** (≈ 13 districts = half the state) | **DEPUTY COLLECTOR & equivalents** — all posts *above Superintendent / above first-level gazetted, up to Deputy Collector* | 🟡 Half-state |
| **State cadre** | **Whole state (all 26 districts)** | Joint Collector, Heads of Department, **IAS** (incl. promotees) | 🔴 Anywhere in AP |

**Irony worth knowing:** if being *near home* is your single biggest priority, the **Tahsildar-level (zonal) posts keep you closer to home than the Deputy Collector does.** Rising in rank *widens* your posting radius. The Deputy Collector is the top of the *directly-recruited* Group-I revenue ladder, and it sits at the **half-state (multi-zonal)** rung.

> **Match-pair bank:** District cadre → **Junior Assistant** · Zonal cadre → **Tahsildar** · **Multi-Zonal cadre → Deputy Collector** · State cadre → **IAS / Joint Collector**.

## 2. The 26 districts → 6 zones → 2 multi-zones (AP Public Employment Order, 2025) ⭐⭐

95% of jobs in a zone/multi-zone are reserved for **locals** (a "local" ≈ studied ≥ 4 of the 7 years before the qualifying exam in that local area, or domicile where no schooling). The 26 districts are grouped as:

### Multi-Zone-I (North + Central Coastal Andhra)

| Zone | Districts |
|---|---|
| **Zone-1** (Uttarandhra / North Coastal) | Srikakulam · Parvathipuram Manyam · Vizianagaram · Visakhapatnam · Anakapalli |
| **Zone-2** (East Godavari belt) | Kakinada · Dr. B.R. Ambedkar Konaseema · East Godavari · Alluri Sitharama Raju |
| **Zone-3** (West Godavari + Krishna) | West Godavari · Eluru · Krishna · NTR |

### Multi-Zone-II (South Coastal + Rayalaseema)

| Zone | Districts |
|---|---|
| **Zone-4** (South Coastal) | Guntur · Palnadu · Bapatla · Prakasam · Sri Potti Sriramulu Nellore |
| **Zone-5** (Rayalaseema — east) | Tirupati · Chittoor · Annamayya · YSR Kadapa |
| **Zone-6** (Rayalaseema — west) | Kurnool · Nandyal · Anantapur · Sri Sathya Sai |

*(13 districts per multi-zone.)* Your multi-zone is fixed by **where you are a local**. As a Deputy Collector you rotate **inside** it; you do **not** cross into the other multi-zone.

## 3. Historical/constitutional backbone (this is the actual exam question) ⭐⭐

| Step | What it did |
|---|---|
| **Art. 371D** (32nd Amendment, 1973; in force 1974) | Special provisions for AP — empowers the President to provide **"equitable opportunities"** in education & public employment via **local cadres** and the **AP Administrative Tribunal**. |
| **Six-Point Formula, 1973** | Political settlement (post-Jai Andhra / Telangana agitations) that mandated organising local cadres and giving **preference to locals** in direct recruitment. |
| **Presidential Order, 1975** (A.P. Public Employment — Organisation of Local Cadres…) | The original order that carved undivided AP into **zones** and defined local-candidate rules. |
| **AP Reorganisation Act, 2014** | Bifurcation → Telangana framed its **own** order (7 zones / multi-zones, 95% local quota, 2018). Residual AP needed a fresh order after its **13 → 26 district** re-carving (2022). |
| **AP Public Employment Order, 2025** ⭐ (Presidential Order **S.O. 5777(E), 15-Dec-2025**) | The current law for AP: **6 zones + 2 multi-zones**, **95%** direct-recruitment posts reserved for locals, and — key for us — **Deputy Collector re-classified from State cadre to Multi-Zonal cadre.** Implementation via **G.O.Ms.No.45/46** (cadre strength) and allotment guidelines. |

> **One-line for Mains:** *"Art. 371D operationalised through the Six-Point Formula and Presidential Orders organises AP's services into district/zonal/multi-zonal/state local cadres with 95% local reservation in direct recruitment — the 2025 Order re-drew these around 26 districts (6 zones, 2 multi-zones) and placed the Deputy Collector cadre at the multi-zonal level."*

## 4. The honest caveats (so you plan realistically)

1. **Half a state is still large.** Multi-Zone-I runs from Srikakulam (far north) down to Krishna/Vijayawada. A Srikakulam-native Deputy Collector could serve at Machilipatnam or Eluru — *same multi-zone, but 400–500 km from home.* "Neighbouring district" is the **best case**, not a guarantee.
2. **Home-district is sometimes deliberately avoided.** Officers exercising land / revenue / quasi-judicial powers (Sub-Collector/RDO, Tahsildar) are, by transfer-policy and vigilance convention, frequently **not** posted to their *own* native mandal/town, to avoid conflict of interest and local pressure. So paradoxically the *nearest* posting can be the one policy resists.
3. **Promotion re-opens the whole state.** The moment you are promoted to **Special Grade Deputy Collector → Joint Collector**, or selected into the **IAS**, you move to a **State-cadre** footing and can again be posted to any of the 26 districts (IAS: anywhere in the AP cadre). Only a minority reach IAS, usually late-career.
4. **Deputation ≠ zone logic.** Stints in the **Secretariat (Amaravati), corporations, missions, or as OSD/Special Officer** follow the assignment, not your multi-zone — often at the state capital or wherever the department sits.
5. **Transfers are frequent** (typically every ~2–3 years), and are shaped by seniority, spouse/health/compassionate grounds, general transfer counselling, and administrative need. You can *request* preferences; you cannot *dictate* them.
6. **You never leave AP.** Whatever the zone churn, a Group-I Deputy Collector's entire service is inside Andhra Pradesh — the "other edge of the *country*" scenario belongs to the IAS/central services, not to you.

---

## 5. Bottom line for the aspirant

- **"Not thrown to the other edge of the state"** → **Largely secured now.** The 2025 multi-zonal classification confines a Deputy Collector to **one half of AP** for the whole directly-recruited career. This is a genuine improvement over the old State-cadre rule.
- **"Posted in a neighbouring district / near home"** → **Possible and common, but not guaranteed.** You stay in your region; the exact district rotates, and your own home mandal may even be off-limits.
- **Want to stay *closest* to home?** The **zonal-cadre posts (Deputy Tahsildar / Tahsildar)** keep you within ~4–5 districts of home — tighter than the Deputy Collector's half-state radius. It's a genuine trade-off between **rank** and **rootedness**.

## 7. Last-mile checklist

1. Group-I Deputy Collector = **state service, always inside AP** (never all-India).
2. **Old rule:** Deputy Collector = **State cadre** (anywhere in AP). **2025 rule:** **Multi-Zonal cadre** (half the state).
3. **6 zones, 2 multi-zones, 95% local quota** — AP Public Employment Order **2025** (S.O. 5777(E), 15-Dec-2025); constitutional base **Art. 371D + Six-Point Formula (1973) + Presidential Order (1975)**.
4. Cadre ladder: **District → Zonal → Multi-Zonal → State** (Junior Asst / Tahsildar / **Deputy Collector** / IAS-JC).
5. Caveats: home-mandal often avoided (vigilance); promotion → State cadre/IAS re-opens all 26 districts; deputation follows the job, not the zone.
6. Closest-to-home Group-service option = **zonal-cadre Tahsildar-level**, tighter than the Deputy Collector's radius.
