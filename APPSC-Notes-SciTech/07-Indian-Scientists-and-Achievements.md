## 2. India's awards & measurement of achievement (asked directly)
- **Rashtriya Vigyan Puraskar (RVP), 2024** ⭐ — new unified national science awards (**Vigyan Ratna, Vigyan Shri, Vigyan Yuva-SSB, Vigyan Team**) replacing 300+ awards incl. the old **Shanti Swarup Bhatnagar Prize** (CSIR, since 1958, age <45).
- **Bharat Ratna** to scientists: **C.V. Raman, A.P.J. Abdul Kalam, CNR Rao, M. Visvesvaraya (engineer — 15 Sept = Engineers' Day), M.S. Swaminathan**.
- Global: **Fellows of Royal Society (FRS)**; India's science-day calendar: **28 Feb (Science), 11 May (Technology), 23 Aug (Space), 22 Dec (Mathematics)**.

## 3. Indigenous technologies & developing new technologies ⭐⭐⭐

### 3.1 Concept
- **Indigenous technology** = designed and developed within the country (reduces import dependence, saves forex, builds strategic autonomy, generates IP & jobs). India's arc: **import → licence-manufacture → indigenise → innovate/export.** The policy word is **Atmanirbhar Bharat** (2020); the older word was **swadeshi / self-reliance (1983 TPS)**.
- **Two kinds:**
  1. **Grassroots / traditional indigenous knowledge** — community innovations, traditional medicine, farming practices.
  2. **Frontier indigenous capability** — self-developed high-tech (cryogenic engines, 700-MW reactors, LCA Tejas, vaccines, UPI, indigenous processors).

### 3.2 Marquee indigenous technologies (the "we built it ourselves" bank)

| Domain | Indigenous achievement (after denial/import) |
|---|---|
| Space | **Cryogenic engine CE-7.5/CE-20** (after 1990s US/Russia denial); PSLV; NavIC |
| Nuclear | **700-MW PHWR (KAPP-3)**; three-stage/thorium; **KAMINI** U-233 reactor |
| Defence | **LCA Tejas, INS Vikrant, Arihant, Agni-V MIRV, BrahMos, ATAGS** |
| Compute | **PARAM supercomputers (after CRAY denial, 1991)**; **PARAM Rudra + Rudra servers (2024)**; **SHAKTI/VEGA/DIR-V** RISC-V processors; **Vikram 3201** space CPU |
| Health | **Covaxin, ZyCoV-D, iNCOVACC, CERVAVAC, Rotavac, NexCAR19 CAR-T** |
| Digital | **Aadhaar, UPI, CoWIN, DigiLocker, ONDC, Bhashini** — indigenous DPI |
| Agri | **Nano-urea/nano-DAP (IFFCO)**, indigenous **genome-edited rice (2025)** |
| Materials/energy | Indigenous solar-cell/module push (PLI), **green-hydrogen electrolysers**, **BharatOS/Maya OS** (defence Linux) |

### 3.3 Protecting & harnessing traditional/indigenous knowledge ⭐ (a favourite Mains angle)
- **Traditional Knowledge Digital Library (TKDL, 2001)** — CSIR + AYUSH; **defeated the US patents on turmeric (haldi) and the EPO patent on neem** — the standard example of biopiracy defence ⭐; **turmeric, neem, basmati (US, RiceTec)** cases.
- **Biological Diversity Act 2002** (+ amendment 2023) & **NBA (Chennai)** — access-benefit-sharing, biopiracy control.
- **GI tags** protecting indigenous products — AP examples: **Tirupati Laddu, Bandar Laddu, Kondapalli toys, Machilipatnam Kalamkari, Uppada Jamdani, Venkatagiri sarees, Araku coffee, Banaganapalle mango, Guntur Sannam chilli** ⭐ (know 3-4 AP GIs).
- **AYUSH** mainstreaming — Ayurveda/Siddha/Unani/Homeopathy/Yoga; **International Day of Yoga 21 June**; WHO Global Traditional Medicine Centre (Jamnagar, 2022).
- **Honey Bee Network / NIF (2000, Gujarat) / GIAN** — institutionalising grassroots innovators (Anil Gupta); **SRISTI**; frugal "jugaad" innovations (Mitticool fridge, pedal washing machine).

### 3.4 "Developing new technologies" — the pipeline India is now building
- **Deep-tech thrust:** NQM (quantum), IndiaAI (AI), ISM (semiconductors), BioE3 (bio-manufacturing), green hydrogen, **6G (Bharat 6G Alliance)**, robotics/drones, space-tech startups.
- **Financing the pipeline:** **ANRF (₹50,000 cr) + RDI Scheme (₹1 lakh cr, 2025) + iDEX + BIRAC + AIM** — the money layer (File 02).
- **Startup/deep-tech proof:** 110+ unicorns; **National Deep Tech Startup Policy (draft)**; Skyroot/Agnikul (space), ImmunoACT (CAR-T), Sarvam/BharatGen (AI).
