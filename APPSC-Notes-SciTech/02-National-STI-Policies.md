## 1. The four (+1 draft) national S&T policies ⭐⭐⭐ (the No.1 Mains zone of this unit)

| Policy | Year / PM era | Core thrust | Signature lines & outcomes |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Scientific Policy Resolution (SPR)** | **1958**, Nehru | Foster **pure/basic science**, build scientific institutions and manpower; science as the foundation of a modern state | "It is an inherent obligation of a great country like India… to participate in the making of the knowledge frontier"; era of CSIR labs, IITs, DAE |
| **Technology Policy Statement (TPS)** | **1983**, Indira Gandhi | **Technological self-reliance**; use technology for employment, rural development, reducing imports | Backdrop of sanctions & import substitution; led to missions (oilseeds, telecom, immunisation) |
| **Science & Technology Policy (STP)** | **2003**, Vajpayee | Science AND technology together; **raise GERD to 2% of GDP**; innovation brought in | Post-Pokhran-II sanctions & IT boom; SAC-PM created |
| **Science, Technology & Innovation Policy (STIP)** | **2013**, Manmohan Singh | "Science, Research and Innovation System for High Technology-led path for India" (**SRISHTI**); position India among **top 5 scientific powers by 2020**; link R&D to socio-economic priorities | Declared 2010–20 the **"Decade of Innovation"**; private-sector R&D push; Science Express etc. |
| **Draft STIP 2020** (5th NSTP — not yet notified) | 2020–21, drafted via 4-track consultation | **Decentralised, evidence-informed, inclusive**: Open Science framework, **One Nation One Subscription**, 30% women in R&D leadership, strategic technology autonomy, STI Observatory, Education-Research-Innovation continuum | Several elements already executed: **ONOS approved Nov 2024 (₹6,000 cr; ~13,000 journals; live 1 Jan 2025)** ⭐; ANRF (below) |

**Evolution one-liner for Mains:** *1958 built the labs, 1983 sought self-reliance, 2003 added money and technology, 2013 added innovation and industry, 2020's draft adds openness, inclusion and strategic autonomy.*

## 2. The proliferation architecture — who runs Indian S&T

### 2.1 Apex & advisory
- **PM's Science, Technology & Innovation Advisory Council (PM-STIAC)** — 9 national missions oversight; **Principal Scientific Adviser** (office created 1999; first PSA A.P.J.-era **R. Chidambaram** 2001–18; current **Ajay Kumar Sood**).
- **NITI Aayog** (AIM, frontier-tech strategy papers); Parliamentary Standing Committee on S&T.

### 2.2 The scientific departments (MCQ staple — match department : year : mandate)

| Body | Year | Mandate (flagships) |
|---|---|---|
| **CSIR** | **1942** | 37 industrial R&D labs (autonomous society; President of CSIR = **Prime Minister**) |
| **DAE** | **1954** | Nuclear power & research (directly under PM) |
| **DRDO** | **1958** | Defence R&D (Ministry of Defence) |
| **Dept. of Space / ISRO** | **1972 / 1969** | Space (directly under PM) |
| **DST** | **1971** | Policy coordination, autonomous institutes, INSPIRE, NQM |
| **DSIR** | 1985 | Industrial research, **NRDC**, recognition of in-house R&D |
| **DBT** | **1986** | Biotechnology; **BIRAC (2012)**; **BRIC (2023)** unifying 14 institutes |
| **MoES** | 2006 | Ocean-atmosphere-polar science (IMD, INCOIS, NIOT, NCPOR) |
| **MeitY** | — | Electronics/IT missions: Semicon India, IndiaAI, NSM (with DST) |

### 2.3 Anusandhan National Research Foundation (ANRF) ⭐⭐⭐ — the biggest structural reform

- **ANRF Act 2023**; operational **2024**; **subsumes SERB**.
- Corpus **₹50,000 crore (2023–28)** — of which **~70% envisaged from non-government sources** (industry, philanthropy) ⭐.
- Governing Board chaired by the **Prime Minister**; DST is the administrative home; funds research **including in state universities** (where <1% of researchers get central funding today — the "proliferation" argument).
- Early programmes: **PM Early Career Research Grant (PMECRG)**, **MAHA-EV** (EV components mission).

### 2.4 The 2025 capstone — RDI Scheme ⭐
- **Research, Development & Innovation (RDI) Scheme, approved 1 July 2025: ₹1 lakh crore corpus** of long-tenor, low/zero-interest financing to crowd **private capital into sunrise/strategic sectors** (deep-tech, AI, quantum, space, biotech). Read it as the financing twin of ANRF.

## 4. Proliferation = taking science to people (schemes bank)

- **Human capital pipeline:** **INSPIRE/MANAK** (school-college scholarships & faculty fellowships, DST), **PMRF** (doctoral), **Ramanujan & VAJRA** (diaspora return), **Ramalingaswami** (DBT bio-diaspora), **VAIBHAV** fellowships (2023).
- **Women in science ⭐:** **KIRAN** & its successor **WISE-KIRAN** umbrella (WISE-PhD, WISE-SCOPE, WIDUSHI), **GATI** charter (Athena-SWAN-style), **Vigyan Jyoti** (girls in STEM), **BioCARe** (DBT). Women ~**18-20% of R&D workforce** — quote with the 30% STIP-2020 target.
- **Science communication:** National Council for S&T Communication (NCSTC), Vigyan Prasar (merged into NCSTC ecosystem 2022-23), **India International Science Festival (IISF)**, **Jigyasa** (CSIR-school connect).
- **Inclusion:** SC/ST-focused **STI Hubs** (DST), TIDE/NIDHI for startups, **Sansad TV + DD Science**; vernacular science via **Bhashini** AI translation.

## 5. The money question ⭐⭐ (evaluation ammunition)

- **GERD ≈ 0.64–0.7% of GDP** — vs China ~2.4%, US ~3.5%, Israel ~5.6%, Korea ~5%, world average ~1.8%. Target of 2% has been in **every policy since 2003** — the gap *is* the critique.
- Composition problem: **Government ≈ 55-60%** of R&D spend (centre + states + PSUs + higher edu); **private sector only ~36-40%** — inverse of advanced economies (~70% private). ANRF's 70:30 design + RDI scheme = the announced fix.
- Researchers ~**260 per million population** (China ~1,600; Korea ~8,000+).
- Outputs improving: **3rd in scientific publications** (NSF); **6th in patent filings by origin (WIPO)** — **1 lakh+ patents granted FY24**, resident filings now >50%; GII 38 (2025).

## 6. State layer & AP tie-ins ⭐

- Every state has a **State S&T Council** (DST-supported): AP's is **APCOST** (AP Council of Science & Technology) — implements science popularisation, remote-sensing applications (with **AP Space Applications Centre-type bodies**), patent facilitation.
- **AP-specific STI moves (label the years):**
  - **Amaravati Quantum Valley (2025)** — IBM–TCS–L&T to install **IBM Quantum System Two (156-qubit Heron)** — India's largest quantum computer, aligned to National Quantum Mission; targeted 2026.
  - **Google AI data-centre hub, Visakhapatnam (announced Oct 2025)** — multi-billion-dollar (~$15 bn) gigawatt-scale AI campus with subsea cable landing — among India's largest single tech investments.
  - **Drone ecosystem:** Amaravati **Drone Summit (Oct 2024)**; planned drone city/hub at **Orvakal (Kurnool)**; AP Drone Corporation.
  - **AMTZ Visakhapatnam (2016)** — Asia's first integrated **MedTech manufacturing zone** (policy exemplar for manufacturing-linked innovation).
  - AP innovation bodies: **APIS (AP Innovation Society)**, incubators in universities; **RGUKT** (2008) — the "IIIT" model for rural gifted students — an AP-original *proliferation* policy ⭐.

## 7. Mains skeletons

**Q. "Trace the evolution of India's S&T policy from 1958 to the draft STIP 2020. Has policy kept pace with needs?"**
Intro: 4 policies + draft (table §1 in prose) → Body: continuity (self-reliance → atmanirbhar; 2% GERD promise) vs change (state-led → innovation ecosystem; ANRF/RDI financing) → Critique: GERD stagnation, private-sector shyness, implementation gap between policy and budget → Close: ANRF + RDI + missions = policy finally acquiring financial teeth; AP's quantum/AI parks as state-level proliferation.

**Q. "Policies proliferate; innovation does not." Examine with respect to India's STI framework.**
Use §5 numbers as the tension; ONOS/ANRF/AIM as counters; end with GII rise 81 (2015) → 38 (2025) as evidence of genuine movement.
