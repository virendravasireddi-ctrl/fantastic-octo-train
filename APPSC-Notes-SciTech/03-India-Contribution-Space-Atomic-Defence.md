## 1. Space ⭐⭐⭐ (India's showcase sector)

### 1.1 The build-up timeline (memorise as a ladder)

| Year | Milestone |
|---|---|
| 1962 | **INCOSPAR** (Nehru–Sarabhai); Thumba sounding rockets (1963 — first rocket carried on a **bicycle/bullock cart** — the famous frugality image) |
| **1969** | **ISRO** founded (15 Aug); DOS/Space Commission 1972 |
| 1975 | **Aryabhata** — first Indian satellite (Soviet launch) |
| 1980 | **SLV-3** puts **Rohini** in orbit — India becomes 6th space-faring nation (project led by **A.P.J. Abdul Kalam**) |
| 1994→ | **PSLV** operational — the "workhorse" (60+ flights; **104 satellites in one launch, 2017** ⭐) |
| 2008 | **Chandrayaan-1** — discovery of **lunar water** (with NASA's M3); Moon Impact Probe |
| 2013-14 | **Mangalyaan (MOM)** — first nation to reach **Mars orbit on first attempt**; ~₹450 cr frugal engineering |
| 2014 | Indigenous **cryogenic CE-7.5** succeeds (GSLV-D5) — after 1990s tech-denial ⭐ |
| 2017 | **LVM3** (GSLV Mk-III) with indigenous **CE-20** cryo engine |
| 2019 | Chandrayaan-2 — orbiter thrives, lander crashes |
| **23 Aug 2023** | **Chandrayaan-3** — 1st country at **lunar south-pole region**, 4th to soft-land; **Shiv Shakti point**; **National Space Day** ⭐⭐ |
| 2023-24 | **Aditya-L1** (first Indian solar observatory, halo orbit at L1, Jan 2024); **XPoSat** (X-ray polarimetry — 2nd only to NASA) |
| Dec 2024–Jan 2025 | **SpaDeX** — India becomes **4th nation to achieve in-space docking** ⭐ |
| Jan 2025 | **100th launch from Sriharikota** (GSLV-F15/NVS-02); **V. Narayanan** takes over as ISRO chairman |
| Jul 2025 | **NISAR** — $1.5-bn **NASA-ISRO** joint SAR earth-observation satellite launched on GSLV-F16 |
| Jun–Jul 2025 | **Shubhanshu Shukla** — first Indian on the **ISS** (Axiom-4); Rakesh Sharma 1984 remains first Indian in space |

### 1.2 Approved future map (quote 2–3)
- **Gaganyaan** — first Indian human spaceflight (~2027); **Vyommitra** half-humanoid.
- **Bharatiya Antariksh Station** — first module **2028**, complete by **2035**; **Indian on the Moon by 2040** (2023 PM directive).
- **Chandrayaan-4** (lunar sample return) and **Venus Orbiter Mission** approved Sept 2024; **NGLV** (Next-Gen Launch Vehicle) approved; **Third launch pad at SHAR approved Jan 2025 (₹3,984.86 cr)** ⭐ (AP tie-in).

### 1.3 Applications & reform (the "contribution to human life" leg)
- Applications trinity: **communication** (INSAT/GSAT: DTH, VSAT, tele-education, telemedicine), **earth observation** (Cartosat/RISAT/Oceansat: crops, water, urban, disasters), **navigation** (**NavIC** — 7-satellite regional system; **GAGAN** aviation augmentation with AAI).
- Societal firsts: **SITE 1975** (satellite TV to 2,400 villages — "the largest sociological experiment"), telemedicine to islands, **PFZ advisories to AP fishers**, cyclone warnings (Hudhud 2014 — Vizag).
- **Commercial/private turn:** NSIL (2019), IN-SPACe (2020), Space Policy 2023, FDI 2024; **Skyroot Vikram-S (2022 — first private Indian rocket)**, **Agnikul Agnibaan (2024 — world's first single-piece 3D-printed engine flight, from India's first private pad at SHAR)** ⭐; Pixxel hyperspectral constellation (2025). Space economy **~$8.4 bn**, target **$44 bn by 2033**.
- **SDSC SHAR, Sriharikota (AP)** — India's only orbital spaceport → every Indian mission lifts off from **Andhra soil** (Mains flourish); second spaceport coming up at Kulasekarapattinam (TN) for SSLV.

## 2. Atomic energy ⭐⭐ (self-reliance under sanctions)

### 2.1 Institutions & the Bhabha vision
- **AEC 1948 → DAE 1954** (under PM); **Homi Bhabha** — architect; **BARC** Trombay (1954 as AEET; renamed 1967).
- **Apsara (1956)** — first research reactor in Asia; CIRUS, **Dhruva** (1985); **Tarapur (1969)** — first power station.
- **Three-stage programme ⭐⭐ (the classic question):**
  1. **PHWRs** using natural uranium → produce plutonium;
  2. **Fast Breeder Reactors** using Pu + U-238 → breed more fuel (**PFBR-500 at Kalpakkam — core loading began March 2024**; BHAVINI);
  3. **Thorium reactors** (U-233 from India's vast thorium/monazite beach sands) — **KAMINI** (Kalpakkam) is the world's only U-233-fuelled reactor. Rationale: India has little uranium, **~21-25% of world thorium**.

### 2.2 Weapons & diplomacy waypoints
- **Pokhran-I (18 May 1974, "Smiling Buddha")** — "peaceful nuclear explosion" (Raja Ramanna-led).
- **Pokhran-II (11 & 13 May 1998, "Operation Shakti")** — 5 tests; India declares NWS status; **11 May = National Technology Day**; scientists: Kalam, R. Chidambaram, Anil Kakodkar.
- **Indo-US civil nuclear deal (2008)** + NSG waiver — ended isolation; India has IAEA safeguards for civilian reactors; member of MTCR (2016), Wassenaar (2017), Australia Group (2018) — **not NSG, not NPT/CTBT signatory** ⭐.

### 2.3 Power programme today (label years)
- **~8.2 GW nuclear capacity, 24 reactors (~3% of electricity)**; under construction/sanctioned fleet to take it to **22.5 GW by 2031-32**.
- **KAPP-3 & 4 (Kakrapar)** — first indigenous **700 MW PHWRs** (2023/24) — the new workhorse design; fleet mode 10×700 MW approved.
- **Kudankulam** (Russian VVER, 6×1000 MW site); **Kovvada (Srikakulam, AP)** — proposed 6×1208 MW AP1000 site (long-pending) ⭐ AP tie-in; **Tummalapalle (YSR Kadapa, AP)** — among the **world's largest uranium reserves**, UCIL mine ⭐.
- **Nuclear Energy Mission (Budget 2025-26): ₹20,000 cr for Small Modular / "Bharat Small" Reactors; 100 GW by 2047 target; announced opening of the sector to private players** (Atomic Energy Act & CLNDA amendments proposed) ⭐ — the current-affairs face.
- Beyond power: **radioisotopes for cancer care (BARC-TMC), food irradiation, desalination; INS Arihant-class** (below).

## 3. Defence R&D ⭐⭐

### 3.1 DRDO & the missile arc
- **DRDO 1958** (~50 labs). **IGMDP (1983, Kalam)**: **P**rithvi, **A**gni, **T**rishul, **A**kash, **N**ag (mnemonic "PATAN").
- **Agni-V** (5,000+ km, canisterised); **Mission Divyastra (Mar 2024) — Agni-5 with MIRV** ⭐; Agni-P (new-gen); **BrahMos** — Indo-Russian supersonic cruise missile, **first major defence export (Philippines, delivery from April 2024)**; **Astra** BVR air-to-air; **Pralay**; **Pinaka** rockets (exported to Armenia); QRSAM/Akash-NG; **VSHORADS**; **hypersonics: HSTDV (2020) and long-range anti-ship hypersonic missile test (Nov 2024)** ⭐.
- **Strategic triad completed** with **INS Arihant** (first indigenous SSBN; deterrence patrol 2018); **INS Arighaat (Aug 2024)**.
- **Missile Test Range at Nagayalanka (Krishna dist., AP)** sanctioned; **NSTL Visakhapatnam** — DRDO's underwater-weapons lab (Varunastra torpedo, Maareech decoy) ⭐ AP tie-ins.

### 3.2 Platforms — indigenous by design
- **LCA Tejas** (ADA-HAL; FOC 2019; **Mk1A deliveries began 2024-25**, delayed by GE engines — cite as import-dependence lesson); **AMCA** 5th-gen fighter programme cleared (2024-25); LCH **Prachand**; ALH Dhruv; HTT-40 trainer; **C-295 transport being assembled in Vadodara (first private-sector military aircraft plant)**.
- Navy: **INS Vikrant (2 Sept 2022)** — first indigenous aircraft carrier (~76% indigenous); **triple commissioning 15 Jan 2025 — Nilgiri (P-17A), Surat (P-15B), Vaghsheer (Scorpene)** ⭐; >60 ships under construction in Indian yards — incl. **Hindustan Shipyard, Visakhapatnam** (fleet support ships) ⭐.
- Army: Arjun Mk-1A, **Zorawar** light tank (2024 trials), ATAGS artillery, Dhanush.
- **Operation Sindoor (May 2025)** — combat validation of indigenous systems (**Akash, Akashteer air-defence grid, D4 anti-drone, BrahMos**) — use as the "indigenisation works" example ⭐.

### 3.3 Indigenisation policy stack (one paragraph in any answer)
- **Positive Indigenisation Lists** (5 services lists, 509 items + DPSU lists — import embargo dates), **SRIJAN portal**, **iDEX (2018)** startups, **Technology Development Fund**, 2 **Defence Industrial Corridors** (UP, TN), FDI 74% automatic.
- Results (label FY): **defence production ₹1.27 lakh cr (FY24) → ~₹1.5 lakh cr (FY25, record)**; **defence exports ₹21,083 cr (FY24) → ₹23,622 cr (FY25, record)** — to 100+ countries; India still among top arms **importers** — the balancing critique.
