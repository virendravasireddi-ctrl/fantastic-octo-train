## PART A — FERMENTATION

## 1. What is fermentation? ⭐

- **Fermentation = a metabolic process in which micro-organisms (or their enzymes) convert organic substrates (sugars) into simpler compounds, usually in the absence of oxygen (anaerobic), releasing energy and useful products.** In **industrial biotechnology**, it is broadened to *any large-scale cultivation of microbes to make a product* (even aerobic, e.g., antibiotics) ⭐.
- **Discovery:** **Louis Pasteur — "father of fermentation / microbiology"** showed fermentation is caused by living microbes ⭐; Eduard Buchner showed cell-free enzymes can ferment (Nobel 1907).
- **The classic biochemistry:** anaerobic breakdown of glucose (**glycolysis**) → **ethanol + CO₂** (alcoholic, by *Saccharomyces* yeast) or **lactic acid** (by *Lactobacillus*). It is the basis of **"old" biotechnology**.

## 2. Types of fermentation ⭐

| Basis | Types |
|---|---|
| **By product/pathway** | **Alcoholic (ethanol), Lactic acid, Acetic acid (vinegar), Butyric, Propionic** |
| **By oxygen** | **Anaerobic** (alcohol, lactic) vs **Aerobic** (antibiotics, vinegar, citric acid) |
| **By operation** | **Batch, Fed-batch, Continuous** |
| **By medium** | **Submerged (liquid)** vs **Solid-state** fermentation |

- **The bioreactor / fermenter** — the controlled vessel (temperature, pH, aeration, agitation) where industrial fermentation happens; **downstream processing** then separates & purifies the product.

## 3. Products & applications of fermentation ⭐⭐

| Product class | Examples |
|---|---|
| **Alcoholic beverages & foods** | Beer, wine; **bread (leavening), curd/yoghurt, cheese, idli/dosa, pickles, soy sauce** |
| **Antibiotics** | **Penicillin (*Penicillium*), streptomycin, tetracycline** ⭐ |
| **Organic acids** | **Citric acid (*Aspergillus niger*), acetic acid, lactic acid** |
| **Enzymes** | Amylase, protease, lipase (detergents, food, textiles) |
| **Vitamins & amino acids** | B₁₂, riboflavin; glutamic acid (MSG), lysine |
| **Biofuels** | **Bioethanol (ethanol blending — E20), biogas/CBG** ⭐ |
| **Single-Cell Protein (SCP)** | Microbial biomass as protein/feed |
| **Others** | Steroids, vaccines, biopolymers (PHB), probiotics |

- **Significance for India ⭐:** underpins **ethanol blending (energy), antibiotics & vaccines (health), dairy & food processing (nutrition), biogas (rural energy)** — a **White-biotech** engine of the **BioE3** bioeconomy (File 11).

## PART B — IMMUNO-DIAGNOSIS TECHNIQUES

## 4. The principle — antigen–antibody reaction ⭐⭐

- **Immunodiagnosis = detecting a disease/condition by the highly specific binding between an antigen (Ag) and an antibody (Ab).** Because the reaction is **specific and sensitive**, it is the basis of most modern medical diagnostics ⭐.
- **Antigen** = a foreign molecule (on a pathogen) that triggers an immune response; **antibody (immunoglobulin)** = the protein that binds it. A test either **detects the antigen** (is the pathogen present?) or **detects antibodies** (has the body responded/been exposed?).
- **Monoclonal antibodies** — identical antibodies from a single clone, mass-produced by the **hybridoma technique (Köhler & Milstein, Nobel 1984)** ⭐ — the workhorse of diagnostics & therapeutics.

## 5. The major immunodiagnostic techniques ⭐⭐

| Technique | Basis / use |
|---|---|
| **ELISA (Enzyme-Linked Immunosorbent Assay)** ⭐⭐ | Ag–Ab binding revealed by an **enzyme-linked colour change**; types = **direct, indirect, sandwich, competitive**; used for **HIV, hepatitis, dengue, COVID antibodies, hormones (pregnancy)** |
| **RIA (Radioimmunoassay)** | Uses a **radioactive-labelled** antigen; very sensitive (hormones); developed by **Rosalyn Yalow (Nobel)** |
| **Western Blot** | Detects **specific proteins/antibodies** after gel separation — a **confirmatory HIV test** ⭐ |
| **Lateral Flow Assay (immunochromatography)** | **Rapid "strip" tests** — **home pregnancy test, COVID Rapid Antigen Test, malaria RDT** ⭐ (point-of-care) |
| **Immunofluorescence (IFA)** | Antibody tagged with a **fluorescent dye**, seen under UV microscope |
| **CLIA (Chemiluminescence Immunoassay)** | Light-emitting label — automated, high-throughput labs |
| **Agglutination tests** | Visible clumping — **blood grouping, Widal test (typhoid), CRP, RA factor** ⭐ |
| **Immunohistochemistry (IHC)** | Antibodies locate antigens in **tissue sections** (cancer typing) |

- **Molecular vs immuno diagnostics (don't confuse) ⭐:** **PCR/RT-PCR detects the pathogen's nucleic acid (genetic material)** — it is a **molecular** test, *not* immunological; **ELISA/rapid-antigen/antibody tests are immunodiagnostic**. COVID-19 made this distinction famous (RT-PCR = confirmatory molecular; Rapid Antigen = immuno).
- **Applications:** **infectious-disease screening (HIV, hepatitis, dengue, COVID, typhoid), pregnancy & hormone tests, blood typing, cancer markers, allergy testing, autoimmune diagnosis** — and **plant/veterinary disease diagnosis** (relevant to **AP's crop & shrimp-disease testing** ⭐).

## 6. Prelims nuggets

1. **Fermentation — Louis Pasteur**; anaerobic glucose → **ethanol + CO₂ (yeast)** or **lactic acid (*Lactobacillus*)**.
2. **Types: alcoholic/lactic/acetic; aerobic vs anaerobic; batch/continuous; submerged vs solid-state**; vessel = **bioreactor/fermenter**.
3. **Penicillin — *Penicillium*; Citric acid — *Aspergillus niger*; Curd — *Lactobacillus*; Ethanol — *Saccharomyces* (yeast)**.
4. Fermentation products: **antibiotics, enzymes, organic acids, vitamins, bioethanol, biogas, SCP**.
5. **Immunodiagnosis = antigen–antibody specificity**; **monoclonal antibodies — hybridoma (Köhler & Milstein, 1984)**.
6. **ELISA (enzyme label; direct/indirect/sandwich/competitive) — HIV/dengue/pregnancy**; **RIA = radioactive label**.
7. **Western blot = confirmatory HIV test**; **lateral flow = rapid strip (pregnancy, COVID antigen, malaria RDT)**.
8. **Widal (typhoid) & blood grouping = agglutination**; **immunofluorescence = fluorescent tag**; **CLIA = chemiluminescence**.
9. **PCR/RT-PCR = molecular (nucleic-acid) test, NOT immunodiagnostic**.
10. **Hybridoma = B-cell + myeloma → monoclonal antibodies**.
