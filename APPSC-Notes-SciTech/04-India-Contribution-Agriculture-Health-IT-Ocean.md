### 1.2 Institutions & modern tech
- **ICAR (1929)** apex; **IARI (Pusa) 1905**; **NBPGR** gene bank; **KVKs** (~730 farm science centres for lab-to-land) ⭐; **CRIDA (Hyderabad)** dryland; **ICRISAT (Hyderabad — international)** semi-arid crops; **DRR / IIRR (Hyderabad)** rice.
- Biotech in agri: **Bt cotton (2002 — only approved GM crop for cultivation)**; **GM mustard DMH-11 (env clearance 2022, sub judice)**; **Bt brinjal (moratorium 2010)**; **CRISPR/genome-edited rice varieties "Kamala/Pusa DST Rice 1" & "DRR Dhan 100" released 2025** ⭐ (India among first to release genome-edited rice — exempted SDN-1/2 from GMO rules, 2022).
- **Nano-urea & nano-DAP (IFFCO, 2021/2023)** — Indian-developed nano-fertilisers; agri-drones (Namo Drone Didi); **e-NAM**, soil health cards, PM-KISAN via DBT, weather advisories (**Krishi/Meghdoot**); **Digital Agriculture Mission (2024, ₹2,817 cr)** — AgriStack, farmer ID.
- Second Green Revolution focus = **East India, millets** (India led **International Year of Millets 2023**; "**Shree Anna**").

## 2. Health, biotechnology & pharma ⭐⭐

### 2.1 "Pharmacy of the world"
- India = **largest supplier of generic medicines (~20% of global volume)**, **60%+ of global vaccine doses**; **Serum Institute** = world's largest vaccine maker by volume.
- **COVID-19 response ⭐ (marquee example):** **Covaxin** (Bharat Biotech, **Hyderabad** — India's first indigenous vaccine, ICMR-NIV collaboration), Covishield (SII); **CoWIN** open-source platform (~220 cr doses); **ZyCoV-D (Zydus)** — world's first **DNA plasmid** vaccine; **iNCOVACC** — world's first intranasal COVID vaccine. Use as the definitive "integration of STI for human life" case.
- Other indigenous vaccine firsts: **Rotavac** (rotavirus), **indigenous HPV vaccine "CERVAVAC" (SII, 2022)**, TB/typhoid conjugate; **UIP** now 12 diseases.

### 2.2 Institutions & platforms
- **ICMR (1911)** apex biomedical; **CSIR-CCMB, Hyderabad** — genomics/cell biology; **CDFD Hyderabad** (DNA fingerprinting — Lalji Singh); **CCMB-linked LaCONES** (endangered-species cloning). **DBT/BIRAC**; **BRIC (2023)** unifies 14 autonomous bio-institutes; **Genome India Project** — 10,000 Indian genomes sequenced (2024).
- **Digital health:** **Ayushman Bharat Digital Mission (ABHA IDs)**, **eSanjeevani** (world's largest govt telemedicine, 30+ crore consults), **U-WIN** (immunisation), Jan Aushadhi generic stores (~15,000).
- Frontier: indigenous **CAR-T cell therapy — "NexCAR19" (IIT-Bombay–Tata Memorial–ImmunoACT), CDSCO approval Oct 2023** — world's most affordable; makes India one of few nations with the platform ⭐. Made-in-India MRI (SAMEER, 2025), Made-in-India antibiotic **Nafithromycin/"Miqnaf" (2024)**.

### 2.3 AP tie-in
- Hyderabad's legacy institutions (CCMB, CDFD, ICRISAT, IICT) are historically the "**Genome/Vaccine Valley**"; post-bifurcation AP is building **MedTech (AMTZ, Visakhapatnam)** and pharma clusters; strong bulk-drug presence.

## 3. Information Technology & digital public infrastructure ⭐⭐⭐

### 3.1 The IT story
- Software services boom from the 1990s (STPI scheme, Y2K); **IT-BPM revenue ~$250+ bn, ~5.4 crore-plus employment ecosystem**; India = back-office and now product/GCC hub (**1,700+ Global Capability Centres**).
- Supercomputing self-reliance origin: **PARAM (C-DAC, 1991)** after CRAY denial ⭐; today **NSM/PARAM Rudra, AIRAWAT**.

### 3.2 Digital Public Infrastructure (DPI) — India's most-copied export ⭐⭐
- **Aadhaar (UIDAI, 2009/2010)** — world's largest biometric ID (~138 cr).
- **UPI (NPCI, 2016)** — interoperable instant payments; **~half of the world's real-time digital-payment transactions**; ~1,800+ crore transactions/month (2025); internationalised (UAE, Singapore, France, Sri Lanka, etc.).
- **India Stack** (identity + payments + data via **DEPA/Account Aggregator**), **DigiLocker, CoWIN, DIKSHA, Bhashini** (AI language), **ONDC** (open commerce), **FASTag**. India offered DPI as a **global public good at G20 (2023 presidency)**; **Global DPI Repository + Social Impact Fund**.
- Governance dividend (DBT): cumulative leakage savings claimed **>₹3.5 lakh crore**.

### 3.3 Frontier compute
- **AI:** IndiaAI Mission (File 02); indigenous LLMs **Sarvam, BharatGen, Krutrim**; AI Impact Summit Feb 2026.
- **Quantum & semiconductors:** NQM; ISM fabs; **Vikram 3201** first indigenous space microprocessor (2025); Digital India RISC-V (**DIR-V**) — indigenous **SHAKTI (IIT-M)** and **VEGA (C-DAC)** processors ⭐.

## 4. Earth, ocean, Antarctic & atmospheric science ⭐

- **MoES (2006)** umbrella. **IMD (1875)** — 150 years in 2025; monsoon forecasting, Doppler radar network, **Mission Mausam (2024)**.
- **INCOIS (Hyderabad)** — **Tsunami Early Warning Centre (2007, post-2004 tsunami)** serving the Indian Ocean; **PFZ** & ocean-state forecasts for AP/coastal fishers ⭐.
- **NCPOR (Goa)** — polar/ocean research; Antarctic stations **Maitri (1989)** & **Bharati (2012)** (Dakshin Gangotri 1983 — first, now submerged); **Himadri** Arctic station (Svalbard, 2008); Antarctic Act 2022.
- **Deep Ocean Mission (2021)** — **Samudrayaan/MATSYA-6000** human submersible (sea trials 2025-26); polymetallic-nodule mining rights in Central Indian Ocean.
- **NIOT (Chennai)** — desalination, ocean-energy; research vessels **Sagar Nidhi/Kanya**.
