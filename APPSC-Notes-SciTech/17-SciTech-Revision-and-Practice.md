## 2. Twenty MCQs (key at the end) ⭐

1. "Green biotechnology" refers to — (a) medical (b) **agricultural** (c) industrial (d) marine.
2. Restriction enzymes act as — (a) glue (b) **molecular scissors** (c) vectors (d) hosts.
3. India's only GM crop approved for cultivation is — (a) Bt brinjal (b) GM mustard (c) **Bt cotton** (d) Golden Rice.
4. The 2020 Nobel-winning gene-editing tool is — (a) PCR (b) ELISA (c) **CRISPR-Cas9** (d) hybridoma.
5. The apex regulator for GMO release in India is the — (a) DBT (b) **GEAC** (c) NBA (d) FSSAI.
6. GM mustard DMH-11 received in July 2024 a — (a) full approval (b) ban (c) **Supreme Court split verdict** (d) patent.
7. The "father of fermentation/microbiology" is — (a) Buchner (b) **Pasteur** (c) Fleming (d) Koch.
8. Citric acid is industrially produced by — (a) *Lactobacillus* (b) **Aspergillus niger** (c) yeast (d) *E. coli*.
9. ELISA is based on — (a) radioactivity (b) **enzyme-linked antigen-antibody reaction** (c) PCR (d) fluorescence only.
10. The confirmatory test for HIV is — (a) ELISA (b) **Western blot** (c) Widal (d) RT-PCR.
11. RT-PCR is a — (a) immunodiagnostic (b) **molecular (nucleic-acid) test** (c) agglutination (d) rapid antigen test.
12. Monoclonal antibodies are produced by — (a) PCR (b) **hybridoma technology** (c) ELISA (d) fermentation.
13. The nanoscale is defined as — (a) 1–100 µm (b) **1–100 nm** (c) 1–100 mm (d) 1–100 pm.
14. The world's first commercial nano-fertiliser is — (a) Nano DAP (b) **IFFCO Nano Urea** (c) urea gold (d) neem-coated urea.
15. India's Nano Mission (2007) is run by the — (a) DBT (b) **DST** (c) MoEFCC (d) ICAR.
16. India has how many global biodiversity hotspots — (a) 2 (b) 3 (c) **4** (d) 6.
17. Conserving species in gene banks/zoos is — (a) in-situ (b) **ex-situ** (c) bioremediation (d) bioprospecting.
18. The National Biodiversity Authority is at — (a) Delhi (b) **Chennai** (c) Hyderabad (d) Dehradun.
19. India's 2024 national biotechnology policy is — (a) NBDS (b) **BioE3** (c) BRAI (d) Genome India.
20. Section 3(j) of the Patents Act bars patents on — (a) drugs (b) **plants, animals & seeds** (c) software (d) processes.

**Key:** 1-b, 2-b, 3-c, 4-c, 5-b, 6-c, 7-b, 8-b, 9-b, 10-b, 11-b, 12-b, 13-b, 14-b, 15-b, 16-c, 17-b, 18-b, 19-b, 20-b.

## 3. Mains answer-skeletons ⭐

1. **Nature, scope & applications of biotechnology in India** → definition/rainbow → Red/Green/White/Blue applications → bioeconomy $130→300 bn (BioE3) → challenges.
2. **"Genetic engineering — a double-edged sword."** → rDNA/CRISPR gains → biosafety/ecological/ethical/socio-economic risks → India's GM-crop saga → regulation.
3. **Fermentation — the versatile biotechnology** → principle/types → products (antibiotics/enzymes/ethanol/biogas/dairy) → bioeconomy role.
4. **Immunodiagnostics & the antigen-antibody principle** → ELISA/Western blot/rapid tests → molecular vs immuno (COVID) → public-health impact.
5. **Nanotechnology — platform technology for India** → nature/scope → medicine/agri (Nano Urea)/water/energy → Nano Mission → toxicity/regulation.
6. **"Biodiversity is the genetic capital of biotechnology."** → 3 levels → bioprospecting/ABS/gene banks → conservation → anti-biopiracy.
7. **ELSI of biotech & nanotech** → ethical/social/legal concerns → regulatory frame → balanced-governance verdict.
8. **"Making biotechnology India's next IT."** → DBT/BIRAC/BioE3/Nano Mission/Genome India → bioeconomy target → skills/R&D/regulation gaps.
9. **India's biotech-regulation reform** → GEAC/RCGM/BD Act/PPV&FR patchwork → BRAI gap → single-window regulator.
10. **Andhra Pradesh's bio-economy potential** → AMTZ Vizag + agri-seed + aqua-biotech + Eastern-Ghats bioresources → constraints.
