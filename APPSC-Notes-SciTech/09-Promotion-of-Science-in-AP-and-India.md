## 0. Why "promotion of science" is a distinct topic
Doing science (research) and **spreading science** (communication, education, temper-building) are different tasks with different institutions. "Promotion" = building a **scientifically literate society** — the constitutional mandate of **scientific temper (Art. 51A(h))**. It works on three fronts: **science education & talent, science communication/popularisation, and science infrastructure for the public** (museums, centres, planetariums).

## 2. National institutions & programmes ⭐⭐ (match body ↔ ministry ↔ job)

### 2.1 Science communication (DST family)
- **NCSTC — National Council for S&T Communication (DST)** — apex coordinator of science popularisation; runs the **National Children's Science Congress**, National Science Day activities, science-communication awards, **scientific-temper** campaigns.
- **Vigyan Prasar (1989, DST autonomous body)** — science media: *Dream 2047* magazine, **India Science** OTT channel, radio serials, books; its functions were **reorganised under NCSTC/a new dispensation (2023)** — quote as "Vigyan Prasar's science-media mandate."
- **CSIR outreach** — **Jigyasa** (student–scientist connect), **One Week One Lab (2023)** opening CSIR labs to the public.
- **IISF — India International Science Festival (since 2015)** — the annual mega science-festival (DST/MoES + Vijnana Bharati).
- **Media & language:** DD Science/India Science channel, podcasts, folk-media troupes; **Bhashini** AI translation for vernacular science.

### 2.2 Public science infrastructure — NCSM ⭐ (Ministry of **Culture**, not DST — trap!)
- **NCSM — National Council of Science Museums (1978), HQ Kolkata** — runs India's science-museum network under the **Ministry of Culture**.
- **Science Cities** (large): **Kolkata Science City (1997 — India's largest)**, Ahmedabad (Gujarat Science City), Kapurthala (Pushpa Gujral); more sanctioned.
- **Regional & Sub-Regional Science Centres**, **planetariums** (incl. flagship **Nehru Planetariums**), **innovation hubs**, mobile science exhibitions & science-on-wheels.

### 2.3 Talent, temper & responsibility
- **INSPIRE-MANAK** (school "Million Minds" idea contest), **Vigyan Jyoti** (girls in STEM), **olympiads** via **HBCSE-TIFR (Mumbai)**, **PMRF** (doctoral).
- **Rashtriya Vigyan Puraskar (2024)** — unified national science awards (Vigyan Ratna/Shri/Yuva/Team).
- **Scientific Social Responsibility (SSR) Guidelines 2022 (DST)** ⭐ — asks scientists/institutions to devote effort to outreach — the "give back to society" mandate.
- **Science days calendar:** **28 Feb** National Science Day (Raman) · **11 May** Technology Day · **23 Aug** National Space Day · **22 Dec** Mathematics Day · Feb–IISF.
- **Historic vehicles:** **Science Express train (2007–)** — rolling exhibition across India; National Science Seminars; science clubs.

## 3. Promotion of Science in Andhra Pradesh ⭐⭐ (AP-core — highest yield)

### 3.1 APCOST — the state's engine
- **APCOST — Andhra Pradesh State Council of Science & Technology (1986)** — the nodal state body to "**foster the spirit of science at all levels of society, especially youth**."
- Functions: science **popularisation & children's science congress (state chapter)**, **Patent Information Centre (PIC)** for inventors/MSMEs, remote-sensing/GIS applications, S&T project funding, energy & environment programmes.

### 3.2 Public science centres & planetariums in AP
- **Regional Science Centre, Vijayawada** — set up by APCOST to democratise science for schoolchildren (science, maths, social studies exhibits).
- **Sub-Regional Science Centre, Rajahmundry (Rajamahendravaram)** — sanctioned by **Ministry of Culture / NCSM**; **inaugurated 26 June 2025** ⭐ (freshest AP fact).
- **Planetariums** across AP cities and university science-outreach (Andhra University, SV University, etc.); district science fairs and exhibitions.

### 3.3 Talent & institutional promotion (AP-original models)
- **RGUKT (IIIT — Nuzvid, RK Valley, Srikakulam, Ongole) (2008)** ⭐ — residential technology universities for **rural gifted students**; an AP-original *proliferation-of-science* model.
- **AP Innovation Society (APIS)**, university incubators, **Atal Tinkering Labs** in AP schools, INSPIRE-MANAK state participation.
- **Amaravati Quantum Valley + Visakhapatnam AI hub** (2025) — aspirational anchors pulling frontier science into the state (see [File 02 §3A](02-National-STI-Policies.md)).

### 3.4 The people's-science movement
- **Jana Vignana Vedika (JVV)** — the Telugu-states **People's Science Movement** — grassroots campaigns against superstition, pro-scientific-temper, literacy & health science drives ⭐ — the civil-society face of science promotion in AP.

## 4. Concerns in science promotion (evaluation ammunition)
- **Reach & language:** English-dominant science content vs vernacular need (Bhashini, JVV as answers).
- **Pseudoscience & misinformation:** the 51A(h) gap — superstition, anti-vaccine/anti-science content; deepfakes.
- **Rural–urban & gender divides** in STEM access; laboratory-to-classroom disconnect.
- **Sustaining interest:** attracting talent to research careers vs the pull of IT/abroad (brain drain — File 05).
