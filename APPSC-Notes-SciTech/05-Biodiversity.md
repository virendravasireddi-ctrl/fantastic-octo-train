# 05 · Biodiversity — Levels, India's Wealth, Threats & Conservation

> In the biotechnology syllabus, **biodiversity = the raw genetic-resource base** for biotech (crops, drugs, enzymes, germplasm) — which is why **bioprospecting, benefit-sharing and gene banks** matter here. (Complements the Environment series' ecological treatment.)

## 1. What is biodiversity? ⭐⭐

- **Biodiversity (biological diversity) = the variety and variability of all life** — the term was popularised by **E.O. Wilson** ⭐.
- **Three levels (learn all three) ⭐⭐:**
  1. **Genetic diversity** — variation of genes *within* a species (e.g., thousands of rice varieties, breeds of cattle) — the base for **plant/animal breeding & biotech**.
  2. **Species diversity** — variety of species in a region (richness + evenness).
  3. **Ecosystem diversity** — variety of habitats/ecosystems (forests, wetlands, deserts, coral reefs).
- **Measures:** **alpha (within a habitat), beta (between habitats), gamma (regional)** diversity.

## 2. India's biodiversity — the numbers ⭐⭐

- India = one of the **17 "megadiverse" countries** ⭐; with ~**2.4% of world land area**, it holds ~**7–8% of the world's recorded species** (and rising with new discoveries).
- India has **4 of the world's 36 biodiversity "hotspots"** ⭐⭐ (a hotspot = high endemism + high threat, ≥1,500 endemic plants & ≥70% habitat lost — concept by **Norman Myers**):
  1. **The Himalaya**
  2. **Western Ghats–Sri Lanka**
  3. **Indo-Burma** (NE India)
  4. **Sundaland (Nicobar Islands)**
- **10 biogeographic zones** (Trans-Himalaya, Himalaya, Desert, Semi-arid, **Western Ghats, Deccan Peninsula, Gangetic Plain, Coasts, NE, Islands**); rich in **crop-origin diversity (a Vavilov centre)** — wild relatives of rice, brinjal, banana, pepper, millets.
- **Endemism** — species found nowhere else (Nilgiri tahr, lion-tailed macaque, Western-Ghats amphibians; **AP's Red Sanders**).

## 3. Value of biodiversity ⭐

- **Direct/consumptive:** food, timber, fuel, fibre, **medicine (Green Pharmacy — File links), genetic resources for biotech**.
- **Indirect/ecosystem services:** pollination, soil formation, water & climate regulation, nutrient cycling, carbon sink.
- **Option value** (future/undiscovered uses — new drugs) and **existence/cultural value** (sacred groves, ethics).
- **Bioprospecting ⭐:** searching biodiversity for **new genes, molecules & drugs** — the biotech–biodiversity bridge; must be paired with **Access & Benefit-Sharing (ABS)** to avoid **biopiracy** (Neem/Turmeric cases — File 06).

## 4. Threats to biodiversity ⭐ ("HIPPO")

**H**abitat loss & fragmentation (deforestation, wetland/coast conversion) → **I**nvasive alien species (Lantana, Water hyacinth, African catfish) → **P**ollution → **P**opulation/over-exploitation (poaching, over-fishing) → **O**ver-harvesting + **climate change**. Result: **species extinction & genetic erosion** (loss of landraces to monoculture — the Green-Revolution cost).

## 5. Conservation of biodiversity ⭐⭐

| Strategy | Meaning | Examples (with AP) |
|---|---|---|
| **In-situ** (in the natural habitat) | Protect ecosystems in place | **National Parks, Wildlife Sanctuaries, Biosphere Reserves, Conservation/Community Reserves, sacred groves, Tiger/Elephant Reserves**; **AP: Nagarjunasagar-Srisailam Tiger Reserve (Nallamala), Seshachalam Biosphere Reserve, Coringa (mangroves), Kolleru** ⭐ |
| **Ex-situ** (outside habitat) | Conserve components off-site | **Gene/seed banks (NBPGR), botanical gardens, zoos, cryopreservation, tissue-culture repositories, DNA banks** ⭐ |

- **IUCN Red List** ⭐ — global species-threat status: **Extinct → Extinct in Wild → Critically Endangered → Endangered → Vulnerable → Near Threatened → Least Concern**.
- **Institutional/legal:** **Wildlife (Protection) Act 1972; Biological Diversity Act 2002 → NBA (Chennai)/SBB/BMC + People's Biodiversity Registers; CBD 1992 & the Kunming-Montreal GBF (2022 — "30×30")** — full legislation in File 06 & Environment-series File 11.
- **Gene banks for biotech:** **NBPGR (plant germplasm), NBAGR (animal), NBFGR (fish), NBAIM (microbes)** — the "national bureaus" that safeguard genetic diversity ⭐.

## 6. Andhra Pradesh biodiversity ⭐

- **Eastern Ghats (Nallamala, Seshachalam, Papikonda, Araku)** — biodiverse hill forests with strong **tribal ethno-botanical (medicinal-plant) knowledge**; **Red Sanders (endemic, CITES-listed)** is the flagship ⭐.
- **Wetlands & coast:** **Coringa mangroves (Godavari estuary), Kolleru Lake (Ramsar), Krishna estuary** — but note the Eastern Ghats/Deccan are **not** in a global hotspot (unlike the Western Ghats), so AP biodiversity is **rich but under-protected** — a good Mains nuance.
- **AP State Biodiversity Board** + BMCs + People's Biodiversity Registers implement the BD Act locally.

## 7. Prelims nuggets

1. **Biodiversity — 3 levels: genetic, species, ecosystem** (term popularised by **E.O. Wilson**).
2. India = **1 of 17 megadiverse countries; ~7–8% of world species on ~2.4% land**.
3. **4 hotspots in India: Himalaya, Western Ghats–Sri Lanka, Indo-Burma, Sundaland (Nicobar)** — hotspot concept by **Norman Myers**.
4. **10 biogeographic zones; India = a Vavilov crop-origin centre**.
5. Threats = **HIPPO (Habitat loss, Invasives, Pollution, Population/over-exploitation, Over-harvest) + climate**.
6. **In-situ = parks/sanctuaries/biosphere reserves; Ex-situ = gene banks/zoos/botanical gardens/cryo**.
7. **IUCN Red List: EX→EW→CR→EN→VU→NT→LC**.
8. **Gene banks: NBPGR (plants), NBAGR (animals), NBFGR (fish), NBAIM (microbes)**.
9. **Biological Diversity Act 2002 → NBA (Chennai)/SBB/BMC + People's Biodiversity Register**; **Bioprospecting + ABS** guards against **biopiracy**.
10. **AP: Nallamala Tiger Reserve, Seshachalam Biosphere, Coringa, Kolleru; Red Sanders (endemic, CITES)**.

## 8. Mains angles

1. **"Biodiversity is the genetic capital of biotechnology." Discuss** — 3 levels → bioprospecting for drugs/genes → ABS/anti-biopiracy → gene banks → sustainable use.
2. **"India is megadiverse but its biodiversity is under siege." Examine** — hotspots/megadiverse status → HIPPO threats → in-situ + ex-situ + legal frame → 30×30 (GBF 2022).
3. **"Andhra Pradesh's biodiversity is rich but under-protected." Analyse** — Eastern-Ghats/wetland wealth vs no-hotspot status → Red Sanders/medicinal plants → conservation & tribal-knowledge benefit-sharing.
4. **"Conserving genetic diversity is a national-security issue." Comment** — crop wild relatives, gene banks, climate-resilient breeding, food security.
