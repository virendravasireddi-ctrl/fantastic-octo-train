# APPSC Notes — Science, Technology & Innovation

**Target exams:** APPSC Group-I Mains (the **Science & Technology** block of Paper-V / General Studies) and Group-II, and useful for other state PSCs and any general-science / current-S&T paper. Source-first and answer-oriented: every file closes with **prelims nuggets**, **bolded match-pairs** for flashcards and **Mains answer-skeletons**, with **Andhra Pradesh tie-ins** throughout (ISRO-Sriharikota, AMTZ Visakhapatnam, agri-seed & aqua-biotech, APCOST, Eastern-Ghats bioresources).

This series has **two blocks**:

- **Block A — Science, Technology & Innovation for nation-building (Files 01–10):** STI in everyday life, national STI policies, India's contributions (space/atomic/defence/agri/health/IT/ocean), concerns & challenges, scientific institutes, Indian scientists, IPR, and the promotion of science.
- **Block B — Biotechnology & Nanotechnology (Files 11–17):** nature/scope/applications of biotech and nanotech, genetic engineering, fermentation, immuno-diagnosis, biodiversity, and the ethical–social–legal concerns and government policies.

## Syllabus items covered

> **Block A —** *"Integration of Science, Technology and Innovation for better human life; Science & Technology in everyday life; National Policies on proliferation of Science, Technology and Innovation; India's contribution in the field of Science and Technology; Concerns and challenges in the proliferation and use of science and technology; Role and Scope of Science and Technology in nation building; Major Scientific institutes for Science and Technology and for Research and Development in AP and India; Achievements of Indian Scientists; Indigenous technologies and developing new technologies."*

> **Block B —** *"Nature, Scope and Applications of Biotechnology and Nanotechnology in India; Ethical, Social and Legal concerns, Government policies; Genetic engineering, issues related to it and its impact on human life; Bio-diversity; Fermentation; Immuno-diagnosis techniques."*
