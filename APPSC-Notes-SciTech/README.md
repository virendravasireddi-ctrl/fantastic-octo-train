# APPSC Notes — Science, Technology & Innovation

**Target exams:** APPSC Group-I Mains (General Studies — Science & Technology component) and Group-II. This series covers the full APPSC syllabus block on **Science, Technology and Innovation**.

## Syllabus block covered

> *"Integration of Science, Technology and Innovation for better human life; Science & Technology in everyday life; National Policies on proliferation of Science, Technology and Innovation; India's contribution in the field of Science and Technology; Concerns and challenges in the proliferation and use of science and technology; Role and Scope of Science and Technology in nation building; Major Scientific institutes for Science and Technology in AP and India; Major Scientific Institutes for Research and Development in AP and India; Achievements of Indian Scientists in the field of Science and Technology; Indigenous technologies and developing new technologies."*

## Exam-craft notes

- **Prelims:** this block is match-the-following heavy — *institute↔city↔agency*, *policy↔year*, *scientist↔achievement↔Nobel*, *mission↔outlay*, *day↔event* are **bolded** throughout for flashcards.
- **Mains:** open with a definition/number, argue with 2–3 named missions/institutions, close with an **evaluation + an AP tie-in** (SHAR, NSTL, Tummalapalle, aqua-tech, AMTZ, Amaravati Quantum Valley, INCOIS cyclone warning).
- **Traps flagged inline:** invention ≠ innovation; NRSC = ISRO but NGRI = CSIR and INCOIS = MoES; BrahMos ∉ IGMDP; Hyderabad institutes are in Telangana, not residuary AP; capacity ≠ generation (cross-ref Economy File 07).
