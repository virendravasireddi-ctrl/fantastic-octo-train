## 1. What is genetic engineering? ⭐

- **Genetic engineering = the deliberate modification of an organism's genetic material (DNA) using laboratory techniques** — inserting, deleting or editing genes to give new traits. Its core is **recombinant DNA (rDNA) technology** ⭐.
- **A Genetically Modified Organism (GMO) / transgenic organism** carries a gene transferred from another organism (or an edited gene).

## 2. The rDNA toolkit — how genes are engineered ⭐⭐

| Tool | Role |
|---|---|
| **Restriction enzymes ("molecular scissors")** | Cut DNA at specific sequences (**EcoRI** the classic) ⭐ |
| **DNA ligase ("molecular glue")** | Joins DNA fragments |
| **Vectors** — **plasmids, bacteriophages; *Agrobacterium tumefaciens* (plants)** | Carry the gene into the host |
| **Host cell** — *E. coli*, yeast, plant/animal cells | Where the recombinant DNA is expressed |
| **PCR** | Amplifies the target gene |
| **Gene guns / microinjection / electroporation** | Physical gene-transfer methods |
| **Selectable markers & probes** | Identify successfully modified cells |

**Steps (learn the sequence):** isolate the gene of interest → cut with restriction enzyme → insert into a vector (recombinant DNA) → introduce into host → select & multiply transformed cells → express the desired product.

## 3. Gene editing — the new frontier ⭐

- **CRISPR-Cas9** ⭐⭐ — a precise, cheap "cut-and-edit" tool (Nobel Chemistry **2020, Doudna & Charpentier**); edits genes *in situ* without adding foreign DNA; used for **crop improvement, gene therapy, disease models**. India: DBT gene-editing guidelines; exemption of **SDN-1/SDN-2 (no foreign DNA) edited plants** from some GM rules (2022) — a major policy easing ⭐.
- Related: **gene therapy** (correcting defective genes — e.g., for thalassaemia/sickle cell), **synthetic biology, RNA interference, antisense technology**.

## 4. Applications & impact on human life ⭐⭐

### 4.1 Positive impacts
| Domain | Impact |
|---|---|
| **Medicine (Red)** | **Recombinant insulin (Humulin — the first rDNA drug, 1982)** ⭐, growth hormone, clotting factors; **recombinant vaccines (Hepatitis-B)**; **gene therapy**; monoclonal antibodies; molecular diagnosis of genetic disease |
| **Agriculture (Green)** | **Bt crops (pest resistance), Ht crops (herbicide tolerance), Golden Rice (β-carotene/Vitamin-A)** ⭐, drought/salt tolerance, longer shelf-life (Flavr Savr tomato), bio-fortification |
| **Industry & environment** | Engineered microbes for **enzymes, biofuels, bioplastics, bioremediation** (oil-eating bacteria) |
| **Forensics & ancestry** | **DNA fingerprinting** (crime, paternity, disaster victim ID) |

### 4.2 GM crops in India — the status you must quote ⭐⭐
- **Bt cotton — the ONLY GM crop approved for commercial cultivation in India (2002)** ⭐ (transformed India into a top cotton exporter; but pink-bollworm resistance & Bt-cotton-farmer-distress debates).
- **Bt brinjal — GEAC recommended (2007) but placed under an indefinite moratorium (2010)** by the Environment Minister (Jairam Ramesh) after protests ⭐.
- **GM Mustard (DMH-11)** — GEAC approved environmental release (**Oct 2022**), but the **Supreme Court delivered a SPLIT VERDICT (July 2024, Gene Campaign v. UoI — one judge quashed, one upheld); referred to a larger bench — not yet implemented** ⭐⭐ (the live current-affairs fact).

## 5. Issues & concerns related to genetic engineering ⭐⭐

| Concern type | Details |
|---|---|
| **Biosafety / health** | Allergenicity & toxicity of GM foods; **antibiotic-resistance marker genes**; unknown long-term effects |
| **Environmental / ecological** | **Gene flow to wild relatives ("genetic contamination"), super-weeds/super-pests, loss of biodiversity & monoculture, harm to non-target species** (the Bt-monarch-butterfly debate) ⭐ |
| **Socio-economic** | **Corporate control of seed & "terminator" technology, farmer dependence & debt, MNC monopoly (IPR), threat to small farmers** ⭐ |
| **Ethical** | "Playing God", **designer babies & germline editing, human cloning, eugenics** (the **He Jiankui CRISPR-babies scandal, 2018**) ⭐, consent, animal welfare |
| **Legal / IPR** | **Patenting of life-forms & seeds (TRIPS), biopiracy**, GM-food labelling, liability |
| **Biosecurity** | **Bioweapons / "dark biotechnology"**, dual-use research |

## 6. Regulation of genetic engineering in India (overview — full in File 06) ⭐

- **Rules 1989 under the Environment (Protection) Act, 1986** govern GMOs; the apex regulator is the **Genetic Engineering Appraisal Committee (GEAC), under MoEFCC** ⭐; supported by the **Review Committee on Genetic Manipulation (RCGM, under DBT)** and **Institutional Biosafety Committees (IBSC)**.
- **Cartagena Protocol on Biosafety (2000)** — international frame for transboundary movement of **Living Modified Organisms (LMOs)**; India is a party.
- The proposed **Biotechnology Regulatory Authority of India (BRAI) Bill** (a single-window regulator) **lapsed** — regulation remains fragmented (a Mains critique point).

## 7. The GM debate in one line (for balanced answers) ⭐

**For:** food & nutrition security, less pesticide, climate-resilient crops, medical breakthroughs. **Against:** biosafety/ecological risk, corporate seed-control, ethical limits, weak regulation. **Verdict line:** *"case-by-case, science-based, transparent regulation with independent long-term testing and farmer safeguards."*

## 8. Prelims nuggets

1. **rDNA tools: restriction enzymes (cut, e.g. EcoRI) + DNA ligase (join) + vectors (plasmid/*Agrobacterium*) + host (*E. coli*)**.
2. **CRISPR-Cas9 — Nobel 2020 (Doudna & Charpentier)**; **SDN-1/2 edited plants exempted from some GM rules (India, 2022)**.
3. **First rDNA drug = recombinant human insulin (Humulin, 1982)**; **Hepatitis-B = recombinant vaccine**.
4. **Bt cotton = India's only approved GM crop (2002); Bt brinjal moratorium 2010; GM mustard DMH-11 — SC split verdict July 2024**.
5. **Golden Rice = Vitamin-A/β-carotene bio-fortified**; **Bt = Bacillus thuringiensis (pest resistance)**.
6. **GEAC (apex, under MoEFCC) + RCGM (DBT) + IBSC**; **Rules 1989 under EP Act 1986**.
7. **Cartagena Protocol 2000 = biosafety/LMOs**; **BRAI Bill lapsed**.
8. Concerns: **gene flow/super-weeds, allergenicity, seed-monopoly, designer babies (He Jiankui 2018), biopiracy, bioweapons**.
9. **DNA fingerprinting — CDFD/CCMB Hyderabad**.
10. **Transgenic/GMO = organism carrying a foreign/edited gene**.

## 9. Mains angles

1. **"Genetic engineering is a double-edged sword." Discuss its impact on human life** — medical/agri/industrial gains → health/ecological/ethical/socio-economic risks → balanced regulation verdict.
2. **"India's GM-crop policy is caught between science and politics." Examine** — Bt cotton success → Bt brinjal moratorium → GM mustard SC split verdict → need for transparent, independent, case-by-case regulation (BRAI gap).
3. **"CRISPR gene editing offers hope and raises alarm." Analyse** — therapeutic/agri promise → germline/designer-baby ethics (He Jiankui) → India's SDN-1/2 easing → governance.
