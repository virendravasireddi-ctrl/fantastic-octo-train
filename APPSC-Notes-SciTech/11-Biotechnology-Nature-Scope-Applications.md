## 1. Nature of Biotechnology ⭐

- **Definition:** **Biotechnology = the use of living organisms, cells or their molecules (enzymes, DNA) to make useful products or processes for human welfare.** The **Convention on Biological Diversity (CBD)** definition: *"any technological application that uses biological systems, living organisms or derivatives thereof to make or modify products or processes for specific use."* ⭐
- **Old vs. new:** **Traditional/old biotechnology** = fermentation-based (curd, cheese, bread, alcohol, vinegar — millennia old); **Modern biotechnology** = **recombinant DNA (rDNA)/genetic engineering, tissue culture, genomics** (post-1970s) ⭐.
- **Interdisciplinary:** draws on **microbiology, molecular biology, genetics, biochemistry, chemical/bioprocess engineering, and IT (bioinformatics)**.

### 1.1 The colour-coded "rainbow" of biotechnology ⭐⭐ (very exam-friendly)
| Colour | Field |
|---|---|
| **Red** | **Medical/pharma** — vaccines, drugs, gene therapy, diagnostics |
| **Green** | **Agriculture** — GM crops, biofertilisers, tissue culture |
| **White (Grey)** | **Industrial** — enzymes, biofuels, bioplastics, fermentation |
| **Blue** | **Aquatic/marine** — aquaculture, marine bioproducts |
| **Yellow** | **Food/nutrition** biotechnology |
| **Brown** | **Arid-zone / desert** biotech |
| **Dark** | **Bioterrorism/biowarfare** (the concern side) |
| **Gold** | **Bioinformatics / computational** biology |

## 2. Core tools & techniques (the "how") ⭐

- **Recombinant DNA technology (genetic engineering)** — cutting & joining DNA with **restriction enzymes + DNA ligase**, inserting via **vectors (plasmids)** into host cells (detail in File 12).
- **Tissue culture & micropropagation** — growing whole plants/cells *in vitro* (clonal plants, **totipotency**).
- **PCR (Polymerase Chain Reaction)** — amplifying DNA (**Kary Mullis**); the backbone of diagnostics & forensics.
- **Fermentation & bioprocess engineering** — File 13.
- **Monoclonal antibodies (hybridoma)** & **immunodiagnostics** — File 13.
- **Genomics, proteomics & bioinformatics; CRISPR gene editing; stem-cell technology; nanobiotechnology** (link File 14).

## 3. Scope & Applications in India ⭐⭐

| Sector | Applications (with Indian examples) |
|---|---|
| **Health/Medical (Red)** | **Vaccines — India is the "pharmacy of the world" / world's largest vaccine producer** (Serum Institute; **Covaxin — Bharat Biotech, India's indigenous COVID vaccine** ⭐); recombinant **insulin, biosimilars**, monoclonal antibodies; **molecular diagnostics (RT-PCR), gene therapy, stem cells; Genome India Project (2020 — sequencing Indian genomes)** ⭐ |
| **Agriculture (Green)** | **Bt cotton (India's only approved GM crop, 2002)** ⭐; tissue-cultured banana/potato; **biofertilisers (Rhizobium, Azotobacter), biopesticides (Bt, Trichoderma)**; marker-assisted & climate-resilient breeding; **bio-fortification (iron/zinc crops)** |
| **Industrial (White)** | Industrial **enzymes, biofuels (ethanol — E20 blending), bioplastics, organic acids**; **biomanufacturing (BioE3 focus)** |
| **Environment** | **Bioremediation (oil/heavy-metal cleanup), biogas, waste-to-energy, effluent treatment** (links to Environment series) |
| **Food** | Fermented foods, probiotics, single-cell protein, GM-free labelling debate |
| **Aquaculture/Marine (Blue)** | Disease-free shrimp seed, feed, vaccines — **crucial for AP's aqua-industry** ⭐ |
| **Forensics & IPR** | **DNA fingerprinting (CDFD/CCMB, Hyderabad)** ⭐, paternity/crime, GI & patents |

- **India's bioeconomy ⭐⭐:** grown to **~$130 billion (2024)**, targeted to reach **~$300 billion by 2030** under the **BioE3 Policy (2024)**; India is among the **top biotech destinations in Asia** and a **major biosimilars/vaccine hub**.

## 4. Institutional & policy landscape (overview — detail in File 16) ⭐

- **Department of Biotechnology (DBT), 1986** — under the **Ministry of Science & Technology**; the nodal agency.
- **BIRAC (2012)** — Biotechnology Industry Research Assistance Council (funds start-ups/industry).
- **Research institutions:** **CCMB (Centre for Cellular & Molecular Biology, Hyderabad), CDFD (DNA fingerprinting, Hyderabad), NIAB (Hyderabad), ICGEB (Delhi), NII, NCCS, inStem, THSTI** ⭐ (Hyderabad's cluster is now in Telangana; still the regional biotech hub).
- **Policies:** **National Biotechnology Development Strategy (2007; 2015–20)** → **BioE3 Policy (2024)**; **Genome India (2020), National Biopharma Mission, Mission COVID Suraksha**.

## 5. Andhra Pradesh dimension ⭐

- **Andhra Pradesh MedTech Zone (AMTZ), Visakhapatnam — Asia's first & largest medical-devices manufacturing park** ⭐⭐ (the AP-signature biotech-infrastructure fact).
- **Agri-biotech:** AP/Telangana form India's **"seed hub"** (hybrid seed, Bt cotton widely grown); **ANGRAU** (Acharya N.G. Ranga Agricultural University) & **CTRI, Rajahmundry** in crop biotech.
- **Aqua-biotech:** disease diagnostics, specific-pathogen-free (SPF) shrimp seed & vaccines underpin AP's **#1 shrimp/aquaculture** status (Environment-series link).
- **Regional cluster:** neighbouring **Hyderabad's Genome Valley/CCMB/Bharat Biotech** (now Telangana) shaped the wider Telugu-region biotech ecosystem.
