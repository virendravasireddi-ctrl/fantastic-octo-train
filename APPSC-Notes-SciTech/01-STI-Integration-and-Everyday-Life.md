## 0. The three concepts (open every Mains answer with this)

| Term | Meaning | One-line example |
|---|---|---|
| **Science** | Systematic pursuit of knowledge about the natural world through observation, hypothesis, experiment (*knowing why*) | Raman effect — scattering of light by molecules |
| **Technology** | Application of scientific knowledge to practical human ends (*knowing how*) | Fibre-optic communication using light physics |
| **Innovation** | Taking an invention/idea successfully to **use and market** — new products, processes, business models (*making it work at scale*) | UPI turning banking tech into free instant payments |

- Chain to quote: **Science → Technology → Innovation → Diffusion → Development**; each stage needs different institutions (labs → industry → startups/markets → public policy).
- **Invention ≠ innovation** ⭐ (classic trap): invention is the first occurrence of an idea; innovation is its first *commercialisation/adoption*.
- **Scientific temper** — Art. **51A(h)**: fundamental duty "to develop the scientific temper, humanism and the spirit of inquiry and reform" (added by **42nd Amendment, 1976**); the phrase is Nehru's (*Discovery of India*, 1946).

### 0.1 Why "integration" is the syllabus keyword

Standalone science produces papers; standalone technology produces imports; **integration** — linking research, industry, government and society (the **"quadruple helix"**) — produces development. India's exemplars of integration: **Green Revolution** (lab HYVs + irrigation + credit + MSP), **space applications** (satellites + broadcasting + fisheries + governance), **India Stack** (identity + payments + data → welfare delivery).

## 2. Science & Technology in everyday life ⭐ (illustrations bank)

### 2.1 At home
- **LPG/PNG** (combustion chemistry; Ujjwala — ~10.3 crore connections), **LED lighting** (semiconductor p-n junction; 2014 Physics Nobel for blue LED), **microwave oven** (dielectric heating of water molecules), **refrigeration** (vapour-compression cycle), **induction cooktop** (electromagnetic induction), **pressure cooker** (elevated boiling point), **water purifier** (RO membranes/UV-C), **non-stick coating** (PTFE), detergents (surfactants), **inverter/UPS** (power electronics).

### 2.2 In the pocket
- **Smartphone** = physics + chemistry + CS in one artefact: touchscreen (capacitive sensing), **GPS/NavIC** (satellite trilateration + relativistic clock correction ⭐ — a favourite "science behind" question), camera (CMOS sensors), **UPI/QR payments**, biometrics (fingerprint/face recognition), OTP (cryptography).

### 2.3 Health & body
- Vaccines (adaptive immunity), antibiotics (watch **AMR** — File 05), **X-ray/CT/MRI/ultrasound** (MRI = nuclear magnetic resonance, no ionising radiation ⭐), pulse oximeter (light absorption by haemoglobin), RT-PCR (DNA amplification — household word after COVID), pacemakers, IVF (ART Act 2021 regulates).

### 2.4 Farm & market (AP-flavoured)
- Soil Health Cards (soil chemistry), drip/sprinkler (**APMIP** — AP Micro Irrigation Project, first such state project, 2003), **PFZ advisories** — INCOIS Potential Fishing Zone alerts used by AP's ~600-km coastline fishers ⭐, weather-based crop insurance, aqua-culture sensors (AP = India's **aqua hub, ~30%+ of fish/shrimp production**), cold chains, **drones for nano-urea spraying (Namo Drone Didi, 2023-24)**.

### 2.5 Travel & civic life
- FASTag (RFID), metro signalling (CBTC), traffic ANPR cameras, weather forecast in every pocket, **Aadhaar e-KYC**, DigiLocker, ration via **ePoS + iris/fingerprint** (AP pioneered biometric PDS), electronic voting machines + VVPAT.

**One-sentence Mains closer:** "From the OTP that authenticates a pension payment to the Doppler radar that empties a coastal village before landfall, S&T is no longer a sector in Indian life — it is the operating system of daily life."

## 3. Innovation ecosystem vocabulary (asked as MCQs)

- **Incubator** (nurtures startups: **Atal Incubation Centres**, T-Hub-style state hubs) vs **accelerator** (short intensive scale-up).
- **Atal Innovation Mission (AIM, 2016 — NITI Aayog)**: **Atal Tinkering Labs (10,000+ schools)**, Atal Incubation Centres; **AIM 2.0 approved Nov 2024**.
- **Startup India (2016)**: ~**1.8 lakh DPIIT-recognised startups (2025), 110+ unicorns** — **3rd largest startup ecosystem** (after US, China).
- **Frugal innovation / "jugaad"**: Mangalyaan at **~₹450 crore**; Mitticool clay fridge; **grassroots innovation** institutionalised by **NIF (2000)** & Honey Bee Network (File 07).
- **Global Innovation Index (WIPO)**: India **39/133 (2024) → 38/139 (2025)** ⭐ — No. 1 among lower-middle-income economies; 4 Indian clusters (Bengaluru, Delhi, Mumbai, Chennai) in world's top-100 S&T clusters.

## 5. Prelims nuggets

1. **Art. 51A(h)** — scientific temper is a **Fundamental Duty** (42nd Amendment, 1976); phrase coined by **Nehru**.
2. **Invention ≠ innovation** — innovation requires commercialisation/adoption.
3. **National Science Day — 28 Feb** (Raman effect, 1928); **National Technology Day — 11 May** (Pokhran-II, 1998); **National Space Day — 23 Aug** (Chandrayaan-3 landing, 2023) ⭐.
4. **GII 2025: India 38th**; top among lower-middle-income economies.
5. **AIM/ATL — NITI Aayog (2016)**; Startup India — DPIIT (2016).
6. **eSanjeevani** — world's largest govt telemedicine platform; **UJALA** — LED distribution (EESL).
7. **MRI uses no ionising radiation**; CT/X-ray do.
8. **INCOIS (Hyderabad)** gives tsunami warnings & PFZ advisories — under **MoES**, not ISRO (trap).
9. JAM = **Jan Dhan + Aadhaar + Mobile**; UPI launched **2016** by **NPCI (2008)**.
10. India achieved **50% non-fossil installed power capacity in 2025** — five years ahead of its 2030 NDC pledge.
