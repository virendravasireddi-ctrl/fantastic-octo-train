## 1. Nature of nanotechnology ⭐⭐

- **Definition:** **Nanotechnology = the science, engineering and application of materials & devices at the nanoscale — roughly 1 to 100 nanometres (1 nm = one-billionth of a metre, 10⁻⁹ m).** ⭐ A nanometre is ~**1/80,000th the width of a human hair**.
- **Why the nanoscale is special ⭐:** at this size, materials show **new/altered properties** — because of (a) a **huge surface-area-to-volume ratio** and (b) **quantum effects**. Gold turns red/reactive, carbon becomes ultra-strong, opaque substances turn transparent, etc. **The same material behaves differently as a nanoparticle.**
- **Two approaches:** **Top-down** (carving bulk material down — lithography) vs **Bottom-up** (assembling atom-by-atom / self-assembly) ⭐.
- **Origin:** conceptually launched by **Richard Feynman's 1959 lecture "There's Plenty of Room at the Bottom"** ⭐; the term coined by **Norio Taniguchi (1974)**; **Eric Drexler** popularised it. **Carbon nanotubes** and **fullerenes/graphene** are its icons.

## 2. Nanomaterials to know ⭐

- **Carbon nanotubes (CNTs), fullerenes (C₆₀ "buckyballs"), graphene** (single-atom-thick carbon — ultra-strong, conductive); **quantum dots** (nano-semiconductors); **nanoparticles** (silver — antimicrobial; gold; titanium dioxide — sunscreens); **dendrimers, nanowires, nanoshells**.

## 3. Scope & Applications ⭐⭐

| Sector | Applications |
|---|---|
| **Medicine ("Nanomedicine")** ⭐ | **Targeted drug delivery** (drugs carried to tumours, fewer side-effects), **nano-diagnostics/biosensors**, cancer therapy (nanoshells), imaging contrast, tissue engineering, **theranostics** |
| **Agriculture** | **Nano-fertilisers & nano-pesticides (controlled release), nano-sensors for soil/crop, nano-encapsulated agrochemicals** — India's **"Nano Urea" (IFFCO, 2021) is a world-first commercial nano-fertiliser** ⭐⭐ |
| **Water & environment** | **Nano-filtration & membranes, nano-adsorbents (arsenic/fluoride removal), photocatalysts, remediation** — big for India's water quality (File: Environment) |
| **Energy** | Better **batteries/supercapacitors, solar-cell efficiency, fuel cells, hydrogen storage** |
| **Electronics/IT** | **Nano-chips, transistors, memory, displays, flexible electronics** (Moore's-law driver) |
| **Materials/textiles** | **Stain- & water-resistant fabrics, self-cleaning surfaces, stronger-lighter composites, nano-coatings/paints** |
| **Food & cosmetics** | Packaging, nano-encapsulated nutrients; sunscreens (TiO₂/ZnO) |
| **Defence** | Lightweight armour, sensors, camouflage |

## 4. Nanotechnology in India — policy & institutions ⭐⭐

- **Nano Mission (2007)** ⭐ — launched by the **Department of Science & Technology (DST)** with ~**₹1,000 crore** (Phase-II continued); India is now **among the top 3–5 countries in nano-science research publications** ⭐.
- **Institutions:** **DST, CSIR labs, IITs/IISc, Centres for Nano Science & Engineering (e.g., CeNSE-IISc), INST Mohali (Institute of Nano Science & Technology)** ⭐.
- **Indian flagship product:** **IFFCO Nano Urea (liquid) — 2021, the world's first commercial nano-fertiliser** ⭐⭐ (cuts urea use/imports — a signature Indian nano success), plus **Nano DAP**.
- **Nanobiotechnology** — convergence with biotech (drug delivery, biosensors, DNA-nanotech) links this file to Files 11–13.

## 5. Concerns (nano's dark side) ⭐

- **Nanotoxicity** — tiny particles can **cross biological barriers (lungs, skin, blood-brain barrier)**; unknown long-term health effects.
- **Environmental** — persistence & bioaccumulation of engineered nanoparticles; hard to detect/remove.
- **Regulatory gap** — few nano-specific safety standards/labelling (India & world).
- **Ethical/social** — the **"nano-divide"** (access inequality), privacy (nano-sensors), dual/defence use.
*(Fuller ethical–legal treatment in File 16.)*

## 6. Andhra Pradesh dimension ⭐

- **Nano-agriculture:** wide use of **Nano Urea/Nano DAP** across AP's farms; nano-agrochemical trials.
- **Nano-water:** nano-filtration relevant to AP's **fluoride belts (Rayalaseema)** (Environment-series link).
- **Research:** university nano-labs (**Andhra University, JNTU, IIT Tirupati, IISER-inspired centres**) and the **AMTZ (Visakhapatnam)** med-tech ecosystem (nano-diagnostics/devices).
