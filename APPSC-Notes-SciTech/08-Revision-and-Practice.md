## 3. 20 MCQs with key

1. National Science Day is observed on 28 February to commemorate — (a) Pokhran-II (b) **discovery of the Raman Effect** (c) ISRO's founding (d) Chandrayaan-3. **(b)**
2. The scientific temper is enshrined in the Constitution as a — (a) Fundamental Right (b) DPSP (c) **Fundamental Duty [Art. 51A(h)]** (d) Preamble ideal. **(c)**
3. India's three-stage nuclear programme uses, in the final stage, mainly — (a) enriched uranium (b) plutonium (c) **thorium** (d) deuterium. **(c)**
4. The world's only reactor fuelled by U-233 is — (a) Dhruva (b) Apsara (c) **KAMINI** (d) PFBR. **(c)**
5. Chandrayaan-3 landed near the lunar — (a) equator (b) **south-pole region** (c) far side (d) Mare Imbrium. **(b)**
6. ANRF, operational 2024, subsumes — (a) DST (b) **SERB** (c) CSIR (d) UGC. **(b)**
7. Which is NOT part of the IGMDP (1983)? — (a) Prithvi (b) Agni (c) Akash (d) **BrahMos**. **(d)**
8. India's first indigenous COVID-19 vaccine was developed by — (a) SII (b) **Bharat Biotech (Covaxin)** (c) Zydus (d) Biocon. **(b)**
9. NavIC is a — (a) global system (b) **regional navigation system** (c) communication satellite (d) weather satellite. **(b)**
10. All Indian orbital launches take place from — (a) VSSC (b) **SDSC SHAR, Sriharikota** (c) URSC (d) Kulasekarapattinam. **(b)**
11. The Technology Policy Statement stressing self-reliance was issued in — (a) 1958 (b) **1983** (c) 2003 (d) 2013. **(b)**
12. TKDL famously helped revoke patents on — (a) tulsi & amla (b) **turmeric & neem** (c) ashwagandha (d) sandalwood. **(b)**
13. UPI is operated by — (a) RBI (b) SBI (c) **NPCI** (d) SEBI. **(c)**
14. INCOIS, which issues tsunami warnings, is headquartered at — (a) Chennai (b) **Hyderabad** (c) Goa (d) Vizag. **(b)**
15. The National Quantum Mission (2023) has an outlay of about — (a) ₹1,000 cr (b) **₹6,000 cr** (c) ₹10,000 cr (d) ₹76,000 cr. **(b)**
16. India's first indigenous CAR-T cell therapy is — (a) Rotavac (b) CERVAVAC (c) **NexCAR19** (d) iNCOVACC. **(c)**
17. The first indigenous aircraft carrier is — (a) INS Vikramaditya (b) **INS Vikrant** (c) INS Arihant (d) INS Vishal. **(b)**
18. India's first supercomputer (1991), after CRAY denial, was — (a) SAGA (b) AIRAWAT (c) **PARAM 8000** (d) PRATYUSH. **(c)**
19. The only genetically modified crop approved for commercial cultivation in India is — (a) GM mustard (b) Bt brinjal (c) **Bt cotton** (d) Golden rice. **(c)**
20. Amaravati Quantum Valley (2025-26) will house — (a) a fast breeder reactor (b) **IBM Quantum System Two** (c) a semiconductor fab (d) NavIC ground station. **(b)**
