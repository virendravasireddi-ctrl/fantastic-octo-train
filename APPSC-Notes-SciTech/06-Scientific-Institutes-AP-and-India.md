---

> **Why a whole file:** APPSC lists this twice — "Major Scientific institutes for Science and Technology in AP and India" **and** "Major Scientific Institutes for Research and Development in AP and India." It is almost pure **match-the-following** territory: **institute ↔ location ↔ parent agency ↔ specialisation**. Learn the *location* and *domain* of each; that is what the MCQ tests.

---

## PART A — INSTITUTES IN ANDHRA PRADESH ⭐⭐⭐ (highest-yield for APPSC)

> After bifurcation (2014), Hyderabad-based institutes are **in Telangana** but remain frequent APPSC/Telugu-states MCQs — a **"legacy Hyderabad institutions"** sub-table is kept separate below and clearly labelled so you do not mislabel their state.

- **City clusters:** *Hyderabad* (CCMB, CDFD, IICT, NGRI, NIN, NRSC, DRDL/RCI, ICRISAT, NFC, AMD), *Bengaluru* (IISc, URSC, ADE/ADA, NAL, JNCASR, RRI), *Pune* (NCL, IUCAA, NIV, C-DAC HQ), *Mumbai* (BARC, TIFR), *Ahmedabad* (SAC, PRL).

## Prelims nuggets
1. **All Indian orbital launches lift off from SHAR, Sriharikota (AP)** — VSSC (Thiruvananthapuram) *designs* the rockets. ⭐
2. **NSTL (Vizag)** makes torpedoes (Varunastra); **NPOL (Kochi)** makes sonar.
3. **CTRI Rajahmundry** — India's only tobacco research institute (in AP).
4. **NARL Gadanki** (near Tirupati) — atmospheric science, MST radar — under **DOS**.
5. **Tummalapalle (Kadapa)** — among world's largest uranium reserves.
6. **AMTZ Visakhapatnam (2016)** — Asia's first medical-device manufacturing zone.
7. **CCMB/CDFD/IICT/NGRI/NIN/NRSC/ICRISAT** — all **Hyderabad (Telangana)** post-2014 (don't call them AP).
8. **ISI Kolkata (1931)** — Mahalanobis, statistics; **IISc (1909)** — Tata/Bengaluru.
9. **IGCAR Kalpakkam** — fast breeder reactors; **BARC Mumbai** — flagship nuclear.
10. **ANGRAU HQ = Lam, Guntur** (post-bifurcation); Telangana got PJTSAU.
