## 1. One-page spine

1. **Ethics** = normative study of right/wrong in human conduct (*ethos* = character). Distinct from **values** (preferences), **morals** (inherited codes), **law** (ethical minimum, codified & coerced).
2. **Essence**: only **deliberate, free, knowing** human acts are moral material (*actus humanus*); judged on **object–intention–circumstances**; ethics is social, prescriptive, conscience-sanctioned, dynamic.
3. **Determinants**: conscience & character ← family ← society/culture ← religion ← education/role models ← law ← organisation/leadership ← economy ← time-place ← technology (Kohlberg's ladder as frame).
4. **Consequences**: individual (self-respect vs Satyam-style ruin) · society (**trust/social capital** — Fukuyama) · administration (legitimacy, compliance vs 2G/CWG cynicism) · economy (investor confidence).
5. **Dimensions**: descriptive · **normative** (deontology-Kant/Gita; consequentialism-Bentham-Mill/Kautilya; virtue-Aristotle/dharma; justice-Rawls) · meta (Moore, Hume) · applied (bio/enviro/admin/AI).
6. **Private vs public relationships**: partiality vs impartiality; care ethics vs justice ethics; **one morality of values, two moralities of roles** (Gandhi vs Nagel/Walzer); collisions resolved by disclose–recuse–document + Talisman + publicity test.
7. **Public service**: foundational values + **Nolan's seven**; **integrity** = wholeness beyond honesty; **accountability** = answerability + enforcement (Friedrich vs Finer); mechanisms — legislature/PAC, CAG, courts/PCA, conduct rules, CVC-CBI-Lokpal, RTI, charters, **social audit (AP 2006 pioneer)**; 2nd ARC's "high-risk, low-reward" design; AP hooks — Spandana, RTGS, secretariats, Meeseva.
8. **Human values & harmony**: values = enduring guides (**Rokeach**: 18 terminal + 18 instrumental); **UHV four levels** — self (*sukh* vs *suvidha*) → family (nine values; trust the foundation, love the completion; respect = right evaluation) → society (*samadhan–samriddhi–abhay–sah-astitva*; five dimensions) → nature/existence (four orders in mutual fulfilment; existence = coexistence); nature-ethics ladder (anthropo→bio→ecocentrism; Leopold, Naess; Bishnoi/Chipko); **gender equability** = equity in relationships (Art. 15(3), 51A(e), 106th Amdt; Vidyasagar→Phules→**Veeresalingam 1881→*Kanyasulkam***); values imparted by **family** (modelling) → **society** (celebrated conduct) → **schools** (Radhakrishnan '49, Sri Prakasa '59, Kothari '66, NEP 2020); leaders–reformers–administrators table (Gandhi/Patel/Ambedkar/Kalam; Visvesvaraya/Seshan/Sreedharan/**Sankaran**).
9. **Attitude**: Allport's "state of readiness"; **ABC content**; **Katz's four functions** (knowledge, adjustment, ego-defence, value-expression); attitude–behaviour gap (**LaPiere**) closed by specificity/strength (**Ajzen's TPB**: attitude+norms+control→intention); behaviour→attitude via **dissonance** (Festinger, $1/$20) and self-perception (Bem); **moral attitudes** (obligatory, emotion-charged; Bandura's moral disengagement) vs **political attitudes** (socialisation; F-scale; officer = non-partisan); **social influence** — conformity (Sherif/Asch), compliance (**Cialdini's six**), obedience (**Milgram 65%**), minority influence (Moscovici); **persuasion** — Yale (source-message-audience), **ELM central vs peripheral**, inoculation/reactance; governance uses — Swachh Bharat framing, **nudge (Eco Survey 2018-19)**; persuade transparently, never manipulate.
10. **Emotional intelligence**: **Salovey–Mayer 1990** ability definition (monitor–discriminate–use) + four branches; **Goleman 1995** five components (self-awareness, self-regulation, motivation | empathy, social skills = personal | social competence); **Damasio's somatic marker** — emotion is part of reason; applications — self (stress, integrity), team (morale, conflict), citizen interface (empathy at counter, riots, **Hudhud 2014 evacuation**), governance (Karmayogi training, appraisal, nudge-communication); dark side — EI without ethics = manipulation; Indian anchor — *sthitaprajna/samatva*.

## 3. Glossary (rapid-fire)

**Ethos** Greek: character/custom · **Actus humanus** deliberate free human act · **Eudaimonia** flourishing · **Phronesis** practical wisdom · **Categorical imperative** unconditional universal duty · **Felicific calculus** Bentham's pleasure arithmetic · **Nishkama karma** desireless duty · **Anekantavada** many-sidedness of truth (Jain) · **Meta-ethics** study of moral language/status · **Naturalistic fallacy** defining "good" via natural facts (Moore) · **Role morality** duties attached to office · **Dirty hands** wrongdoing seemingly required by public role (Walzer) · **Integrity** wholeness of character; consistency unobserved · **Probity** demonstrated adherence to highest standards in public dealings · **Answerability + enforceability** the two limbs of accountability · **Vertical/horizontal accountability** to people / between institutions · **Social audit** verification of works/spend by the affected community (MGNREGA §17) · **Sevottam** DARPG's service-delivery excellence model · **Klitgaard formula** Corruption = Monopoly + Discretion − Accountability · **Terminal/instrumental values** end-states vs modes of conduct (Rokeach) · **Sukh/suvidha** happiness of the self vs facilities for the body (UHV) · **Sah-astitva** co-existence, the final human goal · **Equity vs equality** fairness adjusted to need vs identical treatment · **ABC model** affective–behavioural–cognitive attitude content · **Cognitive dissonance** discomfort of inconsistent cognitions driving attitude change (Festinger) · **ELM** elaboration likelihood model — central vs peripheral persuasion routes · **Inoculation** resistance built by weakened counter-arguments (McGuire) · **Reactance** boomerang against threats to freedom (Brehm) · **Amygdala hijack** emotion overwhelming deliberation (Goleman) · **Somatic marker** bodily emotion signals guiding decisions (Damasio) · **Sthitaprajna** Gita's person of steady wisdom — the self-regulation ideal.

## 5. Seventeen Mains questions with answer skeletons

1. **"Define ethics. How does it differ from morals, values and law?"** — Mackenzie definition → four-way table (source/nature/sanction) → overlaps (law as ethical minimum; legal≠ethical: salt tax; ethical≠legal: civil disobedience) → close: ethics as the critic of all three.
2. **"What is the essence of ethics in human actions?"** — *actus humanus* vs *actus hominis* → knowledge-freedom-deliberation → object-intention-circumstance → characteristics list → example (coerced signature vs free assent).
3. **"Discuss the main determinants of ethics in human conduct."** — layered scheme (individual→family→society→religion→education→law→organisation→economy→context→technology) with one example each → Kohlberg frame → synthesis: conscience decides with materials society supplies.
4. **"What are the consequences of ethics — and of its absence — in public life?"** — four levels (individual/society/administration/economy) → trust as infrastructure (Fukuyama) → Satyam/2G as negatives; Seshan/Sreedharan/Sankaran as positives → ethics as precondition of effectiveness.
5. **"Explain the dimensions of ethics."** — D-N-M-A four branches → inside normative: three families table → note the alternate "levels" reading (individual→global) → Indian traditions paragraph.
6. **"Should public servants be judged by their private conduct? Argue both sides."** — two-moralities (Nagel, Walzer, role morality) vs one-morality (Gandhi, character-leakage, public trust) → balanced: values continuous, application rules differ → conflict-of-interest law as the institutional compromise.
7. **"Impartiality is the first virtue of public relationships, partiality of private ones. Discuss."** — comparison table → Weber's impersonal office → collisions (nepotism, COI) → disclose-recuse-document + Talisman.
8. **"Integrity is more than honesty. Elaborate with examples."** — etymology (*integer*) → honesty as component → kinds (moral/intellectual/professional/institutional) → officer example (lawful-but-wrong refusal) → cultivation (Karmayogi, leadership).
9. **"Accountability without responsibility is hollow; responsibility without accountability is blind. Comment."** — definitions → Friedrich–Finer debate → types (vertical/horizontal) → India's mechanism table in brief → failures (dormant whistle-blower law, vacancies) → blend prescription.
10. **"Evaluate India's institutional framework for ensuring integrity in public service, with reference to the 2nd ARC."** — map mechanisms (legislative/audit/judicial/statutory/citizen-centric) → 2nd ARC 4th Report highlights → why underperformance ("caged parrot," §17A, vacancies) → reforms: code of ethics statute, whistle-blower operationalisation, technology-by-default, AP exemplars (social audit, RTGS, secretariats).
11. **"Explain 'harmony in existence.' How does the UHV framework connect harmony in the self to harmony in nature?"** — sukh/suvidha diagnosis → nine relationship values (trust→love) → societal goals chain (*samadhan→sah-astitva*) and five dimensions → four orders in mutual fulfilment; existence as coexistence → close: ecological crisis as a failure of *understanding*, not of resources.
12. **"Gender equability is a question of relationships, not merely of laws. Discuss."** — equality vs equity → UHV: discrimination = wrong evaluation (by body) → where inequity lives (home/work/public) → law as necessary floor (15(3), 51A(e), POSH, 106th Amdt) but insufficient → reformer lineage ending with Veeresalingam/*Kanyasulkam* → SHG evidence (AP DWCRA/SERP) → synthesis: re-founded relationships + enforced rights.
13. **"Family, society and educational institutions form a relay in value transmission. Examine each runner's role — and where the baton is dropped."** — family (modelling, induction; hypocrisy gap) → society (celebrated conduct, media distortion) → school (committee spine Radhakrishnan→NEP 2020; hidden curriculum; assessment integrity) → repairs: teacher exemplars, service learning, UHV courses, Karmayogi for the in-service stage.
14. **"What lessons do the lives of great administrators hold that codes of conduct cannot teach?"** — codes state floors; lives show ceilings → three exemplars with one act each (Visvesvaraya's expense discipline; Seshan's fearless enforcement; Sankaran's austerity-compassion) → mechanism: moral exemplars work via identification (Bandura), not deterrence → close: institutionalise exposure (case-based training, biographies in academies).
15. **"Attitudes are poor predictors of behaviour — yet governments spend crores on attitude-change campaigns. Reconcile."** — LaPiere gap → moderators (specificity, strength, TPB's three levers) → behaviour→attitude route (dissonance, public commitments — ODF pledges) → design lesson: campaigns work when they pair persuasion with norms and *ease* (Swachh Bharat, tax social-proof letters, nudge chapter ES 2018-19) → close: change behaviour to change minds, not only vice versa.
16. **"Social influence explains both the corruption of institutions and their reform. Discuss with classic studies."** — Milgram/Zimbardo: destructive obedience and situational rot → Asch: committee conformity/groupthink → counters: written dissent, devil's advocate, whistle-blower protection → Moscovici: the consistent minority (reform officers, Gandhi) → Cialdini's principles as both trap (vendor reciprocity) and tool (social proof for compliance) → close: design situations, don't just exhort individuals.
17. **"Emotional intelligence is to administration what fuel is to an engine — invisible in the design, indispensable in the running. Elaborate."** — Salovey–Mayer definition + Goleman's five mapped to administrative scenes (file-rage, counter-empathy, riot de-escalation, Hudhud-style crisis communication) → Damasio: emotion inside rationality → governance level (Karmayogi, appraisal, frontline emotional labour) → caveat: EI without ethics = manipulation → close: pair the *how* (EI) with the *what for* (values).

## 6. One worked case-study frame (transferable)

**Situation:** You are a tendering officer; the lowest technically-qualified bidder is your close friend's firm. *Method:* (1) **Facts & stakeholders** — you, friend, competing bidders, department, public. (2) **Issues** — conflict of interest (real *and* perceived), impartiality, transparency. (3) **Options** — proceed silently / recuse with disclosure / disqualify friend's firm to look clean. (4) **Test each** — legality (conduct rules mandate disclosure), Kantian universalisability, consequences (perceived bias corrodes trust even if award is fair; wrongful disqualification is *also* injustice), virtue/publicity test. (5) **Decision** — **disclose in writing and recuse**; let due process decide on merit; document everything. (6) **Close** with the principle: *justice must not only be done but be seen to be done.*
