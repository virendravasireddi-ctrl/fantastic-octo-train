# APPSC Notes — Ethics (Ethics and Human Interface · Public Service)

**Target exams:** APPSC Group-I Mains (Paper-III Ethics section) and UPSC CSE GS Paper-IV, whose syllabus wording this unit follows near-verbatim. Equally usable for other state-PSC ethics/governance sections and interview preparation.

## Syllabus items covered

> *"Ethics and Human Interface: Essence, determinants and consequences of Ethics in human actions; dimensions of ethics; ethics in private and public relationships."*
> *"Ethics, integrity and accountability in public service."*
> *"Human values: understanding the harmony in existence; human relationships in the society and in the nature; gender equability in the relationships; role of family, society and educational institutions in imparting values to citizens; lessons from the lives and teachings of great leaders, reformers and administrators."*
> *"Attitude: content, functions, its influence and relation with thought and behaviour; moral and political attitudes; role of social influence and persuasion. Emotional intelligence — concepts and their utilities and application in administration and governance."*

## How to use (suggested 8-day cycle)

1. **Day 1:** File 01 — make the ethics/morals/values/law card and the determinants list from memory.
2. **Day 2:** File 02 — three-families table + one Indian parallel per family; drill the D-N-M-A branches.
3. **Day 3:** File 03 — write the private-vs-public comparison table unaided; rehearse the one-vs-two moralities paragraph.
4. **Day 4:** File 04 — mechanisms master table + Nolan seven + committee–year pairs.
5. **Day 5:** File 05 — draw the UHV four-level ladder and the nine relationship values from memory; build your own 6-person leaders/reformers/administrators card.
6. **Day 6:** File 06 — Katz's functions + the study–author pairs (LaPiere→Cialdini); rehearse one governance-campaign example through the ELM/TPB lens.
7. **Day 7:** File 07 — write the Salovey–Mayer definition and Goleman's five from memory; attach one administrative scene to each component.
8. **Day 8:** File 08 — MCQs under time; write 3 Mains answers and 1 case study against the skeletons.

## Exam-craft notes

- **Prelims/Screening:** thinker–book–phrase pairs (Mackenzie, Moore, Rawls, Gilligan, Allport, Katz, Goleman), study–author pairs (LaPiere, Asch, Milgram, Festinger, ELM), committee–year–outcome pairs (Nolan 1995, Santhanam→CVC, 2nd ARC 2007, Kothari 1964–66), and act–year facts (RTI 2005, Lokpal 2013, PCA 2018 amendment, 106th Amendment 2023) are the mined seams — every such pair is **bolded**.
- **Mains:** high-scoring answers (a) open with a definition or quote, (b) run structured middle sections (tables/typologies from these files), (c) give one **administrator or AP example** (S. R. Sankaran, Spandana, social audit), and (d) close with a synthesis line, not a summary.
- **Case studies:** always run the three normative tests (duty · consequence · character) plus legality, and end with the disclose–recuse–document discipline where conflicts of interest appear.
