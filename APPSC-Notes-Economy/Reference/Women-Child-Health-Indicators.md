A flashcard data-sheet for Prelims and for anchoring Mains answers. **India figures with Andhra Pradesh alongside.** Watch the **source**: MMR/IMR come from the **SRS (Sample Registration System)**; nutrition, anaemia and sex-ratio-at-birth come from the **NFHS (National Family Health Survey)** — quote the right one.

> **Rounds:** **NFHS-4 = 2015-16**, **NFHS-5 = 2019-21** (latest full round); **NFHS-6 awaited** — re-verify from the **NFHS-5 India & AP state factsheets**, the **SRS Maternal Mortality Bulletin** and the **SRS Statistical Report** before the exam.

*SDG target: **MMR < 70 by 2030** — India not there nationally, but **AP already is**.*

## 3. Child nutrition (under-5) — NFHS-5

| Indicator | India | Andhra Pradesh | vs NFHS-4 (India) |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Stunting** (low height-for-age — *chronic*) | **35.5%** | 31.2% | ↓ from 38.4% |
| **Wasting** (low weight-for-height — *acute*) | **19.3%** | 16.1% | ↓ from 21.0% |
| **Underweight** (low weight-for-age — *composite*) | **32.1%** | 29.6% | ↓ from 35.8% |

**Memory hook ⭐:** *Stunting = height (chronic) · Wasting = weight-for-height (acute) · Underweight = weight-for-age (composite).*

- **Fully immunised children (12-23 m):** India **76.4%** (↑ from 62%); AP ~73.6%

## The "big five" to memorise

> **MMR 97 · IMR 28 · TFR 2.0 · Stunting 35.5% · Women's anaemia 57%** (India, latest)

## Andhra Pradesh at a glance ⭐

- **AP beats the national average on:** MMR (~45), TFR (1.7), child mortality, and stunting/wasting/underweight.
- **AP lags the national average on:** **child marriage (~29% vs 23%)** and **women's anaemia (58.8% vs 57%)** — the two AP weak spots to flag in an answer.

## Quick prelims pairs

- **MMR & IMR → SRS**; **Stunting/Wasting/Anaemia/SRB → NFHS**.
- **NFHS-4 = 2015-16 · NFHS-5 = 2019-21.**
- **Replacement TFR = 2.1**; India **2.0** (achieved).
- **Anaemia = the indicator that rose** between NFHS-4 and NFHS-5.
- **SDG-3 targets:** MMR < 70 · U5MR < 25 · NMR < 12 (by 2030).
- **Overall sex ratio crossed 1,000 (1,020)** for the first time in NFHS-5.
