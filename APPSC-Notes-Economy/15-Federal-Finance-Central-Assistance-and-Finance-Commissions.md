## 0. Frame — the two imbalances ⭐

- **Vertical imbalance:** states incur roughly **three-fifths of combined government expenditure** but raise well under half of combined revenues — the Constitution deliberately gave the Centre the buoyant taxes and the states the spending functions, then built **transfer machinery** to bridge the gap.
- **Horizontal imbalance:** states differ hugely in capacity (per-capita income of the richest ≈ several times the poorest) — transfers must equalise **without punishing performance** ⭐ (the central dilemma of every Finance Commission formula).

## 1. The constitutional plumbing ⭐⭐

| Article | Content |
|---|---|
| **Art 270** | sharing of the **divisible pool** — all central taxes/duties **except cesses, surcharges and collection costs** (post-**80th Amendment, 2000** "alternative scheme of devolution") ⭐⭐ |
| **Art 275** | **statutory grants-in-aid** to states in need (on FC recommendation; charged on CFI) |
| **Art 282** | **discretionary grants** "for any public purpose" — the legal basis of **centrally sponsored schemes** ⭐ |
| **Art 280** | **Finance Commission** every five years — taxes' distribution, grant principles, measures to **augment state funds for panchayats/municipalities (280(3)(bb)/(c))**, anything else referred |
| **Art 281** | FC report + **Action Taken Report** laid before Parliament |
| **Art 293** | state borrowing; **293(3) Centre's consent** while a state owes the Centre — the borrowing-discipline lever (File 12) |
| Art 268/269/269A | assigned duties, inter-state trade taxes, IGST apportionment (File 14) |

## 2. Channels of central assistance to states ⭐⭐ (map every transfer into one of these)

| Channel | Nature | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| **FC tax devolution** | **untied**, formula-based share of the divisible pool | the largest single flow (41%) |
| **FC grants** | untied/semi-tied: revenue-deficit, local-body, disaster, sector/state-specific | composition changes each award ⭐ |
| **Centrally sponsored schemes (CSS)** | conditional, cost-shared (**generally 60:40; 90:10 for NE & hill states**) | Art 282; "one-size" conditionality critique |
| **Central sector schemes** | 100% central funding, central implementation | e.g. PLI, national missions |
| **Special Assistance to States for Capital Investment (SASCI)** | **50-year interest-free capex loans** (₹1.5 LC-scale annually since FY23) | the post-COVID capex-push instrument ⭐ |
| **Externally aided projects (back-to-back)** | multilateral loans passed through | AP's workaround channel (File 21) |
| *(Extinct)* Plan assistance | **Gadgil-Mukherjee formula died with the Planning Commission (2014-15)** — NITI Aayog holds **no funds** ⭐ | why FCs now dominate transfers |

## 3. The Finance Commission institution

- Composition: chairman + 4 members (qualifications under the **FC (Miscellaneous Provisions) Act, 1951**); recommendations **not binding but by convention accepted** on devolution.
- Landmarks: 1st — **K.C. Neogy (1951)**; **13th (Kelkar)** 32%; **14th (Y.V. Reddy) — the leap to 42%** ⭐ (with plan assistance abolished, states' untied share jumped); **15th (N.K. Singh) — 41%** (1% adjustment for the **J&K UT conversion**); **16th — Arvind Panagariya** ⭐.

## 4. 15th FC (2021-26) — the outgoing award ⭐

- **Vertical:** 41% of the divisible pool.
- **Horizontal criteria/weights:** **income distance 45 · population (2011) 15 · area 15 · forest & ecology 10 · demographic performance 12.5 · tax effort 2.5** — demographic performance rewarded population control, answering the South's 2011-census objection ⭐.
- **Grants:** post-devolution **revenue-deficit grants ₹2.94 LC to 17 states** (AP among the largest recipients — **≈₹30,497 cr**, File 20); **local bodies ₹4.36 LC**; **disaster ₹1.28 LC** (SDRF/NDRF architecture); sector & state-specific grants; a **non-lapsable defence & internal security fund (MFDIS)**.
- AP's inter-se share: **4.047%**.

## 5. 16th FC (award 2026-31) — "the latest finance commission" ⭐⭐

| Element | Recommendation |
|---|---|
| Constitution | **31 Dec 2023**; chairman **Arvind Panagariya**; report submitted 2025, **tabled with Budget 2026-27** |
| **Vertical devolution** | **41% retained** — the states' collective demand for **50% rejected** ⭐ |
| **Horizontal criteria** | **income distance 42.5 · population (2011) 17.5 · demographic performance 10 · area 10 · forest & ecology 10 · contribution to GDP 10 (NEW)** — the **tax-effort criterion dropped** ⭐⭐ |
| The new criterion | **"Contribution to GDP" (10%)** — the first explicit **efficiency/performance reward** for growth engines; all **five southern states gained share** (AP **4.047% → 4.217%** ⭐) |
| **Grants** | ≈**₹9.47 lakh crore** total — **urban & rural local bodies + disaster management**; **revenue-deficit, sector-specific and state-specific grants DISCONTINUED** ⭐⭐ |
| Fiscal rules | states' **fiscal deficit ceiling: 3% of GSDP** (File 12) |
| Character | shift from **entitlement-based** transfers toward **performance/compliance-based** federal finance |
| AP specifics | rural LB **₹16,627 cr** + urban LB **₹12,158 cr** + disaster **₹6,125 cr** (2026-31); special **wastewater-infrastructure grants for Vijayawada & Visakhapatnam (up to ₹5,000 cr each)** ⭐; but with RDG gone, AP is among **13 states whose total grants shrink** |

## 6. Issues of federal finance in India ⭐⭐ (the debate bank)

| Issue | Substance |
|---|---|
| **Cesses & surcharges** | ≈**one-fifth of gross central taxes** sits **outside the divisible pool** — 41% of a shrinking share; states demand their inclusion (needs a constitutional amendment) ⭐⭐ |
| **Equity vs efficiency** | income-distance dominance = a perceived "**devolution penalty**" on richer/performing states; the 16th FC's **GDP-contribution criterion** is the first structural concession ⭐ |
| 2011-population politics | southern states' grievance; managed via demographic-performance weight |
| **Grant compression** | end of RDG shifts risk to deficit states (AP, Kerala, Punjab, HP…) — untied share stable, cushions gone |
| **CSS conditionality** | Art 282 transfers with matching burdens & central branding; crowding of state priorities; rationalisation perpetual |
| **Borrowing control** | Art 293(3), NBC, off-budget clampdown — **Kerala v Union of India (2024, Art 131 suit)** put the Centre's borrowing-ceiling powers before the SC ⭐ |
| **GST-era autonomy** | rate power pooled in the Council; compensation over (File 14) — states' tax sovereignty at historic low |
| **Special Category Status** | 14th FC ended the plan-era distinction; **AP's SCS demand** survives politically (File 20) |
| Freebies debate | competitive welfare vs fiscal capacity — RBI *State Finances* flags; no institutional referee |
| Fiscal-council gap | NK Singh's proposal never adopted; FC remains a five-yearly, not standing, umpire |

## 7. AP capsule ⭐

- Share arc: **14th FC 4.305% → 15th 4.047% → 16th 4.217%** — bifurcation dropped it; the GDP-contribution criterion clawed some back.
- The **post-RDG cliff**: AP's ≈₹30,497 cr cushion (2021-26) has **no successor** — central assistance to AP now flows only via devolution + LB/disaster grants + SASCI + EAPs + Reorganisation-Act channels (Files 20-21), which is exactly why the AP budget's own-revenue and capex strategy matters (File 17).

## 8. Prelims nuggets

1. Divisible pool excludes **cesses, surcharges & collection costs** (80th Amdt) ⭐.
2. **Art 275 = statutory grants; Art 282 = discretionary (CSS)**.
3. Devolution arc: **32% (13th) → 42% (14th) → 41% (15th & 16th)** ⭐.
4. **16th FC: Panagariya; award 2026-31; 41%; grants ₹9.47 LC; RDG discontinued** ⭐⭐.
5. 16th FC formula adds **"contribution to GDP" (10%)**, drops **tax effort**; income distance 45→**42.5**, population 15→**17.5**.
6. **All five southern states gained** under the 16th FC; **AP 4.047 → 4.217%**.
7. AP 16th-FC grants: **RLB ₹16,627 cr · ULB ₹12,158 cr · disaster ₹6,125 cr**; **Vijayawada & Visakhapatnam wastewater grants ≤₹5,000 cr each**.
8. CSS sharing **60:40 (90:10 NE/hill)**; SASCI = **50-year interest-free** capex loans.
9. Gadgil formula/plan transfers ended with the **Planning Commission (2014)**; NITI has no funds.
10. **Kerala v Union (2024)** — Art 131 suit on borrowing ceilings.
