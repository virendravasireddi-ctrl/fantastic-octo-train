## 0. Frame

- A deficit is a **gap between expenditure and receipts** — but *which* receipts you exclude defines *which* deficit you get, and each deficit answers a different question ⭐. File 06 treats deficits inside fiscal policy; this file is the **budgeting-item treatment**: definitions → impact → the management machinery (FRBM and after).

## 1. The deficit ladder ⭐⭐ (definitions to reproduce verbatim)

| Deficit | Formula | What it tells you |
|---|---|---|
| **Budget deficit** | total expenditure − total receipts (incl. borrowings) | obsolete concept — kept ≈0 by definition; dropped after **ad-hoc T-bills ended (1997)** ⭐ |
| **Revenue deficit (RD)** | revenue expenditure − revenue receipts | the government is **borrowing to consume** — the "bad" deficit ⭐ |
| **Effective revenue deficit (ERD)** | RD − grants to states for **capital-asset creation** | introduced **2011-12 (Rangarajan)** ⭐ |
| **Fiscal deficit (FD)** | total expenditure − (revenue receipts + **non-debt** capital receipts) | the year's **net borrowing requirement** ⭐⭐ |
| **Primary deficit (PD)** | FD − **interest payments** | the *current* government's own fiscal behaviour (strips inherited interest) ⭐ |
| **Monetised deficit** | RBI's net new credit to the government | automatic monetisation **ended by the 1994/1997 agreements** (ad hocs → **WMA, 1997**) ⭐ |

- **Financing of FD:** market borrowings/G-secs (largest), small savings (NSSF), provident funds, external (tiny ≈1-2%) — machinery in File 04.
- **Deficit ≠ debt:** FD is a *flow*; debt is the accumulated *stock* — the bridge is the **Domar condition (sustainable if growth g > interest r)** ⭐.

## 2. Where the numbers stand (label the year, update before the exam)

- **FD:** COVID peak **9.2% (FY21)** → 6.7 → 6.4 → 5.6 → 4.8 (FY25) → **4.4% (FY26 RE)** → **4.3% (BE 2026-27)** ⭐.
- **Debt anchor ⭐⭐:** from FY27 fiscal policy is anchored on **central government debt** — ≈**57.1% of GDP (FY25)** → **55.6% (BE FY27)** → target **≈50±1% by 31 March 2031** (announced Budget 2025-26). The annual-FD anchor has been *subordinated* to a debt-path anchor.
- **Interest burden:** interest payments ≈ **3%+ of GDP and roughly a third of revenue receipts** — the crowding of the budget from within ⭐.
- **States:** net borrowing ceiling ≈ **3% of GSDP + 0.5% power-reform incentive**; the **16th FC recommends a 3% GSDP fiscal-deficit limit for states (2026-31)** ⭐; AP's numbers in Files 16-17.

## 3. Impact of deficits (build answers from this table)

| Channel | Mechanism | Counterpoint |
|---|---|---|
| **Inflation** | excess demand; worst when monetised | slack economy → little inflation |
| **Crowding out** ⭐ | government pre-empts savings → interest rates rise → private investment falls | **crowding in** when capex raises private returns (multiplier ≈2.5×, File 06) |
| **Twin deficits** ⭐ | FD (public dis-saving) spills into the **current-account deficit** | not mechanical — depends on private saving |
| **Interest-burden spiral** | deficits → debt → interest → bigger deficits | benign while **g > r** (Domar) ⭐ |
| **Intergenerational equity** | tomorrow taxed for today's consumption | fair if borrowing built **assets** for tomorrow |
| **External confidence** | ratings, FPI flows watch the FD/debt path | credible **rules** (FRBM/debt anchor) buy tolerance |

- **The quality question ⭐⭐:** a 4% FD that finances capex is *not* the same animal as a 4% FD that finances subsidies — hence the twin lens: **RD (composition)** + **FD (size)**. The "golden rule" = borrow only to invest.

## 4. Management I — the FRBM story ⭐⭐

| Stage | Content |
|---|---|
| **FRBM Act 2003** (effective 2004) | eliminate RD & cap FD at **3% of GDP** by 2008-09; annual fiscal statements to Parliament (File 11) |
| 2008-13 | GFC pause → 2012 amendment added **ERD** & Medium-Term Expenditure Framework |
| **NK Singh FRBM Review (2017)** | debt as the anchor: **60% general government (40% Centre + 20% states)**; fiscal council proposal (not adopted) ⭐ |
| **2018 amendment** | debt targets inserted; **RD target dropped** ⭐; **escape clause s.4(2)** — deviation ≤0.5% of GDP on defined grounds (security, calamity, structural reforms, sharp growth collapse) |
| 2020-21 | escape clause invoked; COVID blew the path (9.2%) |
| Post-COVID glide | Budget FY22 promise: **below 4.5% by FY26** — delivered (4.4% RE) |
| **From FY27** | **debt-path anchor: Centre ≈50±1% of GDP by March 2031** ⭐⭐ — flexibility on the annual FD, discipline on the stock |

## 5. Management II — the wider toolkit

- **Expenditure side:** subsidy reform via **DBT** (leakage cut), rationalisation of CSS, capex tilt (quality of deficit), just-in-time releases (File 11).
- **Receipts side:** tax buoyancy (GST 2.0, IT Act 2025), non-tax (dividends — RBI's record transfers), **disinvestment & asset monetisation** (non-debt receipts, File 27).
- **Transparency:** **off-budget borrowings brought on-budget (FCI-NSSF loans, from FY22)** ⭐; states' SPV/guarantee borrowings counted against their ceilings (2022) — the AP-relevant clampdown (File 21).
- **For states:** Art 293(3) consent, NBC, RBI's WMA/OD/SDF ladder; conditional extra space (0.5% power reforms) — *incentive-compatible federal discipline* ⭐.

## 6. AP capsule (details in Files 16-17)

- AP BE 2026-27: **RD ₹22,002 cr (1.11% of GSDP); FD ₹75,868 cr (3.84% of GSDP)** ⭐ — within the permitted band but the **persistence of RD** is the structural story (borrowing partly consumed by current spending).
- The **16th FC's discontinuation of revenue-deficit grants (2026-31)** removes AP's cushion — deficit management now rests on own-revenue growth and capex-led growth (Files 15/17).

## 7. Prelims nuggets

1. **FD = net borrowing requirement**; **PD = FD − interest** ⭐.
2. **RD is the "quality" signal** — borrowing for consumption.
3. **ERD — 2011-12, Rangarajan**; RD target dropped from FRBM in **2018**.
4. Ad-hoc Treasury Bills abolished **1997 → WMA system** ⭐.
5. FRBM 2003 original target: **FD 3%, RD 0 by 2008-09**; 2018 debt targets: **40% Centre / 60% general govt**.
6. **Escape clause: s.4(2), deviation ≤0.5% of GDP** — used for COVID.
7. **NK Singh committee (2017)** — debt anchor + fiscal council idea.
8. Current anchor: **Centre's debt ≈50±1% of GDP by March 2031**; BE FY27 FD **4.3%**, liabilities **55.6%** ⭐.
9. **16th FC: states' FD limit 3% of GSDP (2026-31)**.
10. Off-budget FCI-NSSF loans on-budget since **Budget 2021-22** ⭐.

## 8. Mains angles

1. **"Not all deficits are equal." Explain the types of deficits and argue which matters most for India today.** Ladder (§1) → size vs composition (FD vs RD) → golden rule → debt anchor as the new metric (§2, §4) → verdict: watch RD + debt path, not FD alone.
2. **Examine the impact of persistent fiscal deficits and evaluate India's management framework.** Impact table (§3) → FRBM arc → escape-clause realism → post-COVID glide delivered → residual risks (interest ≈⅓ of revenue receipts; state-level off-budget) ⭐.
3. **From deficit targets to a debt anchor: assess the shift in India's fiscal rules.** Why annual FD targets invite creative accounting & procyclicality → NK Singh logic → 2031 debt path → conditions for success (growth, real rates, transparent accounting).
4. **AP closer:** the state mirrors the national dilemma — welfare-heavy revenue account vs capex ambitions on a post-bifurcation base (Files 16-17).
