## 3. The federal dimension in this budget ⭐

- **First budget of the 16th Finance Commission award (2026-31):** devolution retained at **41%**; **revenue-deficit grants discontinued**; local-body + disaster grants ≈**₹9.47 lakh crore** over five years (detail: File 15).
- **AP angle:** AP's inter-se share **rises 4.047% → 4.217%** ⭐, but the end of RD grants makes AP among the states with **lower total grant flows** — the state budget's problem-statement (File 17).

## 4. Analysis — what the arithmetic says ⭐⭐ (Mains material)

1. **Consolidation *with* capex.** FD falls 4.4→4.3% while capex grows 11.5% — the adjustment is on the **revenue account** (revex +6.6% < nominal GDP +10% → revenue spending shrinks as a share of GDP). Quality-of-deficit logic in action (File 12).
2. **The debt anchor is now operational.** This is the first budget written under the **debt-path rule (≈50% by 2031)** rather than a bare FD target — watch liabilities (55.6%) as the headline metric hereafter.
3. **Demand context.** The FY26 consumption stimulus (income-tax relief worth ≈₹1 lakh crore + **GST 2.0's ≈₹48,000 crore rate giveaway**, File 14) is left to work; FY27 holds rates **stable** and pivots back to supply-side/investment.
4. **Techno-industrial statecraft.** ISM 2.0, rare-earth hubs, biopharma, AI/data infrastructure = industrial policy aimed at **supply-chain security and the "China+1" window** (continuity with PLI, File 30).
5. **Standing critiques to balance an answer:** interest payments still ≈ a third of revenue receipts; execution capacity for ever-larger capex; states' grant compression post-16th FC (13 states worse off); employment intensity of capital-heavy strategy; assumptions risk (10% nominal growth).

## 6. Prelims nuggets

1. Budget 2026-27: total expenditure **₹53.47 lakh crore**; **FD 4.3%**; capex **₹12.22 LC**; effective capex **₹17.15 LC** ⭐.
2. Central liabilities **55.6% of GDP (BE FY27)**; anchor **≈50±1% by March 2031**.
3. Income-tax slabs unchanged; **Income-tax Act 2025 effective 1 April 2026** ("tax year") ⭐.
4. **ISM 2.0 = ₹40,000 cr (semiconductor equipment/materials/R&D)**; **biopharma hub ₹10,000 cr**.
5. **Bharat-VISTAAR** = AI agri-advisory (AgriStack + ICAR) ⭐.
6. Textile trio: **National Fibre Scheme · Textile Expansion & Employment Scheme · National Handloom & Handicraft Programme**.
7. First budget of the **16th FC award — 41% devolution retained; RD grants ended**.
8. Nominal GDP assumption **10%**; revex growth held to **6.6%**.

## 7. Mains angles

1. **"Fiscal consolidation without growth sacrifice." Analyse the current union budget's fiscal stance.** §1 arithmetic → revenue-account squeeze funds capex → debt-anchor context → risks (assumptions, interest burden) → verdict.
2. **Evaluate the budget's industrial-technology strategy.** §2 missions → continuity with PLI/Make in India (File 30) → supply-chain security logic → critiques (fiscal cost, jobs intensity, execution).
3. **What does this budget mean for the states — and for Andhra Pradesh?** 16th FC year 1 → devolution stability vs grant compression → AP's twin result (share ↑, RDG gone) → link to AP's own budget response (File 17).
