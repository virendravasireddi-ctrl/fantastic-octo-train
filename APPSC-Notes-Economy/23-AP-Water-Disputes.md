# 23 · AP's Water Disputes with Neighbouring States

## 0. The legal frame (open every answer with this) ⭐

- **Art. 262** + **Inter-State River Water Disputes (ISRWD) Act, 1956** — tribunals whose awards bind like SC decrees; **Art. 262(2)** can bar court jurisdiction.
- **AP Reorganisation Act 2014 layer:** **S.84 Apex Council** (Union minister + 2 CMs), **S.85 Krishna & Godavari River Management Boards (KRMB/GRMB)**, **S.89** — the Krishna tribunal's remit extended to project-wise allocation between the successor states ⭐.
- AP is party to disputes on **four rivers — Krishna, Godavari, Vamsadhara, (and lesser: Palar/Pennar)** — with **Telangana, Karnataka, Maharashtra, Odisha, Chhattisgarh, Tamil Nadu**.

## 1. Krishna — the main theatre ⭐⭐

### 1.1 Tribunal history
| Tribunal | Holding |
|---|---|
| **KWDT-I (Bachawat), 1973/76** | 75%-dependability allocation: **Maharashtra 585 : Karnataka 734 : (undivided) AP 811 TMC** ⭐ |
| **KWDT-II (Brijesh Kumar), 2010/2013** | Re-allocated incl. surplus at 65% dependability (Maha 666 : Kar 911 : AP 1005); **permitted Almatti raise to 524.256 m** → **never notified** — challenged in SC, then bifurcation supervened |

### 1.2 The post-2014 AP-Telangana dispute
- Interim arrangement splits undivided AP's 811 TMC **AP 512 : Telangana 299 (≈66:34)** — **ad hoc, renewed annually**; **Telangana demands 50:50** ⭐.
- **Oct 2023: Centre issued further Terms of Reference to KWDT-II under S.89** — project-wise, state-wise allocation between AP & TS; hearings ongoing.
- Flashpoints: AP's **Rayalaseema Lift Scheme (Sangameswaram)** vs Telangana's **Palamuru-Rangareddy/Dindi** lifts — each side calls the other's project "unapproved"; **KRMB jurisdiction gazette (July 2021)** over listed projects — handover still incomplete.
- **With Karnataka:** the **Almatti dam height** raise (KWDT-II) threatens lower-riparian AP — dormant but alive while KWDT-II remains un-notified.

## 2. Godavari ⭐

- **GWDT (Bachawat, 1980):** unlike Krishna, built on **inter-state agreements**, leaving upper-state uses defined and the **residual flows to (undivided) AP** — the legal basis of AP's "surplus flood waters" claim (~3,000 TMC average flood discharge to the sea).
- **Polavaram (S.90 national project):** objections by **Odisha & Chhattisgarh** — backwater submergence on the **Sabari/Sileru**; cases in the SC; the Act's deemed clearances vs riparian consent ⭐ (bridge to File 20).
- **Godavari–Banakacherla Link (the live 2025-26 dispute)** ⭐⭐:
  - AP's ≈**₹80,112 cr** proposal to divert ~**200 TMC Godavari flood water** — Polavaram → Krishna (Bollapalli reservoir) → **Banakacherla regulator (Rayalaseema)**, in three segments.
  - Status: **Pre-Feasibility Report with CWC (DPR sought)**; AP seeking central funding.
  - **Telangana objects** (letters Jan & Jun 2025; all-party MP front): violates **GWDT 1980 + APRA 2014** (no clearance from Apex Council/GRMB/KWDT), appropriates "surplus" unilaterally; **Odisha & Chhattisgarh** echo Polavaram-linked objections.
  - Exam value: a *current* case that knits together S.84-85-89-90, GWDT, and fiscal capacity (₹80k cr on a stressed budget — File 16!).

## 3. Vamsadhara (with Odisha) ⭐

- **Vamsadhara Water Disputes Tribunal (constituted 2010; award June 2017):** permitted AP's **Neradi barrage** and the Katragada side weir; broadly even sharing of the river's ~115 TMC; flow-regime safeguards for Odisha.
- Odisha's challenge/implementation friction continues; **Neradi barrage still unbuilt** — cite as "award won, project pending".

## 4. Tamil Nadu & the cooperative counter-example

- **Telugu Ganga (1983, NTR-MGR accord):** Maharashtra-Karnataka-AP each spare **5 TMC (=15 TMC) of Krishna water for Chennai's drinking supply**, conveyed through AP's Telugu Ganga canal ⭐ — **cooperation, not dispute** (use as the contrast case; frictions are only over conveyance losses/timing).
- Minor irritants: **Palar** flows (AP check-dams vs TN objections) — low-stakes but name-checkable.

## 5. Why AP fights so hard — the economics link

- Water is AP's **binding development input**: Rayalaseema's chronic drought (Banakacherla's stated rationale), coastal delta agriculture (Krishna-Godavari deltas), and the **fiscal geometry** — irrigation is where AP's scarce capex and its FRBM-constrained borrowing go (Files 16-19). A water dispute in AP is **always simultaneously a fiscal dispute**.
- Institutional lesson for Mains: annual ad-hocism (66:34), un-notified awards (KWDT-II), and boards without handover (KRMB) show **dispute *management* substituting for dispute *resolution*** — argue for notified allocations + empowered boards + basin-level authorities (Mihir Shah reform line).

## 6. Prelims nuggets

1. **KWDT-I (Bachawat) 1973/76: 585/734/811 TMC**; **KWDT-II (Brijesh Kumar) 2010 — not notified; Almatti to 524.256 m**.
2. Post-2014 Krishna interim split **AP 512 : TS 299 (66:34)**; TS demands 50:50; **S.89 further ToR to KWDT-II (Oct 2023)** ⭐.
3. **S.84 Apex Council; S.85 KRMB (Krishna) & GRMB (Godavari); jurisdiction gazette July 2021**.
4. **GWDT 1980 (Bachawat)** — agreement-based; residual Godavari flows to undivided AP.
5. **Polavaram objectors: Odisha & Chhattisgarh** (Sabari/Sileru submergence).
6. **Godavari-Banakacherla: ≈₹80,112 cr, ~200 TMC flood diversion, PFR with CWC; TS/Odisha/Chhattisgarh object (2025-26)** ⭐.
7. **Vamsadhara Tribunal award 2017 — Neradi barrage allowed** (with Odisha).
8. **Telugu Ganga 1983 — 15 TMC Krishna water to Chennai (5 TMC × 3 states)** — the cooperation MCQ ⭐.
9. Legal base: **Art. 262 + ISRWD Act 1956**; tribunal award = SC-decree force.

## 7. Mains angles

1. **"AP's water disputes are the unfinished business of bifurcation." Examine.** S.84/85/89 machinery → 66:34 ad-hocism → project race (RLS vs Palamuru) → Banakacherla as escalation → fix: notify allocations, empower boards, Apex Council regularity.
2. **"Flood water to the sea is nobody's property and everybody's claim." Evaluate the Godavari-Banakacherla controversy.** AP's surplus doctrine vs GWDT/APRA process objections → CWC gatekeeping → fiscal feasibility (₹80k cr vs File 16) → cooperative alternatives (joint storage, basin authority).
3. **From Bachawat to Brijesh Kumar: why Krishna allocations still aren't settled.** Tribunal timeline → non-notification → bifurcation complication → S.89 reference → reform of the ISRWD framework (single permanent tribunal Bill).
4. **Inter-state water cooperation is possible: the Telugu Ganga precedent.** 1983 accord anatomy → what made it work (drinking-water framing, cost-sharing) → transferable lessons for Banakacherla-type conflicts.
