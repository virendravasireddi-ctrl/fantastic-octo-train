## 3. Match-pairs lightning round

- **ICI weights order:** Refinery 28.04 > Electricity 19.85 > Steel 17.92 > Coal 10.33 > Crude 8.98 > Gas 6.88 > Cement 5.37 > Fertiliser 2.63 (**"RES-CC-NCF"**).
- **Licensing survivors (4):** defence-electronics/aerospace · explosives · hazardous chemicals · cigarettes.
- **PSE 2021 strategic quartet:** atomic/space/defence · transport/telecom · power/petroleum/coal/minerals · banking/insurance/FS.
- **AP NICDC nodes:** Kopparthy-VCIC · Krishnapatnam-CBIC · Orvakal-HBIC.
- **Firsts:** EPZ-Kandla 1965 · strategic sale-Modern Foods 2000 · medical-devices park-AMTZ Vizag · green-hydrogen hub-Pudimadaka · GW-scale integrated RE storage-Pinnapuram · NTPC coastal-Simhadri.
- **Traps:** disinvestment = capital (non-debt) receipt; refinery (not electricity) tops ICI; Srisailam right bank = AP, left = Telangana; MSME limits are 2025-revised; BIFR is dead (IBC lives); NIMZ ≠ SEZ (township vs enclave); Make in India 2014 ≠ NMP 2011.

5. The heaviest item in the Index of Eight Core Industries is: (a) Electricity (b) Coal (c) Steel (d) Refinery products
6. India's first strategic sale (2000) was of: (a) BALCO (b) Modern Foods (c) VSNL (d) Hindustan Zinc

## 5. Ten Mains skeletons (10-markers; intro–3 points–verdict)

1. **Role of industry in development:** Lewis/Kaldor/linkages → India's services anomaly (17% GVA) → why manufacturing still matters (jobs, trade, security) → policy implication.
2. **Policy evolution 1948-91:** state-led → anti-concentration → efficiency turn → 1991 rupture; evaluate against outcomes at each turn.
3. **NIP 1991 impact:** five demolitions → growth/FDI/competition gains → jobs/manufacturing-share caveats → unfinished agenda.
4. **Public sector's contribution:** heavy-industry base, regional spread, cadre → pathologies → PSE-2021 synthesis → RINL vs Air India case pair.
5. **Disinvestment & privatization:** definitions → timeline → 2021 doctrine → assessment (slow pipeline, dividend pivot) → safeguards needed.
6. **Problems of core industries:** ICI frame → 3 sector cases (coal imports, discom crisis, urea distortion) → cross-cutting constraints → responses scoreboard.
7. **MSME problems & policy:** stats → credit/delayed-payment/dwarfism diagnosis → toolkit clusters → 2025 redefinition as growth-friendly turn.
8. **Industrial sickness:** definition/causes → BIFR failure → IBC + MSME frameworks → evaluation (speed vs revival balance).
9. **Make in India/manufacturing instruments:** pillars → wins (mobiles, defence, chips) → critique (share flat, assembly-led) → PLI/corridor/smart-city hardware.
10. **AP industrial strategy:** endowments → IDP 4.0 + corridors/SEZs → bottlenecks ranked → power-transition opportunity → verdict (execution & credibility decisive).
