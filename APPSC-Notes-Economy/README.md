# APPSC Notes — Economy & Development of India and Andhra Pradesh

**Target exams:** APPSC Group-I Mains (Paper-IV: Economy & Development of India and AP) and Group-II (Economy sections). The series is arranged **in the order of the Paper-IV syllabus items**: India's challenges & inclusive growth → resource mobilization in the Indian economy → **government budgeting (union)** → **government budgeting in AP** → resource mobilization in AP → industrial policy & development.

## Exam-craft notes

- **Numbers policy:** figures are labelled by year (**BE 2026-27** for both budgets, FY25/FY26 for outcomes) as of compilation (July 2026). **Budget numbers, GST rates/collections and FC annexures move** — re-verify from the latest Budget at a Glance / AP Budget in Brief / GST Council & PIB releases / the 16th FC report and ATR in your final week; the *structure* of these notes is what stays.
- **Prelims:** *article–provision*, *institution–year*, *committee–reform*, *FC–criteria* pairs are **bolded** throughout — flashcard them (File 24 §7 collects the budgeting set).
- **Mains:** open with a definition/number, argue with 2–3 named instruments or provisions, close with an evaluation and an **AP tie-in** (devolution share 4.217%, the RDG cliff, SGST dependence, the FY27 capex pivot, Polavaram/Amaravati financing, VCIC).
- Classic traps flagged inline: disinvestment ≠ revenue receipt; **interim budget ≠ vote on account**; petroleum is *inside* GST but zero-dated; **capacity ≠ generation** in energy; cesses sit **outside** the divisible pool.
