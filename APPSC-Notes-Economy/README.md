# APPSC Notes — Economy & Development of India and Andhra Pradesh

**Target exams:** APPSC Group-I Mains (Paper-IV: Economy & Development of India and AP) and Group-II (Economy sections). This series covers the Paper-IV blocks on **Resource Mobilization**, the **Industrial Sector**, **major economic challenges & inclusive growth**, and **Andhra Pradesh state finances & resources**.

## Blocks in this series

- **Block A — Resource Mobilization (Financial · Physical · Energy):** Files `01–08`
- **Block B — Industrial Sector, Policy & Manufacturing:** Files `09–16`
- **Block C — Major Challenges & Inclusive Growth:** Files `17–18`
- **Block D — Andhra Pradesh State Finances & Resources:** Files `19–24`
- **Model answers & reference data:** [`Model-Answers/`](Model-Answers/README.md) · [`Reference/`](Reference/README.md)

## Syllabus items covered

> *"Resource Mobilization in Indian Economy — budgetary resources, public debt, FDI/FPI, monetary & fiscal policies, financial markets & development finance, investment in industries and infrastructure, physical & energy resources. Industrial development — role of the industrial sector, New Industrial Policy 1991 & LPG, disinvestment & privatization, core industries, MSMEs, manufacturing policy. Major challenges of the Indian economy; inclusive growth & financial inclusion. Andhra Pradesh — state finances, budgetary resources & constraints, the AP Reorganisation Act & central assistance, public debt, mineral, forest & water resources and inter-state water disputes."*

## Exam-craft notes

- **Numbers policy:** figures are labelled by year (BE 2025-26, FY25 etc.) as of compilation (July 2026). **Budget numbers, policy rates and capacity figures move** — re-verify from the latest Budget / Economic Survey / RBI-MoSPI / AP Socio-Economic Survey releases in your final week; the *structure* of these notes is what stays.
- **Prelims:** *institution–year*, *instrument–regulator*, *scheme–ministry*, *rank–commodity* pairs are **bolded** throughout — flashcard them.
- **Mains:** open with a definition/number, argue with 2–3 named instruments or schemes, close with an evaluation and an **AP tie-in** (Polavaram, Amaravati's multilateral financing, VCIC, Mangampeta, Kia/Sri City, the Reorganisation-Act commitments).
