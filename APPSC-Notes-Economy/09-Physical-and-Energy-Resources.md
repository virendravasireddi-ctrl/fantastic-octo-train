## 0. Why "physical resources" sit in a resource-mobilization unit

Finance buys inputs; **land, water, minerals and energy are the inputs**. Mobilizing them = surveying, titling, auctioning, pricing and building the institutions (mines auctions, water user associations, power markets) that convert endowments into growth — with sustainability as the binding constraint.

## 1. Land

- Geographical area **328.7 million ha**; **net sown area ≈140 m ha (~42–43%)** — among the world's highest cultivated shares; **forest cover ≈21.8% (ISFR 2023; forest+tree ≈25.2%)**.
- Constraints: fragmentation, degradation (~30% land degraded), conversion pressure; **land acquisition (RFCTLARR Act 2013)** = cost & time (File 08); reforms: digitised land records (**SVAMITVA**, AP's resurvey drive), land pooling (**Amaravati's land-pooling scheme — 30,000+ acres, the alternative-to-acquisition exhibit** ⭐), land leasing model law.

## 2. Water

- **~18% of world population, ~4% of freshwater**; utilizable ≈**1,123 BCM** (690 surface + 433 ground); **per-capita availability ≈1,400–1,500 m³ and falling — "water-stressed" (<1,700)** ⭐.
- Groundwater ≈**62% of irrigation** — over-extraction belts; missions: Jal Jeevan (tap water), PMKSY (irrigation), Atal Bhujal; pricing/user-association reform is the mobilization angle.
- **AP:** **Polavaram — national project on the Godavari (AP Reorganisation Act 2014)** ⭐; **Pattiseema lift (2015) — first Godavari→Krishna delta link in operation** ⭐; proposed **Godavari–Banakacherla** link to Rayalaseema (2025 — label proposed); tank-restoration legacy (the Kakatiya tank tradition — history-series tie-in!).

## 3. Minerals

- India's ranks (approx.): **2nd coal producer, 2nd aluminium, 4th iron ore, 2nd cement**; mineral belt: Chota Nagpur–Odisha–Chhattisgarh core; **coal reserves ≈378 billion tonnes**.
- Policy arc ⭐: MMDR 1957 → **2015 amendment (compulsory AUCTIONS, DMF, NMET)** → National Mineral Policy 2019 → 2021 (captive/merchant easing) → **2023 amendment (critical minerals; private exploration licences)** → **Offshore Areas amendment 2023** → **National Critical Mineral Mission (Jan 2025, ≈₹16,300 crore + PSU outlays)** ⭐; **lithium find — Reasi, J&K (2023, ≈5.9 mt inferred)**; **KABIL** (2019) securing lithium/cobalt abroad (Argentina).
- **District Mineral Foundation (DMF)/PMKKKY** — mining revenue back to affected districts (mobilization-with-justice point).

## 4. Energy — the overview numbers ⭐⭐

- India: **3rd-largest energy consumer**; per-capita electricity **≈1,400 kWh** (≈⅓ world average — headroom = growth).
- **Installed power capacity ≈485 GW (July 2025), of which non-fossil = 242.8 GW = 50.1% — the Paris/NDC "50% non-fossil capacity" milestone achieved in July 2025, five years early** ⭐⭐ (THE current-affairs fact of this unit).
- **TRAP: capacity ≠ generation — coal still supplies ≈70% of electricity generated** ⭐ (renewables' low capacity-utilisation).
- Import dependence: **crude oil ≈88%**, natural gas ≈50%, plus **coal imports ≈240+ mt (FY25)** even as domestic output crossed **1 billion tonnes (FY25)** ⭐; crude import bill ≈$135 bn — the macro reason energy transition = resource mobilization.
- Targets: **500 GW non-fossil by 2030; net-zero by 2070 (Panchamrit, COP-26)**.

## 5. Conventional energy

- **Coal:** Coal India + **commercial mining auctions (2020)**; gasification mission (100 mt by 2030); thermal fleet ≈220 GW — still the base of the system.
- **Oil & gas:** **HELP/OALP (2016)** licensing; **Strategic Petroleum Reserves — Visakhapatnam (1.33 MMT) was India's FIRST (2015)** ⭐, plus Mangaluru, Padur; refining capacity ≈**257 MMTPA** (4th largest); city-gas expansion; gas target **15% of energy mix by 2030** (from ~6%).
- **Hydro:** large hydro ≈**47.7 GW** (renewable-classified since 2019); **pumped-storage (PSP) push** for grid balancing ⭐.
- **Nuclear:** ≈**8.9 GW, 25 reactors**; **three-stage programme (Bhabha)** — PHWR → **FBR (Kalpakkam PFBR core-loading 2024 ⭐)** → thorium (AP beach sands!); **Nuclear Energy Mission (Budget 2025): 100 GW by 2047, ₹20,000 cr for Small Modular Reactors/Bharat Small Reactors, amendments to Atomic Energy Act & Civil Liability law proposed** ⭐; **Kovvada (Srikakulam) — proposed nuclear park site**.

## 6. Renewables & the missions

- Standing (2025): **solar ≈110+ GW (world #3), wind ≈52 GW (#4); India #4 in total RE capacity**.
- Mission stack: **National Solar Mission (2010)** → solar parks; **PM-KUSUM** (agri solar); **PM Surya Ghar Muft Bijli Yojana (2024) — 1 crore rooftop homes, ≈₹75,000 cr** ⭐; **National Green Hydrogen Mission (2023, ₹19,744 cr; 5 MMT by 2030)**; offshore-wind VGF (2024); **ethanol E20 blending achieved 2025 — five years early** ⭐; CBG/bio-energy.
- **International Solar Alliance — HQ Gurugram; India-France, 2015** ⭐; One Sun One World One Grid.
- Grid & distribution: **One Nation One Grid (synchronised 2013)**; the weak link = **discoms** — AT&C losses ≈15–16%, reform via **RDSS (2021)** (UDAY's successor); storage: PSP + battery PLI; green open access.
- Financiers: **IREDA, PFC/REC, sovereign green bonds (2023)** — energy transition as a finance problem (Files 04/07 links).

## 9. Mains angles

1. **"India's energy transition is a resource-mobilization problem." Discuss.** 50%-capacity milestone vs 70%-coal generation → investment needs (grid, storage, GH2) → instruments (green bonds, IREDA, VGF, PLI) → import-bill savings as the return.
2. **"Andhra Pradesh can be India's clean-energy warehouse." Evaluate.** Solar parks + wind + **PSP endowment (Pinnapuram)** + ports for GH2 export (Pudimadaka) + ICE Policy 2024 → constraints: discom finances, land, evacuation → verdict.
3. **Critical minerals are the new oil.** NCMM/KABIL/auctions + Reasi lithium → processing gap, China dependence → AP/domestic value-addition (beach sands policy balance).
4. **Water as the binding physical resource.** Stress numbers → pricing/institutions → AP's Godavari surplus-to-deficit engineering (Polavaram, Pattiseema, Banakacherla-proposed) → inter-state coordination caveat (KWDT/GWDT).
5. **Land mobilization without conflict.** Acquisition-vs-pooling (Amaravati exhibit) → titling/records → industrial land banks (APIIC) → justice safeguards (2013 Act, Fifth Schedule areas — bauxite lesson).
