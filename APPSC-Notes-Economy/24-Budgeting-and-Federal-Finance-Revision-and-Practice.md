## 2. The deficit ladder (formulas verbatim ⭐⭐)

| Deficit | = |
|---|---|
| Revenue deficit | revenue expenditure − revenue receipts |
| Effective RD | RD − grants for capital-asset creation (2011-12, Rangarajan) |
| **Fiscal deficit** | total expenditure − (revenue receipts + non-debt capital receipts) |
| Primary deficit | FD − interest payments |
| Monetised deficit | RBI's net new credit to government (automatic form ended 1997) |
