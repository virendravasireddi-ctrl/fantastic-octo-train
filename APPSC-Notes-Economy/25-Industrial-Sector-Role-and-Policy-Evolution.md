## 0. Why industry matters — the theory kit (open every Mains answer with one of these)

- **Structural transformation (Lewis, 1954):** development = moving surplus labour from low-productivity agriculture to high-productivity industry; wages and savings rise as the "dual economy" modernises.
- **Kaldor's growth laws:** manufacturing is the **engine of growth** — it enjoys increasing returns, and its expansion pulls up productivity economy-wide.
- **Hirschman (1958) — linkages:** industry has the strongest **backward linkages** (demand for inputs: mining, power, agriculture) and **forward linkages** (supplies machines/inputs to every sector) ⭐.
- Other channels: productive **formal jobs**, **exports & technology diffusion**, taxable capacity, urbanisation, defence/strategic autonomy.
- **India's anomaly** ⭐⭐: growth has been **services-led**. Industry ≈ **27–28% of GVA**, manufacturing stuck ≈ **17%**, and manufacturing employs only ≈ **11–12% of workers (PLFS)**. **Rodrik's "premature deindustrialisation"** — peaking of manufacturing share at lower income levels than the East-Asian path — is the standard framing.

## 1. Where industry stands — composition & measurement

- Industrial sector = **manufacturing + mining & quarrying + electricity/gas/water + construction**.
- **IIP** (NSO, monthly; base **2011-12**): weights — **manufacturing 77.63%, mining 14.37%, electricity 7.99%** ⭐. Use-based classes (capital goods = investment proxy; consumer durables = urban demand proxy) are frequent prelims fodder.
- **Index of Eight Core Industries (ICI)** carries **40.27% weight inside IIP** (detail in File 28).
- **ASI (Annual Survey of Industries)** = factory-sector database; **Tamil Nadu leads in number of factories; AP ranks in the top 4–5** ⭐ (AP tie-in).

## 2. Evolution of industrial policy since independence ⭐⭐ (the exam's favourite timeline)

| Policy | Core content | Markers |
|---|---|---|
| **IPR 1948** | Mixed economy declared; state monopoly in **arms, atomic energy, rail**; state to start new units in 6 basic industries; rest regulated private | First policy; "middle path" |
| **IDR Act 1951** | Statutory teeth: **compulsory licensing** for scheduled industries, registration, capacity control | The legal engine of the licence raj |
| **IPR 1956** ⭐⭐ | "**Economic constitution of India**"; **Schedule A (17** industries — state monopoly**)**, **Schedule B (12** — state-led, private supplement**)**, **Schedule C** (rest); heavy-industry push mirroring the **Mahalanobis Second Plan**; regional balance; cautious foreign capital | The framework till 1991 |
| **Karve Committee 1955** | Village & small industries as employment strategy | Origin of SSI protection |
| **Hazari (1967) & Dutt (1969) reports** | Licensing had been cornered by big business houses; Dutt (Industrial Licensing Policy Inquiry) proposed the **joint sector** | → led directly to MRTP |
| **MRTP Act 1969** | Assets threshold (₹20 crore) for "monopolies"; pre-entry approval for expansion/M&A; MRTP Commission (1970) | The anti-bigness turn |
| **FERA 1973** | Foreign equity capped at 40% | **IBM & Coca-Cola exited (1977)** ⭐ |
| **IPS 1973** | Defined **core industries**; large houses confined to core/heavy sectors; joint sector endorsed | First official "core" list |
| **IPS 1977 (Janata)** | Decentralisation: **tiny sector** (investment ≤ ₹1 lakh, towns < 50,000), **District Industries Centres (1978)**, expanded **SSI reservation**, curbs on big/foreign business | Gandhian small-is-beautiful phase |
| **IP 1980** | Return to efficiency: capacity **regularisation** (excess capacity legalised), **automatic expansion**, **nucleus plants** for backward areas | Pro-competition U-turn begins |
| **1985–86 (Rajiv era)** | **Broad-banding**, delicensing of ~25 industries, MRTP threshold raised to ₹100 crore, **MODVAT (1986)**, technology missions | "Reform by stealth" — dress rehearsal for 1991 |

**Small-scale reservation arc** (cross-ref File 29): began **1967 (47 items)** → peaked ≈ **800+ items** → phased out, **fully de-reserved by April 2015** ⭐.

## 3. Balance sheet of the pre-1991 regime (argue both sides in Mains)

**Achievements:** diversified industrial base from near-zero capital goods capacity; public-sector heavy industry (steel, machines, power, oil — File 27); S&T/managerial base; some regional spread; self-reliance in a forex-scarce era.

**Failures:** **licence-permit-quota raj** — rent-seeking, entry barriers, capacity caps; high-cost, low-quality protected economy; **"Hindu rate of growth" (~3.5%, Raj Krishna's phrase)** ⭐; export pessimism; SSI reservation froze firms small; controls meant to curb concentration often entrenched incumbents (Hazari/Dutt findings).

## 4. AP box — the early industrial map of coastal Andhra ⭐

- **Visakhapatnam Port (1933)** and **Hindustan Shipyard (Scindia 1941; later a PSU, under Ministry of Defence since 2010)** made Vizag the anchor of Andhra industry.
- **"Visakha ukku — Andhrula hakku" agitation (1966)** ⭐ won the **Visakhapatnam Steel Plant** (foundation 1971; production 1992; RINL — File 27's disinvestment flashpoint).
- Other legacy PSUs: **BHPV Visakhapatnam (1966; merged into BHEL 2013)**, Hindustan Zinc smelter (Visakhapatnam), **HPCL Visakh refinery (1957, Caltex origin; nationalised into HPCL 1976)**.
- Use pattern: pre-1991 AP industry = port + PSU story; private manufacturing depth came only after liberalisation (Files 26, 31).

## 5. Prelims nuggets

1. **IPR 1948** — first policy, mixed economy; **IDR Act 1951** — licensing statute (in force 1952).
2. **IPR 1956** — "economic constitution"; **Schedule A = 17, Schedule B = 12** ⭐; tied to **Mahalanobis** Second Plan.
3. **Karve 1955** (village/SSI), **Hazari 1967** (licensing review), **Dutt 1969** (→ joint sector, MRTP).
4. **MRTP 1969** (₹20 crore threshold; raised to **₹100 crore in 1985**); **FERA 1973** (40% cap → IBM/Coca-Cola exit 1977).
5. **IPS 1977** — tiny sector (≤₹1 lakh), **DICs 1978**; **IP 1980** — regularisation, automatic expansion, nucleus plants.
6. **1985–86:** broad-banding + **MODVAT 1986**.
7. **IIP base 2011-12**; weights: manufacturing **77.63** > mining **14.37** > electricity **7.99**; ICI = **40.27%** of IIP.
8. Manufacturing ≈ **17% of GVA**, ≈ **11–12% of employment**; concepts: **Lewis** dual economy, **Kaldor's laws**, **Hirschman linkages**, **Rodrik premature deindustrialisation**.
9. SSI reservation: **1967 → nil by April 2015**.
10. "Hindu rate of growth" — **Raj Krishna**.
11. Vizag Steel: agitation **1966**, foundation **1971**, production **1992**.
