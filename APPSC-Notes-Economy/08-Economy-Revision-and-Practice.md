## 1. The receipts tree (redraw from memory before the exam)

```
GOVERNMENT RECEIPTS
├── REVENUE RECEIPTS (no asset/liability change)
│   ├── Tax: direct (corporation, income) + indirect (GST, customs, excise) + cesses*
│   │      *cesses/surcharges NOT shared with states (Art. 271)
│   └── Non-tax: RBI/PSU dividends, interest, spectrum, royalties, fees
└── CAPITAL RECEIPTS
    ├── Debt-creating: market borrowings (G-secs/T-bills), small savings, external loans
    └── Non-debt: loan recoveries, DISINVESTMENT & monetisation  ← the classic trap
```

## 2. Deficits ladder (BE 2025-26 markers — update yearly)

| Deficit | Formula | Marker |
|---|---|---|
| Revenue | Rev. exp − rev. receipts | ≈1.5% GDP |
| Fiscal | Total exp − (rev. receipts + non-debt capital receipts) | **4.4% GDP** |
| Primary | FD − interest payments | ≈0.8% GDP |
| Anchor | Central debt-GDP → **≈50±1% by 2031** (from FY27) | debt ≈57% (FY25) |

## 3. Sources of finance — the verdict line (one-line memory hooks)

| Source | One-line verdict |
|---|---|
| Tax | First-best: broaden base (GST 2.0, IT Act 2025), keep rates moderate |
| Non-tax | Supplement — windfall-prone (RBI ₹2.69 lakh cr, 2025) |
| Internal debt | Fine for capex (golden rule), watch r-vs-g and the interest treadmill |
| External multilateral | Cheap & disciplined, keep small (India: <5% of central debt) |
| FDI | Best foreign capital — non-debt, stable, brings technology |
| FPI | Welcome with buffers — hot money (2013 "Fragile Five") |
| Monetisation/deficit financing | Emergency-only (ad hocs dead since 1997) |
| Disinvestment/asset monetisation | Strategic recycling, not revenue (Air India 2021; NMP) |

8. India is the largest borrower of the: (a) NDB (b) ADB (c) AIIB (d) IBRD

## 7. Ten Mains questions with skeletons

1. **Map the sources of financial resources for India's public and private sectors and evaluate their relative desirability.** — Sources table → tax-first hierarchy → golden-rule debt → FDI-over-FPI → close: household-savings dip + remedy.
2. **"Taxation is the price of civilisation, but India under-collects it." Examine.** — Tax-GDP vs peers → causes → GST 2.0/IT Act 2025/technology → equity balance (direct share 56%).
3. **Assess India's public-debt strategy after COVID.** — 9.2% episode → glide path 4.4% → debt anchor 50±1% by 2031 → interest treadmill vs capex multiplier → verdict.
4. **External debt from multilateral agencies: crutch, catalyst or trap?** — Profile ($736 bn, 19%) → agency table (IDA→AIIB arc) → conditionality history (1991) → Amaravati/VCIC as catalyst evidence.
5. **FDI vs FII: which serves India's development better, and how should policy treat each?** — Contrast table → FY25 gross/net puzzle → index-inclusion debt story → court-and-buffer prescription.
6. **How did monetary and fiscal policy cooperate over 2020–25? What does it teach about stabilization?** — Pandemic expansion → 2022 tightening + MPC letter → 2025 easing + consolidation → institutional lessons (FIT, FRBM flexibility).
7. **"Without deep financial markets there is no resource mobilization." Discuss India's markets and remaining gaps.** — Banks-MFs-demat boom → shallow corporate bonds, municipal finance → NaBFID/index-inclusion fixes.
8. **Trace the DFI story and the logic of NaBFID.** — 1948–1990 build → post-1991 fall → infra-finance vacuum → twin balance sheet → 2021 revival design → success conditions.
9. **Infrastructure financing has moved "from budget to balance sheets." Explain with instruments and risks.** — Budget/IEBR → PPP menu (HAM/TOT/VGF) → monetisation & InvITs → risks (renegotiation, ALM) → Gati Shakti coordination.
10. **"For AP, resource mobilization is existential." Discuss post-bifurcation AP's strategy across finance, industry and energy.** — Revenue-deficit + debt facts → multilateral (Amaravati), corridors/ports, Orvakal-Kopparthy → energy endowment (solar-wind-PSP-GH2) → risks: debt transparency, discoms → balanced close.
