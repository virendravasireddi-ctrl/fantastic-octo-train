The deep dives sit next door: **File 16** (how bad are the finances), **File 19** (where the money comes from), **File 20** (bifurcation & central assistance), **File 21** (debt machinery), **File 18** (SFC & local finance).

## 1. The machinery — how AP budgets

- Constitutional frame: **Art 202 (state AFS) – 207** mirror Arts 112-117 (File 11); Assembly votes Demands; the **Legislative Council cannot vote grants**; Governor's recommendation for money bills.
- **AP FRBM Act, 2005** ⭐: RD/FD/debt targets in line with successive FC paths; amended repeatedly to adopt central glide paths and borrowing-ceiling conditions; the **net borrowing ceiling (≈3% of GSDP + 0.5% power-reform add-on)** set annually by the Centre under **Art 293(3)** is the *operative* constraint (File 21).
- Documents: AFS, **Budget in Brief**, DGs, FRBM statements, **Socio-Economic Survey** (laid a day before — AP's mini Economic Survey ⭐), gender/child/welfare annexures.
- Rhythm: full budget ~Feb-Mar; **election years ran on votes-on-account (2014, 2019, 2024)** ⭐; presented since June 2024 by **Finance Minister Payyavula Keshav**.

## 2. Budget constraints (recap in budgeting language — anatomy in File 19)

1. **Born revenue-deficit:** bifurcation took Hyderabad (~seat of the combined state's buoyant services base) — AP has run **revenue deficits every year since 2014-15** ⭐.
2. **Committed expenditure first-charge:** salaries + pensions + interest absorb the bulk of revenue receipts; the **welfare stack ("Super Six" era DBTs — File 16 §2.6)** is quasi-committed.
3. **Narrow flexible-tax base:** SGST (Council-set) dominates own-tax revenue; petroleum VAT + liquor excise are the only levers AP moves alone (File 14 §9).
4. **Cushions withdrawn:** GST compensation ended (2022); **15th FC revenue-deficit grants (≈₹30,497 cr) have NO successor — the 16th FC discontinued RDG (2026-31)** ⭐⭐ (File 15).
5. **Credibility overhang:** off-budget/SPV borrowings and guarantees flagged by CAG (File 21) raise AP's scrutiny and borrowing cost.

## 3. Management of deficits — the AP version

- **The arithmetic (BE 2026-27, verified):** **revenue deficit ₹22,002 crore ≈ 1.11% of GSDP; fiscal deficit ₹75,868 crore ≈ 3.84% of GSDP** ⭐⭐ (implied GSDP ≈ **₹19.8 lakh crore**). FY26 BE ran materially higher RD — the FY27 budget *narrates* consolidation (verify RE outcomes).
- Tools in use: Art 293(3)/NBC discipline; **RBI WMA-overdraft ladder** (AP a frequent user — stress symptom, File 16); capex protection via **SASCI 50-year loans + externally aided projects** (Files 15/21); land-based non-debt financing (Amaravati LPS, File 19); growth-led revenue bet ("Swarnandhra 2047", ~15% growth target).
- The structural test ⭐: RD persistence means borrowing partly funds consumption — the "golden rule" fails until RD → 0 (File 12's quality-of-deficit logic applied to AP).

## 5. Analysis — what the 2026-27 AP budget says ⭐⭐ (Mains material)

1. **A capex pivot on a welfare base.** The ~36% capital-outlay jump is the budget's signature — the "Swarnandhra" investment strategy (Amaravati, ports, corridors, Polavaram) finally shows up in the *state's own* budget lines, not just EAP/central channels.
2. **Deficit optics improve; the structure remains.** RD at 1.11% is the consolidation claim — but it survives on transfer buoyancy (16th FC share ↑ to **4.217%**) while the **RDG cushion is gone** ⭐; slippage risk sits in welfare arrears and guarantees (File 16).
3. **Post-16th-FC adaptation.** More untied devolution + LB/disaster grants + the **Vijayawada/Visakhapatnam wastewater windows (≤₹5,000 cr each)** replace RDG — AP's grant mix shifts from *gap-filling* to *purpose-tied* (File 15 §5).
4. **Welfare-vs-capex reconciliation attempt:** Super Six continuity (Talliki Vandanam, Deepam-2, Annadata Sukhibhava — File 16) plus a capex surge = the classic AP tension (File 19 §Mains) restated at larger scale; credibility depends on revenue assumptions (~GSDP +double digits) holding.
5. **Risks to flag in any answer:** guarantee/off-budget stock (File 21), power-sector subventions, optimistic revenue growth, execution capacity for a ₹54k-cr capex programme.
