## 0. Frame

- A government budget is the **annual statement of estimated receipts and expenditure** — the Constitution never uses the word "budget": the constitutional term is the **Annual Financial Statement (AFS), Article 112** ⭐.
- Financial year: **1 April – 31 March** (since 1867); the **Shankar Acharya panel (2016)** examined a Jan–Dec year and Apr–Mar was retained.
- One-line thesis for Mains: the budget is simultaneously a **statement of accounts, an instrument of macro-policy (File 06), and Parliament's control over the executive** — most "budgeting" questions are really asking which of the three you can explain.

## 1. The constitutional frame ⭐⭐ (match-pair goldmine)

| Article | Provision |
|---|---|
| **112** | **Annual Financial Statement** laid before both Houses |
| **113** | **Demands for Grants** — only Lok Sabha votes; only on the President's recommendation; expenditure "charged" is **not submitted to vote** |
| **114** | **Appropriation Bill** — no money out of the CFI except by appropriation made by law ⭐; no amendment possible that alters amount/destination |
| **115** | Supplementary, additional and **excess grants** (excess needs PAC scrutiny first ⭐) |
| **116** | **Vote on Account** (advance grant for part of the year), vote of credit, exceptional grant |
| **117** | Financial bills — President's recommendation needed |
| **110** | **Money Bill** definition; Speaker's certificate final |
| **265** | **No tax except by authority of law** ⭐ |
| **266** | **Consolidated Fund of India** + **Public Account** (Public Account outflows need no vote ⭐) |
| **267** | **Contingency Fund of India** — imprest with the President; **corpus ₹30,000 crore (raised 2021)** ⭐ |
| **292 / 293** | Borrowing by Union / by states (**293(3): Centre's consent while a state is indebted to it** ⭐) |
| **202–207** | The **state counterparts** (AFS of the state = Art 202) — the frame for AP's budget (File 17) |

- **Charged vs voted expenditure** ⭐: charged on the CFI (non-votable, but discussable) — President's emoluments, Speaker/Dy Speaker, SC & HC judges' salaries, **CAG**, UPSC, **interest on public debt**, court decrees. Everything else = voted via Demands for Grants.

## 2. Structure & components

```
BUDGET
├── REVENUE BUDGET
│   ├── Revenue receipts   — tax + non-tax (detail: File 03)
│   └── Revenue expenditure — salaries, pensions, interest, subsidies, grants
└── CAPITAL BUDGET
    ├── Capital receipts    — debt (borrowings) + non-debt (disinvestment, loan recoveries)
    └── Capital expenditure — asset creation, loans to states/PSEs
```

- **Grants to states that create assets** are still *revenue* expenditure of the Centre — hence the "**effective capital expenditure**" concept (capex + such grants; **₹17.15 lakh crore BE 2026-27** ⭐).
- **Plan/Non-Plan classification abolished from 2017-18** (Rangarajan panel) → replaced by **revenue/capital + scheme classification** (central sector schemes · centrally sponsored schemes · establishment) ⭐.
- **Railway budget merged with the General Budget from 2017-18** (Bibek Debroy committee) — ended a practice begun in **1924 (Acworth Committee)** ⭐⭐.

## 3. The budget documents (what actually gets laid)

| Document | Mandate |
|---|---|
| Annual Financial Statement | Art 112 |
| Demands for Grants | Art 113 |
| Finance Bill | Art 110/117 |
| **Macro-Economic Framework Statement** | FRBM Act |
| **Medium-Term Fiscal Policy cum Fiscal Policy Strategy Statement** (two statements **merged in 2021**) | FRBM Act |
| Expenditure Budget & Expenditure Profile, Receipt Budget, **Budget at a Glance** | explanatory |
| **Output-Outcome Monitoring Framework** (with NITI, since 2017-18) | outcome budgeting ⭐ |
| **Gender Budget Statement (since 2005-06)**, Child Budget, SC/ST welfare statements | equity statements ⭐ |

## 4. The budget process (cycle of four stages)

1. **Formulation (secret, executive):** Budget Division, **Department of Economic Affairs, MoF** ⭐ issues the budget circular (~Sept); RE/BE meetings with ministries; pre-budget consultations; **halwa ceremony → "lock-in"** of officials; Cabinet sees the tax proposals only minutes before presentation.
2. **Enactment (Parliament):** presentation on **1 February** (since 2017; at **11 a.m. since 1999 — Yashwant Sinha ended the 5 p.m. colonial practice** ⭐) → general discussion → **Departmentally Related Standing Committees scrutinise Demands (since 1993)** → discussion & voting on DGs in Lok Sabha — undiscussed demands **guillotined** on the last day ⭐ → **Appropriation Bill (Art 114)** → **Finance Bill** (tax side; under the **Provisional Collection of Taxes Act**, declared duties have force for **75 days** pending passage ⭐).
   - **Cut motions** ⭐: **Policy cut** ("reduce to ₹1" — disapproval of policy), **Economy cut** (reduce by a specified amount), **Token cut** ("reduce by ₹100" — a specific grievance). Rarely passed (would signal loss of confidence).
   - **Rajya Sabha**: cannot vote DGs; returns the Money Bill within **14 days**.
3. **Execution:** controlling officers, **PFMS**; **just-in-time releases & Single Nodal Agency accounts for CSS (2021-)** ⭐ — ended the float of idle funds.
4. **Audit & review:** CAG audit → **PAC** (chaired by an Opposition member by convention ⭐) → ATRs; the accountability loop closes ~2 years after the money moved.

## 5. Recent changes in budgeting ⭐⭐ (the syllabus phrase — keep this table current)

| Year | Change |
|---|---|
| 1999 | Presentation moved from 5 p.m. to **11 a.m.** |
| 2017 | Date advanced to **1 February** (full budget operational from 1 April — largely ended the Vote-on-Account routine ⭐); **Railway budget merged**; **Plan/Non-Plan abolished** |
| 2005-06 | **Outcome budgeting** & **gender budgeting** began |
| 2019 | Briefcase → **bahi-khata**; 2021: fully **paperless budget + Union Budget Mobile App** |
| 2021 | FRBM statements rationalised; **Contingency Fund raised to ₹30,000 cr**; off-budget FCI-NSSF loans brought **on-budget** ⭐ (transparency reform, File 12) |
| 2025 | **Income-tax Act 2025** passed, replacing the 1961 Act — **effective 1 April 2026**; introduces the unified "**tax year**" ⭐ |
| 2025-26 → | Fiscal policy re-anchored on a **debt-to-GDP path (Centre ≈50±1% by March 2031)** instead of annual fiscal-deficit targets alone (File 12) ⭐⭐ |

## 6. Types of budget

| Type | Idea | India marker |
|---|---|---|
| Balanced / surplus / **deficit** | overall posture | India runs deficit budgets by design (File 12) |
| **Line-item** | control over objects of expenditure | the classical/legacy form |
| **Performance** | link outlays to activities | **Hoover Commission (US, 1949)**; India from **1968** on ARC advice ⭐ |
| **Programme (PPBS)** | objectives → programmes | US 1960s import |
| **Zero-based** | justify every rupee from scratch each year | **Peter Pyhrr**; tried in India **1986-87** ⭐ |
| **Outcome** | outlays → **measurable outcomes** | since **2005-06**; now Output-Outcome Framework |
| **Gender** | flow of funds to women (Part A 100% / Part B ≥30%) | statement since **2005-06** ⭐ |
| Cash vs **accrual** basis | when transactions are recognised | India is cash-based; **12th FC recommended accrual**; GASAB roadmap |
| **Interim budget vs Vote on Account** | full statement in an election year vs **Art 116** advance grant (usually 2 months) | interim ≠ VoA ⭐ (classic trap) |
| Lapsable vs non-lapsable funds | March-rush problem | e.g. the proposed **non-lapsable defence modernisation fund (15th FC)** |

## 7. AP tie-in

- AP mirrors the whole machinery under **Art 202-207** with its **FRBM Act, 2005**; election years (2014, 2019, 2024) saw **votes-on-account** before full budgets ⭐ — the AP-side detail and the 2026-27 AP budget are in **File 17**.
- AP publishes its own **gender budget** and welfare-scheme annexures; its **off-budget borrowings** are the transparency counter-example (Files 16/21).

## 8. Prelims nuggets

1. "Budget" is not in the Constitution — **Art 112 = Annual Financial Statement** ⭐.
2. **Charged** expenditure (interest, judges, CAG, UPSC) is **discussable but not votable**.
3. **Appropriation Act** is the only authority to withdraw from the CFI; **Public Account needs no vote**.
4. Rail budget merged **2017-18** on **Bibek Debroy** panel advice; separate since **1924 (Acworth)**.
5. **Plan/Non-Plan ended 2017-18 (Rangarajan panel)**.
6. Cut motions: **policy = ₹1; token = ₹100** ⭐.
7. Finance Bill protection: **Provisional Collection of Taxes Act — 75 days**.
8. **Zero-based budgeting — Peter Pyhrr; India 1986-87**; performance budgeting — India 1968.
9. Gender & outcome budgeting both date to **2005-06**.
10. **Contingency Fund of India = ₹30,000 crore (2021)**; kept by the **Finance Secretary on the President's behalf**.
11. Budget is prepared by the **Budget Division, DEA (Ministry of Finance)** — not by NITI.
12. **Income-tax Act 2025 replaces the 1961 Act from 1-4-2026** ⭐.

## 9. Mains angles

1. **"The Union budget is as much an instrument of parliamentary control as of economic policy." Discuss the structure and process of budgeting in India.** Constitutional frame (§1) → four-stage cycle (§4) → control devices (DG/cut motions/PAC) → dilution points (guillotine, money-bill route) → recent transparency reforms (§5).
2. **Trace the recent changes in India's budgeting process and evaluate their significance.** Table §5 → group into calendar reforms (1 Feb), classification reforms (plan/non-plan, rail merger), transparency (off-budget cleanup, paperless), outcome orientation (OOMF, gender) → verdict: process modernised; the deficit-quality question remains (File 12).
3. **Distinguish types of budgeting and argue which India actually practises.** §6 table → India = line-item base + outcome overlay + gender lens; accrual and true zero-base remain aspirations.
