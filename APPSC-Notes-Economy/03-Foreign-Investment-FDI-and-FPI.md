## 0. The capital-account map

Foreign capital reaches India as: **FDI** · **FPI/FII** · **ECBs** · **NRI deposits** · ADR/GDR issues · trade credit. Legal spine: **FEMA 1999 (replacing FERA 1973)** ⭐ — capital-account transactions regulated by RBI/Government; equity investment governed by the **Consolidated FDI Policy (DPIIT)** and **SEBI (FPI) Regulations**.

## 1. FDI — regime

- **Definition:** investment with **lasting interest & control — ≥10% equity** (IMF convention) ⭐; brings capital **plus technology, management, market access**.
- **Routes:** **Automatic** (no approval — most sectors) vs **Government route** (approval via ministries; FIPB **abolished 2017**).
- **Press Note 3 (2020)** ⭐: investment from **countries sharing a land border** (read: China) needs **government approval** regardless of sector.
- **Prohibited:** lottery, gambling, chit funds, Nidhi, real-estate *trading*, tobacco *manufacturing*, atomic energy & railway *operations* (infrastructure allowed).
- Key caps (check latest): defence **74% automatic/100% approval**; telecom **100%**; **insurance 74% → Budget 2025 announced 100%** (for insurers investing premiums domestically); space sector liberalised **2024**; single-brand retail 100%; multi-brand retail 51% (dormant); banking private 74%.
- **India crossed $1 trillion cumulative FDI (2000–2024)** ⭐.
- Recent trend to quote both-ways ⭐: **gross FDI inflows ≈$81 bn (FY25, +14%)** BUT **net FDI collapsed to ≈$0.4 bn** — record **repatriation/exits** (private-equity sales, IPO exits) + rising **outward FDI** by Indian firms. Interpretation: maturity of exit markets, not just weakness — but flags the financing gap.
- Sources: **Singapore, Mauritius, USA** lead (treaty-routing caveat); sectors: services, software, trading; destination states: Maharashtra, Karnataka, Gujarat, Delhi-NCR dominate — **AP's share small (~1–2%)** → the industrial-corridor push (File 06).

## 2. FPI/FII — regime

- **FPI** = portfolio investment **<10% equity**, plus debt instruments; regulated by **SEBI (FPI) Regulations 2019** (Categories I & II; the older "FII" label survives in the syllabus).
- Instruments: listed equity, G-secs/SDLs (via limits), corporate bonds; **Participatory Notes (P-notes)** — offshore derivatives on Indian securities, periodically tightened (KYC concerns) ⭐; **Voluntary Retention Route (VRR)** for sticky debt money.
- **Index-inclusion milestone ⭐: Indian G-secs entered the JP Morgan GBI-EM index (June 2024)** (and Bloomberg EM index, 2025) → tens of billions of passive debt inflows — a structural cheapening of government borrowing (link to File 02).
- Behaviour: pro-cyclical "**hot money**" — sell-offs in 2008 (GFC), **2013 taper tantrum (India in the "Fragile Five")** ⭐, 2022 Fed tightening, late-2024/early-2025 equity outflows.

## 3. FDI vs FPI — the contrast table ⭐ (guaranteed question zone)

| Dimension | FDI | FPI |
|---|---|---|
| Nature | **Lasting interest, control (≥10%)** | Financial return only (<10%) |
| Assets | Greenfield/brownfield, unlisted too | Listed securities |
| Stability | Durable — "**cannot pack up overnight**" | Volatile, reversible |
| Brings | Capital + **technology + jobs + exports** | Liquidity, market depth, currency support |
| Exit effect | Slow | Sudden stops → rupee/bond stress |
| Policy stance | Court it (ease of doing business) | Welcome but **buffer it** (reserves, VRR, macro-prudential limits) |

## 4. Desirability & consequences of different sources ⭐⭐ (the syllabus phrase — one table to rule the unit)

| Source | Desirable because | Consequences/risks | Verdict |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Taxation** | Non-inflationary, equitable (if progressive), permanent | Excess rates → evasion/distortion (Laffer); indirect taxes regressive | **First-best**; widen base, keep rates moderate |
| **Non-tax (dividends, spectrum, user charges)** | No distortion; cost-recovery discipline | Volatile, windfall-dependent (RBI transfer) | Supplement |
| **Internal borrowing** | Deep market, no currency risk; funds capex (golden rule) | **Crowding out**, interest treadmill, financial repression | Second-best; obey Domar/FRBM anchor |
| **External multilateral loans** | Cheap, long, project-disciplined; real resources | Conditionality, FX risk, tied procurement | Marginal & concessional use |
| **Deficit financing (monetisation)** | Instant; crisis tool | **Inflation tax** (hurts the poor), external imbalance | Last resort (ad hocs dead since 1997) |
| **FDI** | Non-debt, stable, tech/jobs/exports spillovers | Profit repatriation, sovereignty/security (PN3), crowding of domestic firms, treaty-shopping | **Most desirable foreign source** |
| **FPI** | Deepens markets, cheapens govt/corporate borrowing | Hot-money reversals, asset bubbles, contagion | Desirable **with buffers** |
| **ECB/NRI deposits** | Cheap when global rates low | Currency mismatch on corporate books | Cap & hedge |
| **Disinvestment/monetisation** | Unlocks dead capital for capex | One-off; "family-silver" critique; undervaluation risk | Strategic, case-by-case (Air India 2021, LIC IPO 2022) |

## 5. AP investment exhibits (Mains localiser)

- **Kia Motors, Anantapur (2019, ≈$2 bn)** — flagship manufacturing FDI in AP ⭐; **Sri City (Chittoor/Tirupati region)** — multinational manufacturing cluster.
- **Announced mega-projects (label as announced, verify status):** Google's **Visakhapatnam AI data-centre hub (announced Oct 2025, ≈$15 bn with partners)** ⭐; ArcelorMittal-Nippon Steel plant (Anakapalli region).
- The state-level lesson: FDI follows **infrastructure + logistics + power + speed of approvals** — AP's corridor/port strategy (File 06) is its FDI strategy.

1. **FERA 1973 → FEMA 1999**; FIPB abolished **2017**; FDI policy by **DPIIT**.
2. FDI threshold **≥10% (lasting interest)**; FPI **<10%**.
3. **Press Note 3 (2020)** — land-border countries need approval.
4. Prohibited: lottery, gambling, chit funds, real-estate trading, tobacco manufacturing, atomic energy.
5. Insurance FDI **74% → 100% announced (Budget 2025)**; space liberalised 2024; telecom 100%.
6. **Cumulative FDI $1 trillion (2000–2024)**; FY25 gross **≈$81 bn**, **net ≈$0.4 bn** (repatriation surge).
7. Top FDI source: **Singapore**; top destination state: **Maharashtra**.
8. **P-notes** = offshore derivative instruments; **VRR** = sticky-debt route; FPI regs — **SEBI, 2019**.
9. **JP Morgan EM bond-index inclusion: June 2024** ⭐.
10. **Taper tantrum 2013 — "Fragile Five"** (India, Indonesia, Brazil, Turkey, South Africa).
11. FDI = **non-debt-creating** capital; ECB = debt-creating.
12. **Kia Anantapur (2019)**; Sri City; Google Vizag hub **announced 2025**.
