## 0. Definition — know the latest numbers ⭐⭐

- Arc: SSI (investment-in-plant limits) → **MSMED Act 2006** (mfg/services separate) → **2020 composite criteria** (investment + turnover; mfg = services) → **Budget 2025-26 revision (effective 1 Apr 2025)** ⭐⭐:

| Class | Investment ≤ | Turnover ≤ |
|---|---|---|
| **Micro** | ₹2.5 crore | ₹10 crore |
| **Small** | ₹25 crore | ₹100 crore |
| **Medium** | ₹125 crore | ₹500 crore |

(Old 2020 limits — 1/10/50 cr investment; 5/50/250 cr turnover — may still appear in older MCQ keys; the 2025 revision = investment ×2.5, turnover ×2, so units can **grow without losing benefits**.)

## 1. Why MSMEs matter

- ≈ **30% of GDP**, ≈ **45% of exports**, second-largest employer after agriculture (reported employment on **Udyam + Udyam Assist ≈ 25–26 crore**; registrations ≈ **6 crore units**) ⭐.
- Regional spread, low capital-per-job, ancillarisation base for large industry, nursery of entrepreneurship.

## 2. Problems ⭐⭐ (the syllabus phrase — enumerate crisply)

1. **Credit gap** ≈ ₹20–25 lakh crore; only ~1 in 5 formally financed; collateral demands.
2. **Delayed payments** — the silent killer: buyers sit on dues (₹ lakhs of crores locked). Tools: MSMED **45-day rule**, **MSME Samadhaan** portal, **TReDS** invoice discounting (mandate widened — companies above **₹250 crore turnover** to onboard, 2024), **Income-tax Sec 43B(h)** (buyer's deduction only on actual payment) ⭐.
3. **Dwarfism** ⭐ — Economic Survey 2018-19: old, small "dwarfs" vs young "infants"; size-linked incentives made staying small rational.
4. Informality (weak books → no credit), technology & quality gaps, power/logistics costs, marketing reach, skills, and compliance burden.
5. Shock vulnerability: demonetisation, GST transition, COVID (hence ECLGS).

## 3. Policy toolkit (cluster them — finance / market / technology / formalisation)

- **Finance:** **MUDRA (2015** — Shishu/Kishore/Tarun; **Tarun Plus ₹20 lakh, 2024** ⭐); **CGTMSE** (guarantee cover raised to **₹10 crore, Budget 2025**); **SIDBI (1990)**; 59-minute loans; **ECLGS** (COVID lifeline, ~₹3.7 lakh cr disbursed); Self-Reliant India Fund; **Budget 2025:** customised **credit cards (₹5 lakh) for micro units**, Mutual Credit Guarantee for ₹100-cr loans.
- **Market access:** Public Procurement Policy — **25% of CPSE/govt purchases from MSEs (incl. 4% SC/ST, 3% women)** ⭐; GeM; ODOP.
- **Technology/quality:** ZED certification, LEAN, CHAMPIONS platform, **RAMP (2022, World Bank, ₹6,062 cr)**.
- **Traditional/artisan:** SFURTI clusters, PMEGP (KVIC), **PM Vishwakarma (2023, ₹13,000 cr, 18 trades)** ⭐.
- **Formalisation:** **Udyam (2020, free, self-declaration)** + Udyam Assist (informal micro units via lenders).
- Committees to name-drop: **Nayak (working capital = 20% of turnover)**, **Abid Hussain 1997 (SSI)**, **U.K. Sinha RBI 2019** (₹5,000-cr distressed-asset fund, fund-of-funds) ⭐.

## 4. Industrial sickness & the support mechanism ⭐⭐ (asked as a standalone question)

- **Definition (SICA 1985):** industrial company with **accumulated losses ≥ entire net worth**. For MSMEs (RBI): NPA 3+ months or serious net-worth erosion; "incipient sickness" = stress before NPA.
- **Causes:** internal (management failure, under-capitalisation, obsolete tech, diversion of funds) vs external (input/power costs, demand shocks, policy change, **delayed payments** — the MSME-specific cause).
- **Old regime:** **SICA 1985 → BIFR (1987) + AAIFR** — became a debtors' shelter: endless moratoria, ~most references died of delay ("hospital that rarely discharged"). **SICA repealed; BIFR abolished 1 Dec 2016** ⭐.
- **New regime — resolution, not rehabilitation-forever:**
  - **IBC 2016** ⭐⭐: NCLT-run CIRP, **180+90 days (330-day outer limit)**, Committee of Creditors, Sec 29A (promoter bar); **pre-packaged insolvency for MSMEs (2021)** ⭐; behavioural effect — debtors settle to avoid losing the company.
  - **Bank-side tools:** DRTs (1993), **SARFAESI 2002**, ARCs + **NARCL "bad bank" (2021)**, RBI **Prudential Framework (7 June 2019)**, COVID restructuring windows (**K.V. Kamath** parameters).
  - **MSME-specific:** **Framework for Revival & Rehabilitation of MSMEs (2015)** — bank-level committees, correction plans for accounts ≤ ₹25 cr; restructuring without downgrade windows (2019–21).
- Evaluation line: from **"nurse sick units indefinitely" (BIFR) to "resolve fast, redeploy capital" (IBC)** — recovery ≈ 30-plus % and, more importantly, deterrence; but delays are creeping back (NCLT capacity) — reform is maintenance, not a one-off.

## 5. AP box ⭐⭐ — MSME Andhra

- Craft/GI clusters to cite: **Kondapalli & Etikoppaka toys, Machilipatnam imitation jewellery & Kalamkari (Pedana), Mangalagiri–Venkatagiri–Dharmavaram handlooms, Eluru pile carpets** — SFURTI/ODOP material; plus Nellore aqua-feed, Vizag pharma ancillaries.
- **AP MSME & Entrepreneur Development Policy 4.0 (2024-29)** — with the flagship **"One Family, One Entrepreneur"** goal ⭐; MSME parks programme across constituencies; Ratan Tata Innovation Hub (announced 2025) for startups (File 15).
- AP is consistently among the **top states in Udyam registrations relative to size**; delayed-payment and power-cost relief are the two demands AP MSME associations raise most — use as "problems" evidence.

## 7. Mains angles

1. **"MSMEs are India's employment answer but its productivity problem." Examine** — contribution stats → dwarfism/informality diagnosis → 2025 redefinition + credit/market toolkit → verdict: shift from protection to growth-incentives.
2. **Delayed payments as the MSME sector's biggest tax** — 45-day rule, Samadhaan, TReDS, 43B(h); why enforcement stays hard (buyer power); suggest reforms.
3. **"From BIFR to IBC: India stopped nursing sickness and started resolving it." Critically evaluate** — old vs new regime table, recovery/deterrence evidence, creeping delays, MSME pre-pack.
4. **AP tie-in:** design an MSME strategy for AP — clusters + GI crafts + aqua/food processing + "One Family, One Entrepreneur" + park infrastructure; constraints: credit depth, power cost, marketing.
