# 18 · State Finance Commission and Local Finance in Andhra Pradesh

## 0. Frame

- The 73rd/74th Amendments (1992) created a **third tier** of government but left its **money** to two referees: the **State Finance Commission** (state → local devolution) and the **Union Finance Commission** (topping up local bodies from the Centre). This file covers both referees and the actual finances of AP's panchayats and municipalities.

## 1. The constitutional frame ⭐⭐

| Provision | Content |
|---|---|
| **Art 243-I** | Governor constitutes a **State Finance Commission every five years** — principles for (a) sharing state taxes with panchayats, (b) assignment of taxes, (c) grants-in-aid; ATR before the legislature |
| **Art 243-Y** | the same SFC covers **municipalities** |
| **Art 280(3)(bb) & (c)** | the **Union FC must recommend measures to augment states' Consolidated Funds to supplement local-body resources — *on the basis of SFC recommendations*** ⭐ (the SFC-UFC bridge) |
| **XI & XII Schedules** | 29 panchayat / 18 municipal functional subjects — the "functions" whose funding is at stake |
| The 3F test ⭐ | **Funds, Functions, Functionaries** — devolution is real only when all three move |

## 2. What an SFC is supposed to do

- Recommend a **global share** (vertical devolution) of the state's own revenue for local bodies + an **inter-se distribution** (horizontal), **tax assignments** (which levies locals may collect), **grants-in-aid**, and reforms to strengthen local resource mobilization — the state-level mirror of the Union FC ⭐.

## 3. The all-India SFC problem (use as context) ⭐

- **Irregular constitution & delays** — most states are on their 2nd-4th SFC where the 6th-7th should be sitting; reports delayed, ATRs later still, recommendations cherry-picked.
- Weak data on local finances; **"mission creep"** (SFCs loaded with unrelated ToRs); recommendations routinely "accepted in principle" and underfunded.
- The Union FC's response — **conditionality**: 15th FC made local-body grants contingent on **online accounts (PRIA Soft/audited statements) and property-tax floor rates**, and required **SFC constitution + ATR (by March 2024) as an entry condition from 2024-25** ⭐⭐; the 16th FC continues the compliance-based approach (File 15).

## 4. AP's SFC record ⭐

- Composite-state era: SFCs from **1994** onward; the **3rd SFC's benchmark recommendation — devolve 6.7% of the state's tax + non-tax revenue to local bodies, split 72.25% PRIs : 27.75% ULBs** ⭐⭐ — is still the number quoted in AP local-finance debates.
- **Implementation gap (CAG-documented):** of ≈**₹1,274 crore/year** the SFC recommended, only ≈**₹113.6 crore** was accepted as direct devolution, ≈₹664.6 cr was "treated as fulfilled" through existing budget heads, and ≈₹496 cr rejected — the classic dilution pattern ⭐.
- Post-bifurcation AP's SFC cycle has been **irregular** (constitution/reports delayed alongside delayed local-body elections in the 2019-21 period); the 15th/16th-FC entry conditions have forced regularisation — **verify the latest SFC's number, chair and ATR status from the AP Panchayat Raj & Municipal departments before the exam** (a favourite factual question).

## 5. Local government structure in AP (the units being financed)

- **Rural (3-tier):** ≈**13,000+ gram panchayats → 660 mandal parishads → 13 zilla parishads** (the 2022 reorganisation into 26 revenue districts has not been mirrored in ZP structure — label & verify) ⭐.
- **Urban:** ≈120+ ULBs — municipal corporations (GVMC Visakhapatnam the largest; Vijayawada, Guntur, Kurnool, Tirupati…), municipalities and nagar panchayats.
- Delivery overlay: **village/ward secretariats (2019- ; ≈15,000)** with functionaries at doorstep — an administrative innovation that also **bypasses elected local bodies** (the "parallel institutions" critique ⭐; the earlier Janmabhoomi committees drew the same fire).

## 6. Local finance in AP — where the money comes from

| Source | Content | Health |
|---|---|---|
| **Own tax** | **property tax** (ULBs & GPs — AP moved to a **capital-value base, 2021**, with protests ⭐), vacant-land tax, advertisement tax | under-assessed; collection efficiency low |
| **Own non-tax** | water/drainage charges, market/shop rents, layout & building fees | user charges politically capped |
| **Assigned revenues** | **seigniorage (minor-mineral cess), surcharge on stamp duty, entertainment tax, profession-tax share** | volatile, routed via state |
| **SFC devolution & state grants** | per-capita grants, honoraria support | the §4 gap |
| **Union FC grants** ⭐ | 15th FC tied/untied local-body grants; **16th FC (2026-31): AP rural LBs ₹16,627 cr + urban LBs ₹12,158 cr** + **Vijayawada & Visakhapatnam wastewater grants ≤₹5,000 cr each** (File 15) | now the most reliable flow — but **conditional** |
| Scheme flows | MGNREGA/VB-G RAM G, SBM, AMRUT, NRLM-SERP etc. | tied; local bodies as agents |

- Plumbing: releases ride the state's **CFMS**; GP accounts on **PRIA Soft/e-GramSwaraj** — the 15th/16th-FC accounts-online condition ⭐.

## 7. Issues (the Mains checklist)

1. **Fiscal dependency:** own revenue is a small fraction of local spending — grants dominate; local bodies are **spenders, not governments**, without the 3Fs.
2. **The SFC dilution cycle** (§4): recommend → accept-in-principle → underfund.
3. **Parallel structures:** secretariat/volunteer systems and mission-mode agencies deliver *around* elected sarpanches/councils ⭐.
4. **Untied vs tied:** Union-FC untied shares shrink relative to conditional/tied flows; local priorities crowded out.
5. **Capacity:** staff vacancies, accounting, DPR-writing — the binding constraint on absorbing even the grants that come.
6. **Property-tax politics:** the 2021 capital-value reform shows the ceiling on own-revenue reform.
7. **Election delays** interrupt both legitimacy and (via FC conditions) grant eligibility ⭐.

## 8. Prelims nuggets

1. **Art 243-I — SFC every five years; Art 243-Y extends it to municipalities** ⭐.
2. **Art 280(3)(bb)/(c)** — Union FC augments state funds for local bodies **on SFC recommendations**.
3. AP 3rd SFC benchmark: **6.7% of state tax+non-tax revenue; 72.25 : 27.75 PRI:ULB split** ⭐⭐.
4. **15th FC entry conditions:** online accounts + property-tax floor; **SFC constituted with ATR — mandatory from 2024-25** ⭐.
5. **16th FC grants to AP local bodies: ₹16,627 cr (rural) + ₹12,158 cr (urban), 2026-31**; wastewater grants **≤₹5,000 cr each** for **Vijayawada & Visakhapatnam** ⭐.
6. AP rural tiers: **GP → mandal parishad (660) → zilla parishad (13)**.
7. **Village/ward secretariats (2019): ≈15,000** — service delivery at the doorstep.
8. AP property tax shifted to a **capital-value basis (2021)**.
9. **3F = funds, functions, functionaries**; XI Schedule 29 subjects, XII Schedule 18.

## 9. Mains angles

1. **"State Finance Commissions are the weakest link in India's fiscal federalism." Examine with reference to Andhra Pradesh.** Constitutional design (§1-2) → all-India pathology (§3) → AP's 6.7% benchmark vs CAG-documented dilution (§4) → UFC conditionality as the corrective → verdict + fixes (statutory timelines, SFC cells, data systems).
2. **Assess the state of local finance in AP.** Source map (§6) → dependency + §7 issues → the 16th-FC conditional-grant era → reform menu (property-tax modernisation, user charges, PPP at ULB level, strengthening 3Fs).
3. **"Doorstep delivery is not devolution." Critically evaluate AP's secretariat-volunteer model against the Panchayati Raj vision.** Efficiency gains vs elected-body bypass → Janmabhoomi precedent → how to reconcile (secretariats as GP staff, activity mapping).
