## 0. The question, framed properly

"AP is bankrupt" is a **political** claim; this file runs the **technical audit**. A state's fiscal health is not one number — it is a panel of indicators, each with a benchmark. The honest answer to *"is it really that bad?"* is: **genuinely stressed on almost every indicator, but not insolvent, and not in the worst tier of states.** The distinction between *stress* and *insolvency* — and between *official* and *effective* liabilities — is exactly what a Mains answer must show.

> **Numbers policy:** every figure below is year-labelled (BE = budget estimate, RE = revised estimate). Re-verify from the latest PRS state-budget analysis, RBI *State Finances: A Study of Budgets*, and CAG State Accounts before the exam — budget numbers move; the framework does not.

## 1. The measuring instruments (what "bad" means, technically) ⭐

| Indicator | What it tests | Benchmark |
|---|---|---|
| **Revenue deficit (RD)** | Is borrowing funding *consumption*? (golden-rule violation) | 0% — a state should not borrow to pay salaries/subsidies |
| **Fiscal deficit (FD)** | Overall borrowing pace | **FRBM ≈3% of GSDP** (+0.5% power-reform window) |
| **Debt / GSDP** | Stock sustainability | 15th FC glide path for states (falling, low-30s%); FRBM ideal ~20–25% |
| **Interest payments / revenue receipts (IP/RR)** | The "interest treadmill" | ≤10% comfortable; >15% stressed |
| **Committed expenditure / RR** (salary + pension + interest) | Fiscal space left over | Lower is better; >60% = little discretionary room |
| **Guarantees & off-budget borrowings** | Hidden/contingent leverage | CAG/RBI flag anything near ~10% of GSDP |
| **BE → RE slippage** | *Credibility* of the budget itself | Deficits should not routinely worsen mid-year |
| **Domar condition (r vs g)** | Dynamic sustainability | Nominal growth > effective interest rate stabilizes the ratio |

## 2. The prosecution — the numbers that ARE bad ⭐⭐

### 2.1 A structural revenue deficit, every year since birth
- AP has run a **revenue deficit in every single year since bifurcation (2014-15)** — the state borrows for current spending, the textbook golden-rule violation. Root cause is structural: **the 2014 bifurcation left Hyderabad's revenue engine in Telangana** while AP kept ~58% of the population.
- Recent path: **RD 3.0% of GSDP (2024-25 RE) → 1.8% BE 2025-26 (₹33,186 cr) → slipped to 2.3% in RE → 1.1% BE 2026-27 (₹22,003 cr)**. Note the pattern: budgeted improvement, mid-year slippage.

### 2.2 Fiscal deficit persistently above the FRBM norm
- **FD 4.6% (2024-25 RE) → 4.4% BE 2025-26 (₹79,927 cr) → 3.8% BE 2026-27 (₹75,868 cr)** — above the ~3% ceiling throughout, absorbed via central consents and one-off windows.

### 2.3 A debt stock high and (until recently) rising
- Official **outstanding liabilities: ~33% of GSDP (2021-22) → ~36% (2024-25); ~36% estimated for 2026-27** — against a 15th FC path that expected states to *descend* through the low-30s. RBI's comparison: AP at **32.5% (2022-23) vs 30.7% for the median large state**.
- In rupees: official liabilities ≈**₹4.9 lakh crore (2024)**, roughly quadruple the ~₹1.2 lakh crore inherited at bifurcation.

### 2.4 The hidden balance sheet — the sharpest technical criticism ⭐
- **Guarantees outstanding ≈₹1,77,328 crore ≈10% of GSDP (as of Dec 2025)** — RBI had already flagged AP's contingent liabilities at **10.4% of GSDP (2021-22), roughly double the median state**.
- **Off-budget borrowings** via state corporations/SPVs (drink-and-development finance: Beverages Corporation, AP State Development Corporation securitising future revenues) are a **recurring CAG objection** — loans raised **without legislative appropriation**, ~₹33,000 crore identified by 2023-24 in CAG-based analyses, with larger contested estimates.
- The **2024 White Paper** (new government, July 2024) claimed **total debt + liabilities of ₹9.74 lakh crore** (vs ₹3.75 lakh crore in March 2019) once off-budget debt, guarantees and **₹1.35 lakh crore of unpaid bills/dues** are counted. Treat the number as *political and contested* — but the **transparency gap it exposes is itself the audit finding**: nobody can state AP's true liability figure within ±₹4 lakh crore.

### 2.5 The squeeze on fiscal space
- **Interest ₹34,998 cr ≈16% of revenue receipts + pensions ₹21,935 cr ≈10% (BE 2025-26)** — over a quarter of revenue gone before salaries; with salaries, committed expenditure eats the bulk of revenue receipts.
- White Paper arithmetic for FY25: committed expenditure **₹1.64 lakh crore vs untied revenues ₹1.45 lakh crore** — a **₹19,107 crore gap before one rupee of discretionary spending**.
- Cash-flow symptom: **two-thirds of the year's borrowing limit exhausted in five months of 2025-26** (CAG monthly accounts).

### 2.6 Independent risk certification
- RBI's *State Finances: A Risk Analysis* (June 2022) put **AP among the 10 fiscally stressed states**, with a **gross borrowing requirement of 6.1% of GSDP — second only to Punjab**, and a **baseline debt path that does not stabilize** unless growth rises or the primary deficit falls.
- Standing pressure: the **Super Six** welfare package (₹15,000 to school mothers — *Talliki Vandanam*, ₹9,407 cr allocated; ₹20,000 to farmers — *Annadata Sukhibhava*, ₹6,300 cr; ₹1,500/month to women; free bus travel; 3 free cylinders; unemployment allowance) is being **rolled out in phases precisely because full-year cost estimates approach ₹1 lakh crore** — the constraint is visible in the sequencing.

## 3. The defence — what "bankrupt" gets wrong ⭐⭐

1. **Insolvency is the wrong category.** Under **Art. 293(3)** an indebted state borrows only with the Centre's consent, RBI is the debt manager, and SDL coupons are effectively never missed. States face **illiquidity and compression**, not sovereign-style default. Technically: AP has a *flow* problem (revenue deficit) and a *credibility* problem (off-budget), not a *default* problem.
2. **Not the worst tier.** RBI's "highly stressed five" are **Punjab, Rajasthan, Kerala, West Bengal, Bihar** — AP sits in the next band. Punjab's debt ratio is near ~47–50% of GSDP; AP's mid-30s. All-states average debt ≈**27.5% of GDP (March 2025)** — AP is worse than average, not an outlier.
3. **The denominator is working.** Nominal GSDP is growing ~12–14% (**₹18.3 lakh crore BE 2025-26 → ₹19.75 lakh crore projected 2026-27**); the state claims **11.28% real growth in Q2 2025-26 vs 8.7% national**. In Domar terms, **g comfortably exceeds r** — the ratio *can* stabilize if the primary deficit compresses.
4. **The flow trajectory is improving on paper:** FD 4.6% → 4.4% → 3.8%; RD 3.0% → 1.8% → 1.1% (BEs) — right direction, even if BE→RE slippage means discounting the promises.
5. **Own effort is not collapsing:** own tax revenue **₹1,09,007 cr BE 2025-26 (+15%, ≈6% of GSDP)**, SGST the largest head (37%).
6. **Creditworthiness signals:** the **World Bank ($800 mn) + ADB (~$790 mn) Amaravati package (approved Dec 2024)** — multilateral money, via the sovereign channel, does not flow to a state judged unbankable.
7. **The origin is partly exogenous:** bifurcation without Special Category Status, with Polavaram nationalised as compensation and 14th/15th FC **post-devolution revenue-deficit grants** as explicit recognition that AP's revenue base was amputated by statute, not only mismanaged.

## 4. Verdict table ⭐ (the Mains close)

| Indicator | AP (year) | Benchmark | Verdict |
|---|---|---|---|
| Revenue deficit | 1.1–3.0% of GSDP, every year since 2014-15 | 0% | **Fail — structural** |
| Fiscal deficit | 3.8–4.6% (FY25–FY27) | ~3% FRBM | **Breach, narrowing** |
| Debt/GSDP (official) | ~34–36% (FY25–FY27) | falling low-30s (15th FC) | **High, drifting up** |
| + Guarantees | ~10% of GSDP (Dec 2025) | ~½ that for median state | **Red flag** |
| + Off-budget | ₹33k cr (CAG) to several lakh cr (contested) | ~0, on-books | **Transparency failure** |
| IP/RR | ~16% (BE FY26) | ≤10–15% | **Stressed** |
| Budget credibility | RD/FD slip BE→RE each year | none | **Weak** |
| Growth (g vs r) | nominal ~12–14% | > effective interest ~7–8% | **Pass — the lifeline** |
| RBI classification | stressed-10, not worst-5 | — | **Bad, not worst** |

**Three-line verdict:** Yes, the alarm is technically well-founded — a decade-long structural revenue deficit, FRBM breaches, a mid-30s-and-rising debt ratio with another ~10% of GSDP in guarantees on top, and books opaque enough that the true liability number is contested by lakhs of crores. But "bankrupt" is wrong in category and degree: states under Art. 293(3) do not default; in the Punjab–Kerala–West Bengal comparison AP is not the worst; and double-digit growth gives it a Domar escape route the truly stressed states lack. **The binding problems are the *quality* of the deficit (borrowing for consumption) and the *credibility* of the accounts (off-budget + slippage) — not imminent collapse.**

## 5. Prelims nuggets

1. AP has run a **revenue deficit every year since bifurcation (2014-15)**.
2. **FD BE 2025-26 = 4.4% of GSDP (₹79,927 cr); RD = 1.8% (₹33,186 cr)**; 2026-27 BE: FD 3.8%, RD 1.1%.
3. Outstanding liabilities **~36% of GSDP (2024-25)**; official stock ≈**₹4.9 lakh crore (2024)**.
4. **Guarantees ≈₹1.77 lakh crore ≈10% of GSDP (Dec 2025)** — ~2× median state (RBI).
5. **RBI *State Finances: A Risk Analysis* (June 2022)** — AP in the stressed-10; **highly stressed 5 = Punjab, Rajasthan, Kerala, WB, Bihar** ⭐ (classic MCQ).
6. AP's **gross borrowing requirement 6.1% of GSDP — 2nd after Punjab (9.6%)** (RBI 2022).
7. **2024 White Paper: total debt + liabilities ₹9.74 lakh crore (June 2024)** incl. off-budget & ₹1.35 lakh cr dues — contested vs official ~₹4.9 lakh cr.
8. **Interest = 16% of revenue receipts; pension = 10% (BE 2025-26)**.
9. Own tax revenue **≈6% of GSDP; SGST largest head (37%)**; all-states debt ≈**27.5% of GDP (Mar 2025)**.
10. **Art. 293(3)** — Centre's consent gate on state borrowing = why states face *compression, not default*.
11. **Amaravati: World Bank $800 mn + ADB ≈$790 mn (Dec 2024)**; **Polavaram = centrally funded** (AP Reorganisation Act, 2014).
12. **Super Six**: Talliki Vandanam (₹15,000/mother, ₹9,407 cr), Annadata Sukhibhava (₹20,000/farmer), ₹1,500/month to women, free APSRTC travel, 3 LPG cylinders, jobs/₹3,000 allowance — phased rollout.

## 6. Mains angles

1. **"Andhra Pradesh's fiscal position is as precarious as its critics claim." Critically evaluate.** Framework (§1) → prosecution with year-labelled numbers (§2) → defence (§3) → verdict: stress ≠ insolvency; quality-of-deficit and transparency are the real diseases.
2. **"The true measure of a state's debt is what is NOT in its budget." Discuss with reference to AP.** Guarantees 10% of GSDP + CAG's off-budget objections + White-Paper-vs-official gap → prescription: guarantee-redemption fund, all borrowings on-budget, CAG real-time audit, FRBM disclosure schedules.
3. **Bifurcation and the making of a revenue-deficit state.** Hyderabad's loss → SCS denial → FC revenue-deficit grants + Polavaram as partial compensation → why structural handicap still required (but does not fully excuse) the borrowing surge.
4. **Welfare vs wallet: can DBT-heavy politics coexist with FRBM discipline in AP?** Super Six costs vs ₹19,107 cr committed-expenditure gap → growth-led revenue (g>r) as the only reconciliation → sequencing/targeting as the policy answer.
