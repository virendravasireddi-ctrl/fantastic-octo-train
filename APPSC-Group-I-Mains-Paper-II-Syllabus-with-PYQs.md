# APPSC Group-I Mains — Paper-II: History, Culture and Geography of India and Andhra Pradesh

### Syllabus (topic-wise) mapped to Previous Years' Questions

*Marks — 150 · Medium: English / Telugu · Time — 150 Minutes*
*Pattern: three sections; the candidate answers 15 questions, 5 compulsorily from each section; each question carries 10 marks and is set as an internal choice, **(a) OR (b)**.*

---

## About this file

This document takes the **Paper-II syllabus** from the APPSC Group-I Mains syllabus copy (dated 05.10.2018) and, **under each syllabus sub-heading, lists the previous-years' questions** that have been asked on that topic.

The question papers used are the three provided, all for Paper-II:

| Label used here | Paper code | Source file |
|---|---|---|
| **2020** | SAE/105 | `APG1MainsPaperII2020.pdf` |
| **2023** | SAE-105 | `APG1MainsPaperII2023.pdf` |

**How the mapping works** — the three sections of every paper correspond to the three parts of the syllabus:

- **Section I / A** → *Part A: History and Culture of India* (sub-headings 1–5)
- **Section II / B** → *Part B: History and Culture of Andhra Pradesh* (sub-headings 6–10)
- **Section III / C** → *Part C: Geography of India and Andhra Pradesh* (sub-headings 11–15)

Each numbered exam question is filed under the sub-heading it best fits. In the **2020** and **2023** papers the question number matches the sub-heading number one-to-one; in the **2025** paper the Section-II questions were re-ordered, so they are placed here by **content** (noted where relevant). Every question is quoted in English exactly as printed; both internal-choice options are kept together because they are alternatives to the same question.

# Part A — History and Culture of India

## 1. Pre-Historic Cultures → Mauryas → Guptas

**Syllabus:** Pre-Historic Cultures in India – Indus Valley Civilization – Vedic Culture – Mahajanapadas – Emergence of New Religions (Jainism, Buddhism) – Rise of Magadha and the Age of the Mauryas – Ashoka Dharma – Foreign Invasions on India – The Kushans. The Satavahanas, the Sangam Age in South India, the Sungas, the Guptas, the Kanauj and their contributions – Historical accounts of foreign travellers – Early educational institutions.

**Previous Year Questions**

- **2020, Q1:** (a) Critically explain the salient features of urban planning of Harappan Civilization. *(10)* **OR** (b) Critically explain the causes of the emergence of Buddhism in ancient India. *(10)*
- **2023, Q1:** (a) Examine the survival and continuity of Harappan culture in the present times. *(10)* **OR** (b) Examine the socio-religious life and administration of early India with respect to the writings of Chinese travellers such as Fa-Hien, Hiuen-Tsang and I-Tsang. *(10)*
- **2025, Q1:** (a) Discuss the origin and expansion of the Indus Valley Civilization. *(10)* **OR** (b) Briefly analyze the teachings of Buddha and Mahavira. *(10)*

## 2. Pallavas to Cholas → Delhi Sultanate → Bhakti & Sufi → Kakatiyas/Vijayanagara/Bahmanis/Qutub Shahis

**Syllabus:** The Pallavas, the Badami Chalukyas, the Eastern Chalukyas, the Rashtrakutas, the Kalyani Chalukyas and the Cholas – socio-cultural contributions, language, literature, art and architecture. Delhi Sultanates – Advent of Islam and its impact – Religious movements like Bhakti and Sufi and their influence. Growth of vernacular languages, scripts, literature, fine arts – socio-cultural conditions of the Kakatiyas, the Vijayanagaras, the Bahmanis, the Qutub Shahis and their contemporary South Indian kingdoms.

**Previous Year Questions**

- **2020, Q2:** (a) Draw an outline of society under the Eastern Chalukyas. *(10)* **OR** (b) Vividly examine the impact of Bhakti Movement during medieval India. *(10)*
- **2023, Q2:** (a) Examine the manner in which the Chola village administration had contributed to the growth of village autonomy as indicated by the Uttaramerur / Uttaramallur inscriptions. *(10)* **OR** (b) Explain Alauddin Khilji's market regulations and discuss their impact. *(10)*
- **2025, Q2:** (a) Describe the contributions of the Chalukya rulers of Kalyani in the fields of sculpture, architecture, and literature. *(10)* **OR** (b) Describe the characteristics of the Sufi movement and assess its impact on Indian culture. *(10)*

## 3. Mughals → Shivaji & the Marathas → Advent of Europeans → East India Company → Christian Missionaries

**Syllabus:** The Mughal administration, socio-religious life and cultural developments – Shivaji and the rise of the Maratha Empire – Advent of Europeans in India. Trade practices – Rise of the East India Company and its hegemony – Changes in administration, social and cultural spheres – Role of Christian missionaries.

**Previous Year Questions**

- **2020, Q3:** (a) Critically evaluate the central administration under Akbar. *(10)* **OR** (b) Draw an outline of the rise of the Maratha Empire with special reference to Shivaji's contribution. *(10)*
- **2023, Q3:** (a) Discuss the features of Mughal architecture and examine the influence of Hindu elements on Mughal architecture. *(10)* **OR** (b) "The state that was created by Shivaji was not only egalitarian in conception but also a welfare state in nature." Examine. *(10)*
- **2025, Q3:** (a) Discuss the establishment of the East India Company and its early operations in India. *(10)* **OR** (b) What impact did the activities of Christian missionaries have on Indian society and culture? *(10)*

## 4. British Rule 1757–1856 → Land Revenue Settlements → 1857 Revolt → Socio-Religious Reform → Rise of Nationalism → Gandhi, Bose, Patel, Ambedkar

**Syllabus:** Rise of British rule in India from 1757 to 1856 – Land Revenue Settlements (Permanent Settlement, Ryotwari and Mahalwari) – The 1857 Revolt and its impact – Education, press, cultural changes – Rise of national consciousness – Socio-religious reform movements in the 19th century (Raja Ram Mohan Roy, Dayananda Saraswathi, Swami Vivekananda, Annie Besant, Sir Syed Ahmad Khan and others). Rise of Indian nationalism – Indian National Congress – Vandemataram and Home Rule movements – Self-Respect Movement (Jyotiba Phule, Narayana Guru, Periyar) – Role of Mahatma Gandhi, Subhash Chandra Bose, Vallabhbhai Patel – Satyagraha – Quit India Movement – Dr B.R. Ambedkar and his contributions.

**Previous Year Questions**

- **2020, Q4:** (a) Critically explain the role of the Subsidiary Alliance system in the rise and expansion of British Raj in India. *(10)* **OR** (b) Delineate the contribution of Dr. B.R. Ambedkar with special reference to the conditions of untouchables and labourers in colonial India. *(10)*
- **2023, Q4:** (a) Explain the land revenue systems that were introduced by the British. Assess the impact of these revenue systems on the Indian economy. *(10)* **OR** (b) Assess the socio-religious reform movements in the Muslim community during the 19th century. Discuss the contributions made by Sayyid Ahmad Khan towards the upliftment of the Muslim community. *(10)*
- **2025, Q4:** (a) What do you understand by the Permanent Settlement, and what impact did it have on Indian society? Comment. *(10)* **OR** (b) What do you understand by the 'safety valve' theory? Explain it in the context of the Indian National Congress. *(10)*

## 5. Indian Nationalism in Three Phases → Peasant/Women/Tribal/Workers Movements → Communalism → Independence, Partition & Reorganization

**Syllabus:** Indian nationalism in three phases – Freedom Struggle 1885–1905, 1905–1920 and the Gandhi Phase 1920–1947 – Peasant, women, tribal and workers' movements – Role of different parties in the freedom struggle – Local and regional movements – Inter-religious unity and communalism. Independence and Partition of India – India after Independence – Rehabilitation after partition – Linguistic re-organization of states – Integration of the Indian states – Indian Constitution – Economic policies – Foreign policy initiatives.

**Previous Year Questions**

- **2020, Q5:** (a) Examine the recommendations of the Cabinet Mission Plan. *(10)* **OR** (b) Critically evaluate the impact of the Quit India Movement of 1942. *(10)*
- **2023, Q5:** (a) Explain the factors that led to the peasant movement in India and examine its impact on the national movement in India. *(10)* **OR** (b) Has communalism indirectly helped the British to perpetuate their control over India? Examine the indicative traits of the communalists and assess their impact on India's modern history. *(10)*
- **2025, Q5:** (a) Discuss the role and contributions of the moderate leaders of Congress from 1885 to 1905. *(10)* **OR** (b) Describe the major movements led by Mahatma Gandhi between 1920 and 1947. *(10)*

# Part B — History and Culture of Andhra Pradesh

## 6. Ancient Andhra — Satavahanas, Ikshvakus, Salankayanas, Pallavas, Vishnukundins; Eastern Chalukyas, Rashtrakutas, Renati Cholas

**Syllabus:** *Ancient:* The Satavahanas, the Ikshvakus, the Salankayanas, the Pallavas and the Vishnukundins – social and economic conditions – religion, language (Telugu), literature, art and architecture – Jainism and Buddhism in Andhra. The Eastern Chalukyas, the Rashtrakutas, the Renati Cholas and others – socio-cultural life, religion – Telugu script and language, literature, art and architecture.

**Previous Year Questions**

- **2020, Q6:** (a) Discuss the salient features of the agrarian system under the Satavahanas. *(10)* **OR** (b) Write a note on art and architecture under the Pallavas. *(10)*
- **2023, Q6:** (a) Discuss the socio-religious conditions during the Satavahana period with special reference to the prominence bestowed on women. *(10)* **OR** (b) Explain the re-structuring of the socio-religious life of the Eastern Chalukyas with reference to the impact caused by immigrations, caste mobility and social awakening. *(10)*
- **2025, Q7:** (a) What are the various stories about the origins of the Andhra people and the Andhra language? *(10)* **OR** (b) How did far trade routes contribute to the exchange of Buddhist ideas in ancient Andhra? *(10)*

## 7. Medieval Andhra — Andhradesa 1000–1565 AD; Telugu literature (Kavitraya, Ashtadiggajas); Kakatiyas, Reddis, Gajapatis, Vijayanagaras; monuments; Qutub Shahis; Vemana

**Syllabus:** *Medieval:* Socio-cultural and religious conditions in Andhradesa, 1000–1565 A.D. – Antiquity, origin and growth of Telugu language and literature (Kavitraya, Ashtadiggajas) – Fine arts, art and architecture during the reign of the Kakatiyas, Reddis, Gajapatis and Vijayanagaras and their feudatories. Historical monuments and their significance – Contribution of the Qutub Shahis to Andhra history and culture – Regional literature – Praja Kavi Vemana and others.

**Previous Year Questions**

- **2020, Q7:** (a) Write a brief note on the architecture during the period of the Vijayanagar Empire. *(10)* **OR** (b) "The foundation of the Vijayanagara Empire coincided in point of time with the outburst of a momentous literary movement in South India." Discuss it. *(10)*
- **2023, Q7:** (a) Examine the socio-religious and cultural life of the Vijayanagara kingdom with respect to the writings of foreign travellers such as Nicolo Conti, Abdur Razzak, Duarte Barbosa and Domingo Paes. *(10)* **OR** (b) Examine the socio-cultural and religious conditions of the Reddi kingdoms with specific reference to the development of Telugu literature. *(10)*
- **2025, Q8:** (a) Discuss the growth of various Saivite sects during the Kakatiya rule. *(10)* **OR** (b) Discuss the significance of the Warangal Fort complex as a historical monument. *(10)*

## 8. Modern Andhra — European trade; Company rule; missionaries; C.P. Brown, Munro, Mackenzie; Zamindari/Poligari; Social Reformers; Library Movement; Folk & Tribal culture

**Syllabus:** *Modern:* European trade establishments in Andhra – Andhra under Company rule – Role of Christian missionaries – Socio-cultural and literary awakening (C.P. Brown, Thomas Munro, Mackenzie) – Zamindari and Poligari systems – Native states and little kings. Role of social reformers – Gurajada Apparao, Kandukuri Veeresalingam, Raghupati Venkataratnam Naidu, Gidugu Ramamurthy, Annie Besant and others – Library Movement in Andhra Pradesh – Role of the newspaper – Folk and tribal culture, oral traditions, subaltern culture, role of women.

**Previous Year Questions**

- **2020, Q8:** (a) Write a brief account of the library movement in Andhra Pradesh. *(10)* **OR** (b) Critically evaluate the contribution of Kandukuri Veeresalingam in the light of the social reform movement in Andhra Pradesh. *(10)*
- **2023, Q8:** (a) Write a short note on the following: (i) Gurajada Apparao's contribution to social reform through the medium of literature *(5)*; (ii) Gidugu Ramamurthy's pioneering efforts to strengthen the Vyavaharika Bhasha movement *(5)*. **OR** (b) Write a short note on the following East India Company officials and their contributions: (i) Sir Thomas Munro; (ii) Sir Arthur Cotton; (iii) Charles Phillip Brown. *(10)*
- **2025, Q9:** (a) Discuss the role of missionaries in the growth and spread of education in the Andhra region. *(10)* **OR** (b) What factors contributed to the process of social awakening in the Andhra region in the second half of the 19th century? *(10)*

## 9. Nationalist Movement in Andhra — Justice Party, Non-Brahmin & revolutionary literature; Andhra Mahasabhas; Alluri Sitaramaraju; Potti Sreeramulu; Andhra State 1953 → AP 1956 → Bifurcation

**Syllabus:** *Nationalist Movement:* Role of Andhra leaders – Justice Party, Non-Brahmin Movement – Nationalist and revolutionary literature (Gurram Jashuva, Boyi Bheemanna, Sri Sri, Garimella Satyanarayana, Rayaprolu Subbarao, Unnava Lakshminarayana, Tripuraneni Ramaswamy Choudhary and others). Andhra Mahasabhas, Andhra Movement and its prominent leaders (Alluri Sitaramaraju, Duggirala Gopalakrishnaiah, Konda Venkatappayya, Pattabhi Seetaramaiah, Ponaka Kanakamma, Dokka Sitamma) – Grandhalaya (Library) Movement – Potti Sreeramulu and the formation of Andhra State, 1953 – Emergence of Andhra Pradesh, 1956 – Andhra Pradesh 1956 to 2014 – Causes of bifurcation and the impact of 2nd June 2014.

**Previous Year Questions**

- **2020, Q9:** (a) Critically explain the circumstances which led to the formation of the Justice Party. *(10)* **OR** (b) Critically explain the circumstances which led to the formation of Andhra Pradesh in the 1950s. *(10)*
- **2023, Q9:** (a) Examine the role of Alluri Sitarama Raju as the leader of the Rampa Rebellion. *(10)* **OR** (b) The evolution of Telugu Dalit literature has led to an ideological narrative against caste discrimination, and for a just and equal society with focus on self-respect and dignity. In this connection, examine the contributions made by the Dalit poets. *(10)*
- **2025, Q10:** (a) What were the provisions of the Gentlemen's Agreement of 1956? How far could they satisfy the apprehensions of the Telangana region? *(10)* **OR** (b) Outline the role of the students in raising major issues in the 1969 Telangana agitation. *(10)*

## 10. Andhra Pradesh — Bifurcation and its Implications; Loss of Capital & Building the New Capital; Division of Employees; AP Reorganization Act 2014

**Syllabus:** *Andhra Pradesh:* Bifurcation of Andhra Pradesh and its administrative, economic, social, political, cultural and legal implications – Loss of the capital city, building of a new capital and its financial implications – Division of employees and their native issues – Effect of bifurcation on trade & commerce and industry – Implications for the financial resources of the state government. Developmental opportunities – Socio-economic, cultural and demographic impact of bifurcation – Impact on river-water sharing and other link issues – Andhra Pradesh Reorganization Act, 2014 and the arbitrariness of certain provisions.

**Previous Year Questions**

- **2020, Q10:** (a) Discuss the revenue and infrastructural challenges for new Andhra Pradesh after bifurcation in 2014. *(10)* **OR** (b) Discuss the impact of the bifurcation of Andhra Pradesh (2014) on trade and commerce. *(10)*
- **2023, Q10:** (a) Explain the inconsistencies of the Andhra Pradesh Reorganization Act 2014 that had adversely affected the residuary state of Andhra Pradesh with regard to the apportionment of assets and liabilities. *(10)* **OR** (b) Examine the principles envisaged under Schedule Eleven of the Andhra Pradesh Reorganization Act 2014, with reference to the functioning of the river management boards. *(10)*
- **2025, Q6:** (a) Examine the impact of the loss of Hyderabad as the capital city on post-bifurcation Andhra Pradesh. How did this transition affect the state's administrative, economic, and socio-cultural landscape? *(10)* **OR** (b) Discuss the vision behind building a new capital for Andhra Pradesh after the bifurcation in 2014. Critically assess the financial implications involved in the planning, development, and partial execution of Amaravati as the capital city. *(10)*

# Part C — Geography of India and Andhra Pradesh

## 11. Physical Features and Resources

**Syllabus:** Physical features and resources of India and Andhra Pradesh – major landforms, climatic changes, soil types, rivers, water, streams, geology, rocks, mineral resources, metals, clays, construction materials, reservoirs, dams, forests, mountains, hills, flora and fauna, plateau forests, hill forests, vegetation classification.

**Previous Year Questions**

- **2020, Q11:** (a) Give a brief account of the geographical features of Andhra Pradesh. *(10)* **OR** (b) Give a brief account of the drainage system of Andhra Pradesh. *(10)*
- **2023, Q11:** (a) What are the climatic controls? How do they influence India's climate? *(10)* **OR** (b) Describe the characteristic features of the Black and Alluvial soils of Andhra Pradesh. *(10)*
- **2025, Q11:** (a) Explain the physical features of India and Andhra Pradesh, especially the major landforms and geographical characteristics. *(10)* **OR** (b) Examine the features and importance of the major landforms of Andhra Pradesh with special reference to the Coastal Plains, Eastern Ghats, and Deccan Plateau. *(10)*

## 12. Economic Geography

**Syllabus:** Agriculture, livestock, forestry, fishery, quarrying, mining, household manufacturing, industries (agro, mineral, forest, fuel and man-power), trade and commerce, communication, road transport, storage and others.

**Previous Year Questions**

- **2020, Q12:** (a) Critically analyse the components and impacts of the Green Revolution in India. *(10)* **OR** (b) Give a concise description of fisheries management and development in India. *(10)*
- **2023, Q12:** (a) What are the objectives and key features of the Sagarmala Project? Why is it important? *(10)* **OR** (b) Discuss the role of sea-ports in the economic development of Andhra Pradesh. *(10)*
- **2025, Q12:** (a) Discuss the background of agriculture in Andhra Pradesh and critically examine how key agricultural inputs such as rainfall, temperature, irrigation and power are likely to be affected by climate change. *(10)* **OR** (b) Highlight the industrial importance and major manufacturing sectors of Andhra Pradesh. *(10)*

## 13. Social Geography

**Syllabus:** Population movements and distribution, human habitations, density, age, sex, rural, urban, race, caste, tribe, religion, linguistic composition, urban migration, education characteristics.

**Previous Year Questions**

- **2020, Q13:** (a) Write a brief note on the rural and urban population of Andhra Pradesh in the light of the Census of India 2011. *(10)* **OR** (b) Write a brief note on the tribal population of Andhra Pradesh in the context of the census report of 2011. *(10)*
- **2023, Q13:** (a) Give a description of the 'demographic dividend' in the Indian context. *(10)* **OR** (b) What are the different types of rural settlements and the factors affecting them? Add a note on rural settlement patterns in India. *(10)*
- **2025, Q13:** (a) Discuss the spatial patterns of population distribution and urbanisation in Andhra Pradesh. *(10)* **OR** (b) Analyse the composition and spatial distribution of Scheduled Castes (SCs), Scheduled Tribes (STs), and religious communities in Andhra Pradesh, as per Census 2011. *(10)*

## 14. Fauna and Floral Geography

**Syllabus:** Wild animals, animals, birds, reptiles, mammals, trees and plants and others.

**Previous Year Questions**

- **2020, Q14:** (a) In the light of the All India Tiger Estimation Report 2018, critically evaluate Project Tiger. *(10)* **OR** (b) Briefly describe the flora and fauna of the National Parks in Andhra Pradesh. *(10)*
- **2023, Q14:** (a) Discuss various forest conservation strategies in India. *(10)* **OR** (b) Discuss various in-situ conservation strategies of biodiversity in Andhra Pradesh. *(10)*
- **2025, Q14:** (a) Discuss the key aspects of wildlife conservation and biodiversity in Andhra Pradesh. *(10)* **OR** (b) Discuss the vulnerability of Andhra Pradesh to cyclones and floods, highlighting the causes, regional patterns, and impacts. *(10)* *(Option (b) also relates to sub-heading 15 — Environmental Geography / natural hazards.)*

## 15. Environmental Geography

**Syllabus:** Sustainable development, globalization, temperature, humidity, cloudiness, winds, special weather phenomena, natural hazards (earthquakes, landslides, floods, cyclones, cloudbursts), disaster management, impact assessment, environmental pollution, pollution management.

**Previous Year Questions**

- **2020, Q15:** (a) Give a brief account of the factors responsible for environmental pollution. *(10)* **OR** (b) Give a brief account of the concept and importance of Sustainable Development. *(10)*
- **2023, Q15:** (a) What is meant by Environment Impact Assessment? Evaluate it. Also give suggestions to make it effective. *(10)* **OR** (b) Define 'Drought'. What are its different types? Add a note on drought-prone areas in India. *(10)*
- **2025, Q15:** (a) Analyse the disaster vulnerability of the Rayalaseema region in Andhra Pradesh with respect to droughts. Discuss the socio-economic consequences. *(10)* **OR** (b) Assess the role of the Andhra Pradesh Pollution Control Board (APPCB) in post-disaster pollution management. *(10)*
